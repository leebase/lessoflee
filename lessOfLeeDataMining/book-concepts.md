# Five Candidate Book Concepts

These are editorial concepts based on the mined archive. They are not final outlines yet. The story candidates are still leads, concepts still need human selection, and each book needs curated story beats, a timeline, and chapter maps before drafting.

## 1. Former Diabetic

**Working promise**: A source-backed memoir/practical book about moving from Type 2 diabetes diagnosis to medication-free remission, relapse risk, and renewed recovery.

**Core reader**: A Type 2 diabetic or prediabetic who feels trapped by permanent-disease language and needs hope with receipts.

**Book spine**:

1. The terrifying diagnosis and A1c reality.
2. Rejecting permanent sickness as identity.
3. Food, fasting, movement, and labs as feedback.
4. Getting off medication and staying honest about maintenance.
5. Relapse, recommitment, and what remission actually requires.

- Clear language around personal experience versus medical advice.

## 2. Start Where You Are, Make Progress

**Working promise**: A mindset and change book built around Lee's repeated mantras: start where you are, make progress, a little more, a bit farther, always.

**Core reader**: Someone who is too overwhelmed to begin because the destination looks impossibly far away.

**Book spine**:

1. Starting from the actual starting line, not the fantasy one.
2. Progress over perfection.
3. A little more as the engine of change.
4. Failure as information.
5. Making hard normal.

## 3. I Can't Until I Can

**Working promise**: A capability-recovery memoir about regaining a life one ability at a time: walking, running, stairs, yard work, travel, play, family usefulness.

**Core reader**: Someone whose body has become smaller than their life, and who needs a believable path from "I can't" to "I did."

**Book spine**:

1. The body as a shrinking world.
2. First reclaimed abilities.
3. Exercise as proof, not punishment.
4. The cane-to-5K arc.
5. Becoming useful again to the people you love.

## 5. Health Is The Destination

**Working promise**: A broad health memoir about discovering that there is no finish line: weight loss, diabetes reversal, mobility, strength, fasting, food, family, and identity all point to health as the destination.

**Core reader**: A person who has succeeded before, relapsed, and needs a bigger frame than weight loss.

**Book spine**:

1. The original Less of Lee weight-loss journey.
2. The eight-year gap and return with diabetes.
3. Health beyond the scale.
4. Becoming a former diabetic and a capable grandfather.
5. Maintenance as identity: no arrival, only stewardship.

## Editorial Recommendation

The strongest first book is probably **Former Diabetic** because it has the clearest reader promise, strongest evidence trail, and most urgent market problem.

The strongest voice/book-title concept is probably **Start Where You Are, Make Progress** because it is more universal and can use diabetes, exercise, fasting, and family stories as proof.

The strongest memoir umbrella is **Health Is The Destination**, but it needs the most curation before drafting because it spans both health journeys and could easily become too broad.
