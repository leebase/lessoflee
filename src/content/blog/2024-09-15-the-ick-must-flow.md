This is an update about my abscess.  Feel free to skip it – as it’s definitely icky.

For the last few days it has started draining.  As in – my wife can painfully push the puss out.  The last day – SO much is coming out.  I took a warm shower and it started mount Vesuvius all by itself.  Then my wife joined in to make sure that I could experience more pain as she pinched even more out.

After 3 days of this, one might hope that the size of the half tennis ball under my shoulder blade in my back would go down.  But no, it’s still large and tender and sore.

I’m wearing old t-shirts as if they were bandages to be replaced as the back drains.    I can’t reach behind me to the spot in order to change a real bandage anyway.

What has helped:

  – warm showers

  – hot rag as compress 

  – electric hot pad

  – ibuprofen (I know, I take these as rarely as possible)

  – antibiotics

  – antibiotic cream

  – Blue Emu – a cream I used on my arthritic knees

  – time and patience (not that I actually have patience)

It still hurts the way your nose hurts when you have a cold and blow your nose too much.  Raw and red and angry skin pain.

Tuesday I have the surgical consult.  Hopefully they can finish it off in the office.

No push day this Saturday but I did two Supernatural Virtual Reality workouts this week.  The high level are now my normal midweek workout.  Three years and I’m still enjoying these workouts.  It’s amazing what I can do now compared to when I started.

Also – I'm fat and that sucks ![🤪](~/assets/posts/2024-09-15-the-ick-must-flow/1f92a.png)![😎](~/assets/posts/2024-09-15-the-ick-must-flow/1f60e.png)

But I’ll keep on doing what I can – even when yesterday I didn’t.  I’m never going to give up.  I’ll just do more.

How much more?  A little more.

How much farther? A bit farther.

Always.

p.s. Always consult your own health team for your specific needs.  

p.p.s. Educate yourself to fully participate in your health strategy.
