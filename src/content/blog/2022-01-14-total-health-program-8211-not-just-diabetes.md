This past April, my level of type 2 diabetes…my A1c hit 11.2 – seriously high. Not that any level of diabetes isn’t already bad. If fact, if you are told you are prediabetic – it’s already time to get into action to improve your health.

Notice I said “Improve my health” and not simply “do something about diabetes”. The vast majority of type 2 diabetes…and certainly mine…is the cumulative result of not living in a healthy way. The good news…it means that by changing some things..tough as that can be…means you can regain your health.

It’s not as easy as taking a pill…or taking shots. But the “pharma” way of handling diabetes is the “diabetes is a progressively degenerative disease” path. I want to go down the “restore my health” path.

Here are the things I’m working on that have already born fruit..with more health gains to come. Already my A1c means I’m no longer a diabetic. Already I’m no longer taking Metformin, Glipizide and Trulicity, I avoided having to go on insulin.

I have changed:

• What I eat (meat, fish and non- starchy veggies and no industrial seed oils)

• When I eat (intermittent fasting with occasional extended fasts)

• Exercise – started with walking, added aerobics and now adding strength training and stretching

• Getting proper sleep

• Supplements and tools – continuous glucose monitor, Curalin and berberine

There is no such thing as a type 2 diabetic that is ONLY a diabetic. Heart disease, hypertension, obesity are just 3 of the components of metabolic syndrome – the condition that leads to type 2 diabetes.

That’s why I’m working on becoming healthy – total health, not just trying to manage by blood sugar levels.

I am just now recovering from Covid. I was vaccinated and boosted and still got it. And yet…my experience of it was a few days of aches and pains, some congestion and tiredness. It must mean I got the Omicron variant. And I’m sure the vaccines helped it to not be worse. And yet…there is also the reality that I am much more healthy than I was a year ago. Just one of the many reasons I am glad I’ve been working on my health.
