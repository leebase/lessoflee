Too late for me

It’s too late for me…but maybe this message will reach you in time.

Dear Stranger on the internet – thank you so much for educating me that “not everything onthe internet is true”. Also, the very eye opening truth that “ doesn’t work for everyone”.

Wow, I didn’t know.

I guess I’ll go back to that pile of clothes I can’t wear anymore and put them back on. I think I have some of the meds in a drawer that I no longer need…and start taking them again.

I’m pretty sure that if I call my former doctor and ask if it’s still possible to get that insulin pump…that she’d be happy to prescribe one for me…and reinstate the $1000 a month Trulicity prescription.

I’m not quite sure about what to do with the bathroom scale…it’s telling me a lie that I weigh 50lbs less.

Oh wait. You are somebody on the internet telling me to not trust everything you read on the internet.

Geeze…now I am confused. Life as a former diabetic is hard. You need to get your message out to more people sooner to stop them from losing their diabetes the way I have.

It’s too late for me.
