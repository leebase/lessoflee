There are lots of ways to deal with a stall. Among the many options, I am embarking on a 30 day carnivore diet. A month of eating meat/fish and eggs. Some folks go a lot more strict than I intend to. I will allow myself more spices and more dairy than a pure version. I will cut way back on the dairy and the artificial sweeteners.

What am I hoping to see?

Weight loss…of course. While my body recomposition has been encouraging, I haven’t lost any significant weight over the last five months.

Sugar control. While I am no longer diabetic – I don’t see normal blood sugars unless I’m on an extended fast. Even with berberine and Curalin, I don’t see blood sugars below 100. I’d be quite happy to see normal range blood sugars without going without food for days on end.

I hope I can get into ketosis. Even with the low carb eating and intermittent fasting, I’m not in ketosis until day 3 or 4 of an extended fast. If I can get there with a carnivore diet, that would be great.

Lower inflammation. I’ve already experienced a significant reduction in inflammation when I cut the sugars out of my diet. My osteoarthritis in my knees is greatly improved. I no longer take any pain medication nor do I slather on pain lotions and potions. It will be interesting if I feel any further improvement from a further reduction in inflammation.

I gotta say, I’m not looking forward to this. And actually – that’s one of the reasons to try. There is value in pushing yourself, in getting out of one’s comfort zone.

I’m reading The Carnivore Code by Dr Saladino. I’ve done a fair amount of self education on this topic. I’m not 100% convince this is The Proper Human Diet as Dr Ken Berry likes to call it. Nor am I convinced that plants are trying to harm me to the level that Dr Saladino believes. There are enough personal testimonies I know of that lets me think it’s worth trying for myself. You will never know if you don’t try.

It’s an experiment. I’m not committing to this as a way of life.
