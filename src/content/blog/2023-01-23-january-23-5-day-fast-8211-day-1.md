Day one of this month’s extended fast is in the bag. I ate Sunday dinner and I won’t eat again until Friday.

Evening of Day 1 – 24hrs
274.9 (-5.3lbs)
Bs 90
Breath ketones (2.3 after strength training)

I love the normal blood sugars I get when I’m fasting. Some day, those will be my regular numbers.

No – fasting does not cause hypos – some diabetes meds and insulin can cause low sugars.

The ketones are low after my workout. That surprised me a bit – could just mean my body was using them for fuel. By day 2 I’ll be in a good level of ketosis. By day 3 the fat burning will be in high gear.

Down 5lbs in a day. The wise among you know that’s almost all water weight. But that also means when it snaps right back after the fast, it’s still water. It’s fat weight I‘m trying to lose. You will lose approximately a half pound of fat a day.

It can sound strange – fasting like this – but ask yourself – what is that fat there for? It’s to keep your body running when you aren’t eating. You use some fat every night while sleeping. In fact, the only way to lose your fat is for your body to consume it. It’s food for your body.

I do take salt and minerals as you lose salt easily as you lose the water. There are no vitamin deficiencies to worry about for a 5 day fast. In fact, the longest fast was 362 days with just water and b vitamins in the form of nutritional yeast.

Fasting has been a huge lever in my health recovery journey. It’s more than just losing fat. It’s regaining insulin sensitivity and many other things like autophagy.
