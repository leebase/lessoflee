12 days away from my first race. Working to up my speed. I doubt I’ll beat any runner but hopefully I can run faster than the walkers 

I started out running as fast as I could and still maintain for the length of a song, then a slow jog for a song. This is a way to increase your VO2/Max – your body’s ability to take in and use oxygen. It has longevity benefits the higher you get your value.

2 miles in and I felt a twinge in my knee. Nothing too bad but I backed off on the intensity for the rest (walking mostly). Pushing yourself a little more is one thing. Injuring yourself is another. All is well, I got back home fine and no knee pain other than the typical aches post workout.

I’m actually running now. At least some of the time. The day is coming when I can make that fast pace for the whole race. How will I get there?

By doing more and going farther.

How much more? A little more.

How much farther? A bit farther.

Always.
