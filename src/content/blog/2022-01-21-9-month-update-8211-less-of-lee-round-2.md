I have to do it all over again. 9 years ago, I’m my mid 40's, lost 80lbs and got fit. Nine months ago, I was back where I started, only 20 lbs heavier, type 2 diabetic (A1c of 11.2, taking Metformin, Glipizide and Trulicity), osteo arthritic knees – and – now I'm in my mid fifties.

I read The Diabetes Code by Jason Fung and began intermittent fasting, low carb eating. I also got a Freestyle Libre continuous glucose monitor. Those are the 3 pillars among everything else I did.

My diabetes is in remission. 6 months off of all the meds and maintaining an A1c less than the diabetic level. I’ve lost 49lbs even as I'm beginning to add in muscle (verified with Dexa scan).

My knees feel so much better. I sleep better. I weathered a bout of Covid in a few days. No more blurry vision first thing in the morning.

At nine months, diabetes is in my rear view. The rapid decline of my bs is over. From the 260s into the 110s. How long it will take to heal to finally get below 100, I have no idea. But – I'm pressing on to accomplish other health goals.

There isn’t much more to improve in my diet. I eat meat/fish and nom-starchy veggies. I’ve cut out the inflammatory seed oils.

I am adding a monthly 5 day fast to my regular 18/6 intermittent fasting routine. My doctor is the one who recommended doing it “once or twice a month” when I asked about doing a 5 day fast for the first time. I lose about 3 lbs of fat in those 5 days and I have a lot more fat that still needs to go.

The major focus going forward is resistance training. I want to put on about 20lbs more muscle in the coming year. This isn’t “body building”, this is ramping up my metabolic engine and increasing my health span. Muscle is health. It is because I am so spectacularly unmuscled that I can expect that amount of growth. If I put in the work.
