Carnivore-ish Meat Balls

You can easily cook these meatballs in an air fryer. Here's the altered recipe using an air fryer:
Ingredients:

- 1 lb ground beef

- 1/2 cup pork panko (crushed pork rinds)

- 1/2 cup grated Parmesan cheese

- 1 large egg

- 1/2 tsp salt

- 1/4 tsp black pepper

- 1/2 tsp garlic powder (optional)

- 1/2 tsp onion powder (optional)

- 1/2 tsp Italian seasoning (optional)
Instructions:

- In a large bowl, combine ground beef, pork panko, grated Parmesan cheese, egg, salt, black pepper, and any optional spices (garlic powder, onion powder, and/or Italian seasoning). Mix until well combined.

- Using your hands or a cookie scoop, form the mixture into meatballs, about 1.5-2 inches in diameter.

- Preheat your air fryer to 400°F (200°C) for a few minutes.

- Place the meatballs in the air fryer basket in a single layer, making sure they're not touching. You may need to cook them in batches, depending on the size of your air fryer.

- Cook the meatballs at 400°F (200°C) for 10-12 minutes, or until they are cooked through and have reached an internal temperature of 160°F (71°C). It's a good idea to flip the meatballs halfway through the cooking time for even cooking.

- Once cooked, remove the meatballs from the air fryer and let them rest for a few minutes before serving.
Serve these air-fried meatballs on their own or with your favorite carnivore-friendly sides or sauces. Enjoy!

I added a diced jalapeño and Serrano pepper for a spicy kick.
