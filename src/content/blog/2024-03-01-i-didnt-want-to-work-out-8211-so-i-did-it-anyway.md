It’s Friday, and I do strength training Monday, Wednesday and Friday. I worked hard all day, made dinner, and didn’t feel like lifting weights. I watched a show with my wife, then went down stairs and worked out anyway. I felt great after. I always feel great after. “Feeling like it” is not part of the process.

This is where discipline and habit trump motivation. I made a plan at the beginning of the year to make important health improvements. Right now losing fat and gaining muscle are my highest health priorities. I over came Type 2 Diabetes and made great progress in dealing with osteoarthritis in my knees. The knees are far from healed, but I no longer need to walk with a cane or take pain meds and creams daily.

This is my plan for the year. Strength training and extended fasting for the muscle building and fat loss. Sunday I will embark on the next 14 day water only fast. Part of me didn’t want to talk about that until it was over. Who knows, even though I succeeded in December, I might not make it. But then, I’ve said before and I’ll say it again – you can’t fail a fast, you merely end it earlier than planned.

I had hoped to continue losing weight between the last fast and the next, but the weight training has put on weight. How much of that is muscle, how much is extra water weight, and how much is putting back on some fat – I can’t say just yet. I know I have built at least some muscle – I can see my Traps for the first time. I know that lifting weights causes your body to store more water in the muscles. And my wife just mentioned she thought my stomach looked a bit smaller (still way too large). It just highlights the difference between trying to “lose weight” verses aiming to lose fat.

I added 4 more pounds to the weight vest. Part of doing just a bit more. I couldn’t lift the scheduled amount for my overhead press for the first time. So I lifted what I could until I couldn’t lift any more. Always pushing for more.

How much more? A little more.

How much farther? A bit farther.

How much heavier? A few pounds heavier.

Always.

I didn’t feel like working out tonight. I did it anyway.

I did my body good. How’d you do your body good today?

p.s.  Always consult your own health team for your specific needs
