There is confusion about healing as it relates to type 2 diabetes. Is it truly a progressively degenerative disease? If you get better…are you destined to get worse no matter what…because…once a diabetic, always a diabetic? Or can you heal? I prefer healing to “reversal” or even “remission”.

I will unequivocally demonstrate that I have experience some level of healing.

First…let’s eliminate the “peanut allergy” argument. That’s the one that says “you are only thinking you are healed because you stopped eating peanuts…but if you eat peanuts…you will die”. And it’s completely true that a diabetic could achieve a non-diabetic A1c by simply refraining from eating carbs. But that is not evidence that healing has occurred. Still..folks…thats a great state to be in. Healthy because you aren’t eating food you are “allergic” too.

What I’m claiming goes beyond that. Not only is my bs normal because I eat so few carbs…BUT ALSO…because I have healed to some degree.

I offer as proof the change in response to when I eat carbs. When I first got a cgm, my A1c was 11.2 and my bs was in the 260s. It took weeks of fasting and eating low carb to get my first reading under 200.

My first meal wearing a cgm was an “all things in moderation” McDonalds meal. A burger, medium fries and Diet Coke. My bs soared over 400 and stayed there for many hours.

Now that is because I ate sugar, and one of two things were at play. Either I was creating a whole lotta insulin but still not enough….and my fat cells were so insulin resistant that they wouldn’t respond.

Or

My pancreas was damaged (the beta cells which produce insulin in the pancreas). So my fat cells weren’t getting enough signal to tell the cells accept the sugar.

Or some combination of the two. This is what type 2 diabetes is.

Yes, I was a severe type 2 diabetic and many terrible health complications were in my future.

Last week I ate a large bowl of fried rice, a couple crab rangoons, with Chinese food on top (typical syrupy Chinese sauce and lots of it). Far more carbs than the McDomalds meal.

My bs went up, but within the parameters of a normal person's response. I measured at 30 min, 1 and 2 hours. At each point the spike was in the acceptable range and back to 114 by the 2 hr mark. As I said, within normal.

How could this be? Because I have healed. Either my cells are less insulin resistant or my beta cells have healed or some combination of the two.

That is healing folks. I have more healing awaiting. But this is undeniable proof that you can do more than just “manage your diabetes”. Your body can heal if you stop injuring it with continual over consumption of carbohydrates.

My genetics haven’t changed, but they don’t have to. I like healing as the mental model. If you break your ankle, it may heal. Having healed does not mean you are immune from breaking your ankle again. If I eat in a way to damage myself…I can again become damaged.
