A winter storm arrives later today – so out I went to get a walk in. My knees were feeling so good, I walked extra.

When I got back, I had the painful knees when sitting down and taking off my shoes. Not during the walk, and not too long after. I find it interesting that I have less of this after a run then after a long walk.

I love my walks – it does good for my mental health.

I make phone calls to friends and family. I listen to health and tech podcasts. I listen to audio books on health and spy thrillers or sci-fi. And, of course, I listen to music. Great music gets my blood flowing and my pace up.

My heart rate barely budges on walks any more. That’s ok, walks have their place in my health routine. Long gone are the days where that was about all I could do.

Stay safe and warm this holiday season. And as always…do more and go farther.

How much more? A little more.
How far? A bit farther.
Always.
