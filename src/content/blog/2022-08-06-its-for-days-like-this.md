Why work so hard? Why change your diet away from all your favorite foods? Why put in all the hours, days and months exercising?

It’s for days like today – the wedding shower of my first daughter to marry. My wife and I are at Lake Landing early to setup. An entire car load of water, pops, food, table cloths – everything needs to be brought from the car.

I’m dripping sweat but I have no problems walking, no problems carrying heavy things. Later there will be badminton and other active games.

I’m here. Living and healthy at my daughter’s important events.

I’m here – able to actively participate and help and enjoy.

When you have your why – the work isn’t so bad. It’s worth it. It’s worth every time I said no to my favorite foods. Every new thing I had to learn how to cook. Every time I got up off the couch to exercise.

I live for days like today.
