I’m Herbie, Ninja wanna be. I’m a fluffy hippo now, but my trainer (Lee) is helping me get fit.

After this week’s Diamond in a medium level quick hits workout, I think my trainer lost his marbles. He decided we should go for a Diamond in one of those “Pros Only” workouts. 7 songs, not 2.

We didn’t get the Diamond, but we set our personal best on this routine. He tells me we did good because our heart rate kept red lining and dropping, and again and again. Something called HIIT – but I don’t understand it all yet.

I do know I have been able to exchange my cane for a light saber.

He keeps telling me:

How much more? A little more

How much further? A bit further

Always
