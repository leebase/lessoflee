I really enjoy working out with the Supernatural app in virtual reality.  I made a short clip showing what I’m seeing as I work out vs what I actually look like working out in the hotel.

The SN team is constantly putting out new routines to new music.  They outdid themselves this time with a fun Star Wars theme.  It’s hard to convey in a 2 dimensional photo or video – but you feel like you are THERE.  The living room disappears and is replaced by these beautiful locations.  Coaches are encouraging you and spurring you on.  It’s fun whacking away at the targets.  The bats which already feel like light sabers – even more so when set to Star Wars music.

For me, this is super fun and has me working out no matter what the weather outside.  No need to get dressed and drive to a gym.  

The reality is I’m waving my arms around, squatting and lunging.  You can do that without a VR headset or app subscription. What matters is that you are moving your body and getting your heart rate up.
