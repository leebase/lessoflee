My watch has a fitness tracker. Every midnight my rings reset to zero. Every morning I wake up and there is no accomplishment from the day before. Every day I need to get about my health.

Not saying the rings themselves are important, but the mindset is. Each day is a new beginning, a new opportunity and a renewed obligation.

Did I eat well yesterday? Did I eat poorly yesterday? Doesn’t matter. If I did well, I need to do well again. If I stumbled that doesn’t bleed into today.

Today I work on building tomorrow's better me – Always.
