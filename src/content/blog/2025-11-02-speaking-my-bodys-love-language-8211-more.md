My mindset is back, my fasting and eating are back on track.  Time to get back to strength training.  You can entice your body to make itself stronger just by sweet talking to it.  Seriously, it works.  Just go to the gym, or get some resistance bands or equipment at home – you can even just use your body’s own weight.  What do you say?  What are the loving words your body responds to by giving you muscles, strength and vitality?  More.  Just lift, push and or pull a little more.

You have to move your body in a way that it resists.  Lift a weight that’s heavy.  Push a weight that’s heavy.  Pull a resistance band that’s “heavy”.  How heavy?  It’s not a matter of HOW heavy, it’s just MORE heavy.  How much more?  A little more.  It’s called progressive overload and it works wonders.  

Start light, a comfortable weight or resistance level.  Work on learning the particular movement more than worrying about lifting a lot.  You are building habit and muscle memory.  Next time, raise up the weight or increase the resistance.  You’ll know when it gets hard to do.  It’s when you can only do the movement 3-8 times. If you can do so easily, add more weight next time.  if you can’t do even three, it’s too much.  Lift one day, rest the next.  It’s during the rest that the body rebuilds itself back stronger.

There’s a bit more to it and reading a book or getting a coach will help.  But the NATURE of sweet talking your body with the love language of “more” will transform your health.

How much more? A little more.

How much farther? A bit farther.

Always.

The always is key.  You don’t just keep lifting the same amount.  The “more” part is the necessary signal that tells your body to kick off it’s adaptations.  It’s all science-y and really cool.  But it’s simple too.  Just start light and safe. Learn the movements.  Increase the weight.

I’m back in the gym – doing the easy level so that I can learn the particular machines and movements.  Even just finding where the machine I want is located.  One of the machines is way too hard for my current level – so I’ll come up with an alternative next time.  I had to ask a stranger for help to get up from laying on a bench trying to do leg lifts.  I was stuck like a turtle under this huge belly of mine.

But it was a start.  A restart.  I didn’t feel like going – I just did.
