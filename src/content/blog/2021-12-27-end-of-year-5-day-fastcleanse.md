I’ve completed day 1 of a planned 5 day fast/cleanse. I feel fine, a little hungry. As I regularly fast for 18 hours, so far this is just a normal day.

I did talk to my doctor and he is on board with this. In fact, I may be doing this once a month – we'll see how this first one goes.

Why?

1. Weight loss – this one is obvious. I have many days of food, many months of food stored all over my body. I’m actually going to be eating….just eating my own fat stores.

2. Heal insulin resistance – I’m off all my diabetes meds but my A1c is still in the “pre-diabetes” range. There is more work to do and this will help.

3. Cell repair – called autophagy. This is the cleanse part. Your body starts repairing itself when it’s not needing its energizes spent on digestion. Hopefully this will help with my arthritis and glaucoma.

4. Improve hypertension

5. Detox – your body can more readily get rid of toxins

The roughest part will be day 2. Part of the reason to do an extended fast is to get to the “good stuff” and stay there awhile for the benefit.

Nothing earth shattering to discuss about the first day. My blood sugar is below 100. I’ve fasted for 3 days before and know that my blood sugar will not crash.

What if I don’t succeed? No worries. It’s like exercise. You train and build up your abilities? You aren’t an athlete day one.
