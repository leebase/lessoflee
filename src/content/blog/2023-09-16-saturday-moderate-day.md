Last week I had a micro stroke in my right eye. Retina, not brain. I asked the eye doctor about any needed limits on exercise. He thought moderate would be fine but to talk to my regular doctor.

I’ve been measuring my blood pressure before and after exercise and find that my blood pressure goes down nicely.

I won’t be doing any high intensity exercise until I consult my regular doctor.

So…I’m being safe…by exercising. We can be so quick to fear exercise. And yet – being sedentary is absolutely dangerous.

Just because I am no longer diabetic does not mean I’m healthy. I’ve improved my health, but there is much more work to do.

Heart attacks and strokes were definitely in my future on the path I had been traveling.

I pushed myself today – but appropriately so. I put on a weighted vest and went on a 4 mile walk.

How much more? A little more.

How much farther? A bit farther.

Always?

Well, “always with wisdom”. If I had sprained an ankle, I’d not be out running on it.

As always – consult your own medical team.

As also always – educate yourself and don’t merely rely on the sick care systems ways.
