Day 2 of this month's extended fast. The goal is 5 days, but the last two times I only did 3.

Here are my stats after 48 hrs

Evening Day 2 – 48 hrs
Weight 258.8 (-10.2lbs)
BS 85
Breath Ketones 5.8

That’s the lowest weight I’ve reached – started at 336 15 months ago. I ate a Chinese food meal complete with rice right before the fast so 5 of those 10 lbs is just losing the water I put on from that meal. Since I live on very few carbs, my liver replenishes the glycogen stores when I indulge and it’s 2 water for every glycogen molecule.

My job is quite busy right now which makes fasting much easier. “Mouth boredom” is more an issue than actual hunger – when you are fat adapted. At best I might lose 3 lbs of fat in a 5 day fast. I have many more lbs of fat to lose.

I’m starting to see body composition changes again from all the exercise. My stubborn belly fat is losing inches. Some clothes are fitting, others are now too big.

Why the long fast? I’ve written a series of posts on this already and I’ll link to them. Look at the breath ketone value. After 2 days I’m only in moderate ketosis. Optimal ketosis is 10. Ketones are an indication of fat being converted to fuel. I normally don’t get into optimal ketosis until day 3. And then, you want to stay awhile to benefit from being in high fat burning mode.

Fay loss is the most easy and obvious benefit – but there are many more…benefits that don’t come on shorter fasts.

It’s also a free health spa. Fasting saves money and time. I was spending $1000 a month (or my insurance was) for Trulicity and insulin was going to cost another $600. Neither one of them work as well as fasting…which is better than free. You aren’t buying the food, shopping, cooking, eating or cleaning up.

Yes, diabetics can fast. Yes, even if you are on insulin. Work with your doctor as you’ll need to be prepared to adjust dosage.

No, fasting does not CAUSE low blood sugar. Diabetes meds do. And insulin. Fasting helps you stop needing those.

Fasting is like exercise. You get stronger with training. You acclimate.

In fact, a lot of people who try to fast and have a bad experience sound exactly like people who jump into high intensity exercise without building up to it. Read a book like Life In the Fasting Lane by Dr Fung. Look before you leap.
