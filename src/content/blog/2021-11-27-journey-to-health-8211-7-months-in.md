7 months ago I finally met with the diabetes specialist. 2 years before my regular doctor informed me I was now diabetic – A1c of 8.5. For whatever reason I didn’t jump right on it, and then Covid hit and you couldn’t get into see a doctor. So now I’m at A1c of 11.2 and it FINALLY hits me – all of the diabetic complications are in their way, including blindness. I was starting to have blurry vision for a minute or two upon waking. Losing my eyesight is one of my primal fears. Fear, in this context, is a good thing for me. Fear got me moving to address my health 9 years ago.

I read The Diabetes Code by Dr Jason Fung. I started fasting and eating very low carb. I started going on daily walks again. I’ve continued reading and improving my diet and my exercise.

As of a month ago, my diabetes is in remission. I am off all my meds and have been maintaining a blood sugar level below the diabetic level without them for more than three months.

I’ve lost 39lbs. The weight loss rate has slowed way down…a stall. But the inches have been coming off. 3.5” off my belly, 2.5” off my neck. Clothes that fit me this spring are too big. I’m wearing clothes I’d gotten too fat for.

I started aerobic exercising with the Oculus Quest 2 and the Supernatural app. Just like last time, I couldn’t even make it through the warm up at first. I am now doing 30 minute sessions and doing all the moves and having fun. I’m well on my way to my goal of being able to vigorously exercise for an hour. My walks are sometimes an hour and a half.

I can see my metabolic health improving. I can eat the occasional old favorite food, I can eat a holiday dinner with family, and my blood sugar follows a normal response. While this is awesome, it’s just one milestone in the journey to health.

I aim to reverse my heart disease and arthritis as well. That’s my new focus. I have to continue eating meats/fish and non-starchy veggies and avoid seed oils to keep healing the inflammation from my Standard American Diet. I have to keep up the exercise and work on getting stronger.

Some wonderful health improvements have already happened.

⁃ Arthritic knees feel better (much more to go)

⁃ Diabetes in remission

⁃ No more heart burn, acid reflux

⁃ Sleep better

⁃ Fewer skin tags

⁃ No more blurry vision in the morning

⁃ 39lbs weight loss

⁃ Fewer headaches
