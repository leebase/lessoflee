On Saturdays I push my limits. Today I did that by doing Tabata with sprints. Tabata is a form of High Intensity Interval Training. All out exercise of pretty much any form that gets your heart rate up around 95% of max. 20 seconds on, 10 seconds off, repeat for 4 minutes.

Today, I looked in the mirror and saw a man who was stronger, faster, and more determined than ever before. And it wasn't just about the physical gains I made in my Tabata sprints – it was about the mental fortitude I had to summon to keep going, even when my body begged me to stop.

I want to be a man who is constantly pushing his limits. A man who is always striving for more. A man who refuses to settle for mediocrity. And today, I proved to myself that I am that man. This is how I’ve been reclaiming my health.

So to all the folks out there who are looking to make a change, I say this: take a good long look in the mirror. Do you like what you see? If not, it's time to start pushing. It's time to start striving for more. It's time to become the person you were meant to be. The man in the mirror is waiting for you – are you ready to meet him?

How much more? A little more.

How much farther? A little farther.

Always
