I hit the 100k points milestone in my workout app last night. Others are celebrating hitting a million and I congratulate them. I am running my own race and competing against the old me as I build the newer, healthier, fitter me.

You can’t let the accomplishments of others diminish your own sense of pride. In a year and a half, 147 times I showed up for myself with the Supernatural workout. I bought the Meta Quest 2 VR headset so that I could continue making progress on my fitness as the Chicago winter was approaching and I wasn’t going to be able to walk outside. This is my second winter where I’m increasing my fitness and will be going into spring stronger than I was in the fall.

The gamification of exercise can be motivational. I have closed my Apple Watch rings every day – EVERY day for more than a year. I have another app that awards me badges for milestones. BUT – the gaming aspects are meaningless, what’s going on in my body is everything. I’m not working out to get good at working out, I’m reclaiming my health and abilities.

I couldn’t do the squatting and lunging of these exercises at first because of my arthritis. I even fell over a couple times in the beginning. This was on the lowest level and shortest routines. Last night I did 50 minutes of a high level workout and had my heart rate up into the 150s. My legs and lungs are no longer the limiter. My arms and abs were screaming – in a good, muscle building way. I still have arthritis, I’m just more able as my low carb diet took down my inflammation and I’ve built up leg strength and lost weight.

I lost another inch around my belly…for a total of 9.5” since I started. I’ve gone from a 20” neck to 17”. The belly has been so slow to respond but it is happening. Visceral belly fat is the killer and I have a lot more to go.

Meanwhile, muscles are popping out on my calves, shoulders and forearms. My biceps are barely getting bigger, still covered in too much fat to see. Progress is happening. I’m doing this for health and abilities…but I’m quite happy with the ascetic improvement.

How did I get here? By doing more and going farther.

How much more? A little more.

How much farther? A bit farther.

Always
