I cook, she cooks, and sometimes we create culinary magic together. In our tiny kitchen, we're always bumping into each other. It's like dancing, really.

32 years of marriage, and for the first 16, it was all about Hamburger Helper, pizza rolls, and Kraft Mac and Cheese. Yikes!

Hit my mid-forties, and health became a priority. Lost 80lbs and got fit, but the healthy eating habits? Not so much. We did cook tons of good-for-us food, but I couldn't resist the bad-for-me stuff.

Great cooks aren't born; they're made. So start small, learn and improve. One new dish every two weeks, and in a year, you'll have 26 delectable options. Now that's variety!
