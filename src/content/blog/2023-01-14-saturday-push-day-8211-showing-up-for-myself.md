Saturday Push Day – Showing Up For Myself

Once again, I did not feel like it – but its Saturday time to push myself.

I learned from the Supernatural workout coaches and community the concept of “showing up for yourself”. If the fit and healthy me of tomorrow is to be, I need to show up for myself today.

It’s not about grand gestures, rather consistency.

I have closed my fitness rings every day for more than a year. At least 30 minutes of exercise even if that means going for a walk before midnight to get it done.

For the year 2022, I came in the top 2% of those working out with the FitIV app.

I averaged just over an hour a day of exercise – for the year. 411 hours total. Most of that walking. Twice weekly strength training. Plenty of VR aerobics with Supernatural and Eleven Table Tennis. Weekly interval running.

It sounds like a lot but it’s just daily showing up for myself. To what end?

I sleep better, feel better. Last March I had my first few successful steps running. By the end of the year I ran 5k without stopping. Every muscle group I increased my strength by at least 100%. I’ve gone from barely completing the low level Supernatural workouts to crushing the high level ones. My resting heart rate has gone from the 80s into the 50s.

No pill will give these results. No money can buy them. Show up for yourself and work to make it happen.

How much? A little more.

How far? A little farther.

Always
