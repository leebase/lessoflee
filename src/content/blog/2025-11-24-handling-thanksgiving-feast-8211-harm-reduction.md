Thanksgiving is upon us and I am priming myself to enjoy the food and family for the Holiday.  Am I going to “keto Thanksgiving”, swap out mashed potatoes for mashed cauliflower…take just a single bite of pie?  No. That makes perfect sense for a lot of people and those of you holding to your path in the face of Thanksgiving – you have my support and respect.  As for me:

To quote Vinnie Tortorich – I’m going to put a little life into living. To speak according to metabolic science – I’m going to carb cycle to maintain metabolic flexibility.  We have two fuel sources: fats and carbs, and it’s to our body’s benefit to keep both pathways healthy and available.  To speak most honestly – Imma eat my favorite foods because I want to and I’ve planned to do so in a way to not wreck my process.  This is my plan to eat my favorite foods, for four days, then right back into my next cycle starting with a 7 day fast.  I aim to do so in a way that enjoys the food but in a way that is LESS harmful.  I am accepting the water gain, the weight gain, the extra hunger on my next fast.  The beauty of my power tool that is extended fasting, is that I’ll easily reset to make forward progress.

Tonight I have my first meaningful carbs in 50 days; a bowl of rice.  This is to let my body experience that hit of glucose and revive the glucose pathways in my liver and muscles.  It should be interesting to see which happens – am I extra insulin sensitive because of being in low insulin state for 50 days, or temporarily insulin resistant from not using the glucose pathways such that the muscles say “no thanks, save this glucose for the brain”.  Both are real forces.  It also will reduce the stress of having that shock come from a full plate of Thanksgiving dinner, reducing the chance of an upset stomach.  

Now for the juicy details on how to eat the wrong foods the best way.  

- Small pre-feast snack, protein heavy. Maybe a ketochow shake (less heavy cream, use an egg instead).  Maybe just some chicken breast and guacamole.  The protein slows the stomach emptying making me feel full and naturally eat less without trying to portion control on Thanksgiving.

- Food order:  protein, fats and fiber first – carbs last.  Eat turkey, ham and green beans first.  Get a nice layer of “stuff” in your belly that will slow the shock of the coming delicious mashed potatoes and stuffing.  Releases the carbs into your system more slowly while you are still eating the food that makes Thanksgiving something you look forward to.

- Pick your sponge worthy treats (Seinfeld reference, if you know you know).  I like dinner rolls, I don’t LOVE dinner rolls.  But I love a fruit tort, and I love stuffing and gravy.  Eat the stuff you love, and skip the stuff “I could eat” but really aren’t worth the carbs and calories.  Be a discerning hedonist.

- Have protein and fat snacks (meat and cheese on crackers, buffalo wings that aren’t breaded) to START with.  I’m going to snack on “bad foods” that’s part of my plan.  But I’m going to preload with protein and fat for the same reason of food ordering.  Follow on with the carby delicacies after to eat less of them naturally and to blunt the glucose release over time.

- Just say no to Doritos.  My favorite.  It’s just that they are purposely designed to get past your body’s defenses.  Seriously, they are engineered to addict and get you to overeat.  Not a conspiracy theory – it’s true.  I’ll eat tortilla chips and salsa which I also love.  They aren’t health food, but this is a feast – one I’m trying to be smarter in the way I eat.  Doritos are my kryptonite – you’ll have to evaluate yours.

- Walk after the meal.  Give those carbs a purpose.

Finally – Enjoy – this whole process is about enjoying. It is not the every day.  Feasting and fasting is part of the human rhythm.  This is far different from the mindset of “cheating on my diet”.  

p.s. Always consult your own health team for your specific needs.

p.p.s. Educate yourself to fully participate in your health strategy.

p.p.p.s. If you are someone who has an addiction relationship with carbs, this is likely not the plan for you.
