Two years ago, I was facing a life tethered to an insulin pump. Today, I'm running 5Ks med-free! Just three months prior I had met with the diabetes specialist for the first time. My A1c was 11.2. The $1000/month Trulicity was added to the Metformin and Glipizide I was already taking. And we scheduled the next appointment for July 20th for me to start insulin.

I was scared straight. Maybe not perfectly straight, but I left the doctor terrified of the fate I was facing with type 2 diabetes. The “diabetes is a progressive disease” was happening to me. I have always had a primal level fear of going blind…of which type 2 diabetes is the number one cause.

I had lost 80lbs in less than a year in my mid 40s. Even got fit. But that was ten years prior and I had gained all that weight back plus 20 more. Plus type 2 diabetes. Plus osteoarthritis in my knees. I needed a cane to walk and drove with a handicap sticker.

![](~/assets/posts/2023-07-20-two-years-med-free-8211-health-recovery/img_8252.jpg)

With the help of a CGM (continuous glucose monitor), the Diabetes Code book by Dr Jason Fung, and the knowledge about low carb eating from my previous journey, I got into high gear. No playing around like I had been for the last ten years. A switch flipped in my mind and I ate meat, fish and non-starchy veggies. Dr Fung taught me about fasting. And I walked, which is about all I could do at the time. With my cane, walking 15 minutes in the neighborhood.

So that day, two years ago today, I didn’t need to be put on insulin. It was the day I got off the last of my meds. Some people live where the government pays for the meds. But meds, while helping control blood sugar, do not bring health.

Today my A1c is 5.2. I’m running 5k's. I walk with ease. Stairs are no obstacles. No more heart burn. No sleep apnea. I don’t even snore (well…not often). Headaches are a rarity now. My vision isn’t blurry first thing in the morning. I sleep better and have a lot less anxiety. I can mow the lawn and carry the groceries in from the car.

How long must I eat healthy? For only as long as I desire…to be healthy. I can go back to being sick any time I want.

How long must I exercise healthy. Only as long as I desire to be able to move freely and and have ableness. I can become disabled again any time I wish.

Who would wish for those things? That’s the choice I’m faced with every meal every day. The choice every time I don’t feel like working out. And I don’t always make the right choice. I’m down 50lbs, but it was 85lbs last year.

I hit a huge milestone this day two years ago. The photo reflects how I feel. I'll never be that muscular. It’s a fake photo. I share it because I realize what a huge accomplishment in turning my health around. I did something mighty. I feel strong like the photo shows. Of course, that’s not the real me, yet. Just an AI generated representation of how mighty I feel inside.

I have not arrived because there is no such thing. I’m on a journey to continue reclaiming my health and building my body because that’s how EVERYONE's health works. I don’t get to go back to the way I used to eat and the sedentary life. Being healthy feels a lot better than any of my old favorite foods ever tasted.

Imagine yourself in that photo. Imagine the future you who is more healthy and more able. The mighty you. Then go do the work that makes it happen. It’s not perfection that’s needed. Just keep working at it.
