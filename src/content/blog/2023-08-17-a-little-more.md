This morning, as rain tapped against my window, I embarked on a Supernatural VR workout. I had initially set my sights on the "medium level," but an unfamiliar routine caught my eye: a Hard level, accompanied by my favorite workout music. O.M.G. This workout had been mislabeled. It was a league beyond "Hard"; it belonged in the realm of "Pro's Only," a level of difficulty harder than the norm.

Imagine "fast-moving metal music," with targets hurtling at you with lightning speed. Knee strikes, cross-body strikes, lunges, and rapid-fire strikes on targets in the center and on the periphery. A virtual bat became my instrument of force to smack down these targets. This exhilarating half-hour was a delightful yet demanding start to my workday.

Had this been my initial experience, I might have packed up the Quest 2 headset and sent it back. This routine would have seemed insurmountable—akin to watching pro basketball players executing dunks, knowing I could never achieve that. Yet, it was me, hitting 99% of the targets, performing knee kicks and lunges while rapidly striking away at the targets.

How did I manage this? Well, for almost two years, I've been steadily engaged in these workouts. I began with the simplest, shortest routines, which were challenging back then. I never felt compelled to tackle the high-level routines when the easiest ones were already tough.

I've embraced "a little more" for two years now. If I had confined myself to the 15-minute easy level for these two years, I'd certainly excel at that level. It would be a substantial improvement over being couch-bound. But I chose to do a little more, a little harder, and a little longer.

Two years might sound like an eternity when anticipated, yet it went by in the blink of an eye. During this blink, I've been reclaiming my health, my life, and my capabilities.

Why not join me in your own health journey? Grand ambitions and goals are not required. All you need is to start where you are and gradually do a little more. Keep pushing that boundary.

How much more? A little more.

How much farther? Just a bit farther.

Always.
