Fasting is an amazing health practice,.  Intermittent fasting helped me get off all my diabetes meds and put type 2 diabetes into remission.

As of December, I have been fasting for 5 days once a month.  There are processes in the body that don’t kick off until you are fasting for more than 24-48 hours.  I’ll speak to one benefit each day.

The most obvious one is burning fat.  You lose about .57lbs of fat for each 24 hours you fast.  So call it 3lbs of fat loss for a 5 day fast as I also exercise during my fast.

I have 75 more lbs of weight to lose to reach my goal.  If I complete my monthly 5 day fast, that 36 lbs of fat.  According to my dexascan, I have 114lbs of fat mass.  As you can see, this is not “instant weight loss” program.  I may or may not reach my goal weight, but I hope to make some excellent progress.

With the weight loss will come further insulin sensitivity and improved cardiovascular health and improvement in my knee arthritis function.

I'm not just working on my diabetes, I'm working on my total health.
