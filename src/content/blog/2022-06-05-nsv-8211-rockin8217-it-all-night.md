My wife and I went to the Styx, REO Speedwagon and Loverboy concert. It’s fantastic being able to enjoy life. The show was over 4 hours and standing at least half of the time.

Not just standing, but dancing and doing the Rockin' Body Bob…you know what I mean. And singing…so much singing.

These arthritic knees of mine have come a long way. For every pound lost, 4 pounds of pressure are taken off the knees. So I have 240lbs less pressure. Plus the benefit of much reduced inflammation from the low carb diet. I think the autophagy from the fasting has helped as well. No way to prove that.

But the proof is in the living. I admit, I was at my limits by the end of the night. My knees ached, well, my whole body ached by the time I got home. Yet I was there! I was living. Reclaiming abilities. Reclaiming life.
