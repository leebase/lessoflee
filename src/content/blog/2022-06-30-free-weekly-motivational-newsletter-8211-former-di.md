You all have willed it to be – Former Diabetics Club is coming alive. To start with – a free newsletter. Then a book.
For now, I'm beginning with a free weekly (I hope) newsletter aimed at inspiring people to become a former diabetic and remain so for the rest of their lives.

I'm a veteran IT professional, but a complete newb to newsletters and such. So, be prepared to participate in some growing pains with me.

I have some expansive thoughts on what this can become. I'd really love to help people share THEIR stories and not just chronicle my own journey as I do here. I'd love to build a great catalog of success stories to help people come to understand that Type 2 Diabetes need not be forever and we aren't fated to "progressively degenerate".

This is no longer just a mindset, but a real signup: [http://newsletter01.formerdiabetics.club](http://newsletter01.formerdiabetics.club)

If any of you are talented in the ways of such things and want to assist – let me know. Designers, marketers, SEO specialists – stuff like that. I'd love to be able to focus mostly on putting out the motivating content.
