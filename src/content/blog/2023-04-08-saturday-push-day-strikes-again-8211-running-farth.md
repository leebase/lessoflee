Hey folks, it's a brand-new week and we're back at another Saturday push day! Today's challenge: running farther than ever before.

I set out on the same route where I completed my first non-stop 5k just four months ago. Back then, I felt like a champ for conquering those 3.1 miles. But today, I aimed higher and pushed harder. I kept running...and running...and running, all the way home. By the end, I'd clocked in 4 or even 5 miles!

Now, I won't sugarcoat it: there were moments when I wanted to throw in the towel. That’s how I know I’m pushing is when I want to quit. But every time that little voice chimed in, I shot back, "I can quit...but not yet." And I kept going, one foot in front of the other, until I reached my doorstep.

Today's victory is a testament to the power of persistence and the growth that comes from pushing our limits. Just a few months ago, I was celebrating my first non-stop 5k. Now, I'm breaking personal records and setting my sights on even bigger goals.

So, as we kick off this new week, let's remember the mantra that keeps us moving forward:

How much more? A little more.

How much farther? A bit farther.

Always.
