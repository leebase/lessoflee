Yesterday was Saturday and on Saturday’s I push myself.

Yep, had to change my mantra with the weather. Can’t always run ![🏃‍♀️](~/assets/posts/2022-11-20-saturday-push-11-20-22/1f3c3-200d-2640-fe0f.png) in the winter but I can do something else – a bit harder, a bit longer.

I did my VR workout, Supernatural, for 70 minutes. I started with a medium boxing – I find boxing harder. All the bobbing, weaving, ducking…tough for me with my arthritic knees. But tough is the point of Saturday’s. Then I did a medium flow workout and then a short high level one.

I wanted to stop halfway through the medium flow. Did I? Nope. Just kept pushing. Funny thing – my heart rate wasn’t that high. As I’m getting more fit, my heart rate doesn’t go as high – half to push harder next time.

Today is Sunday and it is very cold. 11f/-11c “feels like” temp. Bright sun shine and no snow or ice so out I went for an hour walk. it’s nice to be outside even when it’s cold.

Put on my wife’s sweats to go outside. Not really, I just thought they were because they look so small. From the legs down I’m a smaller person ![😜](~/assets/posts/2022-11-20-saturday-push-11-20-22/1f61c.png). Long way to go with my gut.

How much more? A little more
How far? A bit farther
Always
