Reading a book on HIIT – High Intensity Interval Training. The value comes in getting your heart rate up to 95% of capacity. Which I did for the last song. Once you learn what that feels like, it will be easier to push there for each song. Making progress.

Herbie, my imaginary workout hippo had to push me this morning. I was thinking about doing a high level and he challenged me to do a medium first and THEN do a long high level. It is a push day after all.

I was ready to quit after the medium workout. Looking at my heart rate I knew I hadn’t really pushed. But could I complete it after having done another workout first? Only way to find out is to try.

You can see by the heart rate that not only did I make it through but I gave my hardest effort at the end. I remember being totally gassed by the last song the first time I did this workout.

That’s why I have a push day once a week. Pushing back the limits more each time.

How much more? A little more.

How much farther? A bit farther.

Always
