What happened to the guy who lost 80lbs and got fit? Clearly, as shown in this photo, he was still obese even though he'd lost all that weight and got fit. Because once obese – always obese. Isn't that the only possible explanation?

And yet, here I am speaking of being a former diabetic. Will I never learn my lesson? Once you experience something bad healthwise – that's you forever. Why did I become obese again? Because no matter what I do, once I became obese, that's my fate forever.

Or...

There is the health model. Nobody has a state of health that exists forever. Health must be maintained. Look at all those professional athletes that get fat. What happened to their athletic bodies? They didn't maintain their health.

The muscles I developed – the ability to do 25 pushups with my feet on a chair – didn't "just happen". My ability to run a 5k didn't just happen. The weight I lost didn't just fall off. I brought them all about by eating well and by exercising. I was pathetic at first, couldn't do a single pushup. Couldn't run at all. Was so fat I needed an extender to buckle up on a plane.

The version of me in this photo? Came AFTER I had achieved so much improvement in my health. It didn't come back in a day. It didn't "just happen". It wasn't fate. I stopped doing all the good things I did to get healthy in the first place.

Over time, I started eating in my old fashion. Over time I stopped being active and became sedentary again. And I gained even more weight than I lost and developed type 2 diabetes and osteoarthritis. I went from getting off my blood pressure meds to needing them again.

That's why I say there are dues to pay to join AND to remain in Former Diabetics club. There is a cost. Not to my newsletter, that's free. But if you want to remain free of type 2 diabetes, you have to eat healthy, move healthy, sleep healthy and handle stress healthy forever. Do that – and you can indeed be a former diabetic. And remain a former diabetic.
