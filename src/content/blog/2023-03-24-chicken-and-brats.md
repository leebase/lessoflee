Air fryer chicken and a couple brats and an egg surprise (mostly eaten before the pic).

Pork Panko, Parmesan, Italian seasonings, salt, pepper, paprika on a plate. Egg wash the chicken, then coat with the ”breading”.

Into the air fryer at 375f for 12 minutes flipping over half way.

Delicious.

But I had most of that egg left over and a bunch of the breading. I added a second egg and out the breading into the eggs and scrambled. Then flatten out and turn into an omelette with cheese.

Man was it tasty. The wife loved it too. Going to try that again.

Ended my monthly fast at 4 days.
