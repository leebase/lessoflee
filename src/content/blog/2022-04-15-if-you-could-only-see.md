If you could know the joy of walking across 8 lanes of traffic with no problem doing so in time for the light … when not so long ago it was difficult.

If you could know the joy of walking up and then down tall flights of stairs…like a normal person…when you didn’t used to be able to.

If you could make a quick-walk pace across more than a mile of city blocks, cracked side walks…hustling to make your train…and you make it…when not so long ago you couldn’t.

If you could know that you could feel better, be better, look better, move better and regain abilities that you’d lost…

Then you would give up the bread and the pasta.…

You’d get off the couch and walk….

You’d lift weights…

You’d learn to fast…

You’d not stop at discomfort…

You’d keep at it…

You’d do what you could do today and do that much more tomorrow…

You’d read a book….or two…

If you could see yourself a year from now having regained health as I have…

You’d begin the journey. You’d stop making excuses. You’d stop filling your mind with all the sorrow of having to give up your old foods and look forward to discovering all that is both delicious and good for you.

Type 2 diabetes can be the blessing in your life. It can be the impetus to transform and reclaim your health and vitality. You can do so much more than wait for diabetes to steal your life away.

You can live, you can thrive. See it in your mind – then do the doing that will make it your reality.
