Couples cooking. I made breakfast. Brown a pound of hamburger, with sliced jalapeño and Serrano peppers. Added in some Eat Hapoy Kitchen putenesca sauce. Top with Parmesan, American cheese and a couple fried eggs.

Wifey made dinner. Egg washed the chicken then dredged in almond flour with some seasonings. Pan seared for crust and then finished in the oven.

Delicious

Two meals – now I fast until tomorrows for meal.
