I did not feel like it. I just finished a monthly step challenge at work. My exercise activity was up 50% for the month – I was pushing myself to walk 10k steps a day.. Surely I could take the day off. Plus it’s colder now, summer is over.

But it’s Saturday and on Saturday I run intervals. I don’t go by how I feel. I don’t rely on motivation. And while cooler, it was a gorgeous day. Winter is coming and I won’t be able to run outside. Am I going to waste one of the few remaining glorious days? No.

It’s Saturday, and on Saturday's I run intervals.

No personal bests were set this day. I worked on my form and posture and stride. I'm almost up to a normal stride instead of the “crouch run”. my arthritic knees required. I'm shocked by how far I’ve come. I barely dreamed that this could happen. I literally couldn’t get both feet into the air at the same time a year ago. Yes, some of this is the weight loss…but I believe I'm experiencing some level of healing in my knees.

People say “I wish I had your motivation/discipline/commitment” and it saddens me. If you look at me, don’t wish you have what I have. Go get it. You aren’t born with it. You make it happen. Go out in the strength you have. And do a little more tomorrow.

How much more? A little more.

How far? A bit farther.

Always

You can do it too.
