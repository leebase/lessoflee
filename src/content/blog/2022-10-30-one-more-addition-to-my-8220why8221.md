I can tell the world now – my daughter is expecting our first grandchild – and it's a boy!

What an extra incentive to continue making progress for health and longevity. This little man is going to need his grandpa, and a healthy one who is still earning at that ![😉](~/assets/posts/2022-10-30-one-more-addition-to-my-8220why8221/1f609.png)

We took a load of furniture from our home in Illinois to theirs in Michigan. No movers. Me, the father-to-be, a pregnant woman and my -year-old wife. So yes, I had to be one of the carriers of very heavy things. Up stairs. Plus drive a truck 4 hours away.

Arthritic knees? Yes. But I've been working on fitness for a year and a half, and strength training for almost a year. I'm not a particularly strong man even still...but twice as strong as I was at the beginning. It wasn't that long ago I was walking around with a cane an having difficulties with stairs.

I could barely get through this move, but I got through it. I'll keep pressing on to be able to get through whatever the next challenge is.

There is so much more health to regain – control of blood sugar is just one of the milestones along the way.

Would I rather "eat tasty treats" or be able to help out my growing family? Not really that hard of a choice when you put it like that.
