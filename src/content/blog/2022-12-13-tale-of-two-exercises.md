I went for a nice brisk 45 minute walk before a winter storm this morning. Later in the evening I did a 45 minute aerobic workout with my VR headset (Supernatural).

Both activities are good for my health. Which do you think improved my heart health more?

There’s a wonderful thing about your body – it gets better the more you use it. On the other hand – that means you have to do more to get the same heart benefit.

It’s like 2 people going to school for 12 years. One goes to kindergarten each year for 12 years. The other steps up a grade each year.

By the end of 12 years one is ready to graduate high school. The other is the tallest kid in kindergarten.

How much more must you push, how much farther just you go?

How much more? A little more.
How much farther? A bit farther.
Always
