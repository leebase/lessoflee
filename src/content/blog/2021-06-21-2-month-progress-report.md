It’s time for the 2-month update as it was 2 months ago yesterday that my diabetes Dr told me my A1c was 11.2.  For those that don’t know, that’s in the “scary high, you’re going to go blind” type level.  It was a kick in the pants for me and I finally got back on my health journey that I launched the Less of Lee blog to document.  First, my goals:

- Diabetes:  To reverse my Type 2 diabetes defined as “control my blood sugar at a non-diabetic level via diet and exercise”.
- Weight:  Get down to a healthy weight – defined as “not overweight”, which for me is going to be somewhere around 180lbs
- Fitness:  Be able to vigorously exercise for an hour.

Two months in:

- My blood sugar is in the 110s down from the 260s.  That would be at the 5.0 to 5.5 level as an A1c if I keep this up for 3 months.
- With my Dr’s counsel, I am off 2 of my meds and down to a half dose of the third.  And still my bs is trending down!
- I have lost 15 pounds
- I have lost an inch around my neck and 2 inches from my chest.  My belly girth has yet to budge
- My arthritic knees feel better.  Not cured, but like I’m taking ibuprofens for pain management – but I’m not taking the pills.  It’s a feature of getting off sugar so my inflammation is down.
- My walks are up to an hour and a half.  I’m walking 2 to 4 miles a day.
