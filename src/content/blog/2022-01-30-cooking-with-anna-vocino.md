I cooked with Anna Vocino today with her Keto Brunch Party. Shrimp and “grits” (riced cauliflower) and a Tomato, Cucumber, Bacon and avocado salad. Absolutely delicious. Brought her cook books to life (Eat Happy, Eat Happy Too) Wife loved the food.

Several other dishes and cocktails were made and I’ll try them out later.
