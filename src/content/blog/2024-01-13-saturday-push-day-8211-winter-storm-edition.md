On Saturday’s I try to push my heart rate up into the 95% of max range. You can look up HIIT (High Intensity Interval Training) for more information on why. I’ll put a link at the end. For me the most easy way to accomplish this is by running sprints. Running full out for 20 seconds and then walking for 10 and doing 8 sets of these. It is HARD to do, but is over in a relatively short amount of time. However, it’s winter with 10” of snow on the ground, and sub zero windchills so sprints are out.

I love my Supernatural VR workouts. As my fitness improves, these don’t get my heart rate up to the levels I’m aiming for. So today I put on a weight vest to make the exercise a bit harder. I did get my heart rate up to the 85% – and I certainly felt the challenge of this workout. I started with 6 pounds, and I can go up to 20. Next time I’ll add a bit more weight to see if I can get my heart rate into the “push day” range.

What about safety? I’m doing this in order to be safe. I am exercising my heart to get it in shape and that’s far safer than “playing it safe by never challenging the heart to do more”. Not pushing your heart is the unsafe lifestyle.

Caveats – one doesn’t go from sedentary to HIIT exercise in a day. I built up to it over time. And, I’m still building up to it. I didn’t actually get my heart rate into my desired range. That’s ok. I did my body good and I’m going to keep learning and keep problem solving.

What about my arthritic knees? Same thing. You don’t heal your knees by not using them. BUT – you don’t start out with high impact exercises either. Typically it would be WAY too painful to do such exercises on arthritic knees to begin with. And yet, putting more and more load over time has indeed healed my knees such that I really can sprint. The Supernatural workouts are not high impact – but when I first started, the squats and lunges were very challenging for me to do.

You need to push your body to do more and go farther in order to be safe. But travel the journey to physical health in a safe way. How? You know whats coming.

How much more? A LITTLE more.

How much farther? A BIT farther.

Always.

What I’m doing now has been enabled by starting where I was – and just pushing that bit more – and continuing to up the ante over time. From walking with a cane to running 5k’s. From sedentary to active. From highly diabetic to non-diabetic. From morbidly obese to….well, this one is a work in progress.
