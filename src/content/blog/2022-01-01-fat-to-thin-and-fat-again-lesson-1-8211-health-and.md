It’s time I finally got around to speaking about what went wrong. How did I lose 80lbs and get fit and then gain 100lbs and and lose all of that hard won fitness.

Lesson 1 – Health and Fitness is not a Destination

You never arrive. One can look to HOW weight was lost or fitness was achieved. For me, I don’t think the answer is there. It is not what I did to lose weight or get fit that was deficient. It was how I lived after.

Health has to be maintained. Fitness has to be maintained. It’s not some earth shattering reveal. After all that transformation I was super happy and proud of myself. I loved my new body. I lost the fear that had been driving me. The fear from that terrible night in the hospital when it finally dawned on me – I wasn’t going to die young, I was going to live sick.

For a solid year, the highest priority, the top of my mind every day was my health. Then “life happened”. Other things needed my attention. My kids were in their teens and then the “launching into adult” years. Those were some very rough times. Job losses. The death of my mother. Then Covid.

Excuses, excuses. But true. I took my eye off the ball and gradually returned to my old ways. My old ways of eating and my old sedentary ways.

This relates to my current status of having my diabetes in remission. Nobody understands better than I, that the health gains I’ve made can be lost. But I refuse to buy into the “diseased forever” mindset. I am no longer diabetic. Just as at one time I was no longer obese. But health has to be maintained.

If I once again revert to my old ways…I’ll redevelop health problems, including but not limited to type 2 diabetes.

I have a lot more work to do to get back to the size of the photo on the left. And after that – I have a lifetime's work of continuing to eat and move healthy.
