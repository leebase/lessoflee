I love my Supernatural workouts. I’m in my hotel room but Supernatural transports me to beautiful places.

As Twisted Sister Sings “We're Not Going To Take It” – I reflect on my journey. Not going to take being weak and diseased, at least of the kind I can do something about.

This routine is fast paced with knee strikes. I couldn’t have done this routine when I started 2 years ago. Now it’s just a light workout.
