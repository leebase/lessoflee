I bought the Meta Quest 2 VR headset and subscribed to the Supernatural workout app more than 2 years ago. Winter was coming and I couldn’t walk steady enough for winter conditions in Chicago. I was able to continue working out in my own home. It was the first winter that I got more in shape than less.

Now I take it with me when traveling as it’s a great workout in a hotel room. The room melts away and is replaced by beautiful locations. The top of a mountain, on the sandy shores of an ocean beach. Even the moon!

Today’s workout was with Madonna songs. You bet this 58yr old was doing his best to dance while singing out the lyrics. Alone in my hotel room were only my wife could hear me ![🤪](~/assets/posts/2023-12-24-christmas-eve-workout/1f92a.png)![😎](~/assets/posts/2023-12-24-christmas-eve-workout/1f60e.png)

You know you’ve crossed a bridge when you feel a bit achy from a long drive and think – I need a good workout to get the blood and fluids moving so I’ll feel better. Rather than – I can’t work out because I’m achy.

And I do feel better post workout.

I wish you all a Merry Christmas and happiness for whatever your holiday festivities.
