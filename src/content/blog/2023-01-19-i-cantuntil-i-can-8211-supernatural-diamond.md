I’ve been exercising with the Supernatural app on my Quest 2 VR headset for almost a year and a half. When I started I had trouble doing the shortest, lowest level workout.

As my fitness improved, my levels improved and the scores improved. Platinum, Double Platinum even Triple Platinum. Low, medium, high, Pros Only – yet never a Diamond on any of the levels.

I Can’t get a diamond.

Until today.

Today I got a diamond on a medium level quick hits (just 2 songs). 99.5% of the targets hit with 99.6% power.

Between ”I Can’t” and “I can” is …

Doing more and going farther.

How much more? A little more
How much farther? A bit farther
Always
