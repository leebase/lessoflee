A few months ago I had a micro-stroke in my right eye. A blood vessel burst and the fluid in my eye was so much that I couldn’t read. I went to a specialist right away, and had a follow up a month later. At that time I was just on the verge of needing shots in my eye due to swelling/inflammation of the retina. Not at all uncommon for diabetics. We decided to give it another month, the doctor expected that it would have progressed to the point of needing shots in that time.

I am happy to report that at today’s appointment, my eye had healed! Yep, the little bump that had him concerned – not only had it not gotten worse – it had gone away, praise God! The blood/fluid in my eye has slowly and surely been reabsorbing. I can now read again in that eye. It’s not completely gone, but it’s improving. The doctor said it can take a long time.

I mentioned that I had done a 3 day fast, and I’d been taking a lozenge (NO2U) that helps with creating Nitric Oxide – which is supposed to help blood vessels relax, lowering blood pressure among other good things. I have also been ramping my exercise intensity slowly back up. He said “whatever you are doing seems to be working, keep it up”. I have no idea if the lozenge is helping other than it does indeed help lower my blood pressure (which I also take medication for, but still need more help). I know this because I can and do measure my blood pressure at home.

So with that relatively clean bill of health, I came back home and went out for a Saturday Push Day run. It was glorious. I got my heart rate up to 95% which is my goal for push days. I did this by running a half mile, then finishing off with a sprint. And then a set of 8 Tabatta HIIT sprints (run full speed 10 seconds, walk 20 seconds). It’s very challenging for me to do and my heart rate got up into Zone 5 (above 90%). Then I walked until I got to “my hill” and ran up the hill. That effort pushed me to the 95% of max heart rate. Push day accomplished. I walked back home completing 4 miles total of walking, running, and sprinting.

I am encouraging and enabling my body to heal itself. Along with a keto/carnivore diet and fasting, I push myself physically by doing more and going farther.

How much more? A little more.

How much farther? A bit farther.

Always.
