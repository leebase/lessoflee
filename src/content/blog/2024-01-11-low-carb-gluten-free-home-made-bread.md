I like the “keto flour” and the bread from Victoria’s Keto Kitchen. It genuinely feels and tastes like bread. I’ve also made biscuits that were fantastic. The main ingredient is bamboo fiber. It’s pricey and hard to find. I bought mine on Amazon.

It’s a lot of hassle to make the flour first, then the bread – to make a ham sandwich. But sometimes I really want a sandwich. It also toasts well.

She says you can use this flour in any way you use regular flour. It has 4g of net carbs for the entire loaf. Net carbs as most of the carbs are indigestible fiber. Fair warning, it’s mostly fiber so you feel it on the way out of you eat a lot at once.

I also use this flour when cooking liver and other breading situations where I might otherwise use pork panko.

I intend to try making crackers, pizza crust and cookies in the future with this flour.

I mostly just go without keto versions of bread, pasta etc. I ate them more so in the beginning of transitioning from the Standard American Diet. Changing to keto is how I reversed my Type 2 diabetes. It’s a nice change of pace to have a “flour” that tastes like regular flour but doesn’t raise blood glucose.
