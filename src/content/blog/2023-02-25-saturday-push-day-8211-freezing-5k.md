It’s Saturday, time to push my limits. It’s just below freezing outside with a dusting of snow on the ground. 3 layers and I’m out the door to go for a run.

I originally planned to do sprint intervals but I didn’t want to tempt the snow and ice gods. I ran a 5k without walking for the second time. Really happy that my Supernatural VR workout is keeping the fitness improvement going over the winter.

I’m getting below 10minute kilometers. Not winning any races…but I am pushing back my limitations… by doing more and going farther.

How much more? A little more

How much farther? A bit farther

Always
