You’ve probably heard the obligatory disclaimer whenever you celebrate rapid early weight loss on a fast or a low-carb diet: "Don't get too excited—that's just water."

It’s true that much of that water will return upon refeeding. But to dismiss it as "just water" is to miss the entire point of metabolic healing. That whoosh is the most immediate, tangible sign that your efforts are working.

I’m coming up on Day 3 of my current fast. I’ve dropped 7 lbs so far, and this time, I’m not downplaying the excitement. I’m celebrating it, because I understand the underlying physiology.

Why the Whoosh is Your Best Motivator

Why do we lose so much water that the scale moves so quickly at the beginning of a fast or a low-carb diet? The answer is simple, powerful, and central to metabolic health:

 * Insulin Drops: When you eliminate food or carbohydrates, your insulin levels plummet—often to their rock-bottom fasting state.

 * Kidneys Release Sodium: Insulin is the primary signal that tells your kidneys, "Hold onto salt!" When insulin drops, that signal is removed. Your kidneys immediately flush out excess sodium (salt).

 * Water Follows Salt: Water always follows salt. 

The body releases the sodium, and the water—which your body was unnecessarily retaining due to years of high insulin—is flushed out.

That rapid scale movement is the sound of your body releasing that excess fluid.

The True Meaning: Healing is Happening

So what? Lowered insulin is the entire ball game for overcoming metabolic disease.

 * Insulin Resistance: Type 2 Diabetes is not a disease of high blood sugar; it is a disease of too much insulin (hyperinsulinemia) driving insulin resistance. This is the root cause of T2D and many other ailments.

 * The Whoosh is the Proof: The &#8216;whoosh' is not a proxy for fat loss, but it is a direct, real-time confirmation that you have successfully lowered your insulin. You have achieved the single most important action necessary for healing.

The excess fluid you were holding onto was literally a byproduct of a disease state.

The healing doesn't happen in a day. You didn’t develop insulin resistance in a day, either. But seeing that scale drop directly proves that your eating and fasting choices are allowing for the long periods of time in a low insulin state necessary to, in time, heal your insulin resistance.

So go ahead and be excited about that whoosh. Let the scale delight you. That water was a byproduct of the disease state (hyperinsulinemia), and when some of it returns upon refeeding, it’s no problem. See it as the sign it is: you are healing.

 * Disclaimer: Always consult your doctor for your own specific medical needs and health planning.

 * Note: Aggressive electrolyte management (salt, potassium, magnesium) is crucial to avoid cramps when flushing this water and sodium.
