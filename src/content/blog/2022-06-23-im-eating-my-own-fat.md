It’s morning of the first day of my 5 day fast for June. I like to say, I’m not going without food – I’m eating my own fat. I’ve stored the fat away for a winter or famine that never came. So now I fast. The food is all over my body just waiting to be eaten.

This is my first fast on my carnivore-ish diet. I’m starting at a weight 14lbs less than last month. I’d say my experiment with the carnivore diet has been a smashing success. Or at least a “stall breaking” success.

My bs was 108 this morning and was 127 the first morning last month. I would expect to be in the 90s and stay in the 70s to 90s the rest of the fast as has been my experience with the 6 previous ones.

My breath ketones are 3.8 – I’m in the beginning of ketosis already. Last month they were 1.6 – they weren’t higher than 3.8 until the evening of the third day. When people ask me what kind of diet I’m on, I’ve often replied “keto” as that’s the label closest to what I do. But, the reality is that I’m almost never in ketosis the way I normally eat. Until this month with the carnivore-ish diet.

I am interested to find out what difference there is with hunger this time around. I have definitely noticed that I’ve had fewer cravings and less hunger on my veggie and fruit free diet. (Not perfectly free, but close enough).
