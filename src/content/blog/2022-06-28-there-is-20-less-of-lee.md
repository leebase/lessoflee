Hit a nice milestone.  I have lost 20% of my starting weight of 336lbs.   The scale isn’t everything, but it’s not nothing either ![🙂](~/assets/posts/2022-06-28-there-is-20-less-of-lee/1f642.png)

I’m 2 days past the end of this month’s 5 day fast.  Which turned out to be a 4 day fast.  I’ve always said you can’t fail at fasting.  You simply end the fast and live to fast again another day.  

Lessons from this month’s fast.  It was definitely better going into the fast in a state of ketosis from the 3 weeks of carnivore/zero carb diet.  I may not be sticking to carnivore but I’ve learned that my carb tolerance is a lot lower than I had thought.  Nuts are probably off the table for me for awhile.   There was no magical “because you were on carnivore, the fast will be easier”.  It was a tough as always.  I feel like a “fasting athlete” ![🙂](~/assets/posts/2022-06-28-there-is-20-less-of-lee/1f642.png)

Two days after the end of the fast and my breath ketones are 14 – with 10 being “optimal ketosis”.  Not bad.  And a lot higher than I am able to get to without the fasting.  The benefits of the extended fasting last longer than the fast itself.

After the 2 day “bounce back” I am down 8.1 lbs from the 4 day fast.  That’s better than my other fasts which I think goes to the power of being in ketosis from the very beginning.

I had my Dexascan yesterday.   I lost a pound of visceral fat.  That’s the killer fat.  I lost 7lbs of fat since April.   The best thing – the “volume” of my visceral adipose tissue is down 36 cubic inches.  That’s a 16% decline in the volume of my visceral adipose fat since April.  There is definitely Less of Lee.  My first couple of scans I was too fat for the machine to be able to discern my visceral fat.
