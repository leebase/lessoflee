Had a great sleep the night before and a bad sleep last night. Sleep is a vital component to health in general and to weight loss and type 2 diabetes specifically. I’ve been working on improving my sleep.

I haven’t come up with a magical cure as of yet. I am confident that exercise has helped greatly. Look at the resting heart rate. I used to be in the 80s and am now in the 50s. That’s from the exercise for sure.

But – some things I know lead to a bad night of sleep: social media before bed time, social media when I wake up to pee in the middle of the night, and in general not winding down a good hour or two before bed time.

That’s what I did last night. I argued on social media all the way up to bed time. Had a late time in going to bed. And then peeked on my phone in the middle of the night.

Shocking that such behavior and a good night’s sleep don’t go together.
