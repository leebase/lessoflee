I looked up how much weight I had to lose to go from morbidly obese to ones. Geez, I have to lose 25 more pounds…to JUST be obese. I’m sharing this as everything isn’t sunshine and rainbows for me. I was sad for a bit….not too long, but I then pushed that out of my mind. That goal will have to wait.

It’s important to have near and mid term goals to go along with long term goals. Celebrate all the steps along the way because it’s EASY to get discouraged at a multi-year task in front of you.

So…50lbs Less of Lee and getting into the 280's are on deck. I think Ove seen the last of seeing 3xx on the scale.
