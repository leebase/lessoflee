As part of my ongoing series about pushing myself to new heights in fitness, I faced a unique challenge this week: I went out of town for a family event. Therefore I couldn’t keep up with my Saturday Push Day routine?

Or…could I?

After returning to the hotel at 7 pm, I was glad I had planned ahead and brought my Meta Quest 2 VR headset with me. The amazing thing about this headset, paired with the workout app Supernatural, is that it transports me to incredible places. I'm no longer in my house or the hotel room; instead, I find myself on a mountain or in another stunning location.

With Coach Chesney by my side, I embarked on a medium-level workout using Supernatural. She has come a long way in her own health reclamation journey, proving that you don't have to be a fitness model to inspire others towards better health.

Although I aimed for a diamond score, I fell short once again. Triple platinum would have to suffice. But that was just a warm-up, really – the hard level was up next.

The next challenging routine, complete with complicated choreography and great music, left me in awe. I couldn't believe I was doing knee strikes, squats, and lunges with my arthritic knees. The score read platinum, but my heart rate screamed, "PUSH Day Baby!" My heart rate soared into the mid-150s, reaching 95% of my max heart rate. Drenched in sweat, I felt a sense of accomplishment.

By the way – the push is the same. Walking down stairs without a cane or holding a rail was hard. Walking around the neighborhood with a cane for 15 minutes was hard. Then 30 minutes. Then a bit faster. Then a bit longer. Today's workout was hard. The same kind of hard all along. But what I can do keeps improving.

How much more? A little more.

How much farther? A bit farther.

Always.
