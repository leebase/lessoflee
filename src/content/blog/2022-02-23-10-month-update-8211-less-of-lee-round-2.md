I have to do it all over again. 10 years ago, I’m in mid 40's, lost 80lbs and got fit. 10 months ago, I was back where I started, only 20 lbs heavier, type 2 diabetic (A1c of 11.2, taking Metformin, Glipizide and Trulicity), osteo arthritic knees – and – now I'm in my mid fifties. Maybe I count as late fifties now.

I read The Diabetes Code by Jason Fung and began intermittent fasting, low carb eating. I also got a Freestyle Libre continuous glucose monitor. Those are the 3 pillars among everything else I did.

My diabetes is in remission. 7 months off of all the meds and maintaining an A1c less than the diabetic level. I’ve lost over 50lbs even as I'm beginning to add in muscle (verified with Dexa scan). I have gone from morbidly obese to just obese.

My knees feel so much better. I sleep better. I weathered a bout of Covid in a few days. No more blurry vision first thing in the morning.

I am a former diabetic. That ruffles some people's feathers. You can consider yourself “forever a diabetic” if you want. I’m working on healing my body and my A1c is below the diabetic level and continuing to improve.

I keep learning and keep improving and keep stepping up my efforts. With my doctor's counsel I have added a monthly 5 day fast. I have also started strength training twice a week and have been going back to the health club for some of the aerobics style machines.

I walk with so much more stability. I can walk down stairs without holding onto a rail. I can walk across the yard instead of sticking to the sidewalk. It’s winter in Chicago but I have not gone into “exercise hibernation”. I’m going to go into spring weighing less and being more fit than I entered winter. A rarity for me.

I can honest-to-God workout now. Feels amazing. I played pickle ball – which is a racket sport. I’ve gone bowling, These arthritic knees of mine aren’t cured but I’ve gained back ability. Badminton and tennis – here I come! Not yet, but I’ll get there.

I eat more cleanly. Not perfectly, but I snack far less and go off plan far less. My two meals a day are delicious feasts. I can continue to live like this.

I have gone from “trying to reverse you 2 diabetes” to “working on my total health”. Muscle is health, so I'm working to build muscle. I’m still very obese but am casting my efforts as “body recomposition” and not merely “losing weight”. I’m working on sleep and stress management.

So many of you have cheered me on and encouraged me and I thank you. I hope you can remember how I started only 9 months ago. All I do now I couldn’t do then. All I know now, I didn’t know then. All I’ve progressed to now, hadn’t happened. Make the decision and start the journey. Learn, grow and improve along the way.

If I can do it, you can.
