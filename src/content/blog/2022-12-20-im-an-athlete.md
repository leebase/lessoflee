A bit more than a year ago I bought the Quest 2 and subscribed to the workout app, Supernatural. I was looking for something to workout at home during the winter as the health clubs were still closed and I couldn’t walk in snow and ice.

“Hello athlete”…coach Leanne greets me as I try the very first workout…a low level routine. I could barely get through it and I couldn’t do the squats and lunges at all.

Fast forward to this morning and it’s winter again. Below freezing outside and I’m reading about a new routine that was just released. A fellow SN user complained about how humiliating the new workout was with targets smashing into her face (virtually, of course). I knew the feeling the first time I tried a medium intensity.

Well, of course I had to try it….it had the song Freebird! It’s a HARD level and it was 36 minutes long. And I KILLED it scoring a double platinum! Man my arms were burning. I loved it…and did all the squats and lunges while whacking away at the targets.

I am an athlete.

My heart rate only averaged 121 with 139 for the peak. A slow jog used to push me into the 150s. I’m getting fit enough that my heart/breath isn’t my first limiter. My arm muscles were aching from the exertion. Felt great. My arthritic knees didn’t complain at all during or after.

If the lovely person who felt humiliated happens to read this…take heart. The future you is totally going to be able to handle this routine. Take your time. Put in the work and your body will adapt.

You will have to push for more and go farther.

How much more? A little more.

How much farther? A bit farther.

Always

I’m am an athlete…you can be too.
