Day 3 was indeed easier than the first two days. I didn’t feel any hunger at all until 1pm and then it wasn’t so bad. Also feeling a bit of hunger now that it’s dinner time. I miss eating, because I like to eat. But I’m not being tortured with hunger.

My ketones were 4 the first morning, 3 the second and – finally – 12 today. That’s in the high fat burning mode. Well…well…well below ketoacidosis – which is something that can happen to type 1 diabetics. I already know my pancreas is creating insulin so I’m not in danger it ketoacidosis.

I worked out this after noon. No problems, no feeling of being week. I took a tbl spoon of ACV and tsp of minerals after the workout.

My weight has hit a new low, there are now 49lbs less of Lee. The 50lb milestone is tantalizingly close. I was on the Vinnie Tortorich podcast 9 years ago after I had lost 80lbs. We agreed for me to go on again when I’d lost 50 with this journey. I’ll also being doing a podcast episode with one of our members here soon.

Two more days to go. I’ll be breaking my fast for dinner New Year's Eve.
