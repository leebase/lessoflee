Feels like the fist day of school – starting a new job today.

I was kind of “saved” by the Covid lock down. Last time I worked in Chicago – I had to walk with a cane and it was difficult to make it from the train station to the office.

Now – I’m 50lbs lighter and I didn’t even take my cane as backup. Don’t need it. Didn’t bring a “sweat rag” to mop my forehead – don’t need it.

The people meeting me today won’t know the me from a year ago. But they will get to see the me next year that will be in far better shape than the guy showing up for his first day.

My diabetes hitting 11.2 last year was a wake up call to improve my health – not just my bs readings.
