Since it can be done – it’s just a matter of “what must YOU do”? We aren’t all exactly the same. What works for someone else might not for you. You might actually have an easier time.

The asnswer is – try and keep on working it out.

If you cut out sodas and desert and go for a daily 15 minute walk…and that fixes your diabetes…great!

If you eat everything in moderation and jog 3 times a week and that fixes your diabetes. Great!

If you cut down to 50g of carbs a day and do yoga and that fixes your diabetes..fantastic!

If you go vegan or carnivore and that fixes your diabetes. Hurray.

I intermittent fast daily, extended fast once a month, eat a very low carb diet…and dabble with carnivore…as well as exercise an hour or so a day plus strength training. Why? Because that’s what I’ve had to do to fix my diabetes. And I continue on because diabetes is just the visible part of metabolic syndrome/insulin resistance syndrome – and I have a long way to go to fix my health.

Do what you must. As easy as it can be, or as hard as it needs to be.

One thing we know does not work. Diabetes meds and insulin do NOT fix diabetes. Neither does berberine or Curalin or any other pill. Take them as needed to help control your bs – but do whatever level of diet and exercise and fasting that it takes to HEAL your body.

It can be done. You can do it too.
