Look at the photo. That is a morbidly obese man on the way to having a heart attack or any of a number of other dire health consequences.

But Lee — you’ve made progress. Yes. And I’m happy I have. Happy, however, isn’t the only appropriate response. I am always mixing and matching my motivations. Happy and positive reinforcement CAN lead to complacency. So I put on this shirt that shows my fat rolls in unflattering, but true detail. I’d do one without the shirt but I wouldn’t want to gross everyone out.

Here’s an analogy. You are speeding along in sports car going 180mph. Extremely dangerous. So you slow down to 140mph. That’s progress. That’s a good thing. In terms of health, it takes a lot of effort to “slow down”. But — here comes the big BUT — you are still speeding at a very dangerous pace.

If you didn’t know me, didn’t know the improvement over the last year and saw me. You’d think “there’s a man with severe metabolic syndrome on the way to an early death”. And you’d be right. I’m not healthy, I’m “less unhealthy”.

They stopped calling it “morbidly obese” – mustn’t be politically incorrect. Now it’s Obese Class I,II and III. I’m still obese class three. Not too far away from obese class II….but even when I get there…it’s still a bad place to be.

You can’t get from 180mph to a safe driving speed without going through 140mph, 120mp, 100mph etc. All of them still too fast. I’m still driving too fast but working hard to slow down.
