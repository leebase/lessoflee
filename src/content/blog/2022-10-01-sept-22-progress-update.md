The belly is shrinking. This is a February photo compared to now. Your clothes are the best measure. As always, I like data and I just had a dexascan which measures fat, lean, bone density. Completely unnecessary – yet I wanted to track where my weight loss was coming from as some folks are sure fasting wastes away muscles (it doesn’t).

In the last 3 months I’ve lost 13.4 lbs. During that time I lost 13.5lbs of fat. Yes, I lost more fat than weight as I gained in lean mass. Lean mass isn’t just muscle, it’s the organs, blood and stomach contents. As I had the scan after a five day fast, pretty sure my stomach was empty.

Importantly, I lost 1.5lbs of visceral adipose fat. That’s the killer fat. I’m still in the red/high risk category, but I’m making improvement. A year ago I was so fat the machine couldn’t get a reading in this area.

Going back a year to my first scan (I’d already lost 35lbs before this) I’ve lost:

- 46lbs of weight

- 40lbs of fat

- 5lbs of lean

I’m doing a good job of losing fat and keeping my muscles. Keep in mind my belly has lost 90cubic centimeters in volume. There is less skin needed to cover it and I do not yet have loose skin. I’m sure the fasting and autophagy have assisted in recycling this unneeded protein.

While I have no measurement for muscle weight, I can see my muscles starting to pop. I’m 25% to 2x stronger in every muscle group since the first of the year. Not saying I’m strong, just not as weak ![🤣](~/assets/posts/2022-10-01-sept-22-progress-update/1f923.png)

I’m thrilled to see the weight starting to come off my belly.
