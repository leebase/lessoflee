What a joy it is to meet some ambitious goals. When I started this 2nd journey to health I made 3 goals. I accomplished the first- getting off all the diabetes meds and put my diabetes into remission. I’m making good progress on the third which is getting to my goal weight of 215lbs. I just crossed the rubicon in my middle goal which was to be able to vigorously exercise for an hour. I played table tennis for a half hour and then did a 45 minute “Super Monster” aerobic exercise via the Supernatural app.

There were 13 songs I worked out to. I was wanting to quit by the end of the third. I kept telling myself “just do one more”. And I’d do that one more and want to stop. But then “just one more”…until I completed all of them. It felt terrific. My heart rate was in the 140's for most of the workout.

This is a long way from working on just walking. That’s where I started and had to walk and walk and walk….building up my ability to walk for a good 90 minutes.

I’ve come up with the phrase: You can’t…until you can.

Well, today I checked off “working out vigorously for an hour” off the list of things I can’t do.
