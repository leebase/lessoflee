Rainy and cold day outside, but no problem as I have my Meta Quest 2 and Supernatural VR workout at my beck and call. Man do I enjoy this type of exercise. I look forward to it. Good music, challenging and ever changing routines, and motivational coaches.

I did a couple of them since the first Medium level one was only 4 songs long. I choose a High level, quick strike, two song as an add-on. This wan’t a push day, just a nice midweek working up a sweat and loosening up my muscles and tendons.

Coach Doc mentioned the joy of movement during his workout and it encapsulated what I was feeling. My thumb and toes are still healing but I can get my regular workout regimen going again. Waking up with that “I worked out yesterday” ache in the muscles is nice. Not “I worked too hard and now I’m so sore” but that bit of ache where you feel the repair and build back stronger going on.

It can take a bit to get past the initial adjustment to pushing your body in exercise. I assure you, it’s a key to feeling and living better.

p.s.  Always consult your own health team for your specific needs

p.p.s. Educate yourself to fully participate in your health strategy
