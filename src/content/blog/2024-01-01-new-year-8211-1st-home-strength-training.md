One of my resolutions was to find and follow a strength training program I can do on my own from home. Building muscle is important for metabolic health and vitality – the ability to do whatever needs to be done around the house. I had become so weak and diseased that just walking up and down the stairs in my house was a challenge.

I love my virtual reality workout, Supernatural. It’s so fun and engaging that I’ve kept up with it for more than 2 years. It has helped my fitness tremendously. And yet, I have really failed to follow through on strength training for the past year.

A friend of mine recommended the 5&#215;5 routine that he’s followed successfully for years. I’ll post a link at the end. It’s free. The program is designed around barbells and a squat rack. I don’t have those, but I do have an adjustable dumbbell set and a weight bench that I bought a year ago and they have sat gathering dust ever since.

Weight lifting intimidates me. I don’t know why. Maybe it’s hold over from feeling inadequate in the high school weight room. Time to get past that and do my body good by building up muscle strength.

3 times a week, 3 exercises each time. 5 sets with 5 reps for each exercise. It took me about a half hour. You start off with the empty bar and add weight each session. Therefore it’s very easy at first, giving you time to work on form.

You keep increasing until you can’t. Then go back down and then build back up. At least, that’s my current understanding.

I started with only 20lbs. 5 sets of 5 reps of squats, bench press and rows. It was easy to find videos on YouTube for the dumbbell version of these exercises.

Funny thing, my core is so weak that I couldn’t get up off the bench. So for the first two sets I pretty much had to roll over and fall off into the floor, then get up.

I subsequently set the bench to have a slight incline. Then I could do my reps and sit up after and gracefully stand up from the bench.

Clearly I’m no weight lifting savant. But then, everyone starts somewhere. I showed up for myself and started. That’s the victory.

I will eventually outgrow the dumbbells and will go to the gym. By then, I will have more confidence and knowledge. Yep, I’m vain and really hate showing weakness and ineptitude. How sad. Because of my pride, I have remained weak and inept.
