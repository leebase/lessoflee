It’s Saturday and time to go push my limits. Woke up not feeling like going out to push myself. Then I got a late start as my month old grandson was having himself a nice nap on my belly. Some cuddle time with my main little man and it was time to go out and work on me.

I took inspiration from my daughter, Cathy, and decided to see how fast I could run a mile. Sure, it’s a shorter distance than a 5k but that meant I could focus on running faster. Plus it’s nice to change things up. I certainly labored with my breathing and had that “I pushed myself, I may want to puke” feeling after. My heart rate hit the 98% of max so I know it was a good push despite the shorter distance and duration.

I may have neglected my health a long time but that is the past. For the last two years I have been turning it around. If I keep this going I may get to see him graduate high school. I have people who depend on me, who love me. Plenty of good reasons to keep putting in the hard work.

Eating right and building up your body isn’t a punishment for having type 2 diabetes and osteoarthritis. It’s how I should have been living the whole time. Not only do my loved ones deserve me to be as healthy as I can – I deserve it too.

I implore you to go out and do your body good. Your future self and your loved ones will thank you then for the work you put in today. Add one new habit. Say no to the foods that aren’t good for you. Push past a few minutes of pain. Do more and go farther.

How much more? A little more.

How much farther? A bit farther.

Always.
