I just did a cardio workout with Supernatural (a VR app) – a “Super Monster” high level routine. 12 fun rock songs with amazing choreography.

What about my micro-stroke in my eye? I am being careful. I’ve been slowly upping my exercise intensity and paying attention to how I feel, my eyes, heart rate and blood pressure.

The app shows your heart rate at the end of each song. I kept my effort back from “all out” and my heart rate stayed below Zone 4. My aerobics are normal mostly zone 4 with peaks into zone 5. That’s the whole point – giving my heart a workout.

But – given my eye I have dialed back. The eye specialist said the moderate exercise was ok and to talk to my regular doctor about anything more.

After 5 songs I stopped and took my blood pressure. Then again after the next 4. And after the last 3. At each point it was lower than what I wake up to.

After the last my systolic (top number) was 95! I have noticed consistently that at moderate levels of exercise, my blood pressure goes down. Now I can see that during the exercise I’m not going very high.

Why not just “play it safe” by taking it easy. I agree with being safe – that’s why I am on the Highway to Well. I was on the road to illness, a pretty good ways down that road.

I just know that being sedentary isn’t safe. Step by step I’m figuring out what my new limits are. Certain forms of exercise could be problematic for me. I’m definitely not jumping into power lifting while holding my breath.

It would be great if there was a book that specifically talked about my exact situation. I am doing what I can and I educate myself, I am talking to my doctors and I’m paying attention to my body.

I know that I had a high risk for heart disease, stroke and the like. That’s why I haven’t stopped at just getting my blood sugar under control and why I didn’t accept just taking meds while eating tasty treats.

I am measurably healthier than I was two years ago but I am far from the picture of perfect health. In an instant my eye sight has been diminished from the micro stroke. If I didn’t have two eyes I’d already be visually impaired. In another instant, I could be dead of a heart attack.

That’s why I’m still working on my health and striving to keep exercising – but safely.

I am on the Highway to Well and I’m not stopping.

p.s. Always consult your own health team for your specific needs

p.p.s. Educate yourself and be a full participant in your health strategy
