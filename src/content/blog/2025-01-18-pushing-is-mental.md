It’s Saturday Push Day and I once again restart my weight lifting.  Didn’t I restart two weeks ago?  Yes, and I may have to restart again two weeks from now.  I don’t like lifting weights but I do like regaining ability and vitality.  I was in such a good zone for the first half of last year.  Now I’m battling a mental block.  So today's push wasn’t about setting a new record, but pushing myself past my reluctance.  If you wait to FEEL like working on your health, you won’t do anything.  Just do it.  It may be a commercial slogan, but it’s true.  

I do my 5 x 5 workout with dumbbells at perhaps 60% of what I had worked up to and was feeling great.  Hard enough to be a challenge, but nowhere near max.   I was about to do a quick hits Supernatural boxing workout as that “bit extra” that Push Days are made of, when I tweaked my back as I was putting away the weights.

So now we have to talk about risk vs reward.   Lifting weights is not scary risky as so many of us believe.  If you move your body, you invite injury risk.  There are ways to exercise in unsafe and actually flirt with injury.  There is no way to be completely sure you couldn’t possibly pull a muscle or break a bone or have a heart attack.

I have pulled muscles in my back bending down to pick something off the floor.  I’d much rather be working to strengthen my back and tweak it than just be so weak that normal life is dangerous.  

The reward for lifting weights is muscle strength.  Strong muscles are the ability to live independently, to have vitality even in old age.  I am far more able to participate in life today than three years ago when I started.  I was walking with a cane, stairs were a challenge, I feared falling while showering.  I almost couldn’t cross Wacker Drive in Chicago in the time allotted for walking.  I could go on.

I am not taking any anti-inflammatories for the pain.  It’s a minor pain and acute inflammation is part of the healing process.  I did use a massage gun, and took a cold shower.  I’m sitting comfortably and allowing the pain to guide me to take it easy.

The call of the moment is to rest and let the body build itself back up stronger.

You know my mantra:

How much more? A little more.

How much farther? A bit farther.

Always.

Some days that “more” isn’t “more than ever before”.  It’s more than I want to do in the moment.  I really didn’t want to lift weights.  I also really don’t want to be weak and in poor health.  So today I pushed past my mental block.  Again.

p.s. Always consult your health team for your specific needs.

p.p.s. Educate yourself. Be your own best advocate.
