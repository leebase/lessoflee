Mindset is such a vital component to regaining health. I took this photo to prepare my mind for the work ahead this year. I made a lot of progress in the last nine months. And yet, there is so much more work to do.

It’s a fine balance to walk. Being dissatisfied and striving to improve…being mindful and grateful of accomplishments already achieved. Both are necessary to keep one positive and moving forward. Either one can also work against progress.

If you are too involved in being dissatisfied with where you are, you can become overwhelmed and burdened into inaction. If you are living too much on past accomplishments…you can likewise cease the necessary work to keep making further progress and actually lose gains already made.

I do a lot of celebrating all of the milestones and victories of my health journey. I find it very beneficial. This photo is the balance, the reminder, the kick in the pants to keep on keeping on.
