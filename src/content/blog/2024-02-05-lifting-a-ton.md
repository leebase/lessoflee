Just completed the first workout of week 6 of my StrongLifts 5&#215;5 workout and realized that I passed the ton mark.

I started as light as my dumbbells would go – 5lbs. This workout was 5 sets of 5 reps of squats, overhead press, and one set of 5 deadlift.

So at the beginning that would be (5+5) for the two dumbbells. So that’s 10x 25 = 250 for the squats, another 250 for the overhead press and 50 for the deadlift. Grand total of 550 lbs / 249 kilos.

Today my squats were 42.5lbs each dumbbell for 5&#215;5 = 2,125 lbs which is just over a ton!

Add in the overhead press and dead lift for a total of 3,775 lbs or 1,712 kilos.

Sounds like a lot when added up…but it’s just a 80lb squat and 50lb over head press. That’s barely more than the empty Olympic bar.

I started light so that I could practice the forms and get some “muscle memory” going. Then it’s just adding more weight each session – which is progressive overload.

Or as I say –

How much more? A little more.

How much farther? A bit farther.

Always

Or in this case just a bit heavier. It will be interesting to find out where I will hit my first limits. My overhead press is already difficult. Not sure I’ll make all 5&#215;5 next time with the additional weight.

I’ll soon hit the 50lbs each that my dumbbells go up to for the squats. I have a 20lb weight vest I’ll add so that I can keep going. Then I’ll add resistance bands to keep progressing. I don’t have room for barbells and such. I’ll eventually have to go to the gym.

It was hard to get myself going on the strength training at home. I bought my bench and powerblock dumbbells a year ago Christmas.

I just couldn’t get myself to use them. This beginner program I’m now following has made it simple. It’s not nearly as difficult as I had thought. Just start light so that you can get used to the motions and form.

I did my body good today. Looking forward to hearing how you did your body good. How did you push that bit more and farther?

p.s.  Always consult your own health team for your specific needs
