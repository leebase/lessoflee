It’s hard for me to capture in a selfie, but I’ve developed muscles between my neck and shoulders for the first time in my life. I even had to look up what they were called: trapezius muscles. I’ve been working out with the Supernatural app for more than two years. I’ve progressed in a lot of ways. Most importantly was regaining balance and the ability to walk up and down stairs leading eventually to me ceasing to need a cane. Now I have these muscles visible like never before. All those waving my arms over head smashing targets to great music, coaching and wonderful locations has been transforming my body.

It’s recovery day between lifting weights on Wednesday and Friday. I’d normally just go for a walk but it’s cold and frozen outside. I did the Tansiberian Orchestra routine which has a nice winter theme and great music. I remember the first time I did this workout as it was the very first time I’d tried a Medium level. I could barely do the Low level at first.

It was quite the challenge. My arms and abs were on fire. I wanted to quit but pressed on and finished. Tonight it was a nice warmup routine. After I finished this one I did a Medium level boxing. Here too I can see progress. The boxing sets are a lot harder for me with the bobbing and weaving.

All in all a nice midweek workout. I kept active despite the weather.

There is no exercise version of a “crash diet”. You have to put the work in over time. That “a little more, a bit farther” adds up. This is body building in the best sense of the phrase. No judges, no posing, no competing with others. But I’m living in a much better body than the one I lived in 3 years ago.

I did my body good today. Hope you did too.

p.s.  Always consult your own health team for your specific needs

p.p.s. Educate yourself to fully participate in your health strategy
