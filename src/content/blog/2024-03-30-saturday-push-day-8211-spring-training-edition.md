Out for a run. 5k? No, not yet. I have to work my way back into running shape after winter break. I did make it 1k without stopping.

My thumb is still recovering, so no weight lifting for now…but I could push my heart rate to 95% by running. That’s my goal for push days, it’s my heart I'm pushing to make it stronger.

I’m not focusing on running this year, but it’s what I can do while my thumb heals and it’s always an “easy” way to work out my heart.

So, like a baseball player, I’m in Spring Training.

I did my body good today.

How’d you do your body good?
