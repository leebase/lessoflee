Beef, butter, bacon and eggs.  A simplified carnivore diet that is taught and advocated by Dr Ken Berry.  It’s great as an elimination diet and for remaining a fat burner (being in ketosis).  I’m on my third cycle of my protocol and I’m making some deviations.  I’m bringing back in cheese and dairy (but not milk), and I’m not adding gratuitous fat.  No butter just to add fat.  What comes with the fatty meat, and to flavor and cook.  Why?  I want to burn my own fat.  I’m not being “low fat”, just less “high fat”.  I’m also adding lean protein add ons.  This is to up the protein and make the meals more satiating.  Not because fat is bad, but because I am trying to find an optimal mix that nourishes my body, satisfies and burns my body fat.  There are experts on both sides of the “high fat” vs “medium fat”.  I’m navigating my own way between the two.

I ate two meals today, photos below.  Breakfast felt huge.  I loved it.  Was totally full and didn’t eat again until dinner time.  Wasn’t tempted to snack.  Really nailed satiety.   What is it:  4 eggs cooked with 1 tbsp of bacon grease, a tbl spoon of jalepenos, 1/4 cup shredded cheddar.  2 sausage patties.  You see three on the plate, but not the dog who begs and gets one from me.  And a chicken sausage (leaner) with gouda cheese (small carbs).  This was a glorious breakfast feast.  I thought I was eating too much.  But you’ll see that I ended the day in a calorie deficit and didn’t hit my protein goal.

Second meal was 2 large Wendy’s hamburger patties.  2 slices of Kraft Deli Deluxe cheese (actual cheese, not their cheese like processed food).  A cup of 2% cottage cheese and 2 hard boiled eggs.  Not a huge feast, but I was nicely full, but not stuffed.  A lot of carbs in the cottage cheese that I didn’t expect.  They come from the lactose, not glucose or starch.   My cgm said “no problem” – didn’t even break 100.  Still, I think I’ll hold off on cottage cheese for awhile.  It was my attempt to add leaner protein.  In the future I’ll add a thin chicken breast or pork chop.

• Protein: 145 g 

• Fat: 132 g 

• Carbs: 23 g 

• Calories: 1,860 cal 

• Ratios: 31% protein / 64% fat / 5% carbs 

I do not plan to do macro breakdowns of every meal.  I’m just paying attention now to get an idea of what my meals look like to adjust the template.  

Did I hit satiety?  Yes. I was not hungry all day.  Felt comfortably stuffed after the first meal, satisfied after the second.   I could go to bed now and be happy.

Calories?  Shocked me. I thought I was pigging out.  I actually ate in a calorie deficit.  There is definitely something to high protein medium to high fat that brings satiety such that you eat less than you think without being hungry.  This is a LOT nicer than “just eat anything and count your calories”.  I’ve done that and was hungry all the time.

Protein?  Not enough.  Surprisingly, I only managed to hit a level that keeps muscle loss.   But I want to be optimal, to build muscle.  I need to up my protein.  My goal is 170-190g.  

Fat?  Perfectly happy with this amount of fat and the percentage.

Carbs?  More than I intended.  I’ll adjust going forward.  My cgm says it was fine.  It’s not like cottage cheese is my favorite food.  I can take a pass on it for awhile.

My breath ketones are 47 — yeah, that’s blazing ketosis still – four days after ending the fast.  This is longer in significant ketosis than my first cycle. Clearly today’s 24g of carbs was a metabolic blip.  Am I healing?  Will keep tabs.

I may go for an evening snack on turkey slice/cheese roll ups to boost my protein.   I’m not worried about the day to day intermittent fasting at this point since I’m cycling 7 day fasts every three weeks. 

Was the food delicious?  Yes.  Easy to make?  Yes.  Satisfying?  Oh yes.  Am I suffering on BBBE?  No.  Am I doing BBBE right?  No, I modify it to make me like it better, aim it more squarely at my goals, and eat flavor veggies.

This may not be the eating plan for you – but I hope the way I think through things is something to learn from.
