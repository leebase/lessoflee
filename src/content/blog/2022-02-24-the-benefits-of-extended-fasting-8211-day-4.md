Fasting is an amazing health practice. Intermittent fasting helped me get off all my diabetes meds and put type 2 diabetes into remission. With my doctor’s recommendation I have begun a monthly 5 day fast. This is a series of posts to describe the benefits. I have just finished day 4.

Day 4 is a continuation of the good things that have been happening. Here are some I haven’t brought up yet.

1. [Rise in stem cells](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC4102383/): your body creates new white blood cells and gets rid of bad proteins in the muscle, stem cells replace those tissues. Muscle fibers heal and adapt much faster.

2. [Reset DNA:](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/001457939500719P) We all have certain bad genes that are turned on. Fasting turns these off the most (obesity genes, allergy etc.).

3. [Reset microbiome:](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1111/1574-6941.12442#citedBy) There is a change in cravings, and a change of the ratio of good/bad bacteria, to help with losing weight and keeping it off
