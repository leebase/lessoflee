I’ve been strength training since Feb. You can see my progress in the carts below.

I didn’t know much about it. I read a couple of books (of course) – and then decided to spend the money to join a program rather than my usual “do it myself”.

I go twice a week for 20 minutes. Progressive overload using isokenetic machines. Think “nautilus” but with computer controlled hydraulic pistons instead of pulleys and weights.

It’s not so much about the method as it is consistency. I’ve missed very few sessions. They may only be 20 minutes, but I go all out to the extent I can.

And then rest so that the body can respond and build my muscles back stronger.

The program I use is [http://www.theexercisecoach.com](http://www.theexercisecoach.com) – they have studios all over the US.

The book 15 Minutes to Fitness teaches the principles. The two aren’t related but I find their teachings quite similar. The book is by Dr Ben Bocchicchio.

I also like the free [http://www.hybridchalestinics.com](http://www.hybridchalestinics.com) body weight program. I don’t have the room for a home gym so body weight exercises have always interested me.

It’s sad how weak I had let myself become. It’s amazing how the body can respond if you just work at it.

Muscle is health. Muscle is ability. Muscle is the ability to participate in life. Muscle is mobility. And sure, it looks good (I may test that theory a bit).

Building muscle is a vital part of putting type 2 diabetes into remission and regaining health.
