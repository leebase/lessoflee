There are 40.8 lbs less of Lee! Boy it took a LONG time to go from 36 to 40.

I’m under 300lbs. I’ve lost half the weight I did last time.

I play mind games with the scale. My weight is always whatever the lowest weigh in is….until the next lower time. I probably lost and gained the same 10lbs every week the last 2 months. But, what I considered “my weight” to be stayed the same. And it finally started nudging downward again 2 weeks ago.

Onward to 50lbs Less of Lee. 25 more to go to be merely obese instead of morbidly obese.
