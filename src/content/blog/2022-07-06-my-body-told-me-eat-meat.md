A nutritious dinner is served!

Tis true. Beef is filled with vitamins. "Beef contains several essential nutrients including protein, iron, zinc, selenium, riboflavin, niacin, vitamin B6, vitamin B12, phosphorus, pantothenate, magnesium, and potassium."

Every meal isn't going to have every nutrient. You'd want to have eggs and liver and other organs if meat were all you were going to eat. I'm not carrying forward a pure carnivore diet at this point but my eating has been shaped by what I learned and experienced over the last month.

What about the saturated fat? It's delicious and good for you. I showed my lipid results. Good total cholesterol. Good HDL. Good Triglycerides. The best values I've ever had. Slightly elevated LDL but it used to be quite high. (Read The Great Cholesterol Myth).

Oh...and half the fat in beef isn't saturated. It's the same class of fat that's in olive oil.

Your body is made of protein and fat. Nothing in your body is made of fiber. People speak of needing carbs for energy. As if fat is nothing but material to clog your arteries. Fat is energy. It's energy that doesn't raise your insulin...doesn't raise your blood sugar.

Ponder this – all those studies claiming that meat is bad for you, particularly red meat. What else are people eating with the meat? How many people are doing what I do and just ordering the patties? A whole food vegan diet is going to be a lot healthier for you than a whole McDonald's diet. It's not the meat. It's the buns, the fries and the cokes and milk shakes.

Coca Cola, Oreos, Doritos and McDonald's French Fries are all vegan. But nobody gathers all the garbage eaters and claims "see what eating plant food causes"? But they do that with meat.

I didn't want to continue on with the carnivore diet – I said so from the beginning. I like veggies. And I will be eating some, just not as much as I used too. I want to keep myself in the good cholesterol category. It's the first time I ever had good values across the board. My body has spoken and I'm paying attention.
