If you don’t do that which is hard you may find the day comes when that which is necessary has become impossible.

Today the lawn needed to be mowed and my wife (who likes to mow the lawn) is out of town. I didn’t used to be able to mow the lawn as I needed a cane just to walk.

Truth be told, I don’t like mowing the lawn. It needs to be done. It’s a responsibility. I also don’t like running, lifting and pushing myself with exercise. Unlike mowing the lawn, hard exercise is optional.

And yet – it is pushing myself just that bit more and working that bit harder that has taken me from walking with a cane to being ABLE to mow the lawn and much more:

⁃ walk my daughter down the aisle

⁃ carry heavy things in from the car

⁃ walk from the train station to the office

⁃ stand up to give a lecture

⁃ walk up and down stairs

⁃ not fear falling while taking a shower

On and on I’m regaining abilities that I NEED. Abilities I had lost because I had considered taking care of my health optional.

Doing that which is hard is mandatory lest one lose abilities. If you are young, let my life be a warning to prioritize health. If you are in poor health, then I hope you can find inspiration to do something a bit hard and then a bit harder and reclaim your own abilities.
