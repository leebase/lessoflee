Last time my daughter and I visited Chicago, I relied on a cane to support me while walking. Today, I can't even recall the last time I needed the cane or the handicap sticker for my car. It's been about two years, and I'm now able to walk up and down stairs with ease. In fact, I can even complete a 5k run without stopping for a breather.

While at the game, I treated myself to a Cubs hoodie from the official store near the park. Though it was expensive, it represented another personal victory. In the past, I wouldn't have been able to fit into any of the clothes from a regular store. I may still be on the larger side, but I've made significant progress.

Days like today are the reason I've put in all the hard work and maintained persistence. My goal hasn't been to become an expert at exercising. I haven’t lost my taste for my old favorite foods. Rather, my objective has been to reclaim my health, infuse life into each day, and ultimately, extend my lifespan.

Is the journey difficult? Absolutely. Do I experience frustration and discouragement? Without a doubt. Have I ever felt like giving up? Indeed.

However, days like today serve as a poignant reminder of the immense value of my efforts. If you find yourself at an earlier stage in your journey, struggling with discouragement or dreading the necessary changes, envision your future self experiencing your own personal triumph. Believe in yourself – you can do it too.
