No running of intervals in this weather – so to the bat cave I go – and put my VR headset on.

I did the Monster Metallica Hard level workout that I had done for the first time earlier this week. I even improved my score a tiny bit.

But it’s Saturday and I push myself on Saturdays to do more and go farther. So I then did the Sweet Symphony medium level workout. What a switch from Metallica to Tchaikovsky ![🤪](~/assets/posts/2022-12-24-saturday-push-day-christmas-eve-22/1f92a.png)

Why? I’m reclaiming my health. There are no ribbons or trophies. My reward is being able to step into the shower and not fear falling. To be able to bend down and pick the towel up off the floor.

I’m still obese and I’m hardly the picture of a fit man. But I’m making progress. I can’t get to where I want to be – from where I started – without going through where I am. The muscles are coming and with them – health and vitality.

How much more? A little more
How much farther? A bit farther
Always
