What do you call a failed omelette? Scrambled eggs. My taco hamburger omelette didn’t go quite as planned – still tasted good. Because we ate dinner early to go to the concert the night before, the fast was 20 hours.

Dinner was shrimp and salmon again. I could eat this a lot – very tasty.

My bs was 96 in the morning and 91 at dinner. This is truly excellent. I had been running 125 or so lately.

I gained and lost 2lbs – so I’m still at 8lbs lost. Since I’m doing this for a month – compared to my monthly 5 day fast – it will be interesting to see the difference. I know I’m not going to be losing 2lbs a day.

I remain at low levels of ketosis. Better than I normally am but nothing like day 4 of an extended fast.

And yes – I pooped today. Twice even. People ask, so now you know.

I am indeed less hungry and I am eating a lot. Breakfast was a pound of hamburger and 4 eggs. But then nothing for 6 hours. And I won’t eat any more for another 18
