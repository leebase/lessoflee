Did you know that giving blood lowers your A1c? It also happens to save lives but what is that compared to a minuscule benefit to oneself? ![😎](~/assets/posts/2022-07-23-give-blood-8211-save-lives/1f60e.png)![😜](~/assets/posts/2022-07-23-give-blood-8211-save-lives/1f61c.png)

Seriously – removing a pint of blood makes your body create new blood cells. These won’t yet be glycated with sugar and thus your Hemoglobin A1c will be somewhat lower.

You can give twice in 3 months if you time it right and so that’s 7% “new blood” you’ll have. Ok…seriously, the real reason to give blood is that it saves lives. And maybe one day it’s you or someone you love who will need blood.
