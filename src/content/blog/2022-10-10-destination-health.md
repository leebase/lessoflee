It may have taken the specter of daily insulin shots to get me going, but from the beginning my goal was to regain health. Reversing type 2 diabetes was an important milestone for sure. Off very expensive meds (Trulicity) and avoiding needing insulin shots and getting to a normal A1c may be the headline. And yet, there is so much more. I’ve been regaining abilities, recovering life itself.

I didn’t just make it to my daughter's wedding…I needed no accommodations. No one needed to help me get around. No cane. I was there and I was carrying the heavy boxes. Walking up and down stairs. The treacherous transition from grassy hill to rocks and sand was no more difficult for me than anyone else.

I was able to dance with my daughter. I walked her down the aisle. I got my minimum 30 minutes of exercise in each day. At the hotel I chose to walk up and down the stairs to the third floor because I could.

I’m not a strong man nor a healthy one. I am stronger and more healthy than at the start a year and a half ago. A year and a half from now I will be stronger and more healthy than I am today.

There are more weddings to be had. And I can now say – a grandchild is on the way! My oldest daughter will bear our first grandchild next spring.

So much to live for. So much need for me to be healthy. It’s worth all the work to change the diet and to exercise. I keep on, pressing for more.

How much? A little more.

How far? A bit farther.

Always

The destination is health.
