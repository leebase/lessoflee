My first daughter to marry – it’s in the books. A great day. Let’s discuss my decision to eat “off plan”.

It was a decision. One doesn’t have to indulge just because there is a family or holiday event. There are many ways to enjoy yourself and not eat unhealthy foods. Here was my thinking.

Like everyone else, I like eating sugar and carb laden foods. That’s how I became morbidly obese and type 2 diabetic in the first place. However, some people have a much stronger addiction relationship to such foods. I can indulge and the get back with healthy eating. Not everyone can and so be careful. Know yourself lest a family event becomes a months long regression back into poor health.

I have good blood sugar control. I used to wear a cgm (continuous glucose monitor) and know that my response to sugar is at a safe level. My highs don’t go too high and I come back in a reasonable amount of time. But that’s in large extent a factor of not over eating carbs regularly. I know I could redevelop type 2 diabetes. But a weekend? I know my body can handle it. And I do still take Curalin and that too is a buffer against out of control blood glucose.

Blood sugar isn’t everything. You are sick with chronically elevated insulin years before you are so damaged that you can’t control your blood glucose levels. Likewise – you haven’t healed completely just because your bs numbers look good. So an indulgence really needs to be rare so that you can continue healing.

Sugar doesn’t just evaporate just because it isn’t staying in your blood. I’m sure I gained back some fat. I had to weigh out the consequences (pun intended). I decided it was worth having to relose the fat that I’d surely gain.

Did I learn anything? Yes. All my favorite foods still taste great to me. And they are so convenient. And …..

And I would easily go back to over eating. Man can I put away carb heavy food…and then in 30 minutes, be hungry for more. I can’t help knowing what I know about what cr@p this “food” really is. But knowing it doesn’t keep me from reaching for more.

I like the new way of eating I have. The food is ALSO delicious. It’s not nearly as convenient because it doesn’t have Big Food making it so available and enticing.

Meat, fish and non-starchy veggies are nutrient dense. They satiate me more. They make me feel better.

We got home today after a 4 hour drive. I was still in “indulgence weekend” mindset and thought of ordering a pizza. But no, I had plenty of good food in the fridge and freezer. Wasn’t in the mood to cook, so I had some deli sliced roast beef, cottage cheese and cheddar cheese slices. I also made a protein shake with frozen strawberries and a little heavy cream. More convenient than ordering a pizza and waiting for it to be delivered.

Not quite a year and a half into this health journey and the pay off has been tremendous. From an A1c of 11.2 on 3 meds to 5.6 off all meds. Down 85lbs (at my lowest last month). Arthritic knees so much better. No more acid reflux. No more headaches or anxiety attacks. Sleeping better. I’m stronger and more fit.

I’m confident that my decision to go off plan for a weekend will be just that – a weekend. I like the new more healthy me. I like the food I now eat. I still like the food I used to eat but the price such food extracts, it’s just not worth it.
