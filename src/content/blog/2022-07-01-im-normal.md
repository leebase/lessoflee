Oh no! One month of carnivore and my triglycerides and HDL came into the normal range. My total cholesterol is normal and my LDL is just barely elevated.

Y’all saw all those eggs, steak, hamburger and cheese I was eating right? And the sausages?

OMG – I’ve been looking for this improvement the entire year and hadn’t yet seen much improvement in the triglycerides and HDL.

Hold the phone – my A1c just came in. 5.6 – I’m in the normal range!

Not diabetic and not pre-diabetic.

F a duck. I didn’t want to keep on to the carnivore diet. Hmmmmm – got some thinking to do.
