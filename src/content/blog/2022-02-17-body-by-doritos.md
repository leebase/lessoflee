I was hungry at midnight last night. I almost got up and went to raid the fridge. Aren’t I the one who proclaims your body tells you when to eat and how much? My body was demanding food. It didn’t care that I was fasting from dinner to lunch time.

Oh wait – there is a part of the story missing. The kids were home for the weekend and there were left over….Cool Ranch Doritos. My wife brought the bag down as we were watching the Olympics.

TV – at night – Doritos an arm's length away.

I had eaten well all day. Several days in fact. So…I get a simple (large) handful of Doritos. Portion control, don’t you know. Just a moderate amount.

And 15min later….I had the rest of the bag. Maybe 10% of the bag left. Just the crumbs…don’t you know.

My bs was fine the next morning. 107 – which isn’t too bad for me.

And yet – my LORD – the HUNGER. No wonder I got morbidly obese eating that junk. You eat…and having eaten…you are starving for more.

No thank you. Wasn’t worth it. I’ll leave those things in the past.
