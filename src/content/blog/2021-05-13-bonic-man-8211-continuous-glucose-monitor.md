Continuos Glucose Monitoring

I have become a bionic man. To track my sugar, I have installed the [Freestyle Libre](https://www.freestyle.abbott/us-en/home.html?utm_source=Google&utm_medium=SEM&utm_campaign=Brand&utm_content=Freestyle&gclid=EAIaIQobChMIlMyQ7onI8AIVGf_jBx2lVwKoEAAYAiAAEgLtL_D_BwE&gclsrc=aw.ds) Continuous Glucose Monitoring system in my arm.  Now I can use an app on my phone to check my sugar any time I want to.  No more pricking my finger to draw blood.  It was quite easy and painless to put on my arm.

They aren't exactly cheap.  With insurance I still paid $75 for 2.  Each one lasts for 14 days.  At least I didn't need to buy their reader device since they work with the phone.

I have so much more visibility into what I eat and how it spikes my blood sugar.  I get up multiple times a night to relieve myself, and I take readings.  So I have a pretty good around the clock idea of of my blood sugar levels.  I believe [Dexcom](https://www.dexcom.com) (an even more expensive option) automatically sends updates to your phone every 5 minutes.

Watching my blood sugar has made it much more easy to say no to the types of food I shouldn't be eating.  Of course, I still do...but then I see my sugars spike.  It's interesting WHEN the spike occurs.  Sometimes it's right away, sometimes it may be 2 hours later.

3 Week Results

One of the great features of the Freestyle Libre system is that I can set it up to send my readings to my Dr.  I had a meeting with my dietician (something new, I now have a dietician).  We didn't exactly see eye to eye on diet.  The ADA has changed their tune on low carb diets.  They don't endorse them (or any particular diet anymore), but they do say that it can be effective for some patients.

My wonderful dietician has more than 30 years experience, so she's fairly steeped in the "heart healthy whole grains".  But, to my surprise and joy, she looked at the results and said "carry on" (not an exact quote).

In only 3 weeks, with the 2 meds (I stopped 1 after some research), I am blowing out all the metrics.

I've also lost 7lbs.

I will be continuing to experiment with intermittent fasting with both Two Meal a Day and One Meal a Day.  More on that later.
