Binging and purging. I think everyone understands what a bad idea that is.

I want to clarify something about my 5 day fast/cleanse. I'be been planning this for a good while. I talked it over with my doctor who fully approves. He even suggests I might want to do this monthly for awhile.

You might want to read The Diabetes Code by Dr Jason Fung to understand why extended fasting can be very beneficial to type 2 diabetics.

I have made reference to losing the weight gain from the holiday eating. In no way was I binging. I’m not above falling off the wagon – but that didn’t happen this time. If it did, I would forgive myself and move forward just as we all should.

It just happens to be nice to see the pounds I put on, come off. It’s almost all water anyway. When you’ve been eating low carb and intermittent fasting – as I do – you won’t have as much stored glycogen in your liver.

When you have some delicious and carb laden food over the holidays…you will fill up the liver's glycogen store. And with the stored sugar, are two molecules of water for each sugar.

Fasting after – just empties the glycogen and with it, the water. Your actual fat loss will be just over a half pound a day. So, I’ve lost about a pound of fat and 7lbs of water in the first two days. Sue me for knowing this and STILL being happy to see a low number on the scale.

Don’t binge and then fast to fix it. That’s not going to be healthy. Fasting is indeed healthy – and natural – just no longer normal.

I don’t feel bad for eating some treats over the holidays. It was part of the plan. Putting a little “life into living” as my friend Vinnie Tortorich says. You can’t be a monk all the time. I was within range with my glucose 99% of the time the last two weeks. I’m going to be just fine.
