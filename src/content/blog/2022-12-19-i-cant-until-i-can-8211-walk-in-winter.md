The last couple of years I’ve practically been a shut in once winter arrived. I couldn’t walk across my lawn in summer as I was too unsteady. Snow and ice on the sidewalk? Yeah, I have to stay inside due to my arthritic knees.

That was then…before I restarted my health reclamation journey. Cutting the sugars out of my diet for diabetes also greatly lessened the pain in my knees. Strength training to build muscle for my metabolic health, improved my stability and ability to walk. I walked and walked as that was all I could do.

Then I walked further and then faster. I couldn’t run at all, not even slowly. I’d try and I felt like a newborn deer not knowing what these knobby kneed legs were for.

I kept at it. In time I began to get a few jogging steps in at a time. I was elated. Could I do it? Could I regain the ability to run? Yes. Yes I could and yes I have. I’m training to run a 5k. I may be the slowest person in the race, walkers might zoom past me. But I’ll be running.

Winter has come and I’m a year and a half into my journey. It’s cold outside and there is snow on the sidewalk. I’m out walking. Carefully to be sure, but confidently.

I have added back another ability. Took one more off the list of things I can’t do.

I can walk outside in the winter.
