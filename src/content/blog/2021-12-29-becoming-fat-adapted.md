I continue to make progress with my exercise. I’m doing longer workouts on the low level. It allows me to work on my form. In the beginning it feels too easy, but by the end of the workout it’s hard. working up a good sweat which is the point. Had no problem exercising on day two of my fast.

Did you know your body changes when you use it? With exercise it’s obvious. But it’s also true with your diet. You body can burn carbs and fats for energy. If you eat lots of carbs all the time, your body is always burning carbs and the fat burning pathways become less developed.

As you switch to a low carb diet, there is some time it takes for your body to upregulate the fat burning pathways. Stick with it, push past some of the discomfort – and you will become quite good at burning fat. It’s called becoming fat adapted.

This isn’t some new fangled strange process. It’s how your body has evolved. You feed and you rest. When you are feeding, you have more energy than you can use right away so it’s stored away as fat for use later.

Later when you are resting – your body goes to the fat stored away to use. All of this is entirely natural.

What is unnatural is how our food supply and eating habits have changed. We feed and feed and feed and never rest long enough to tap into those fat stores. What we are feeding on has also changed.

Our bodies can heal when we get back to eating and resting along the pattern we’ve have evolved for many thousands of years to handle.

Times of eating natural foods followed by times of not eating at all. If you are over fat – you are going to need more time away from feeding so that your body can finally get around to burning the fat that was stored away for that very purpose.
