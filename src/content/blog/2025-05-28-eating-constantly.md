It seems like forever ago I had become a fasting athlete.  For more than a year, at my doctor’s encouragement, I fasted with only water for 5 days each month.  It was just a year and a half ago I completed a 14 day water only fast.   These days I’ve been eating constantly.  Today I was able to say “no” to the urge to eat after dinner.  Breakfast at noon for a solid 16 hour intermittent fast.

I had biscuits and a frozen pizza when I did eat so it wasn’t a GREAT day.  But I started where I was and made improvement.  It’s not ok that I ate poorly.  I’m not advocating poor eating.

I’m just going to have to rebuild all the disciplines.   I remember distinctly the many times folks would comment on my posts saying “I wish I had your discipline”.  I’d say – have you seen my photos?  I didn’t get morbidly obese – and again – and again because I have some special super power of discipline.

Discipline is like physical fitness…you build it.   You start where you are, you exercise a LITTLE discipline because that’s all that you can muster.  And you will get a bit more disciplined in response.

So I failed today to eat as I should.  But I did work out a bit of discipline, and tomorrow I’ll build upon that.

All it takes is doing more.

How much more?  A little more.

How much farther? A bit farther.

Always.

p.s. Always consult your own health team for your specific needs.  

p.p.s. Educate yourself to fully participate in your health strategy.
