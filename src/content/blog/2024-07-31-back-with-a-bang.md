It’s been about six weeks since I’ve done more than walk. At first I was dealing with a bursitis flare up in a shoulder. That took about four days. Then a new job which is intense (in a good way). I lost that spark in my mind that kept urging me to push my body. It’s hard to describe that feeling of intense focus that I feel at times that can drive me. But it was gone.

So today, feeling or not, I put on the Quest 2 headset after work day was over. I intended to do one of my favorite medium level routines to break myself back in. What did I see? A new hard rock Pros Only routine by coach Dwana! Should I get back on the horse at the most intense and difficult level? Yep, sure did!

What fun – this is an amazingly difficult and inventive routine set to songs from Rob Zombie and Nine Inch Nails. I was shocked and pleased at how I was able to keep up. I hit 98% of the targets which came flying at me with intricate cross body patterns. I will definitely return to this routine time and again.

My heart rate was in zone four the whole time with a peak of 150 beats per minute, 1 shy of zone 5. 4 songs, 17 minutes – nice welcome back experience.

There is definitely a mind game component to my health recovery journey. Each day I didn’t workout I began to lose some confidence. Then I faced a mental block that was hard to overcome. I’ve been doing these Supernatural app, aerobics routines for 3 years. This was my 271st workout according to the app. And STILL I had to overcome that “something” in my mind.

As is so often true with me – I have to act first and let the feeling and motivation follow. I did not FEEL like working out. I just did it anyway.

I haven’t arrived – the journey continues. I have more to do.

How much more? A little more.

How far? A bit farther.

Always.

3 years and counting in my health recovery journey. A lifetime more to go.

p.s.  Always consult your own health team for your specific needs

p.p.s. Educate yourself to fully participate in your health strategy
