I’m not talking about driving a Jeep, or riding a motor cross bike. Nope, I’m celebrating my reclaimed ability to walk across grass.

My osteoarthritic knees had me walking with all the stability of a drunk toddler. About all I could handle was a flat sidewalk.

That was then – this is now. Out for a walk with my daughter and dog when she (the daughter) started chasing a Pokémon across the field.

Then later out for a walk with the wife and “let’s walk up this hill to see the view”.

No problem ladies – these knees can go off roading.

My new job has a beach volleyball team. I love volleyball – but not ready to be jumping in sand – yet. That’s still in the “I can’t” category. Maybe next year ![😎](~/assets/posts/2022-05-11-i-cantuntil-i-can-go-off-roading/1f60e.png)
