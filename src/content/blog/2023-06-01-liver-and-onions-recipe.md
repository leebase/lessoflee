A simple recipe for Liver and Onions using Pork Panko instead of flour:

**Ingredients:**

– 500g beef liver, thinly sliced

– 1 cup pork panko

– Salt and pepper to taste

– 2 tablespoons of beef tallow

– 2 onions, thinly sliced

– 2 tablespoons butter

– 1 cup beef broth

– 1 tablespoon Worcestershire sauce

**Instructions:**

1. Rinse the liver slices under cold water, and pat dry with paper towels.

2. In a shallow dish, combine the pork panko, salt, and pepper. Dip each liver slice into this mixture to coat it well. I put in Italian spices as well.

3. Heat the beef tallow in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add the coated liver slices, and cook until nicely browned on one side. Flip and cook on the other side. Once cooked, remove the liver slices and set them aside.

4. In the same skillet, add the butter and sliced onions. Cook over medium heat until the onions are caramelized, which should take about 10-15 minutes. If the onions start to burn, reduce the heat a bit.

5. Once the onions are caramelized, add the beef broth and Worcestershire sauce to the skillet. Stir well, scraping any bits off the bottom of the skillet.

6. Return the liver slices to the skillet, nestling them into the onion mixture. Simmer for about 5 minutes until the liver is cooked to your preferred level of doneness.

7. Adjust the seasoning if needed

Please remember that cooking times and heat settings can vary between different stoves, so adjust as necessary.
