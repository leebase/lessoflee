Some of you may know who Vinnie Tortorich is, some may not.  He’s a trainer to the stars, but not the kind that gets on Tv himself.  He’s the guy Hollywood comes to in order to get their movie stars in shape for a role.  He went on from there to teach all of us how to be healthy by not eating sugars and grains.  He calls it NSNG.  He’s a best selling author and has a long running podcast.  He’s helped tens of thousands of people improve their health, including me.  I was a guest on his show and we taped the episode last night.  It was my 6th appearance, the first was 15 years ago.  That was my first health recover journey were I lost 80lbs in 10 months and became fit in a year.  Now those who follow me on social media are wondering two things that are actually the same thing.  What happened, and how in the world could LEE of all people dare to talk about never failing or backsliding?

My friend Vinnie, guru that I dearly love and respect and has given me time on his show to tell my story and give me help — has concluded — it must be that Lee is too smart for his own good.  How else can you explain that NSNG works, Lee knows about it, uses it, has gone far and beyond reading books and becoming an expert in metabolic health science – still has failed time and again.  The problem isn’t NSNG. I agree, it’s a fantastic healthy eating plan for life.  Therefore, the difference between Vinnie and his lifetime successful adherents and Lee — is too much education and thinking and reasoning on Lee’s part.  That’s what distinguishes him, and that’s surely where the problem lay.

The reality is – smart people are human too. I fail for the same reason the vast majority of us do. It has nothing to do with being smart – it's that there IS no safety net that even being amazingly intelligent, educated and experienced makes you immune from. Who I am isn't the same as who you are – but if you struggle, it's not because of what you lack.  We don’t get to choose all of the struggles we face.  Some of us have inbuilt demons, natures that we just have to keep working on, falling down against, and getting up the next day to renew the fight.

They don’t call it comfort food without reason.  It is known that stress is a killer of the body and the mind.  We have an entire food and media system designed to promote the consumption of food that makes me sick – and you.  That system was in place before I was born and was how I was raised.  I’m not shocked that I fail time and again.  I’m impressed that I ever over come.  

So – what about that guarantee? What is Lee’s sure-fire way to Never Fail or Backslide again?

I lied. Whoever has that plan has kept it secret from me. I’m of the Chumbawamba tribe (look up "Tubthumping" on Youtube). I get knocked down, but I get up again. And again. And when I do, I kick ass.

The good in me is better and more powerful than my struggles, weakness, and pain. Each time I learn more. Each time I do better. Each time my health improves. At 60, had I simply accepted my fate and given up—I’d be 500lbs, highly diabetic, maybe blind or without a toe or two. That is, if I was still alive at all.  The good in you is more powerful than you know.

If you are wired like Vinnie—if you can find your healthy lane and say there—I highly recommend it. Eat NSNG (No Sugar, No Grains), Keto, Low Carb, Mediterranean, Vegan, Carnivore—whatever makes YOU healthy. Do it always. Never divert. Exercise to perfection and never drift.

But if you do drift, make it a day only.

I have no shame in being me. Well, scratch that, shame is part of my journey too. But I feel the emotion for a short time, and then I let it go. I move forward. I start where I am and I make progress.

If "where I am" is the same place I was 5 years ago and I shouldn't have to be doing this all over again? Then I forgive myself. And I press onward. Always onward.

How much more? A little more. 

How much farther? A bit farther. 

Always.

I don’t give up. I don’t quit. I’m the Terminator attacking my weaknesses, demons, and character flaws.

Man, do I wish these weren't the cards I was dealt—this fight that never ends. But I’d rather fight the good fight than live sick and die.

Because that is the only alternative.
