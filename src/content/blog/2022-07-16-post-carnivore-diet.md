Mostly meat, a few veggies for taste. My “post carnivore” way of eating continues.

Steak and shrimp. Sous vide the steak and seared in a cast iron skillet. Some sautéed mushrooms and shrimp.

A taco bowl – all the goodness of tacos without the shell. Not even “low carb” shells.

Air fried pork loin. Put Parmesan cheese on a plate. Add spices: paprika, garlic powder, sage, thyme, salt and pepper. Mix. Brush the loins with olive oil then dredge them in the mix.

12 min on 380f or until 145f in temp.

Delicious.
