Tried to get a morning walk in before the rain – nope, got rained on.

I was 20min away from home – so I was going to get as wet as could be. No sense fretting. Wet doesn’t kill you.

It made me reflect on fasting. You get hungry, hungry doesn’t kill you. You can also only get “so” hungry. Then it goes away. And it comes back later…and then goes away.

I’ve just finished day 4 of this month's extended fast. It’s been a hungry one. Lots of thoughts of quitting on day 3.

But day 3 I finally got into mild ketosis. Morning of day 4 I hit moderate ketosis and by the end of day 4 I'm in the ”therapeutic level” with a GKI of 1.1. That’s the level you want to help with epilepsy and Alzheimer’s.

This doesn’t happen for me until days 3 and 4. I don’t have a meter to show…but so many other good things are going on. Autophagy is recycling and turning over proteins. HGH is 200x the normal amount. People pay top dollar for HGH shots. Brain cells are regenerating as are immune stem cells.

Other than hunger, I'm feeling fantastic. My knees always feel great by day 4.

0h, and I'm down 14lbs.
