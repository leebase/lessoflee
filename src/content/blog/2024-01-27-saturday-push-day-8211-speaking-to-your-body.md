It’s Saturday, time to tell my body I want more: more heart strength, more endurance, more will power, more abilities reclaimed. I am also telling my body that I want less: less heart disease, less disability, less pain, less limitations, less fat. Exercise is communication to your body. Intense exercise is communication that your body needs to change. That’s the message you send, and your body responds with adaptations.

Supernatural, the VR app I love to workout with, came out with a Pros Only routine set to Metallica music! I saved it for my push day. I am so glad I did. This routine was wicked – hard – intense. The targets were flying from all over. I have a lot of room to improve my score. But as hard as it was, I wanted to tell my body “MORE”.

My fitness has been improving such that these routines don’t push my heart rate as high as they used to. I added a weigh vest, and this morning I upped the weight from 6 to 8lbs. I can go all the way to 20, but I’ll do so a little at a time.

How did I go from the guy on the left to the person on the right? By having constant chats with my body telling it I wanted to do more and go farther.

How much more? A little more.

How much farther? A bit farther.

Always.

Getting off the couch and walking the neighborhood for 15 minutes with my cane was a start. I couldn’t possibly do back then what I accomplished this morning. The short walk itself was the challenge. The constant push for just that bit more and farther…adds up over time.

I did my body good today. Looking forward to hearing how you did your body good. How did you push that bit more and farther?

p.s.  Always consult your own health team for your specific needs

p.p.s. Educate yourself to fully participate in your health strategy
