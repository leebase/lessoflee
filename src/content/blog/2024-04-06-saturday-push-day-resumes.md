Once a week I aim to push my heart through some form of exercise that gets my heart rate into the 95% of max. It’s strength training for my heart. The last few weeks I was in recovery and healing mode so my push days weren’t full on. But I’m back in the grove.

I started with lifting weights. I’m not very strong, yet, but managed to lift 2.5 tons of weight. Sounds like a lot but it’s just 100lb squats, 40lb overhead press and 100lb dead lifts.

Then straight into a Pros Only Supernatural cardio workout. When I do both types, I do the lifting first. My heart rate was mostly in zone 4, 80-90%, but I did peak into zone five for just a tiny bit. As I get more fit, it’s harder to get my heart rate into zone 5 via SN. But it’s been raining so I didn’t got out to run on muddy trails. And frankly, I just love these workouts.

2 years ago this routine was way too hard. I had to try it because it had one of my favorite songs, Pull Me Under by Dream Theater. I’d never done a Pros Only before. I wanted to quit after every song but persisted. And now I do a workout before this routine and then crush it the whole way through.

My reward? When I shower after I don’t struggle stepping into the tub. Or worry about out falling because my balance is so bad that soap on the bottom of the tub is dangerous. And I no longer need a cane to walk.

This is body building that has nothing to do with looks or competitions. It’s ceasing to be disabled and regaining vitality.

When I started, three years ago , I couldn’t imagine getting to this place. I was able to come this far by doing more and going farther. And I’m going to keep on pushing.

How much more? A little more.

How much farther? A bit farther.

Always.

I did my body good today. Love to hear how you are doing your body good.
