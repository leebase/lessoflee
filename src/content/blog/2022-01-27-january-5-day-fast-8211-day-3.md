It’s morning day 3 of January’s 5 day fast. Feeling good. Went to bed early and got up early. Exercising and playing with no noticeable difference.

Ketones up nicely (23, these are breath ketones, not blood) – sure sign that fat burning is going on.

Hunger comes and goes just like a normal day. I miss eating as I like to eat, but I’m not suffering.

Why? Why an extended fast? There are obvious fat loss benefits…but that’s only one reason and that can be achieved in other ways. There are health benefits that come from an extended fast that DON'T come another way.
