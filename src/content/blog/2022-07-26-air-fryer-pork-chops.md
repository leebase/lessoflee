Air fryer pork chop with roasted veggies, and a sliced avocado.

Dry and oil up the pork chops.

Put Parmesan cheese, salt, pepper, onion powder, garlic powder and paprika on a plate and mix.

Dredge the oiled chops in the mix and coat all over.

Air fry on 385 for 15 minutes, flipping halfway through.

Make sure internal meat temo reaches 185f

Coat a sheet pan with butter. Slice up a zucchini and squash, bell pepper and mushrooms (or any mix of veggies you like). Spread over the pan.

Pour over melted butter and olive oil. Salt, pepper and rose marry.

Put in 400f oven for 20minutes. Cover with cheese and put back in oven for 5 more minutes until nicely melted.

Enjoy.
