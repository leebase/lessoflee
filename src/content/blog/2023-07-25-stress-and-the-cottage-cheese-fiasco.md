I came home last night in need of rest and de-stressing. I open the fridge and a tub of cottage cheese falls out and makes a mess.

“Why me, God, oh why meeeee” I screamed. It’s one thing after another. And I started to spiral before my better mind kicked in.

“Don’t make it worse”.

Am I helping myself by constructing visions of a world against me as I tie one unrelated frustration to another? No. It’s just cottage cheese on the floor. I’m stressed for other reasons. God isn’t against me nor is “the universe”.

I didn’t want to be cleaning up a mess on the floor. I wanted to be sitting down and zoning out to the tv. I “needed” that.

I didn’t want to be dealing with ants from the day before. Or . And now I didn’t want to be cleaning up a mess.

I’ve been working on stress management for many years. Nothing can fix “whatever the life stress is”, at least, not immediately in the moment. But I can at least do this: Don’t make it worse. In this case I was tempted to “stack tragedies”. As if the ants and the cottage cheese and  were related.

I’m a man going through  which is hard enough. I don’t need to amplify my pain by constructing a mythology around all these little annoyances.

I’m tired, I want to rest but I’ve just made a mess. Messes happen. Just clean it up.

Simplify focus and do the thing that most needs to be done in the moment. You can’t fix everything all at once. You can most always do one thing, even if you are tired. The mess will be worse if I wait till later. Other things can wait. I can’t do anything more than one thing at a time anyway.

When “work on stress” comes up we all tend to put our focus on what is CAUSING the stress instead of OUR REACTION. So much of the time we are not in control of the stressor. So we think “how can I be less stressed when ”?

There are many ways one learns to handle stressful situations. This post is about just one way: don’t make it worse by turning a simple mess into a world out to get you. Step one – don’t make it worse. Step 2 – don’t stack unrelated annoyances together. Step 3 – do the most important one thing that CAN be done in the moment.

Then later you call your mom, dad, sister, or best friend and bitch about it.

Ps: God is always willing and available for those bitch sessions.
