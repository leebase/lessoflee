I saw the podiatrist about my foot pain – a flare up so bad I had trouble walking.  X-Ray ruled out fracture of the bone and gout.  So the suspects are bursitis or plantar fasciitis.  I was given steroids for a week plus NSAID anti-inflammatories.  Those worked to reduce the pain, and with shoes on I am walking just fine.  Without shoes, the pain returns.

The prognosis is a month or more of taking it easy on the feet, adding orthotic inserts to my shoes, and not walking around the house barefoot.

At this point I’m sure people are wondering about the title.  It’s because I’m ALSO going to do a 5 day water only fast to see if that helps.  It will definitely help me lose some of my excess body fat so it’s a good idea even if it doesn’t help my foot. 

I used to do 5 day fasts once a month, at the encouragement of my doctor.  I even did one 14 day water only fast.  I’m a knowledgeable and experienced faster.  This is not something I saw on Tiktok and decided to do.

By day four of a fast my arthritic knees feel AWESOME.  I call them “magic knees”.   Even though I no longer need a cane, I still  have arthritis and don’t have the movement freedom I used to.  But on day four of a fast I get my normal knees back. They feel and work great.  It was experiencing this effect that led me to researching to see if this was a known effect of fasting.

Yes, fasting is proven to reduce inflammation.  There are a host of other great reasons multi-day fasts are healing and health building.  I’ve written extensively on this topic. 

Since I clearly have an inflammation issue of some kind in my foot – and fasting helps with inflammation – it only makes sense to give this a try.

Fasting also kicks off autophagy—your body’s self-cleanup mode, breaking down cellular junk and misfolded proteins. It’s hard to say, ‘My foot healed because of autophagy,’ but the science says healing accelerates when we stop feeding and start repairing.

I have a good 130lbs of excess weight to lose.  So, if nothing else, I’ll drop a couple pounds of unhealthy excess fat.  Fingers crossed on the reduction of the foot pain. 

Fasting is also a great tool for resetting your relationship with food and discipline. I’ve been trying and failing miserably to get back to fasting.  I can FEEL the brain tingle of motivation that the opportunity to relieve my foot pain is giving me to fast.

If you are diabetic, have chronic conditions like arthritis – fasting should be in your toolkit.  Refer to Dr Jason Fung’s The Diabetes Code or look up Dr Jamnadas on youtube.
