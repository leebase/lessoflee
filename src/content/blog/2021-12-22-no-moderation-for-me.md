“Everything in Moderation” – it just has the flavor of wisdom about it. It’s repeated all the time. It must be true. But no, I reject it. I do not believe in moderation.

And people realize this about me when I show what I eat. Too much! And we don’t like what’s on his plate either…but everything in moderation, I guess.

No. I am not about eating the wrong-for-health food which is magically ok “in moderation”. I’m also not about eating the right foods in “moderate sizes”. Why?

Two main reasons. One: I only have so much will power. Eating small amounts of any food takes will power. I have will power, I use it every day. But I do not have endless will power for all day every day.

Two: there is a better way. Eat nutrient dense proteins and fat. That’s meat, fish and non-starchy vegetables. When you aren’t eating highly processed food-like substances your bodies satiety and hunger hormones start working again.

Your body will tell you “hey bud, you're full”. Being full feels awesome. By using will power to shop for and cook real food…I don’t have to have will power 24&#215;7.

Then – stop eating. Allow the insulin to fall which opens up the fat stores of your body to be used. “But you are eating so much fat and too much calories”. Well, apparently not. I’m down 44lbs. Ergo – I must be in an energy deficit or I couldn’t be losing the weight.

Don’t I have to use will power to fast? Some. Some every day. But not all day every day. Eating proteins fat and fiber keeps one satisfied longer…and knowing a filling meal is coming soon helps you get past those last hour or two of fasting. Will power is needed…some of the time every day. Just not all of the time all of the days.

Moderation is not the key – not for me
