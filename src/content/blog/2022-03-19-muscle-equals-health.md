4 minute video on why we need more muscle. Ted Naiman, through his book The P:E Diet, is the one who connected ”muscle equals health” to me.

I’d always thought muscles were for the jocks and the vain. What a tragic blind spot and limiting belief that was.

Here I am losing ability after ability – become feeble in my 50s – and looking to be ”elderly” in my 60s – and all the while it never occurred to me that “muscle equals health”.
