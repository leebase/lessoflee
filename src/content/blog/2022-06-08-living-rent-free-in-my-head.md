“If you can jog, you must not have had osteoarthritis very bad”.

Really now? So the cane I walked with, the handicap parking sticker, the…the…

Well sh#t – I’ve been letting an envious and crass person live in my head – rent free. I was out for one of my beloved walks – yes of the kind I no longer count as exercise but put in it’s own special place of honor for healthful activities.

Anywho…I was walking and composing in my head all the ways I struggled, all the pain, all the limitations, how my world was becoming smaller and smaller. All the painful and expensive treatments I tried…the surgeries the ortho recommended. The NSAIDs I wanted/needed to take … all the natural remedies I tried so that I would destroy my kidneys with NSAIDs. Yep, I was giving this person rent free accommodations in my head.

Then I got to thinking about the motivation behind the post. “That’s great for you Lee, but I have BAD arthritis”. And I feel for the person’s pain. It doesn’t matter who wins the “I hurt worse than you” contest. What we really want to know is “how do I stop the pain”.

I am no authority. Not even close. I wasn’t even working on my osteoarthritis, I was working on my Type 2 diabetes. There I had perfect credentials…A1c of 11.2, on 3 meds and scheduled to go on insulin. Class III obesity at 336 pounds. Trouble…we had trouble in River City.

As I worked on improving my health, removing sugar from my diet, walking, then aerobics and eventually added strength training. What I do now, I could NOT do then. I couldn’t get through the easiest lowest level VR aerobics workout and couldn’t do the squats and lunges. But I did what I could and then I did more as I was able. More and then more and then more.

It’s been a bit over a year since I started. I have no idea how much more health I’ll be able to reclaim. I know this – I’m going to keep on keeping on and see. And if I lose my “you are a diabetic” card or my “you can’t have been an arthritic” card….I’m as happy to lose them as the handicap parking sticker that I no longer have or need.

Speaking of losing – my favorite shirt has become a dress. Some things are worth losing. It took a LOT of work to get this far and I have a lot more work to do.
