That’s right, I’m running! I’ve been working on my awkward “crouch jogging”. Think of it as an accommodation. A way of allowing an exercise you wouldn’t normally be able to perform but modify it and you can. Like doing push-ups against the wall when you can’t do a single push-up on the floor.

I’ve looked to see if somebody else has done this. I’ve found some videos on ways to run with less impact but nothing with my “crouch”.

Yes, I can do other forms of exercise that are easier on the knees. And I do such exercise already. I just WANT to reclaim the ability to run just as I wanted to reclaim the ability to walk across the grass or easily walk down stairs.

It’s only recently that I’ve been able to get both feet into the air at the same time and then only for a few steps.

Today I did interval training with my crouch-running. Jog until my heart rate elevated. Then walk until my rate got down between 110 and 120. Then jog again.

It feels glorious. I’m currently only limited by the strength of my quads and ham strings. When I began training to run 5ks 10 years ago, the constraint was my aerobic fitness – or lack thereof.

I can see in my mind that I will be able to run a 5k again – even if I’m quite slow and running in this awkward crouch-jog fashion.

I can’t do it – now. But like so many things – I can’t – until I can!
