The morning started with leading songs at our church’s men’s retreat. Fun day. Then I lost in the first round of the VR table tennis tourney. That was a blast. Win or lose – it was fun and great exercise.

But it is Saturday – and on Saturday I run intervals. Once again I did not want to. I was tired. The weather was gorgeous and it’s Saturday. I don’t decide if I want to, I follow through.

A lot can be learned by paying attention to your body as you push it. I’m being generous by calling my very slow run “pushing it”. But for me, it is.

I don’t have it today. I couldn’t make the whole 3.1 miles. I did run intervals for over two and walked the rest of the 4 mile trail. I worked on my form and posture. I ran past my initial tiredness and picked up my pace.

I did my body good. I improved my mindset. Stress your body and it will respond by getting stronger and more fit. It doesn’t happen in a day, but it does happen.

How much? A little more.

How far? A bit farther.

Always
