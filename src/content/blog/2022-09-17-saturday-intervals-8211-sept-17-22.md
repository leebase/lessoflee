It’s Saturday, and on Saturdays I run intervals working towards being able to run a 5k. A year and a half ago I couldn’t walk a 5k nor run at all.

Today's milestones:

⁃ I ran for a mile and a half before walking besting the mile interval I achieved 3 weeks ago.

⁃ I took 6 minutes off my best 5k time at 36m 15s

I also played in a VR table tennis tourney. One match before the run. I lost 4-1 and another after that I lost 3-2. Had a blast playing someone from South Africa and Poland from my living room near Chicago.

I track my activity with an app that records METs – Metabolic Equivalents. 1 is the base and represents the oxygen spent while inactive. It allows you to compare the relative effort between different types of exercise AND different levels of physical fitness. A younger fitter person will always be able to do far more than me. But the MET levels the field a bit because they have to compare their effort from their own baseline level.

For the year to date, my effort puts me in the top 4% of the 30k people using this app. Yes, I’ve been working hard on my fitness.

I got here by doing what I could and then a little more.

How much more? A little more.

How far? A bit farther.

Always.
