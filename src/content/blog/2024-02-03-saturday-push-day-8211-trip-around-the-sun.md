I’ve circled the sun 59 times now. More than 34 trillion miles ![🤪](~/assets/posts/2024-02-03-saturday-push-day-8211-trip-around-the-sun/1f92a.png)

Reflecting over the past year are victories and defeats. I did well pushing my fitness forward and lost the handle on my diet, gaining 40lbs.

In one way that’s a 2 year set back. The year when I should have been continuing to lose fat weight and the year it will take to lose the fat once again.

2 more years until I can get to maintenance mode. Another way of looking at it is that I’m in health building mode. And that is a journey and not a destination. There will be progress and setbacks.

To keep making progress, I aim to push myself a bit more on Saturdays. I did my strength training workout that I skipped yesterday, my birthday. I bought a couple new additions to my equipment. A platform, bar and resistance bands. I’m getting close to maxing out my 50lb dumbbells. I will combine the two to keep on increasing the resistance.

I rested a bit and then did a Pros Only Supernatural workout. Man is that a killer workout. Still can’t quite get my heart rate up into zone 5. Yet my arms and abs were screaming and the sweat pouring off me let me know that I was pushing pretty good.

I’m feeling great and optimistic for the coming years. You can’t stop aging but you can do something about feeling old. Build your health and vitality by doing more and going farther.

How much more? A little more.
How much farther? A bit farther.
Always.

I did my body good today. Looking forward to hearing how you did your body good. How did you push that bit more and farther?
