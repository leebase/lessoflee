Morning after feasting on so much “off plan” foods and my bs reading was 105!

That’s 3 days of pie, potatoes, biscuits, gravy and the like. How can this be? Am I cured?

No. I have certainly healed a good bit. A year and a half ago my bs averaged 260 and that was WITH taking Metformin, Glipizide and Trulicity.

A couple things I do know. Measuring the next morning doesn’t tell you what your bs were after you ate pie. It just doesn’t. I used to wear a cgm and could see exactly what my bs was doing all day long.

Curalin helped, but bs doesn’t evaporate. If you use insulin, meds or supplements to help “control your bs” but you are eating sugar – you are simply moving the sugar out of the blood and into the cells – as fat. Yep, I’m putting on fat weight and now have to work to reburn it off.

You don’t become T2 diabetic in a day. You don’t heal in a day. And you don’t redevelop T2 diabetes in a few holiday meals. But, if I took the wrong lesson from this morning’s reading to suggest that I can now eat this way regularly – I would assuredly become diabetic again.

Healing doesn’t change my fundamentals. I am someone who becomes morbidly obese and diabetic when I eat my old ways. By changing my diet, exercise and fasting I have healed a great deal. I am no longer a diabetic. Just as at one time I was “no longer” obese. Just as at one time I was physically fit.

Health is not a steady state. You don’t arrive. You have to continue living healthy far more often than you indulge.

So yes, I enjoyed time with family and traditional holiday feasting. It was nice. But it is even more nice to be in a healthy body.

Back on plan. Onward I go rebuilding my health.
