Another workout in the bag. Mondays and Thursdays are my strength training, and Saturdays are my push days. Tuesday are walks and a light aerobics workout with my Quest 2 and the Supernatural app.

Well, it was supposed to be a light workout but they dropped a brand new Super Monster, Medium WITH knee strikes that I had to try.

I marvel at how far I’ve come. Knee strikes?! I could barely do the squats and lunges for a 15 minute low level workout and now a 45 minute medium with squats, lunges AND knee strikes is a light workout. My heart rate only got into zone 2 – which is a nice aerobic capacity building level. More in that in future post.

The choreography was fun as well. I’m not quite dancing but there is a bit of “Lee flair” going on…in my own home…where nobody can see me ![🤪](~/assets/posts/2023-01-18-i-cantuntil-i-can-8211-knee-strikes/1f92a.png)![🤣](~/assets/posts/2023-01-18-i-cantuntil-i-can-8211-knee-strikes/1f923.png)

Showed up for myself today building the healthier tomorrow me.
