Ten years ago I had my fist health transformation and lost 80lbs. I started running 5ks. At first I couldn’t walk a half hour. But walked and walked and kept extending the time until I was walking 90 minutes. This took many months.

Then I started training to run a 5k. I couldn’t run one minute at first.

About 2 or three months later I could. Then I’d just wake up one morning and think “I’m going to go for a run” and go run 5k.

I could do that any time I wanted. A couple times a week.

Fasting is exactly like that. I didn’t start with 5 day fasts. I’ve been building up “fasting muscles” for a year.

I can’t…until I can
