*"Two people are better than one, because they can reap more benefit from their labor. For if one has a cyst drained on his back, their companion can help change the bandage. But pity the person who falls down or has a cyst and no one to help them, for doing it alone is near impossible."*

Ok, so I revised the scripture a bit.  The half tennis ball sized cyst has been drained and feels a lot better.   I had it for years with no problem and my doctors has always said it could be ignored for now.  Until it got infected and inflamed and became a puss volcano!

My wife was out of town helping out our very pregnant daughter and our rambunctious toddler grandson.  Trying to change the bandages on my own back was hilarious.  Well, now that she’s back and doing so for me, it’s funny thinking of the gymnastics I went through trying to clean and bandage a spot I couldn’t quite reach.

Fortunately I revived my clothes washing skills, and a pile of t-shirts is mostly not ruined.

Hopefully this is the last post about this condition.  All is currently well.
