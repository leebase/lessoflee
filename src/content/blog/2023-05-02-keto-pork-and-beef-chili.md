My own chili recipe

Ingredients:

• 1 pound ground beef

• 1 pound ground pork

• 1 bell pepper, diced

• 1 jalapeño pepper, diced

• 1 serrano pepper, diced

• 2 packets of Hormel hot chili

• 1 (28-ounce) can crushed tomatoes

• Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions:

1. Heat a large cast iron skillet over medium heat.

2. Add the ground beef and ground pork to the skillet and cook until browned.

3. Add the bell pepper, jalapeño pepper, and serrano pepper to the skillet and cook until softened.

4. Add the Hormel hot chili packets to the skillet and stir to combine.

5. Add the crushed tomatoes to the skillet and bring to a boil.

6. Reduce heat to low and simmer for 30 minutes, stirring occasionally.

7. Season with salt and pepper to taste.

8. Serve hot with your favorite toppings.

Macros (per serving):

• Calories: 350

• Fat: 18 grams

• Saturated fat: 6 grams

• Cholesterol: 100 milligrams

• Sodium: 1,000 milligrams

• Carbohydrates: 10 grams

• Dietary fiber: 4 grams

• Sugar: 4 grams

• Protein: 30 grams

Alternate method using spices instead of a packet:

If you don't have any Hormel hot chili packets, you can use your own blend of spices. Here is a recipe for a basic chili powder blend:

• 1 tablespoon chili powder

• 1 teaspoon ground cumin

• 1 teaspoon garlic powder

• 1 teaspoon onion powder

• 1/2 teaspoon paprika

• 1/4 teaspoon cayenne pepper

To use this blend, simply add it to the chili along with the crushed tomatoes. You may need to adjust the amount of spice to your liking.
