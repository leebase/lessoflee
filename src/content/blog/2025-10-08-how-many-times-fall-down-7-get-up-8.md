Man it’s been tough getting back on track.   Starting again again.

I cleaned out my shirts today.  I’m back to 3x-tall, or even 4x-tall.  New short term goal is to get back to 2x-tall where I have a LOT of clothes that I currently can’t wear.

I actually got down to XL.   Those clothes are somewhere and I can’t imagine wearing them.  But if I ever will, it will be because I started again, again and got down to 2xl – tall.  And by tall I mean “more material for the huge gut”.

My eye doctor, the one who handled my eye stroke a year ago took one look at me and said – what happened?  Life.  No excuses.  I ate in a way that has made me fat and diabetic every time.  “Well, you know what to do”.  FYI, I got a clean bill of eye health – no complications from the eye stroke one year later.  Won’t need to see him again for another year.

Yep, I know.  It’s the DOING that is hard.  RySo back to fasting.  Fasting is the most powerful of all the tools I’ve come across in my journey.  Fasting is hard but it’s a skill and a physical fitness style conditioning.  One need merely “work out” to build fasting muscles.

Fasting is strong medicine.  Everyone doesn’t need to fast though a lot more would benefit greatly were they to try.

I’ve fallen – and need to get back up. Once, twice, three times, every time.
