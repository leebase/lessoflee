It’s day eight of my fast and all is well. As this is my second time, so there isn’t the fear of the unknown. I’ve done this before and can do it again.

I’m fasting primarily to burn through my fat. I have copious amounts of fat and I’m tired of it. It’s not about looks, but the health consequences. I have the power tool of fasting, so I’m using it. I didn’t start with long fasts. Like an athlete, I have worked myself into shape. I’ve built “fasting muscles”.

While fat loss is the primary goal, there are many health benefits to extended fasts. Two of which are autophagy and stem cell creation. Autophagy is the body cleaning up proteins that are misshapen, and deficient in other ways and replacing them with new ones. Stem cells can become any type of cell the body needs. People have put serious diseases like cancer, epilepsy, and Parkinsons into remission from fasting.

It may be a coincidence, but my own eye health has improved in a way that has surprised my eye specialist. Last fall I had a micro-stroke in my right eye. A tiny blood vessel burst, and my eye filled with fluid to the extent I couldn’t read. It was frightening. Was I down to one eye? What would happen if I lost that one too? It was the fear of going blind that kicked off my journey to reclaim my health from Type 2 diabetes. And even though I have my blood sugar under control without meds, I have years of damage I’d already done.

The doctor was concerned about swelling and bleeding in my retina. At our second appointment he was going to start giving me shots in my retina to control the swelling. Many diabetics are all too familiar with having to get these shots. It was my turn. Only, at the next meeting, my swelling had gone down. The doctor was quite surprised about this. I’m not saying he had never seen this, but he certainly wasn’t expecting it.

We’ve been following up every two months and at yesterday’s appointment my eye is doing so well that we won’t check on it again for six months. All the fluid in my eye has resolved and my vision is back. In fact, my middle distance vision has improved.

I write this on my computer in my lap and I don’t have glasses on. I see better in the middle distance without my glasses than with them. I’ve been wearing glasses since I was 5 years old and have never experienced this. I noticed this ability after my first 14 day fast.

Do I know for a fact that the improved eye health and eye sight are related to the fasting? No, of course not. Nor am I claiming it to be a fact. That extended fasts bring healing, though, is a well known and documented fact. All kinds of repair and refurbishing is going on in my body that I’ll never be able to pinpoint and say “this is because of fasting”.

For further information read The Oldest Cure In The World by Steve Hendricks.

I started fasting to reverse my Type 2 Diabetes, which I have done. As a surprise, my osteoarthritis in my knees has greatly improved. I’ve gone from walking with a can and struggling with stairs to running 5ks. And now I’m seeing improvements in my eye health. Fasting has been a huge part of this, along with a low carb/keto diet and exercise. An N=1 experiment or personal anecdote doesn’t prove anything. Always do your own education to make informed decisions about your own health journey.
