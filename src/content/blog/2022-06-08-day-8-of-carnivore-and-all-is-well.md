After an 18hr fast, breakfast was eggs and cheese and sausage and some Vienna sausages.

Dinner was a grilled chicken breast, cheese burger, bacon and some cottage cheese.

I pooped today. No problem there.

The muscle bound guy is a world record holder in rowing. He’s also a medical doctor, surgeon and proponent of a carnivore diet. He hasn’t had vegetables in many years. Many many years.

See the scurvy and rickets he’s riddled with? The man hasn’t pooped in years, obviously. Those muscles are really just his colon extremely backed up.

This diet may not be for everyone, it may not be for me past this month. But we’ve known for more than 100 years that people can and do live on animals with little to no vegetables and are healthy.
