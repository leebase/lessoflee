Got into the old boxes for the clothes I had fatted out of. This time as I grew fat again, I kept all those clothes for the day I would be able to wear them again.

That day was today.

Plus, what a wonderful walk in the cold. My knees felt terrific. I still have osteoarthritis but the pain level has come down quite a bit from removing the sugars from my diet. And being 50lbs lighter has lessened the pressure on my knees by 200lbs. I was walking briskly and it felt glorious. No fear of falling because of the ice as I’m far more stable due to the strength training.
