Last Saturday I fell off a chest while changing the batteries in a smoke detector and sprained my pinky toe in addition to twisting my knees. So, no push day last week.

I kept off my foot best I could for a day, then walked a small amount the next two days and then more. By Thursday I did a moderate Supernatural VR aerobics workout. Friday I resumed my strength training.

3500 lbs in dumbbell squats

1800 lbs in dumbbell bench press

1800 lbs in dumbbell rows

7,100 lbs – 3200 kilos – 3.5 tons ![🙂](~/assets/posts/2024-02-24-saturday-push-day-8211-after-the-fall/1f642.png)

Sounds like a lot but it’s just 100lbs for each squat, and 60lbs bench and row. Nobody brags about a 60lb bench press as an empty barbell weights 45 lbs lol!

My foot is still sore but I was ready to resume my Saturday Push Day. I did my favorite Pro’s Only Supernatural VR workout a mixed metal workout with coach Mark Harari. I first did this workout 2 years ago and it was a huge challenge. I was gassed after the second song and really had to push myself to keep going all the way to the finish.

Saturday Push Day’s are about getting my heart rate up and pushing my limit just that bit further. So today I added a Tabata-style HIIT (High intensity interval training) workout to start. Using a light resistance band, bar and step, I did sets of 10 quick squats to over head press, followed by 20 seconds of rest. 5 sets and I was warmed up. I can’t run at the moment so this was a nice way to do HIIT. I will work with this more in the future.

I then did the Pro’s Only workout and even though I first did the HIIT routine, I was able to perform far better than when I first did this workout two years ago. As I had warmed up with HIIT, I spent most of the workout in Zone 4, with a few peaks into Zone 5.

Push Day Achieved.

Why? I have come to understand that I need to exercise just as I need to eat and sleep. It’s required for building and maintaining a healthy body. In my case, it’s necessary to reclaim abilities that my sedentary life had robbed me of.

You will get very hungry if you don’t eat. You’ll get sleepy to know you must sleep. There is no similar drive to exercise. Life itself was exercise for most of our millions of years of existence as humans. As a work from home IT professional, there is next to no movement at all in my daily life. Combine that with a terrible Standard American Diet and I my health grew poor.

It feels SO GOOD to be active. I sleep better, handle stress more easily. I have less pain and more mobility. I used to walk with a cane and had to hold onto the railing to go up and down stairs. Not because I was diseased – but because I had neglected to take care of my body. Osteoarthritis and type 2 diabetes are just “disease labels” to describe how I had let my health go.

Onward I go to rebuild my health by doing more and going farther.

How much more? A little more.

How much farther? A bit farther.

Always.

I couldn’t possibly have worked out this hard 3 years ago, not even close. But my doing what little I could and just a bit more…I have been rebuilding my body and health back up.

I’d love to hear how you did your body good by going doing just a bit more and farther yourself!

p.s.  Always consult your own health team for your specific needs

p.p.s. Educate yourself to fully participate in your health strategy
