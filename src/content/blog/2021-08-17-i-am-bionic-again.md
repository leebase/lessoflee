I am bionic again.  I'm having a procedure done next month and so I'm timing my use of the Freestyle Libre cgm (continuous glucose monitor) so that I won't be wearing one the day of the procedure.

For several days I've been managing my (lack of) diabetes with finger-prick blood testing.  Like an animal :->  Some observations:

- It is, of course, entirely possible to manage your bs without checking it every hour
- I don’t WANT to only have the once or twice a day bs number
- I like seeing what’s going on all day long
- Measuring once in the morning over states the “problem” of the Dawn Phenomenon.   For me, that’s the high point of the day not “my average bs level”
- I like knowing the average of my bs throughout the day you get with many readings.  You don’t over sell yourself on a low reading or scare yourself with a high reading
- My blood meters all read a good 20-30pts higher than the Libre.  It doesn’t really matter, it’s the trend that matters.  I don’t know which one is “right”.  And the distance between them isn’t always the same.  Sometimes they match.  Sometimes different blood meters (I have 3 kinds) differ from each other.  Sometimes they are very close
- I am motivated to change my behavior in light of “current bs conditions”.  What I eat.  How long I fast.  Having only a single or a few measurements a day does not give me the information I need to change my behavior “in the moment”
- Finger-prick blood measurements daily is a LOT better than waiting for your A1c at the doctors.  Again – it allows you to respond much more quickly
