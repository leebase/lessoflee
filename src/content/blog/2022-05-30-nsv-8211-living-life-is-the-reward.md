Visited my wife’s family over the weekend. They live in a lovely area in Michigan. We went hiking through woods, up and down hills and over sand dunes.

I was able to participate and that was a great reward for the past years efforts in improving my health and fitness.

What began as an effort to bring diabetes under control became a total health and fitness effort. My arthritic knees are so much better. The muscles in my legs are stronger. My walking is this greatly improved. From struggling to walk down a flight of stairs to hiking – in a year.

Time for a happy dance…but I’m not quite up to dancing…yet ![🙂](~/assets/posts/2022-05-30-nsv-8211-living-life-is-the-reward/1f642.png)
