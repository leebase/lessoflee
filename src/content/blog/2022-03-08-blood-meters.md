My insurance switched my blood meter on me – and it turns out – I like it. I’ve been using the Accuchek meter for years. I don’t know if it’s better or worse, but it’s been the one I started with and know.

Now I have the OneTouch Verio Flex. It’s the easiest to get a drop of blood and have the strip soak up the blood that I’ve tried. Only needs a tiny drop – the lancet is a lot less painful. The strips are about $.56 each where the Accucheks are $.45 (on Amazon)

The Verio Flex meter talks via Bluetooth to your phone and has a nice app. But it’s the easy finger prick that has me sold. The values I get from each meter are the “same”.

I’ve also tried Dario – and it’s kinda high tech with a dongle that goes into your phone. Nice compact kit great for traveling with. But the readings are always a lot higher. And day to day use it’s a lot more hassle and the bells and whistles aren’t worth it. The strips are about $.50 each.

Walmart’s Relion brand strips cost about half the price. If I was paying out of pocket instead of insurance – I’d go with the Walmart Relion.

I’ve also tried the Keto-Mojo which does both blood glucose and blood ketones. The strips are individually wrapped and are a hassle to open IMHO. I only use them when doing an extended fast and my breath meter says I’m In the danger zone. The blood meter is more reliable per my doctor. But it’s a dollar per test.

I wear a cgm, the Abbot Freestyle Libre – and it’s wonderful. It’s nice to know your bs all day long without sticking your finger. I am starting to wear it 2 weeks on and 2 weeks off. My diet is dialed in and my bs are stable for the most part.
