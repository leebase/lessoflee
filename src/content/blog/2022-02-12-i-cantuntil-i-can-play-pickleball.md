I have osteoarthritis in my knees. Because of that, there are a lot of things I can’t do. I can’t run. I can’t jog. I can’t sprint. I can’t jump. I can’t play basketball. I can’t play tennis.

Yet.

Until I can.

With incremental but sustained effort, I have been reclaiming my abilities. I cut out the sugars and grains from my diet and my inflammation and the pain in my knees have greatly decreased. My workouts with the Oculus Quest VR system and Supernatural app with it's squatting and lunging have increased the strength in my legs.

With knees that are hurting less, and muscles that are getting stronger…my “I can’t” list is shrinking and my “I can” list is growing.

Today I added pickleball to the list of things I can do. Never played before so I was terrible. I fell over…once. But I had a great time and was markedly improved by the end of the hour than I was at the beginning. Ok, so it was only a half hour…but it was fun. A fun I couldn’t have…until I could.

Sustained incremental improvement. Can’t wait to see what my strength training I’ve embarked on brings to my life.
