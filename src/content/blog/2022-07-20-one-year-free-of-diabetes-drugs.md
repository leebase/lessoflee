Today marks the first anniversary of getting off of all diabetes meds: Metformin, Glipizide and Trulicity. It was the day I was scheduled to start insulin – but I'd turned my diet and lifestyle around and was free of the need for expensive meds and shots.

That's $12,000 in Trulicity and $6,000 in insulin that I (or my insurance) did not have to pay.

In April 2021, my A1c was 11.2 and Trulicity was added to the Metformin and Glipizide I had already been taking for a couple years. I weighed 336lbs.

Today my A1c is 5.6 which is normal – not even pre-diabetic. My weight this morning was 261lbs.  

My total cholesterol is now normal.
My HDL and Triglycerides are now normal.
My LDL is slightly elevated but it used to be high.

No more acid reflux
No more snoring
My skin tags have lessoned
I can walk so much better
My resting heart rate is in the excellent range
I have regained abilities.
My fitness had greatly improved
I am getting stronger, muscles are starting to appear
I'm sleeping a lot better
I haven't had an anxiety attack or anxiety related sleep issue all year

I do take Berberine and Curalin but the time is coming when I won't take them either.

I intend to live this way forever. I am a Former Diabetic. Health has to be maintained, and if I don't keep eating well and moving well and sleeping well and handling stress well — I can lose the health I've worked hard to build.

I have more work to do to continue reclaiming abilities and rebuilding my health. Diabetes is late in the game up metabolic syndrome. It's like a sky scraper. Metabolic syndrome starts at the second floor and gets worse as you go higher and higher. Around floor 57 you become pre diabetic. At floor 65 you become diabetic.  

I'm in the down elevator and I've passed the 65 floor, I've passed the 57th floor.  

I still have high blood pressure and still need my bp meds.
My waist is still in the "risk of heart disease" range as is my BMI.
I'm still obese.

I have work to do. I will do the work and clear up all of metabolic syndrome.  

I am not unique. This is not a miracle. This is how health works. Diabetes need NOT be a "progressively degenerative disease".
