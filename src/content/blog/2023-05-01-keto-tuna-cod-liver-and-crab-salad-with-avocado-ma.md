Recipe:

Ingredients:

• 1 can (5 oz.) tuna, drained

• 1 can (3.75 oz.) cod livers, drained

• 1/2 cup crab meat, cooked and shredded

• 1/2 cup avocado mayo

• 1 tbsp Dijon mustard

• Salt and pepper, to taste

• 1/2 cup celery, finely chopped (alternatively, use bell peppers or jalapeños for desired crunch and heat)

• Optional garnish: fresh parsley, chopped

Instructions:

1. In a large mixing bowl, combine the drained tuna, cod livers, and shredded crab meat. Mix gently to avoid breaking up the seafood too much.

2. In a separate small bowl, whisk together the avocado mayo and Dijon mustard until smooth and well combined. Season with salt and pepper to taste.

3. Pour the avocado mayo mixture over the seafood mixture in the large bowl. Gently fold the ingredients together, making sure the seafood is evenly coated with the dressing.

4. Add the finely chopped celery (or bell peppers or jalapeños) to the salad and mix gently to incorporate. If you want to add some heat, you can choose jalapeños, but make sure to remove the seeds and chop them finely.

5. Taste the salad and adjust the seasoning with additional salt and pepper, if needed.

6. Cover the bowl with plastic wrap and refrigerate the salad for at least 30 minutes to allow the flavors to meld together.

7. Before serving, give the salad a gentle stir and garnish with chopped fresh parsley, if desired.

8. Enjoy your Keto Tuna, Cod Liver, and Crab Salad with Avocado Mayo as a main dish, on a bed of lettuce, or with cucumber slices for a low-carb option.
