In a perfect world where you ate a perfect diet filled with perfect food, you would have all the nutrients, all the vitamins, and all the minerals that your body needs for perfect health.

Of course, none of us live in a perfect world. For the most part, none of us eat a perfect diet. But let’s imagine that one would. No need for supplements – just eat the food that you are supposed to and that’s the best vitamins and minerals that exist. And that’s true. Or would be true in a perfect world.

In the real world even the good food isn’t perfect. Our mass production “food” factories putting out highly processed foods have taken so much of the nutrients out. So much that the food has to be “fortified” to put —some— of those vitamins back in. But you say — you don’t eat processed foods. Good for you.

What is your food eating? You know…the cows…what are they eating? Are your cows fattened up for market with grains? Yep. And the chickens too. The same over feeding of grains that makes us fat and sick makes our food fat and not optimally healthy for us.

But wait — you buy nothing but organic food and grass fed and grass finished beef and free range chickens. What about that grass. Does it have all the minerals and nutrients that it’s supposed to? If the magnesium and other minerals have been depleted from the ground, it’s not in the grass and therefore not in the beef. Same for the organic vegetables.

It’s hard to find a perfect pipeline that delivers all we need to us in a form that’s optimal for our health. And then for us to forgo the foods we shouldn’t be eating and eat enough of all the perfect foods we should in order to have optimal health.

And so, I take vitamins and mineral supplements to supply what SHOULD be in my diet but most likely isn’t in optimal amounts. Notice I said “optimal” amounts and not the “Recommended Daily Amount” the government has determined as the LEAST amount needed to not develop disease. I not only want to be disease free, but I am aiming for optimal health.
