It’s morning of day 5. My last food was 109 hours ago.

My ketone levels let me know I am burning fat at the highest rate. My GKI is in the therapeutic level. I didn’t get to this point until 3 full days had gone by. I keep on fasting because I want to reap the benefits of getting to this rate of weight loss and health improvement.

Yesterday was tough. Yes, my knees were feeling awesome and I walked all over down town Chicago carrying a backpack with 2 computers…no problems. However it was day 4 of a fast and there was all kinds of snacks at the office. Lunch was catered into the office and there was a meeting around lunch so it wasn’t like I could hide in my cubicle. Then there was a work party afterwards at a bar with food and drinks. Normally I would have just ended the fast, 4 days is pretty good. But I gained so much weight over the holidays that I really needed to double down and keep going.

I did have fun socializing. My long dormant pool skills came into play. You can see me in the photo making a behind the back shot. It’s a horrible angle to take a photo of me. I’ll use it as motivation to keep working on that still prodigious gut.

By the time I got back home, I was beat. Sore, achy, tired. It wasn’t just the walking with a heavy back pack, but all the standing. Even sitting in an office desk was challenging for my knees. In my home office I sit in a recliner and work with my laptop in my lap. I have made lots of progress, but I still have arthritis and I felt it big time yesterday.

Now I’m faced with WHEN to end the fast. I’m leaning towards lunch which would be 4 hours before the full 5 days. No big deal. The imaginary Herbie the Ninja Hippo in my head is saying “don’t you do things just a bit more, go a bit farther”. I’m still not sure who will win this but I MIGHT just go another day. I’ve never gone past 5 before.

“Aren’t you hungry”. Yes. At times. It comes and goes. I’m more bored than hungry. I love to eat. I miss eating. I miss my diet sodas (go ahead and shoot me for drinking them). Thing is…I ask myself “do you want to stop the fat burning, autophagy, BDNF, stem cell regeneration….or can you go just a bit longer”. If I wasn’t feeling well besides the hunger, I’d surely end it. Last year I did a fair amount of ending my fasts short. I want to improve.

If I can get through yesterday, maybe I can convince myself to keep going until dinner. Or even until tomorrow’s breakfast. We will see.

But no doubt, I did rise to the challenge yesterday. I showed myself I was a person tougher than the challenge.

How much more? A little more

How much farther? A bit farther

Always
