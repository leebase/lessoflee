Three nights in a row of relatively good sleep. Good sleep is vital to health, and I’m not a good sleeper.

I’ve been working on winding down before bed time. One of the things that keeps me awake is an over active mind. So programming or learning tech right up to bed time will wind me up. Same for social media. Watching tv or reading a book doesn’t have the same effect.

I’ve also started taking low dose melatonin and L-Theanine before bed. But not right before bed…I’m finding it more helpful about an hour and a half before bed. L-Theanine doesn’t make you drowsy, but it helps with stress reduction and anxiety.

Exercise is helping as well. Not right before bed either…but in general.

I’ve also broken myself from the bad habit of checking my social media when I get up 3 or 4 times to pee during the night. Pretty stupid thing to do if you want better sleep.

I’m still not getting 8 hours..,but 6 hours of sleep with 3 hours of deep sleep is a huge improvement. And my heart rate is in the low 60s now. It used to be in the 80s or 90s.

A little progress here, a little progress there. It all goes to the benefit of your overall health.
