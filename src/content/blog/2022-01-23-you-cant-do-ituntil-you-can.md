I just finished a high level workout in Supernatural – and scored platinum. 5 months ago I couldn’t complete a low level routine. The warm up had me tired. Now I’m doing workouts that get my heart rate into the 150s. I haven’t had workouts that intense in years. Years.

So many things you can’t do – until you can. Until you start with what you CAN do and build from there.
