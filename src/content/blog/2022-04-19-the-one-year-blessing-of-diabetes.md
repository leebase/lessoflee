Diabetes is a blessing? Well, it’s been a year since I talked to the diabetes specialist and found my A1c was 11.2. That scared me into action. And the action definitely resulted in an abundance of health blessings.

I’ve gotten off all the meds: Metformin, Glipizide and Trulicity

My A1c went from 11.2 on meds to 5.8 off

My Type 2 diabetes is in remission

I’ve lost 50lbs of weight

I’ve put on muscle

I’ve lost 8.5” from my waist

I’ve gone down 2 sizes in clothes

I’ve gone from sedentary to active

I have so much less pain in my arthritic knees

No more acid reflux

I sleep better

I walk so much better and longer

No more blurry vision first thing in the morning

Far fewer headaches

Fewer skin tags

It was the fear of what I was headed for that spurred me to finally make the health changes I’ve been needing to do all along. Blindness, heart attack, stroke, kidney failure, amputations – they were not “things that happen to other people” anymore.

If I had been able to envision how much better I could feel in a year…it wouldn’t have taken fear to motivate me. I had let my health deteriorate so far from pure neglect. I had become less able – on the way to early “elderlyness”.

I have far more work to do in regaining my health. It can seem daunting. I’ll be a year older in a years time. I can be a year more healthy. I WILL be a year more healthy. The blessings will continue.

How I Did It

The Diabetes Code by Dr Jason Fung was the book that taught me how to reverse type 2 diabetes.

Very low carb Diet (NSNG Diet Plan [https://nsng.vinnietortorich.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/05/IntroToNSNG-3.pdf)](https://nsng.vinnietortorich.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/05/IntroToNSNG-3.pdf)

Basically meat/fish and non-starchy veggies.

Intermittent fasting (read Life in the Fasting Lane by Dr Jason Fung)

Exercise. Walking, then aerobics, then strength training. Read The P:E Deit by Dr Ted Naiman

Getting a cgm (continuous glucose monitor) was a huge help in figuring out what I should and shouldn’t eat or drink. I wear the Abbot Freestyle Libre.

Start somewhere. Make progress. Don’t let perfection be the enemy of progress. Learn and improve as you go. Start with the strength you have and develop more.
