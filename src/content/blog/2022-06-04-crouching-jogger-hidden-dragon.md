I can’t until I can…jog/walk a 5k. Actually, I went 4 miles this morning. I’m so excited that I am actually jogging. It’s this really stupid looking “crouch jog” which is all I can do with the current state of my arthritic knees. Think “elliptical trainer” but on an actual trail and not gym equipment.

I’m living up to my “Walking Isn’t Exercise” ethos. You see, I LOVE my walks. We are at the beginning of summer and I’m back to my morning and evening walks. I just love them. I like to think, I like to listen to books or podcasts or talk on the phone as I walk. Now that the weather is nice enough for walking after a long and dark winter – I have been walking INSTEAD of doing my VR aerobic workouts. And that was the impetus to my “Walking isn’t Exercise” post. I had to remind myself that I needed to do things that get my heart rate up. Just because I like my walks, doesn’t mean they are all I need to help me continue improving in my health.

I’ve come a long way in this past year. One of the benefits is better heart health, better cardiovascular fitness. My walks USED to be more of a challenge. It used to be that my resting heart rate was in the 80s and going for a stroll would get my heart rate up. Now my resting heart rate is in the 50s and my strolls never break 100. Unless I up the intensity. Lacking hills, I’ve upped my intensity via jogging.

Well…pathetic attempts to jog. But hey, my heart rate is up and that’s the point. I do strength training a couple times a week with a trainer. I belong to a health club but almost never go. I like my VR workouts and I like my walks.

In the photos – there is my heart rate after a bit of crouch-jogging. 144! I can’t keep that up for the whole 5k, but I’m going to keep working at it. The photo with the bench is 2 miles from my home. I have this beautiful trail I can walk on through a prairie. It’s been a long time since I got to that spot. It used to be my daily walk and will be again some day. Some day I’ll be able to run to that spot.

The next photo is the measly couple steps up to the house. Usually at the end of my 4 mile walk I can barely walk up those couple steps. Then I can barely sit down in the chair as going from standing to sitting — my arthritic knees aren’t happy. But I’m getting better. I actually jogged some of this 4 miles. I shaved 13 minutes off of the time and wasn’t nearly as sore going up the steps. Didn’t even need to sit down to take off the shoes. I could lift them up and untie them from standing. My sense of balance is so much better.

The final two photos are of my heart rate. This morning’s walk with intermittent crouch-jogging. And a typical “I like to walk” walk. Which isn’t aerobic exercise or strength training. But it’s still good for me.
