Ok folks, here I am.  It's been 8 years since I lost that incredible 80lbs and got fit.  I'd love to report that I kept it all off, but I haven't.  So, as in 9 years before when I started my journey, I am announcing before any success...that I intend to get healthy.  Again.  Last time I started at 315, this time I start at 336.   More importantly, this time I start as a diabetic with out of control A1C of 11.6 and osteoarthritis in my knees.  This time I have to accomplish the task in my mid fifties.

![](~/assets/posts/2021-05-03-i8217m-back-8-years-later-8211-now-diabetic/IMG_6310-S.jpg)

I will definitely revisit what has gone on between the two times.  "What happened!".  It's no big mystery.  I feel great about how I went about losing weight and getting fit last time.   But no matter how you go about losing weight, if you return to your old ways, you will get your old results.   And basically, that's what happened.

Mostly though, I will be blogging my journey as I go forward.   New lessons to learn.   I will be focused more on getting my sugar under control than my weight.   They go hand in hand, but the measure of success will be the complete reversal of my type 2 diabetes.  Success will be living life without any of the diabetes medications I'm taking now.   I think it's safe to say that I'm going to be losing a lot of weight and getting fit again in the process.

I have tried many times to regain that mojo, that motivation, that all encompassing passion that I had which allowed me to succeed last time.  Clearly, without success.  Like last time, I have been faced with a health emergency which has jumped started me.  I can feel it.  I am ready.

As always, this blog is just to share my journey.  I'm not a doctor, don't do anything I do without your own research and consultation with your own doctor.
