Loving the Pros Only Metallica workout – via the Supernatural app on my VR headset.

Over 7,500 targets to hit ad I’m furiously swinging my arms, squatting and lunging to the driving metal music. I can feel the improvement as I set my own personal best on the routine – even though it’s the worse score I get these days, a gold, rather than a triple platinum.

For those not following – there is a scoring system to “gamify” the exercise, adding to the fun.

My body lets me know I was pushing hard. My arms, abs and shoulders are on fire and copious amounts of sweat drip down my face. Loved it.

I looked up my first exercise two and a half years ago. 1,000 targets – but I didn’t complete the workout. The routine was a short, easy level and I still couldn’t complete it. I was still walking with my cane back then.

I went from there to here by doing more.

How much more? A little more.

How much farther? A bit farther.

Always.

Now for a two week fast. I will not be doing high intensity exercise during the fast.

p.s.  Always consult your own health team for your specific needs

p.p.s. Educate yourself to fully participate in your health strategy
