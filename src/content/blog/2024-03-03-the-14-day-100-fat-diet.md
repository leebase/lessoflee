For the next 14 days I will eat nothing but fat – my own fat. I did my first 14 day fast last December after more than a year of monthly 5 day fasts. There are a lot of amazing health benefits to extended fasts. Many have actually cured themselves of what had been thought incurable via fasting. [The Oldest Cure in the World: Adventures in the Art and Science of Fasting](https://www.amazon.com/Oldest-Cure-World-Adventures-Science/dp/B0B8T2HKZS/ref=sr_1_1?crid=2D1KUDTMKB0TS&dib=eyJ2IjoiMSJ9.a_59y2s2khsJgNT7tpXmRB79RsRvekvs9DPjl7q0bubruuHDeavi9Z1Ph3c71TLc0fDOPhkOx2775_Vu0I16fD9QmbQwAV06wuU4cgUcqNWDNYNa8ti-FCaYuRi21rxfwsdD8qwhb7E6z_2LeAkGR2SIaUrBIwuxOZGA5RWSlAifAY8gpygtI17-GlY3XRx_4ciddarpu2D_QiZlHUjJSQ.s2VeD4_sC2R1c70qfXuHh75gKUZNbpv54xOSmI7dLik&dib_tag=se&keywords=the+oldest+cure+in+the+world&qid=1709510732&sprefix=the+oldest+cure+in+th%2Caps%2C192&sr=8-1) by Steve Hendricks is a good read on the topic.
