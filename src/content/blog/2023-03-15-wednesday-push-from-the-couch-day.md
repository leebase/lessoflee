I’ve been writing about my Saturday Push Days…going just a little more and a bit farther.

This week I found myself feeling lazy. Too lazy to go for a walk. I haven’t missed doing some sort of activity for 30 minutes each day for almost 2 years.

This Wednesday I hadn’t gone for a walk, it was now dark and cold outside. I say to myself “I can skip today”. And, of course I could.

So I’m sitting on the couch resolved to miss my first day in a long time…and my wife comes by “did you get a work out in today?”

Dang nab it. I was all set to skip a day. But I didn’t. I put on my VR headset and played table tennis for a half hour.

Is that “working out”? Not really, but neither is a 30 minute walk. They are, however, “being active”. I work from home and I can literally sit in a chair for days, weeks, even months at a time. That’s how I got so weak in the first place. If you don’t use it, you will lose it.

Every victory isn’t about pushing the limits. Some victories are just pushing up from the couch.
