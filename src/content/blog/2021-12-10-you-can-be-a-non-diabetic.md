Is this guy fat? Obese? Morbidly obese?

What if he USED to be fat. Not just fat but obese. Not just obese but morbidly obese?

No? You say the guy in the photo (me, 8 years ago) is not fat. Clearly you have eyes and can see. How ridiculous to say he is fat.

But wait – what if later he is going to become fat again. Morbidly obese again. Does that prove that when this photo was taken that, indeed, he was ACTUALLY fat?

No – of course not.

It is the SAME with Type 2 diabetes. You work hard to improve your health. You clean up your diet. You start exercising. You lose the weight. You start feeling and looking better. You get off all your meds.

You are not diabetic. It is in remission. It is reversed.

Now, just like you can become fat AGAIN, you can become a type 2 diabetic AGAIN.

That is not because you are diabetic the whole time. Just like you aren’t fat the whole time. Being healthy is not a steady state. Health has to be built and it has to be maintained. Not because you have a disease called diabetes. But because that’s how your body is.

The guy in this photo could do 25 pushups with his feet on a chair to make them harder. The guy in this photo could run 3 miles any time he wanted to, and did so a couple times a week.

Can I still? No, I am back to not being able to do a single push-up. Fitness isn’t a permanent state. But you can always be fit if you ALWAYS do what it takes to stay fit.

It’s the same with Type 2 diabetes. Type 2 Diabetes is a progressively degenerative disease ONLY if you don’t make the necessary changes to your diet and your fitness…and keep doing what it takes to maintain your health and fitness.

You can be a non-diabetic. Just like I wasn’t fat in that photo, I am not diabetic now.
