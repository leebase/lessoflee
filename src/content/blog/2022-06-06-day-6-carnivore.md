It’s day six and all is well. I did my strength training and played VR ping pong. My heart rate definitely got up.

Bs was 96 in the morning and 90 at dinner. Not taking Curalin or Berberine as my bs is in the normal range without them.

Breath ketones are in the slight ketosis range. Less than the day before.

Weight is holding at 8lbs loss. Makes me feel good, like hopefully it will be a sustained loss and a true break of my stall.

Breakfast after 17hr fast was 5 eggs, bacon and a cheese stick.

Dinner was ribeye, scallops and melted butter for dipping. A couple slices of cheese and some pork rinds.

The quality of full that I feel is definitely better. My urge to snack has reduced. I didn’t eat at all between breakfast at 11 am and dinner at 5:30pm.

This is a lot easier than than an extended fast. I still plan to do my 5 day fast at the end of the month.

Then I’ll have my blood work done. I already can see that this has been great for my blood sugar and I’m at least in ketosis a bit. One of the big hopes is that my triglycerides will finally come down and my HDL will go up.

What about cholesterol? 80% of the cholesterol in your body is manufactured by your own liver – from carbs. Think about that the next time you are eating rice cakes. But don’t eat rice cakes as a diabetic.
