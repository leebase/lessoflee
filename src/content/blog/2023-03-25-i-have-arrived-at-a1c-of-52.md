Yes, my A1c hit a new low…5.2 – 

I haven’t been on Metformin, Glipizide and Trulicity for a year and a half. At the start my A1c was 11.2 …WHILE…taking those meds.

But wait, looking at the chart, my reading in January was 6.1 – which is pre-diabetic. Hadn’t I arrived at a normal A1c (below 5.7)? Yes I had. But then my daughter's wedding in Oct. and through the holidays and into the first of the new year I lost discipline in my eating. I gained back 30lbs and rose back to pre-diabetic.

You never arrive. Health has to be maintained. Always. That’s how health works. If you lift weights and build muscles….then become sedentary. You will lose those muscles.

You never arrive…not because you “are diabetic” but because that’s how everybody's body works. Nobody stays healthy when they live unhealthy, when they move unhealthy, when they have unhealthy stress and unhealthy sleep.

So, I got back closer to on track. My fitness continues to improve. My eating is far from perfect, but I’m CURRENTLY at a healthy A1c.

I’m not at a healthy weight…yet. It’s currently my greatest struggle. I press on. I’m regaining my health. I haven’t arrived. There is no such thing. It’s a life long journey.

The journey to better health is an ongoing process that requires consistent effort, mindfulness, and self-compassion. As I continue to work towards my ideal weight and maintain my A1c levels, I'm reminded of the importance of staying committed to a healthy lifestyle – not just for myself, but also for the people I love and care for.

I encourage everyone to embark on their own health journeys and remember that it's never too late to make positive changes in your life. Embrace the ups and downs, learn from your experiences, and never forget that the pursuit of good health is a life-long commitment. Each step, no matter how small, brings you closer to a healthier, happier, and more fulfilling life. Keep pushing forward, and let's make the most of this incredible journey together.
