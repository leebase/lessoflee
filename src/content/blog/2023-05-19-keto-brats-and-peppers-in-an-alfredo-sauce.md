This was absolutely delicious. Easy to customize with your own favorite sausage and veggies. Every ingredient is diabetic friendly. All from scratch, no mystery ingredients.

Ingredients:

– 3 jalapeño brats

– 1 tablespoon of olive oil

– 2 bell peppers, any color, sliced

– 1 large onion, sliced

– 1 can of mushrooms, drained

– Alfredo sauce (using the recipe provided below)

Instructions:

1. Start by slicing up the jalapeño brats.

2. In a large pan, heat the olive oil over medium heat. Add the sliced brats to the pan and sear them on both sides until browned. This should take about 5 minutes per side. Once they're browned, remove the brats from the pan and set them aside.

3. In the same pan, add the sliced bell peppers and onions. Sauté them over medium heat until they're softened and slightly caramelized. This should take about 5 minutes. Once they're cooked, remove the vegetables from the pan and set them aside with the brats.

4. Make the Alfredo sauce in a separate saucepan following the recipe provided below.

5. Once the Alfredo sauce is ready, add the sautéed brats, bell peppers, onions, and the drained mushrooms to the sauce. Stir everything together to make sure the sauce is evenly distributed.

6. Let the mixture simmer for about 10 minutes, stirring occasionally. This will allow all the flavors to meld together.

7. Serve hot, garnishing with some extra Parmesan or fresh herbs if desired.

This recipe is quite flexible, so feel free to adjust the ingredients and their amounts to your preference. Enjoy your meal!

Alfredo Sauce

Ingredients:

– 2 cups of heavy cream

– 1 cup of grated Parmesan cheese

– 4 cloves of garlic, minced

– 1/2 cup of unsalted butter

– Salt and black pepper to taste

– Optional: fresh or dried parsley for garnish

Instructions:

1. In a medium saucepan, melt the butter over medium heat.

2. Add the minced garlic to the pan and sauté until fragrant but not browned, about 1 minute.

3. Pour the heavy cream into the saucepan, and bring the mixture to a simmer. Let it simmer for about 2 minutes.

4. Slowly whisk in the grated Parmesan cheese until it's fully melted into the sauce.

5. Season with salt and black pepper to taste. Be careful with the salt, as Parmesan is already quite salty.

6. Reduce the heat to low and allow the sauce to cook and thicken for another 3-5 minutes.

7. If the sauce is too thick, add a bit more cream to thin it out. If it's too thin, add more cheese.

8. Optional: Garnish with fresh or dried parsley before serving.

Remember, this Alfredo sauce can be used on top of a variety of Keto-friendly dishes, such as grilled chicken, sautéed vegetables, or spaghetti squash. Enjoy!
