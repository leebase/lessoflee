I feast and I fast. Two glorious meals a day followed by a time to let the body have rest from digestion, letting insulin levels fall, burning fat and healing insulin resistance.

But the feast…the feast is what makes this a lifestyle easy to live.

Definition: A meal that is well prepared and abundantly enjoyed.

One need not mourn over the food we should not eat the way we used to. You can eat well. You can eat until you are satisfied. You can enjoy the food that is good your body. The food that brings health.

Meat, fish and non-starchy veggies. Endless variety. #NSNG

Breakfast was an omelette with diced ham, cheese and jalapeños and Serrano peppers.

Dinner was baked Orange Roughy with lemon pepper, garlic and Parmesan with a garnish of peppers. Thin steaks pan fried. Sautéed onions and mushrooms. Mixed beans. And fresh smashed avocado.
