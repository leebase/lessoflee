I’ve been playing table tennis in “virtual reality” for a year. I am currently playing in a tournament. The fun is off the chart. Tonight I played the best match ever. I lost, but the play was extraordinary. Every point hard fought. Every game a nail biter. 48 minutes of intense exercise with my heart rate averaging in the 130s.

This was after two walks today totaling 12k steps – 4 miles. I’ve been listening to a fantastic book as I walk. Last night I was on my second walk in order to get my 10k steps in (my goal for the month). I was talking to a friend on the phone and ended up with 13k steps.

There are days when I “work out”. Like Saturdays when I go run intervals or twice a week strength training. Then there are days like today when I’m playing and having fun and it happens to be exercise.

A year and a half ago I weighed 335lbs and was sedentary and had trouble walking with arthritic knees. Today I’m 262 and very active. I still have arthritic knees but I have less pain and I’ve regained so many abilities.

When I was walking around the neighborhood with a cane – it wasn’t play. But it was necessary to start there and do what I could. A year ago I was past using a cane but I could barely play table tennis. My mobility was limited compared to this evening.

Find what you love to do – and then do what you can. Then do a bit more.
