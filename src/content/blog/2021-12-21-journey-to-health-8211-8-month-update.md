This past April I got a shock to the system when I learned my Type 2 diabetes had worsened to an A1c of 11.2 – which is really bad. Diabetes starts at 6.5.

All of a sudden the fear that “what happens to diabetics will happen to me” – chief among my fears – going blind. And I was starting to have blurred vision first thing in the morning.

I was on Glipizide, Metformin and Trulicity. I was scheduled to get an insulin pump. I finally kicked into gear to change my diet and exercise. I read The Diabetes Code by Dr Jason Fung and embarked on a low carb/keto diet with intermittent fasting.

At 8 months: My diabetes is in remission. I am off all the meds, never went on insulin and have maintained a “below diabetes” A1c without the meds…for five months and counting. Currently my A1c is 6.2.

I have lost 44lbs even though I’ve gained 3 lbs of muscles in my arms. I’ve lost inches around my waist, belly, chest and neck. I’m down a size in clothes.

I can walk an hour and a half and exercise vigorously for a half hour.

There have been other health improvements:

My arthritic knees feel better

I am sleeping better

I am breathing better

No more blurry vision in the morning

No more acid reflux – no more taking Pepcids and Tums

My resting heart rated has gone from the 90's to the low 60s

Fewer skin tags

My “morning wood” has returned – hadn’t even realized it had stopped

There is more work to do. More milestones to achieve. Putting type 2 diabetes into remission is a huge step, but there is a lot more work to do to completely heal the insulin resistance. The Journey to Health continues.
