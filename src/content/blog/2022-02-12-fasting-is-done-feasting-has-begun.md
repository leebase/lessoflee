Fasting is done, feasting has begun.

Bacon and eggs, doesn’t get much easier or healthier than this.

Regarding my phrase: feasting and fasting

The basis is eating whole foods – real food that is naturally low in carbs. Meat, fish and non-starchy veggies. Eat them to satiety. Until you are “comfortably stuffed” as Dr Berry says. When you cut out highly processed sugars and grains, your body's hunger and full signals start working again.

I eat two meals a day. Yes, they are sometimes large. But, I put them on salad plates (old weight watchers trick) so it’s likely not as much food as a photo makes it appear.

Still, I’m losing weight. More importantly, I'm losing fat. You can’t eat “too much” and lose fat. As I’m not snacking (or not nearly as much) and I’m eating one less meal a day…I’m actually eating fewer calories. I just am able to do so without having to count them and without having to leave the table still hungry.

The fat burning comes from the fasting. Not eating allows your insulin levels to fall. When your insulin is low, your body can finally get burning your own fat for fuel. These daily extended periods of low insulin that fasting gives…also heals your insulin resistance. A double dose of goodness for the type 2 diabetic (losing fat and increasing insulin sensitivity).

The longer the fast, the more the fat burning. Yes, there is hunger…but it is bearable in light of the two daily feasts where I get to be comfortably stuffed and satiated for hours.

This is far easier than “everything in moderation” with ”portion control” which leaves you hungry all the time.

That is how I’ve become an ex-diabetic and why I continue to feast and fast as I work to heal the rest of my metabolic syndrome and lose the rest of my excess fat.
