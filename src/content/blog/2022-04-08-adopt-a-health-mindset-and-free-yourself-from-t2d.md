T2D need not be forever. Don’t listen to those trying to chain you to the disease mindset.

Adopt a health mindset. Health is not a steady state. You never arrive – no matter what aspect of health you might consider. All health can be lost if you don’t maintain it.

The photo is of me (10 years ago). Anybody who would look at that photo and declare “that man is morbidly obese” … has a diseased mindset.

I wasn’t working on my health and became morbidly obese. I started working on my health and – imagine that – I got healthy.

But – at some point I stopped prioritizing my health and stopped eating healthy and moving healthy and sleeping healthy and handling stress healthy.

And I became morbidly obese again – and type 2 diabetic as well.

But – I wasn’t unhealthy the whole time. We T2D do not have a forever disease. We have a temporary disease state that we can do something about.

I’m off all my meds going on nine months and I am not diabetic. My A1c went from 11.2 on meds to 5.8 off them. None of the terrible things diabetes causes is happening or progressing in me right now. I have a lot more work to do to improve my state of health. But right now – I am a former diabetic.

I have lifetime of maintaining my health. Not because I’m “still a diabetic” – but because EVERYBODY’s health is a temporary state that must be maintained.

To free yourself of this disease – you need to start with freeing yourself of the disease mindset.
