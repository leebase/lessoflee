I have elephant butt – elephant buttitis – a heretofore unknown subset of NoAss-itol that I inherited from my mom.

I'm gazing into the bathroom mirror at my still-prodigious gut. My lord – when will this thing go away?! Mind you, it's lifting. The overhang is noticeably less. I can see parts of me I haven't seen in a long while.

What things? More fat of course. I have fat EVERYWHERE. My fat rolls have fat rolls. I have fat in places I'm not going to describe. I have fat under my arm pits. Man boobies of course.  

And I have fat in places I never see – like the morning when my bathroom mirrors were opened in such a way that I could see a reflection of a reflection that showed my butt. My foldy elephant butt.  

You can't control where your body decides to burn fat from. It's nice when fat comes off your face, jowels and neck. I love fat coming off there.  

But who cares about a fat butt? All that fat was welcome to stay. It's my GUT – take the fat away from my GUT! But no, my butt is deflating like a balloon and folding up like an elephant. I already had a flat @ss. Didn't know I had fat to lose there.
