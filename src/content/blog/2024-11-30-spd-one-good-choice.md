It’s Saturday – and Saturdays are for pushing my limits as part of my Saturday Push Days.   But what about all the days I missed?  What about the weights I haven’t lifted in months?  What about the weight I’ve gained?  What about my plan to turn 60 in Feb as a physically fit man that I shattered over this past year?

I don’t know what goes through your head but these thoughts all have had their moment in mine.  What I focused on is this – I could make a choice today in the now.  I can’t change the past.  I can’t predict the future.  I can make one good choice at a time.

And so I pushed myself today.  It’s below freezing outside but I was warm and sweaty working out with the Supernatural virtual reality app.  I revisited the High level flow routine set to music from Disturbed.  Love this workout with its crazy hard patterns.  It was sufficient for a nice Monday workout earlier this week.  Saturdays are for pushing my limits so I added a group boxing workout to the Red Hot Chili Peppers.  I like this new group workout feature.  Neither person had their chat on, but the format makes me push myself to “win group points” – to not let my team down, not quit early.   Boxing is harder for me and that’s also an advantage when trying to push myself.

One good choice at a time.  You can’t undo the past at all nor fix it in a moment.  You can’t do everything you plan to do in the future all at once.  It will take time and resilience.  One choice, one moment at a time.  That you can do.

And so I did.  I may not start my 60s in quite the shape I planned, but I WILL get fit some day by making good choices one at a time.  Choices to do more.

How much more?  A little more.

How much farther? A bit farther.

Always.

p.s. Always consult your own health team for your specific needs.  

p.p.s. Educate yourself to fully participate in your health strategy.
