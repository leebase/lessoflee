Time to break out the unflattering shirt that shows all my fat (you don’t want to see the no-shirt version).

15 months and 70lbs lost in my latest journey to health. It’s now easy to come up with before/after photos that show progress. Celebrating every imaginable milestone is one of the ways I keep motivated.

However – I also need to take realistic stock of how much more work that needs doing. I’m still obese. I’ve moved from obese class 3 to obese class 1.

I have 50lbs more to lose to get to my goal of 215. And that would still be “over weight”. I’d have to get down to 185 – 65 more lbs to lose – to get to a normal weight.

The fact that I was even FATTER before doesn’t change the health consequences of being as fat as I still am.

And thus the photo to remind me. Lee – you gotta do something about all that fat or you’ll go to an early grave. And you’ll be sick and poorly for years before dying.
