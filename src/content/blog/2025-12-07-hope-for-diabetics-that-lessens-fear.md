I've been reading threads lately with new comers who are overwhelmed, scared, and hopeless.  My heart goes out to all who are there because I've been there too.  Almost all of us who confidently say "all you need to do is xyz and you'll be free of diabetes" did NOT start there.  We too were told this was a progressively degenerative disease.  We too have loved ones and friends who went blind, lost toes or feet, or were on dialysis because of diabetes.  We too have lost loved ones to this disease. Frankly – fear and hopelessness is a rational response to anyone who has seen what Type 2 diabetes does to someone.

The source of hope comes from the reality that Type 2 can indeed be overcome. Whether one calls it remission, reversal, or my favorite – healing.  Type 2 is one of the best deadly diseases to have because there is a way out.  One that, once you put in the work, not only removes you from a disease state but gives health and vitality – better than you've been for many years.

Ah but you know where this is going. I'm going to talk about how you must never again eat any of your favorite foods. Those of us who have overcome have had ALL those same fears. We too thought eating right was complicated. We too had (and still have) our favorite foods that we can't see ourselves living without. We too were so sick, tired and weak that we can't imagine ourselves exercising. We too didn't know how to cook. We too wondered how we could afford healthy food. Every fear, every obstacle – you aren't alone – and all of us who have overcome had those same fears and obstacles.

So – here is the perfect plan that works for everyone in two weeks!  Sorry, couldn't resist. This post isn't about what to do. I've written so many of those. Truthfully, I've helped hundreds of people and I'm not even a doctor – not a nurse – not credentialed in any way. I'm just a computer geek who treated this affliction like any of the hard problems I solve every day as a highly sought after enterprise data consultant. I'm not saying I personally figured the medical part out – I just read the books from the doctors and experts who HAD figured it out and I did the work. 

My role – how I helped others – well...let me blush a little bit and say what one of them called me: Lee,you're my hope dealer. I didn't set out to help others. I set out to help myself and I just share what I learn along the way. And the MOST IMPORTANT lesson I've learned is – there is HOPE, the body CAN HEAL. I am living proof. So many others are too. I'm not the guy who found perfection (don't I wish). I fall down and get back up. I take two steps forward and one step back. I keep going because I'm worth it.

Let me give you a glimpse of the other side of your journey. You WILL feel better. You WILL have less pain. You WILL find foods that you love to eat that bring you health. You will regain abilities you thought lost due to "getting older". You will have to buy new clothes. You will...well, don't let me LIMIT you with just a few of the things I've been able to see. The view from this part of the journey is awesome. It's worth every hard choice, every frustration, every "I messed up, but I'll do better tomorrow". My journey hasn't been easy, and it's not over. If I could give you an hour in my body before I started working and after – you'd forget all the sacrifice and jump at the chance to have a healthier you.

Here are SOME few pieces of information that don't take a lot of time that will get your started. I can't wait to hear future you sharing how you've experience relief of fear and healing of body.
