On the topic of: can I ever eat my favorites again

Someone recently posted about their fantastic progress…and their having gone back to eating popcorn 3x a week. Only to be beset by the hungries.

There is more to sugar that’s bad than just high blood sugar/diabetes. Like that person, I currently have my blood sugar under control and I have shown that I can indeed eat some of my old favorites.

And YET!

I didn’t do all this hard work for the ability to eat chips and salsa (my own favorite). I did this hard work for my health. And Type 2 diabetes isn’t my only health problem.

Metabolic syndrome causes many health problems along the way to creating diabetes. Just because I am not currently diabetic, doesn’t mean my metabolic health is completely restored. I am still morbidly obese, have high blood pressure and heart disease. If you are a type 2, you also have some subset of all that metabolic syndrome causes.

Being morbidly obese is just the most visibly obvious. And the type 2's who are blessed to NOT be overweight, you still have most all of the rest. Because those problems happen first on the way to diabetes.

So I will use my hard won ability to tolerate the occasional indulgence. Occasionally. Special events. There is a saying “nothing tastes as good as skinny feels”. I have to remind myself that no matter how much I enjoy my favorite highly processed carb rich foods – I enjoy being healthy more.

And I have much more of my health yet to reclaim. More work to do.  The journey is to health, not to Doritos
