How to come up with a meal?

Pick a protein

Choose a cooking method

Select spices/seasonings

Choose a veggie or two

That's pretty much how all of my meals are put together. Meat, fish and non-starchy veggies. You can't start with "I love lasagna, can I eat lasagna as a diabetic"? Seriously. Just stop. I mean, yes, there are ways to figure out how to make low or at least lowER carb versions of our favorite foods. But for the most part, just dig in to the foods that don't have many – or even any – carbs to begin with.

We had chicken drumsticks in our freezer (we love ButcherBox for our meats).

Lots of ways to cook, but the air fryer is so nice for these. Some garlic salt, a cajun spice mix and fresh ground pepper for spices. 15min at 400f.

What veggies? Not pictured, but we microwaved some brocolli and cauliflower that was frozen in a bag just for this purpose. A little butter, salt and pepper to go with it.

And a nice salad.

How many carbs? I don't know. I don't have to count them because this meal is all from foods that don't have many carbs. Healthy, delicious, filling.

Swap out the chicken for pork chops, or hamburgers. Swap out the salad with roasted veggies. Or green beans. Or a nice sauteed mushrooms and onions.

It doesn't take a culinary degree to cook like this. Just a little practice. And google to find out the time and temp on the air fryer.
