Nobody likes to diet. Everybody loves a hobby. One of the best things I did on my first health journey was to make a hobby out of getting healthy. I lost 80lbs and got fit in a year. I actually enjoyed the process. But like many kids with hobbies…I unfortunately set it aside when “life happened” and now I'm having to retrace that same journey. I'm enjoying it though, as once again I'm making a hobby out of it.

Diet

Fasting

Mindset

Walking

Aerobic exercise

Strength Training

Cooking

Self education

Goal setting

Celebrating the milestones

I write about all of these as blogging my journey is also part of the process, part of the hobby. I made a lot of progress and learned a lot of lessons the first time. I am benefitting today from yesterday's work. And blazing new trails today for tomorrow's benefit.

If you see it as a hobby, it’s all part of the fun. There is a lot to “total health” to keep you busy and learning and improving. Hopefully…for a healthy lifetime.
