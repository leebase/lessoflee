It’s been a few weeks since I had a proper push day. Push days are my once a week “get my heart rate up” efforts to improve my heart health. Ideally, I get my heart rate up above my 95% of maximum. It typically takes running or sprinting for me to get there, and I can’t always do that.

Last month I did a 14 day water only fast. That’s hard enough as a stressor on the body so I kept to moderate exercise, mostly walking. Then I managed to overdo the walking, going for two and a half hours on one day. That’s a lot for me and my osteoarthritic knees. Too much, causing me a painful hip and knees that took a couple weeks to get past. I wasn’t injured, per se', but I was in enough pain that I took anti-inflammatories and pain creams for a few days. I really try to not take pain remedies and hadn’t in a long time. I typically just put up with pain now that I understand what NSAIDS do to your kidneys. Still, the pain was enough that I gave in and sought temporary relief for a couple days.

I’m back to my norm so it was time to resume my once a week push days. As it’s winter and snow is on the ground, I had a great workout with the Supernatural Virtual Reality app. I simply love these workouts. While I didn’t get to 95% of max, I was in zone 4 most of the workout and pushed into Zone 5 at the end – all while singing out to Free Bird :). I will try adding a weight vest to see if I can’t get my heart rate up higher.

I have kept my resolution to build muscle via strength training. Monday, Wednesday and Friday I do my 5&#215;5 StrongLifts.com workouts. Since I’m at the beginning, I'm using ridiculously light weights. But the weights go up each new lifting day. This allows you to work on your form before getting to the heavy lifting. I’m using dumbbells for now as that’s all I have. By mid February I’ll have to figure out how to keep progressing as my set only goes to 50lbs each.

I also walk every day for a half hour to an hour. My puppers needs to be walked 3 times a day. You need rest between lifting days to allow your body to repair the muscles. That’s when you get stronger, on the resting day. Walking is “active recovery”…recovering without complete rest.

It’s hard, but I can do hard things. You can too ![🙂](~/assets/posts/2024-01-08-saturday-push-day-on-sunday/1f642.png)

I’m going to put on muscle the same way I’ve been improving my health these past two and a half years, by doing more and going further.

How much more? A little more.

How much harder? A bit harder.

Always.

p.s.  Always consult your own health team for your specific needs

p.p.s. Educate yourself to fully participate in your health strategy
