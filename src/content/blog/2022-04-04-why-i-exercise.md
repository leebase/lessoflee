I work from home and “wiggling my fingers” is pretty much all the physical exertion my job requires. As such, I have to intentionally exercise or I’m sedentary.

One might think “to lose weight”. But no, I do not exercise to lose weight. Read Herman Pontzer's book, Burn, sometime for some fascinating science on metabolism and the body's use of energy. Beyond that – there are many studies that put the lie to “calorie in, calorie out”. Study after study proves this doesn’t work long term.

So, is exercise pointless? Of course not. Diet is for weight loss, exercise is for health and looking good nekkid.

#1 – the number one reason I exercise is it’s effects on metabolism. Exercise increases insulin sensitivity … which is how you put your diabetes into remission. Strength training builds muscle…the more muscle you have, the more sugar has somewhere to go…which is how you put your type 2 diabetes into remission.

#2 – reclaiming ability. Walking down stairs. Walking across uneven ground. Walk across an airport. Not needing a handicap parking spot because you can walk steady for long enough to shop. Don’t have to sit down to put pants on or use the latrine. Don’t break out in a sweat bending under your desk to plug the computer in. Walk up stairs without getting winded. Walk faster. Reach down and pick something up off the floor. Take a shower without worrying if you are going to fall stepping into or out of the tub.

#3 Sleep better – which also helps with insulin resistance

#4 Stress relief

#5 Feel better

#6 Have fun. I’m enjoying playing virtual table tennis…having a blast. When I started I couldn’t lunge, couldn’t really move side to side fast enough. Now I can. I’m no longer limited by my legs. I look forward to playing badminton again. I haven’t been able to in a long time. Is tennis in my future? I sure would love that. So many things I used to love to do but had lost the ability because I’d stopped moving.

#6 Look good nekkid- well, one can hope
