I treated myself yesterday. I don’t call them cheat meals as I’m a grown man and can eat what I want. And suffer the consequences too.

I went to one of my favorites – Culver’s. I had a burger melt, cheese curds, fries and — AND — a small milk shake.

Bad, bad, more bad and terrible. It was a meal of indulgence — something I can’t do every day but used to be what I’d eat regularly.

How’d it go? Look at my bs chart. I‘d say it went fantastic. My bs didn’t even hit 150. Last year — on 3 diabetes meds — a McDonald’s meal that didn’t have cheese curds or a milk shake took my bs to 400. It was never lower than 200 even with the bs lowering meds.

How? Why?

I’ve been healing for a year. Cutting the carbs WAY back – intermittent fasting and exercise. I have regained some of my insulin sensitivity. Clearly.

But – there is more. Diabetes is a bs overflow problem. Like having your bathroom sink partially clogged. If you put the water on full blast, you’ll over flow. But if you slow the water to a trickle, the sink can keep emptying without overflowing.

I didn’t eat any carbs that day so my sink started empty. I also took a walk after the meal burning off some of that sugar. And I took berberine and Curalin. Both help – some. The diet, fasting and strength training are the big “clog removers” allowing me to handle the “stream of sugar” at a higher rate than a year ago.

Some want you to believe that you are forever a diabetic. You can never eat your favorite foods. But – I am living proof that healing is possible. That you can get to the point of having your favorite foods once in awhile without raising your bs through the roof.

Do the hard work – it’s so worth it.
