I made it past day three this time. It's hard to describe that I'm not beset with "hunger pains" but in the evening I definitely had the "I wanna eats" that I gave into the last 2 months.

I'm feeling fantastic. You can really tell the difference in the inflammation at this point as fas as my osteoarthritic knees are concerned. Gives me hope for more healing in the future.

Also future looking is my weight – which is currently 255lbs( down 14lbs for the fast). I've hit the 80lbs lost mark which is how much I lost the last time I worked on my health, lost weight and got fit. I was 10 years younger then – and I have 25 lbs more to lose to get to the weight I was then. Why future? A lot of the "weight" you lose on an extended fast is water, your liver glycogen being depleted, and the contents of your digestive system being emptied. You can expect about 3lbs of fat loss in 5 days of fasting – plus whatever exercise you do on top of that. So call it – 5lbs for me. So I view these "fasting lows" as previews of the future. 

My morning bs reading was 82. I love seeing these readings and look forward to those being my daily normal instead of just during extended fasts.

You can't measure (without a lab) but the real victory is being in a low insulin state. Two important results – fat burning and regaining of insulin sensitivity.

The fat burning can be measured via the ketones – which are made as a by product when your body is burning fat for fuel. Then the ketones themselves also are used as fuel. Your brain loves them, which is why mental clarity is also a feature of being on an extended fast. I am in high fat burning mode. My GKI is 2.6 is a therapeutic level.
