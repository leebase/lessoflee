Sitting in urgent care awaiting an xray for my foot.  Didn’t fall or sprain, just a sharp pain, mid outer foot.  

Had to wipe the cobwebs off my cane which was in the garage.   I overcame my arthritis like I overcame type 2 diabetes.

THING IS – health is not a steady state.  I ate myself into sickness, then ate myself into health.  I’ve been in a bad down cycle and have been eating myself back into poor health.

So far my knees have not needed the cane and hopefully my foot issue is temporary whatever it is.

Make no mistake – one CAN overcome type 2 diabetes.  If not everyone – then certainly lots of people, millions.  I am one such.

But if you return to the food that made you sick and diseased in the first place, you will redevelop the disease.  Just like you can regain weight.  I’m the weight regain champion.

Our bodies respond to being treated well.  What you eat, exercise, sleep, stress management, fasting – these aren’t miracle cures.  But they are the hard work of building and maintaining health that our modern world works against.

So the cane haunts me – even in temporary use.  I need to restore my health building practices and disciplines before the cane becomes necessary and my world shrinks again.
