Active recovery is light exercise like taking a spring walk after you’ve pushed yourself. You want to rest so your body can rebuild, but low intensity activity can help.

Now that I’m walking through the field again I went to one of my favorite spots…this bridge. I remember discovering for the first time years ago, I was walking through the field, going “just a bit further”. I didn’t even know there was a bridge because I had never walked the 3km to get there.

I love this time of spring when the trees have budded but you can still see the branches.
