Today I Turn 57

It was a good year. I made huge gains in my health. I am prepped to continue making more progress in the coming year.

I am shocked in how huge a blind spot I have had in this area. I would hope that some younger folks can learn from me to NOT WAIT until you are experiencing ill health to realize the value of working on all aspects of health.

A few lessons

1. Eat healthy. The foundation of health is what you eat. That’s not “being on a diet”. Eating crap highly processed food isn’t supposed to be the norm. Eating real whole foods isn’t a punishment, isn’t a diet. Time to change your perspective on food.

2. Move healthy. Use it or lose it – this isn’t just a phrase, it’s how your body works. You have to build activity into your life because our modern society is sedentary.

3. Make it fun. It’s a mindset. Invest the time to find your way to joy in developing all aspects of healthy living.

4. Muscle is health. Muscle isn’t just for body builders or athletes or the vain. Muscle is the ability to move and live independently and feel great. You are going to have to lift heavy things.

5. Embrace discomfort. You have to stress your body to let it know where to put it’s energies. The easy path leads to infirmity. It will suck a LOT more to be sick and limited – trust me.

Enjoy life – and you will enjoy life far better in a healthy body.
