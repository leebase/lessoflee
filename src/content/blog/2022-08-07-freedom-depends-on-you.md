Freedom from Type 2 Diabetes.  If you want to read a comprehensive analysis of the science on Remission/Reversal of Type 2 diabetes – here you go:  [https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC6520897/](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC6520897/)

Clearly the “Type 2 Diabetes is a Progressively Degenerative Disease” is an idea who’s time has come and gone.  It is more accurately stated:  Type 2 Diabetes is a destructive killer which you can delay a bit – if you only follow the conventional treatment of diabetes: taking meds and insulin.

If you just take the pills and the shots – you have a snowball’s chance of reversing your type 2 diabetes – see that article.  And since pills and shots are all the medical profession has been offering, it’s no wonder doctors have been convinced that the disease will only get worse over time.

There are 3 ways proven to reverse or place type 2 diabetes in remission (synonymous phrases).  Bariatric surgery, severely restricted low calorie diets, or very low carb diets.  Debating between them is a task for another day.

I want to talk about the “recidivism rate”.  All the discussion about diabetes coming back is tied to people no longer following the diets.  And that’s where YOU come in.  It’s YOU that has to change how you eat and KEEP eating the new way.   Even those who have their stomach’s maimed have to continue to eat well or they can regain their weight and their diabetes.  They are just physically unable to over eat at first due to their much smaller stomachs (that can regrow).

You can’t look at me for personal proof.  I’ve only been a Former Diabetic for a year.  But – I have a clue.  You can learn to be hungry all the time…and just gut it out forever....OR…

Or you can put in the effort to learn how to love eating meat/fish and non-starchy veggies.  I post my feasts regularly.  They are feasts in that I LOVE what I’m eating.  Delicious to me foods.  In ample amounts.  Sure, I then fast and there is some hunger in fasting…but it’s not “all hunger all the time”.  I get to eat two feasts a day and be comfortably stuffed.   I eat less overall because animal proteins and fats fill you up and satiate you longer.  

Is this sustainable?  Subscribers to [www.formerdiabetics.club](http://www.formerdiabetics.club) newsletter read about Steve Cooskey last week who has been free of type 2 diabetes 13 years and counting.  You want to be free – put in the effort to learn to cook and to learn to love all the delicious animals an non-starchy veggies.  Heck, you can even do away with the veggies if you want.

This week we hear how our own Karen Gray has overcome all of her symptoms.  I also respond to some of the pushback I got last week from those committed to the paradigm of “once a diabetic, always a diabetic”.
