An ode to my favorite Facebook Group:

[We are Winning Type 2 Diabetes Together](https://www.facebook.com/groups/261568611056436/)

What a name for a facebook group...a mission...and a reality. Curalife (the company that sponsors and administers this group) has been heavily promoting their group in light of hitting the 100k member milestone. And they should be very proud of what they have built here.

Having a supportive community is a huge help in making progress and overcoming diabetes. Most of you know I have a supportive wife and partner and I'm grateful. Did you know that YOU — yes, you reading this and all those who put likes on my posts and send me encouragement — YOU are also my support system.

I take screen shots of some of the comments you write to me and I have a special folder in my photo album where I keep them. I've named it "Impact". It motivates me to know that my story is helping others.

I'm asked quite a bit "how do you have such motivation". I don't have a recipe for it....but I do know that the interractions I have with you is part of how I keep motivated. Thank you.
