If you have a peanut allergy – you can get very sick and even die — if you eat peanuts.

Sad – as peanuts are delicious. But you can live a long and healthy life by just not eating peanuts.

THAT is Type 2 diabetes – only it’s sugar/carbs instead of peanuts. Don’t listen to the fools who would chain your mind forever declaring “once a diabetic, always a diabetic”.

Complete and utter rubbish. Eat within your personal carb tolerance and you’ll be fine for ever. Well, until you die of something else, hopefully extremely old age.

You can keep your feet, keep your eye sight, keep your kidneys, have a healthy heart etc. The devastating health consequences of type 2 diabetes need not happen to you. You can heal and live healthy for the rest of your days free of the consequences of type 2 diabetes.

Welcome to being a Former Diabetic. Join the club [http://www.formerdiabetics.club](http://www.formerdiabetics.club)
