Fasting is an amazing health practice. Intermittent fasting helped me get off all my diabetes meds and put type 2 diabetes into remission. With my doctor’s recommendation I have begun a monthly 5 day fast. This is a series of posts to describe the benefits. I have just finished day 3.

I’m just hitting the highlights of the beneficial changes that happen with extended fasting. The win on day 3 is the big jump in Human Growth Hormone. We are talking 13 to 20 times the normal amount. You could pay for expensive HGH shots…or just fast for 5 days.

What is so good about HGH?

The following is from: [https://superhumanentrepreneur.com/boost-hgh-1300-2000-intermittent-fasting/](https://superhumanentrepreneur.com/boost-hgh-1300-2000-intermittent-fasting/)

Anyone interested in burning fat and reducing the aging process should be very interested in Human Growth Hormone (HGH). Here are some benefits:

• Builds and maintains muscle mass

• Burns adipose tissue (burns fat)

• Maximizes exercise capacity

• Increases bone density

• Enhanced immune system

• Optimizes mood and well-being

• Has power anti-aging abilities

Nice, huh? Day 3 is also when your insulin really drops. Your ketones skyrocket. You are burning fat like crazy and your body is running on the ketones.

Why do we care about the low state of insulin? This is needed for your body to regain its sensitivity to insulin. Why do type 2 diabetics have high blood sugar? Because their cells have stopped listening to the insulin hormone that’s telling them to store away the sugar. Insulin sensitivity returning is your type 2 diabetes going away.

Day 3 was not as hard as day 2. Yes, there was hunger. Yes, I want to eat. But I have in mind the health I’m trying to gain.
