People grapple with understanding how diet and exercise help with type 2 diabetes…particularly when compared to taking drugs.  Here are three analogies that each describe an aspect of dealing with Type 2 Diabetes.

Taking aspirin to reduce a fever does nothing about the infection in your body that’s causing the fever.  That’s what taking diabetes meds is like.  Elevated blood sugar is the symptom, insulin resistance is the root cause.  Insulin resistance leads to chronically elevated insulin levels that destroys pretty much every organ system in the body.  Diabetes is just ONE of the problems that chronically elevated insulin causes.   If you just lower your blood sugar with meds, the underlying insulin resistance continues to get worse.  That is why “type 2 diabetes is a progressively degenerative disease”.  If all you do is manage the symptom of high blood sugar, your disease will get worse.

If you have a peanut allergy, and you eat peanuts…you get sick.  You may even die depending on how severe your allergy to peanuts is.   If you eat peanuts…but not enough to kill you…but it makes you sick over and over again – your health will suffer.  What if you simply refrain from eating peanuts?  There is no difference in the health outcomes of an allergic person who refrains and someone who isn’t allergic.   That is, at a minimum, the experience of being a Type 2 diabetic but not eating sugar beyond your tolerance.  You’ll be healthy and none of the outcomes of uncontrolled blood sugar will occur to you.  Why take meds to mask a symptom while the underlying disease progresses when you can stop eating sugar beyond your personal tolerance and stay healthy?

Is there anything more than can be achieved?  Yes.  You can heal.  Set your leg, put it in a cast, and give it time to heal – and you’ll be able to walk again.  Not day one, but with use and rebuilding of your leg muscle, you can go on to walk like you’d always been able to do. You are healed.  You will never need crutches – unless you break your leg again.  However, nothing is lurking inside you ready to bring that broken leg back.

That is the analogous experience of someone who heals their insulin resistance.  Not just getting their blood sugar under control, but continuing to heal the the root cause that gave rise to Type 2 diabetes.  A person who heals their insulin resistance will find that they can eat some of their favorite sugar rich foods – some of the time – with no ill effect.   You will never be able to eat beyond your personal tolerance day in and day out without redeveloping type 2 diabetes.  This is like someone who jumps off the roof of their house and breaks their leg – again.  

A type 2 diabetic who heals their insulin resistance is going to have the broken-leg-healing experience, not the aspirin-symptom-masking experience of diabetic meds and insulin shots.  Type 2 diabetes need not be “a progressively degenerative disease”.  Do the hard work of removing sugars from your diet and exercising.  First to the level of getting your blood sugar under control.  Then press on to heal your insulin resistance.

Eat healthy, move healthy, be healthy – always.
