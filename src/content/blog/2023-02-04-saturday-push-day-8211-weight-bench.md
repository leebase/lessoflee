Today’s push is tackling lifting weights. I bought a weight bench for my birthday. I’ve been doing strength training for a year at a wonderful studio, [www.theexercisecoach.com](http://www.theexercisecoach.com). I chose it after doing a bit of reading on the topic of weight training. This is not my forte. I didn’t want to get injured by lack of knowledge and inexperience, not to mention the sorry state of my weak muscles.

It’s been a year and my strength has improved. Time to move on from the mentally comfortable studio with trainers into the intimidating world of just lifting weights on my own. I bought a set of adjustable PowerBlocks dumbbells but haven’t done anything with them. I spent the money and then went and spent more money with the exercise coach instead of using them.

Anything new can be intimidating. Throw in the adolescent scars of being the skinny kid in the weight room and you end up 58 years old and still having about as much knowledge of what to do as the 15yr old kid in high school. No Pain. No Gain. Time to put that lesson from all those years ago into practice, including the mental pain.

But first – it’s round one of the VR table tennis tourney. Looking forward to that. What a joy that piece of technology, the Meta Quest 2, has brought to me. Playing table tennis in VR is a LOT like playing in real life. It’s not “video gaming with your thumbs”….it’s swinging my arms, lunging after balls, working up a sweat. Better still, there is always someone to play an you don’t have to gather up the balls from around the floor.

Yesterday I couldn’t sleep so I gave up trying around 4am and decided to put in a hard workout in Supernatural with Herbie, my imaginary Hippo Ninja wanna be. We did the High level monster workout to metal music. I continue to see improvement. My heart rate averaged 133 with peaks into the 150s. Instead of gasping my way into and through the end….I kicked it up a notch and pushed myself harder for the last song.

How much more? A little more

How much farther? A little farther

Always (and in all ways)
