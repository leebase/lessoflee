No, not Jazzercise – I simply felt in control of my strength training today. A book I’m reading encourages you to think of yourself as “practicing weight lifting”. It’s a mindset that has you focused on your form and not on “burning calories” or how heavy you are lifting.

The StrongLifts 5&#215;5 program starts you out with light weight and then progressively add more. I know…I’ve heard that somewhere before. Just about through with week three and feeling confident in the 5 lifts that are part of the program.

I blast my favorite rock playlist. I have my lifts planned out ahead of time. I can change the setting on my adjustable dumbbells without struggling. I have figured out what to do on those lifts where my dumbbells won’t go to the exact weight according to the plan (I add more reps at the old weight). I review the proper forms on YouTube. And then I give a good 30 minutes going through the reps and rests.

It feels like I’m playing jazz. Instead of fingers bringing a piano to life, my lifts are bringing more life to my body. I should have started this far sooner. It’s not nearly as difficult as I feared.

My heart rate gets going now that the weights are getting past “light and easy”. I’m up to 20lbs each dumbbell. I’ve already been exploring what to do when the next progression is past the 50lbs that my set goes to.

I’m doing my body good.

I went out for a walk in the cold and snow. The weather “warmed” to 15f. I’d been cooped up for days with -30f windchills. Yes, I can walk in the snow – a far cry from when I needed a cane to walk on the sidewalk during summer.

You really can build your body back up. You get there by doing more and going farther.

How much more? A little more.

How much farther? A bit farther.

Always.

Let me know how you are doing your own body good.

p.s.  Always consult your own health team for your specific needs

p.p.s. Educate yourself to fully participate in your health strategy
