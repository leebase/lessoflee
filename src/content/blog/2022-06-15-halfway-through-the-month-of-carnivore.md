It’s day 15. Have not developed scurvy or rickets. I have lost 11.6lbs. For the last few days the weight has gone up and down a few pounds. That’s a good thing, it means I’m past the initial water weight loss. I’m almost as far down as I’ve gotten on a 5 day fast. If I ended the month with an 11lb weight loss, I’d have considered that a victory. It’s nice to see regular weight loss again after having been stalled for about 5 months.

My bs was originally fantastic, in the 90s. The last few days it’s crept back up into the 115 range. I’ve resumed taking Curalin and berberine. I’m not 100% sure, but I think the rise correlated with the addition of a bowl of berries and greek yogurt between my two meals. That would suck if that held. That’s less than 10g of carbs. I’ll forgo the berries and yogurt for the rest of the month. The body is not a machine with direct input/output reactions – so you can’t read too much into short term results.

Last night was my first night of cravings. Related to the berries? Can’t say. But I really wanted to bust into my remaining jar of cashews or some chips. I didn’t. I ate after dinner, but held it to parmesan crisps, a meat stick and some cream cheese. For the most part it really hasn’t been a hungry or cravings filled experience. I eat a lot – just in two meals without snacking (for the most part). It’s certainly nowhere near as hard as doing a 5 day fast.

I’m not as prepared for meat only meals as I am for meat and veggie meals. I need to up my game, learn some new things.

About the pooping. I don’t poop as much. I do not poop daily. I only “felt” constipated the one day and only in a minor way. I just don’t have to go as much. When I do, there is no problem.
