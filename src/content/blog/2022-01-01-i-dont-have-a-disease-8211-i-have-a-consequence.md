I don’t have a disease, I have a consequence.

This thought struck me. Since I am able to heal my “disease” by eating and moving healthy – maybe I should stop thinking of it as a disease. I should have always been eating and moving healthy.

I have a consequence from my own actions. That is both harsh – and – liberating. Liberating because I am not at the mercy of a disease, I have power and agency to change and come to health.

It’s the processed modern crap “food like substances” and industrial oils that are the disease. Complaining about not being able to eat them any more is like complaining that you can’t smoke any more. We were never supposed to do either.
