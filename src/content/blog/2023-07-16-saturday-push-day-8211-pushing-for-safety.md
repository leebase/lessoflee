A very humid day and my trails were muddy so my run was on asphalt. I got my heart rate up but could not sustain it on this day. I did a little running and a lot of walking. Sometimes the victory is in getting out the door.

Every now and then I get a “be safe” comment when I talk about exercise. I appreciate folks caring, I do. However, the danger most of us face comes from NOT pushing ourselves.

Even if I wasn’t working to recover my health, I’m fighting what we all are: muscle loss as we age. You have to put in work to keep that natural loss at bay.

Moving your body is step 1. That old adage is true, if you don’t use it, you’ll lose it.

However, building muscle takes a bit more. You have to put a bit more stress on your muscles; this damages them a tiny bit which kicks off processes that rebuild them back a bit stronger.

My aim isn’t to improve my physique. Although, I must say that seeing some muscle definition start to develop is nice. What I’m working for is to reclaim abilities now, and set myself up to be self sufficient into my 80's and beyond. I already know what it’s like to be limited. I don’t want that kind of life anymore.

The safe thing is to be strong and fit. You get there by doing more and going farther.

How much more? A little more.

How much farther? A bit farther.

Always.

Come join me – your better body awaits and we can build our better bodies together.
