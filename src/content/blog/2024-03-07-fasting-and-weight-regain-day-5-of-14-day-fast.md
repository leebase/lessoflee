It’s day 5 without food and I’m still going strong. I feel great. I’m quite busy with work which helps immensely. I’ve also started allowing myself some non-caloric drinks, a diet soda per day .

I’m down 15lbs for 3lbs a day. I normally don’t talk about weight loss during the fast because it’s misleading. If I stopped today my weight loss would not actually be 15lbs. There is a lot of transitory weight that doesn’t matter and comes back (water, glycogen in the liver, contents of your digestive system).

You can expect to lose about a half pound of fat a day, one pound of weight. Any more than that and it’s the transitory weight that comes back quickly.

The fat lost doesn’t just come back. You have to over eat – which is true whether you fasted or not.

Feeling great. I’m in the therapeutic level of ketosis – where cancer patients, epileptics and the like try to get to in order to heal. Worked hard all day and went for an hour walk.
