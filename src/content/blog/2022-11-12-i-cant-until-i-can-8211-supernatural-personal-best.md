It’s Saturday, and on Saturday's I run intervals. Except last Saturday it rained all day and today it was freezing outside. Not to worry, I have a replacement goal to push myself: exercise with the Supernatural app for an hour or more and get my heart rate up into the same levels as when I run intervals.

I set some personal bests last week and today.

Last week I completed the longest workout in the Supernatural catalog – 18 songs and an hour long in a single routine with a single warm up and cool down. It’s a medium level workout and I wanted to quit about half way through. But like with my running, I just kept going. One more song. One more song. And eventually I was able to complete the set and do so with a “double platinum” score. I was dancin' to the beats as I was swinging my arms and squatting and lunging. They’ve added fast feet runs and leg lifts. I threw in the body rolls myself. And no, there is no video evidence of what I am sure looked a lot more pathetic than it felt to me. To me, I was dancin' in some of the worlds most beautiful locations (in virtual reality).

Today I could have run. It was cold, but not THAT cold. Still, I’m enjoying the virtual reality workouts. Today I started with a boxing routine. I’ve really not liked them, they are much harder for me than the “flow” style.

But, harder is the purpose of my Saturday intervals. To get better, you have to push yourself past your comfort zone. I did improve things by recalibrating my height. Wow, what a difference. It only took me a year of exercising to realize the setting was wrong. Boxing is still tough, but so much nicer with the proper height set. The crouching, bobbing and weaving, ducking along with the punches really gets my heart rate up.

I did a medium boxing routine and then a medium flow one. Both set to Imagine Dragons music. So fun. Just under an hour for the two routines combined.

With the help of the proper height calibration and my improved fitness level, I earned my first “triple platinum”. I am not particularly motivated by the gamification elements, but it is nice to see progress.

You can do aerobics routines and dances without a VR headset. I just find it fun. And now that winter is almost upon us, it’s great to have an indoor workout option. That’s the whole reason I bought the Oculus Quest 2 in the first place. I wanted to come into spring without having to restart my fitness efforts. And it worked. I will continue building upon my fitness this winter as well.

How do you turn I can’t into I can?

Doing more and going farther.

How much more? A little more.

How far? A bit farther.

Always
