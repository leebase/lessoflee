Today I did it, I ran a 5k without stopping!

I set this as a goal back when I couldn't run at all.  Not "couldn't run fast" – I couldn't get both feet in the air at the same time. 

I have osteoarthritis in my knees.  I've needed a cane to walk and drove with a handicap sticker.  I've been wheeled across an airport and needed to take a tram to get across a hospital.  Running?  Not even on the radar.

When I cut the sugars out of my diet for my diabetes, I noticed my knees started feeling better.  Working out strengthened my legs and my walking improved.  I could walk down stairs like a regular person.  But I couldn't run.  It's like my body had forgotten how.  It was so frustrating.

But little by little I made improvements.  This year I finally made those first couple "running steps".  I had to crouch down to run so that my knees wouldn't kill me.  Kind of like how one runs on an eliptical machine.  I set my sights on being able to run a 5k.

Today I did it – ran a 5k without stopping.  Slow as can be, but actually running not "fast walking".
