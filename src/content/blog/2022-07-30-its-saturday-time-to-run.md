Saturday morning and I’m up early for no reason. I’m tired and a bit achy – but it’s Saturday morning – and I go for a “run” on Saturday’s. I don’t think about it – I don’t worry about motivation or what I feel like doing. It’s Saturday and that’s the day I go “run” intervals.

It’s so beautiful this time of year. Winter is coming and I’m not going to waste a beautiful Saturday morning “not feeling like going for a run”.

I put “run” in quotes because with my arthritic knees, I can barely qualify as running. But I crouch down a bit so that I’m not landing with the foot and knee in a line which would send a shock from the ground up through my knees. I land with my knees bent, as if I’m on an elliptical machine. It works for me. I get my heart rate up and I’m improving over time.

Within minutes of getting out the door, I’ve walked off the aches and I arrive at the path ready to run. I make it past the half mile point without stopping. I go a bit further past the first bench before stopping. How far? A bit farther. Always.

I make it to the second bench – more than a mile in with only two walking parts. How much faster? A bit faster. I look ahead and pick my spots. I’ll keep running until I get to that bush. Sometimes I make it, sometimes I stop and walk. The walking parts are getting shorter and the running parts are getting further.

I get to “the hill” and I run up past the first bend. I’ve not been able to get this far without stopping. I pushed myself more. How much more? A bit more. Always. I’m pushing back the limits – a little more each time.

Past the two mile mark I hit the third bench after running up the next hill. I made it all the way up the hill and to the bench with my “run”. A little better than last time. And now I’m 2 miles from home – and time to enjoy the walk. I call one of my daughters and have some great daddy/daughter time on the phone.

I arrive home. 80 minutes and 4 miles of walking with some “running” of intervals. I can’t run a 5k – yet – but I’m making progress towards that goal. I weigh myself – 262. 74lbs less of Lee. A new low. More work to do.

And I’m doing the work because – it’s Saturday. Saturday I do my long walk/run as my habit, my protocol. I didn’t feel like doing so when I woke up. I don’t wait to feel motivated. It’s simply Saturday, and on Saturday I go for a long walk/run.
