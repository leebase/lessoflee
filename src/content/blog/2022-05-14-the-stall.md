It’s official, I’m in a stall. Weight loss isn’t even a stall until it’s lasted 3 months.

Fasting 16-18 hrs a day, eating very low carb, and fasting 5 straight days once a month – and I’m stalled. Exercising an hour or more a day and still stalled.

Stalls happen. I know this. I know that EVERY way people lose weight includes stalls. And yet – it’s frustrating. Not gonna lie.

Time to do something different, something more? Fast longer – stop fasting. Eat less. Eat different. Something has to change.

Or does it? I’m wearing a shirt in this photo my daughter bought me when she was in college. It was the largest they had – a 2xL. Not 2xL-Tall, just 2x.

It’s snug but it fits and covers my belly. The same belly who’s measurement at the widest part has also not budged in months. Hard to measure but my “over hang” is lifting up.

I’m also feeling great. My strength is improving across all the exercises I do twice a week. My walking continues to improve. My sleep ![🛌](~/assets/posts/2022-05-14-the-stall/1f6cc.png) is improving. My resting heart rate is in the low 60s with a few in the high 50s – down from the 80s.

I am building muscle. I know it. Not enough to take a picture of, but when I flex my bicep – there’s a little bump. Very little, but it’s there.

Someone who hadn’t seen me since January commented that she could clearly tell my body composition has changed – for the better.

My A1c continues to improve. My total cholesterol is now in the normal range and my LDL greatly improved.

And here comes the kicker. I’m loving my lifestyle. I love what I eat and how I fast. I’m enjoying the exercise. What you love, you can do forever.

I’m not saying that no changes are needed. The body is mysterious and there is always something you can do better. But I’m going to keep on keeping on for awhile. My body is healing. I didn’t break my body in a day and healing isn’t going to happen in a day. Healing is also not limited to how much you weigh or how big around the widest part of your belly is.
