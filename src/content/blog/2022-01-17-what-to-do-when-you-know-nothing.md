I’m aging myself, but I remember a Tv character, Sargent Schultz, from the show Hogan's Hero’s. His signature line was “I know nothing, NOTHING!!” – said with a German accent. That’s how I currently feel about fitness. How can I craft a fitness regimen when I know so little?

Well…let me tell you how I am as it’s pretty much how I do anything for the first time.

I could hire a trainer. But which one? How can I choose when I know so little? Plus, that's just not my style…though if you can do so, I’d recommend it.

I’m reading books. I’m reading web articles. I’m watching YouTube videos. I’m following people on twitter. I’m talking to my friends who are knowledgeable in this subject.

I’m also taking action. One doesn’t have to have perfect understanding before getting started. I started with walking. I got into Virtual Reality to do some aerobics that are fun and I can do in my home. I bought some weights to add strength training. I am reading up on beginner routines. I am reading up on HIIT training and trying to figure out how I might do some of that with my arthritic knees (I can’t currently run, sprint or jump).

If it seems I’m a bit scattered, it’s because I am. Clarity and focus will come as I learn more and eventually settle on a plan of action. But I need more exposure and understanding. That comes in time as I read, watch and talk.

I also sat down and worked out just what my goals are. Start with the ending in mind – I use this technique all the time in my job building database and software solutions.

I’ve written before about how I’ve turned “getting healthy” into a hobby. There was a time I didn’t know anything about tennis…and learned. Didn’t know how to play chess, and learned. Didn’t know photography and learned. Didn’t know how to program and learned. Just because you don’t know now – doesn’t mean you can’t learn.

Every journey starts with a step.
