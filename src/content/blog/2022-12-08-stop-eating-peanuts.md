“Why should I stop eating peanuts?”

Because you have a peanut allergy and eating peanuts is making you sick and could even kill you.

“It’s not my fault, it’s genetics. I was born with a peanut allergy”.

So…then don’t eat peanuts and you’ll be fine.

“But peanuts are my heritage, my culture, the food I was raised on”.

Eating peanuts is making you sick. There is plenty of other food to eat.

“It’s not fair, all the best tasting foods have peanuts”.

Life isn’t fair and there is a lot of healthy food that tastes great. You may have to learn to adjust your pallet. It can be done. Lots of people have done it.

“I’m so sick and tired of being told I can’t eat peanuts”.

You can eat peanuts….if being ACTUALLY sick is ok with you.

“I’d rather just die!”

We all are going to die. Do you want to be sick, in pain, blind, missing a foot for many years before you die? Is eating peanuts worth that much to you?

“I can take a pill or a shot and keep on eating peanuts, My Dr said so”.

Your doctor would love for you to sop eating peanuts and be healthy as he/she knows the pills and shots have their own problems and at best, delay the sickness. Your doctors knows you could regain your health. If only you’d stop eating peanuts.

“My dietician says peanuts are healthy. Everybody knows peanuts are healthy”.

Peanuts are healthy for those who don’t have a peanut allergy. You have a peanut allergy. For you, peanuts are not healthy.

“Changing my diet is too hard”.

Being sick is harder.

Choose your hard.

Type 2 diabetics are allergic to sugar. Not EXACTLY the same as a peanut allergy, but very close to it. Particularly in this – if you just stop eating sugar (beyond your personal tolerance), you can be healthy.
