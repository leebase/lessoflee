Hard-boiled eggs, re-imagined.

I meal-prepped some hard-boiled eggs — great for a quick snack or a fast egg salad. But what if I wanted warm eggs? That’s where this experiment comes in.

The Experiment (That Went Right):

1. Peel and mash the hard-boiled eggs.

2. Add salt, pepper, diced jalapeños, and a pat of butter.

3. Mix and microwave for 30 seconds.

4. Top with shredded cheese and serve with a side of guacamole.

Quick. Easy. Satisfying.

What started as a random idea turned into a simple, tasty dish that I’ll definitely be making again!
