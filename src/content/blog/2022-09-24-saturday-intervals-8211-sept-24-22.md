It’s Saturday, and on Saturdays I run intervals working towards being able to run a 5k. A year and a half ago I couldn’t walk a 5k nor run at all.

Today's milestones:

⁃ I ran for 2 miles before walking besting the mile and a half last week

⁃ Set a new personal best at 33m 49s about 9min better than at the start

I’m learning to not go by how I feel at the beginning. It takes time for the muscles to warm up. I hit a nice comfortable pace and just kept it there.

I can see the goal of running the entire 5k without stopping coming to fruition.

I got here by doing what I could and then a little more.

How much more? A little more.

How far? A bit farther.

Always.
