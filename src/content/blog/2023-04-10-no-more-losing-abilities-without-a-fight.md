I’m working so hard to gain back abilities I’d lost. I was aging well before my time. My world was getting smaller and I just kept accepting it.

A new loss has hit me. I’ve lost my tenor singing register. I’m not a great singer by any stretch. I have enough passion and personality to pull off song leading at church. I sing all the time because I love to sing. More than a month ago, in an instant I simply couldn’t hit the notes. I’m stunned and saddened.

Sitting in church this Easter, I continued to be frustrated. I can’t “not sing” when the church is singing praises to God. And then a lyric made me think of all those times I sang my daughters to sleep, all the singing in the family mini van. Would I not be able to sing to my grandson who’s arriving in a couple weeks?

My range used to go from baritone to tenor. All I know are the tenor and lead parts. I can’t read music and just sing. I don’t know what I’m going to do. I know this….I’m learning from my journey that I’m no longer just letting abilities go away.

Maybe it’s an infection or something else that can heal. Maybe I have to learn the bass parts. Maybe I have to learn how to make the most of where my voice is now. I sing by feel and nothing currently feels right.

I don’t have a strategy just yet. I have a determination to no longer just accept the loss of abilities without a fight. I was able to make the changes to get off diabetes meds and put type 2 diabetes into remission. I’ve gone from needing a cane to walk to running a 5k. I have so much more to regain. This is just a new front in my battle. I am not just going to accept the loss of the ability to sing without a fight.
