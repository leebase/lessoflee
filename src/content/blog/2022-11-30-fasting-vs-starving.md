I'm 65 hours into my 5 day fast. I do this once a month advised by my doctor – and with a lot of personal education on the topic. I recommend The Complete Guide to Fasting by Dr Jason Fung.

I started intermittent fasting and then extended fasting to heal from type 2 diabetes. Which I have accomplished. I was on 3 meds with an A1c of 11.2, and have been off all meds for a year and my A1c is now 5.5 – fasting has been a huge part in my health turn around.

But don't you starve? Aren't your muscles being used up? Isn't that malnutrition? All excellent questions. All answered in Dr Fung's book. All things I considered before embarking on this discipline.

But let's tackle starvation. Can a morbidly obese man starve? Sure, with enough time. But long before starving, such a man will cease to be obese. The difference between starvation and fasting is — body fat.

What do you think the fat is there for? Even without being obese, you use fat daily. You don't eat "just in time". You eat, and your body uses a bit of that as fuel for now, and saves the rest for later. As fat. Every day later comes and your body has no fuel because you haven't eaten in awhile, and it goes and burns some fat. (and yes, some sugar is stored in the liver, but that's a side issue to the main point).

You aren't starving every day because...every day you put some away for later, and then got get it as needed. This is normal.

If you over eat and store more than you need, the fat begins to accumulate. The purpose for the fat doesn't change. It's there for when you need it. Except – the famine never comes. "Food Winter" never comes. The fat is there to fuel you for when there is no food, but there is always food. In fact, there is always EXCESS food (for those of us getting fatter and fatter). I haven't starved for 65 hours, I have been eating my stored fat. Just as you do every day. Just as I do every day. Every day you eat and some of it is put back as fat, and then used when you haven't eaten. Have you ever noticed that you "lose weight" every night when you sleep? It's because you kept on eating as you slept, your body was fueled by fat. Completely normal. There is plenty of science behind this. Read the book if you'd like more information. As I've mentioned, this is my 12th monthly fast. I'm stronger than when I started (due to strength training). My bs control is better than when I started. I haven't gotten scurvy. My blood sugar didn't crash. I live life during a fast like any other time. Yes, there is hunger, but this post is long enough already. Your body isn't starving when you are fasting and have body fat. You are merely, FINALLY, getting around to "eating" the fat that you stored away for that very purpose.
