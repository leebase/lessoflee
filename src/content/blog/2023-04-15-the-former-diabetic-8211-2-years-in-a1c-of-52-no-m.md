Two years ago, on April 22nd, I received a life-changing diagnosis. My A1c was 11.2, and my future looked bleak. “I’m going to go blind” and other fears grabbed my heart. I was prescribed the $1000/month Trulicity to go along with the Metformin, and Glipizide I was already taking. I was told I would soon start insulin shots. Instead of giving in to despair, I made a radical decision: I defied the conventional wisdom, took charge of my health. Today, I'm celebrating my 2-year victory over diabetes, and I want to share my story with you.

Facing the Doubters:

When I shared my plan to change my diet, fast, and exercise, I was met with skepticism and disbelief. The doubters were quick to tell me that my plan was dangerous, that I was destined to fail, and that I should just accept my fate. But I refused to let their negativity define my journey.

The Turning Point:

"The Diabetes Code" by Dr. Jason Fung became my guiding light. Through his work, I discovered the power of taking control of my health and defying the so-called "expert" advice.

The Results Speak for Themselves:

Twenty-one months ago, I stopped taking all diabetes medications. Today, my A1c is a healthy 5.2, and I've gone from walking with a cane and driving with a handicap sticker due to osteoarthritis to running a 5k. The transformation has been nothing short of miraculous. My health has improved in many other ways as well.

Debunking the Myths:

I was told, "Diabetics can't fast," "Diabetes is forever," and "Keto is a dangerous diet." Yet, here I am, standing tall and proving them wrong. I am a living testament to the power of taking control of one's health and not blindly accepting the status quo.

To the Skeptics – Time to Rethink:

This is for all those who doubted me and tried to discourage me from pursuing my plan. I want to scream it from the rooftops: I took charge, I defied the odds, and I emerged victorious.

The Power of Knowledge and Perseverance:

My journey has taught me the importance of never giving up, of seeking knowledge, and of having faith in the human body’s ability to heal. The transformation I've experienced is not only physical but also mental and emotional.

Conclusion:

If you or someone you know is battling diabetes or any other health challenge, I hope my story serves as inspiration. Don't let the naysayers hold you back – take control, push the boundaries, and believe in your power to transform your life. The best revenge is living well, and I'm living proof that it's possible.
