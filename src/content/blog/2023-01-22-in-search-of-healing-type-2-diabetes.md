Remission or Reversal? I prefer the term “healing”. It’s the notion that Type 2 Diabetes is NOT a “progressively degenerative disease” where the BEST thing you can hope for is the MANAGE your blood glucose with meds and insulin as the INEVITABLE march of disease progresses to steal your foot or your eyes or your kidneys…heart attack or stroke.

In promoting the notion that healing can happen there is the inevitable push back “not everyone”. And that’s true. If you’ve let your type 2 diabetes progress to the point of losing your foot, no amount of diet change, fasting or exercise will bring it back.

I admit that I’m less concerned about the “too far past the point of healing” being exposed to the message that healing can happen – than I am for those who’s doctors are damning them with low expectation. My own doctor told me “diabetes is not your fault, it’s genetic” as she added the $1000/month Trulicity to the two other meds I was already taking and scheduling me to start insulin at our next appointment.

When I kicked into gear and made the changes…cut out the carbs, fasting and exercise…and in three months got off all the meds…we met again. It was the very appointment I was supposed to be starting insulin. I asked her “why isn’t what I did what you teach?” Her reply “not everyone is willing to make the changes you did”.

She was willing to damn me to a lifetime of expensive meds and declining health rather than tell me that difficult changes could turn my health around. How could she know that my pancreas hadn’t been injured past the point of healing? She couldn’t. Neither could I. Nobody will know until they try.

Which is the worse option? Tell someone who can’t heal their pancreas that healing is possible….or tell someone who could heal “it’s not your fault, take these meds”?

Everything I did that has brought me healing would improve anyone's health – even if their foot won’t grow back or their pancreas won’t completely heal.

The longer you wait…the more likely it is that you can’t heal. We have plenty of testimonies from people far older than me turning their health around. There is no AGE that means you are past help. Do what you can and the best things that can happen for you, will come about.

I have gone from walking with a cane to running a 5k. But I still have OA. I may always have it. Or…my cartilage may heal. I don’t know. I know this….I’m far better off for trying to heal even if I am never OA free.
