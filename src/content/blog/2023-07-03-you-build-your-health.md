I’m enjoying vacation and reflecting how far I’ve come over the last two years. I’ve healed my type 2 diabetes, and made great progress in overcoming osteoarthritis in my knees.

I could still be taking the $1000/month Trulicity AND be on an insulin pump. I could be walking with a cane, driving with a handicap sticker and be MUCH heavier.

Instead, I’m off all the meds, never started the insulin pump and running 5ks. Praise God. Lots more work to do.

There is a phrase “If you have your health, you have everything”. I’m so enjoying my revitalized health. I went to the Air Show with my family and I can not only walk the mile from the car to the venue across grass, I carried both my wife and my chairs. I’m back to being the “carrier of heavy things” – which, forgive me, in my mind is still the man’s job. There are steep stairs at the AirBnB we are staying at. I have no problem with them. I will be walking all over the tourist town and by the lake shore. All day. I pick my grandson up and give him rocket rides.

These may seem like small things, but they are the “everything” that phrase “if you have your health” is talking about.

I do have a change to make – “If you BUILD your health, you’ll have everything”. I had been losing my health for years. My world got more painful and smaller. I didn’t notice at first and then one day I am huffing and puffing at the smallest tasks, and was simply unable to do so many others. I developed type 2 diabetes and went from 1 med to 3 and was scheduled to start insulin. I hadn’t “got old” – I had failed to maintain my health.

I finally got sick and tired of being sick and tired. I began the hard work of building my health back up. Diet and exercise – what you eat and how you move.

I haven’t arrived – there is no such thing as arriving at good health. It’s a life long responsibility – an ever present opportunity to build yourself up.

I never intended to “manage my diabetes”. I wanted to over come. I didn’t want to have a surgeon replace my knees. I wanted to heal.

Diabetes isn’t keeping me from enjoying my favorite foods. Those foods were keeping me in poor health. They are bringers of disease. It’s my job to to make better choices.

I welcome you to join me in the change of mindset. Aim for health, not disease management. Health isn’t an inherent trait. Health is built – one good choice at a time. Choose to say no to unhealthy foods. Choose to say yes to the foods that bring health. Choose to move your body – a little more and a bit farther.

The healthier you is waiting for you to build it.
