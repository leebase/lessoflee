Which wing on an airplane is the important one? Is it the right, or the left? Well, I’ve been guilty of flying my “health plane” as if only the “exercise wing” was important. I have been neglecting the discipline in my diet. I just finished vacation and ate terribly. Now, one might think “it’s vacation, time to enjoy”…but I’ve been having too many “times to enjoy” and I’m gaining weight when I need to still be losing. I had to buy new bigger clothes it’s so bad.

I was tired and achy yesterday and thought I might just “vacation mode” my Saturday Push Day. Instead, I got up and put on my VR headset where I see the fine folks at Supernatural put out their longest workout ever – a High level Super Duper Monster workout. 20 songs, 64 minutes of sweating to dance music. So Push Day was on baby!

I made it the whole way and pushed hard at the end. I was able to do so first – because I chose to show up for myself. One of my knees was achy and the list of 20 circles (representing the songs) looked intimidating. Am I really going to do this? I paced myself and after a couple songs my knee was fine. 19 to go, 18 to go…7 to go…4 to go time to start making a push to the end. Once I got to 3 I knew I was going to make it and didn’t need to hold anything back.

I’m learning that my body can be at a certain level of effort and tiredness but I can just keep going. The music, the beautiful virtual locations, the coaches all work together to make an hour go by pretty fast. Running for an hour on a treadmill is torture for me by comparison.

I did my body good. I’d be doing my body even better by regaining the discipline in my diet. Anybody else have a tendency to favor one healthy area and neglect another?

Go do your own body good by doing more and going farther.

How much more? A little more.

How much farther? A bit farther.

Always.
