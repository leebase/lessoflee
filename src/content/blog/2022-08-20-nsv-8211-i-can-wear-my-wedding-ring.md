It’s been MANY years since I’ve worn my wedding ring. I fatted out of it – had it resized and fatted out of it again. So in the jewelry box it went. Years and years ago.

Today it fits – a bit snug, but it fits. Feels weird to be wearing a ring.

Also NSV – having an outing with the wife to see a Cub game. Lots of walking, lots of stairs. I didn’t even take a Diclofenac which I’d relegated to “only on trips” a year ago. Today I thought “why take it assuming I’ll need it?”

Hope the game isn’t rained out and the Cubs win. Either way, I’m spending the day with my lovely wife – out and about. I’ve already won.

NSV = Non-Scale Victory
