Had a great active day. I’m no longer sedentary. doing things I simply could not do a year ago. Went for a nice brisk hour long, 3 mile walk with my lovely wife. “Walking” was what I had for breakfast.

Then in the evening I put on my Meta Quest VR headset. I played table tennis. Then some fun Beat Sabre. I started with “normal” but they was simply too easy. Bumped it up to hard and worked up a sweat. Then I bowled a few games. And finished off with a high intensity – short duration – Supernatural workout to Lady Gaga’s Bad Romance.

What fun – what sweat Inducing, heart racing fun.

An hour and 15 minutes worth. Remember when I challenged myself to someday be able to exercise vigorously for an hour?

I could not do this a year ago. It’s just now becoming walking weather and I’m already in “mid summer” shape. I couldn’t even make it through the low level VR workouts last August.

I’m squatting and lunging like a mad man. I couldn’t do those at first.

I feel so much better.
