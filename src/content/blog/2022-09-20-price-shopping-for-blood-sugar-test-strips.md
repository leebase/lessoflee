My new job's insurance is such that I no longer "just order" my bs test strips. I was a bit shocked at the $110 price I was charged for 100 strips of OneTouch Verio. That's more than $1 per test.

I then did some price shopping. With [http://www.goodrx.com](http://www.goodrx.com) I learned I could have saved $31 at Walgreens, and $40 at Meijer's. Still, $70 for 100 strips sounded like a lot.

100 Accu-chek strips would run me $40 on Amazon. I like Accu-chek, it's what I used for years. I like OneTouch better, but for the price difference, I can't see using them.

Dario test strips are $50 for 100 on Amazon. Not bad, and the Dario kit is nice and works with your Smartphone. I found the readings to be much higher. I have some strips I'll use up, but don't see myself sticking with Dario. I use a different app to record my bs readings. If I were traveling a lot, the nice compact kit of the Dario might make me choose it.

But the price winner by far is ReliOn strips at Walmart $18 for 100. I used ReliOn pretty much all summer. It's my least favorite "user experience", but I eventually got the hang of getting a reading. (I went through numerous strips where I messed up).

Which brand is "most accurate"? At this point, my bs is under control so it really doesn't matter. It's all about the trends and just keeping an eye on things.

I like the lancet that comes with the OneTouch best. And the OneTouch needs only a very tiny drop to read. But, the price is such that I can't see using them after my current supply runs out.

The ReliOn didn't come with a lancet. I use the one that came with my Accu-chek. It's also very nice and I have a huge supply of lancet heads in a drawer.

I check at least once, but sometimes 2 or 3 times a day at this point. $3 a day vs less than a dollar. That adds up over time.

BTW, after a few years of pricking my finger – I finally discovered that I should hold the meter still, then bring the finger with the blood drop to the strip instead of the other way around. I don't "misfire" as often any more.
