I Can’t…Until I Can – I’ve made it a mantra, an ethos, a mindset.  I’m reclaiming abilities that I’ve lost.  More than a year ago I set my sight on regaining the ability to run a 5k.  At the time, I couldn’t run, had trouble walking across uneven surfaces like…my yard.  I have osteoarthritis in my knees and I was morbidly obese.

Little by little I’m reclaiming my life.  I can still remember wondering if I could ever jog again – at all.  When I started, I couldn’t get both feet into the air at the same time.  So I walked.  Then I walked longer.  Then longer still.  Then faster, then faster still.  By then I was speed walking with that funny butt wiggle.  It was the best I could do.

Until…I could do more.  I remember crying tears of joy the day I took my first couple jogging steps with both feet in the air.  It was just a few, but it was a start.

It’s Saturday, and on Saturday’s I do my “running intervals”.  I call it running, but a much younger and fitter lady blew by me on the trail.  She was power walking ![😊](~/assets/posts/2022-08-13-i-cantuntil-i-can-run-a-5k-with-intervals/1f60a.png)

I didn’t want to go today. I was tired and achy.  But it was Saturday, and Saturday’s I go for a run.  I put my running play list on and by the time I made it to the trail, I decided today was the day I was going to run intervals for an entire 5k distance. 

The first leg was near 3/4 of a mile without stopping.  My longest to date.  I made it to the first bench, past the mile mark, only stopping to walk once.  (See my Three Benches story: [https://lessoflee.wordpress.com/2022/07/19/three-benches-a-tale-of-belonging/](https://lessoflee.wordpress.com/2022/07/19/three-benches-a-tale-of-belonging/) )

On I went – running as long as I could, then walking until my heart rate fell to 120bpm, then running some more.  Repeat until 3.2 miles or 5 kilometers.  I did it.  42 minutes, 23 seconds.  I now have my starting time.  The time I will improve upon.

I can’t run a 5k without stopping — yet.  That’s a victory for another day.   The photo is of me at the farthest point.  I was coming up to the third bench and this elderly couple was eying that same bench.  “Can you take a photo of me?”  The gentleman did.  That’s me, sucking my gut in.  Which, btw, is something else I can now do that I couldn’t.  Still a work in progress to lose this gut.  A victory for another day.

Today I took “running/walking for 5k” off the list of things I can’t do.
