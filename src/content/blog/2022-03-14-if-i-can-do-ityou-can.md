“New” – old clothes for church. I am on a bit of a roll…in the pocket. Making good choices and building upon them. Seeing great results.

However, I think it’s important to put my life in a bit of context. I hear in many comments “I wish I had your commitment, drive, will power, etc”. If I was a person that had some of these innate qualities…how did I become obese, morbidly obese – in the first place?

Not only that – I eventually did something about it 11 years ago. And then AGAIN, became morbidly obese, type 2 diabetic etc.

I'm glad many of you have been able to see this transformation since the first day I came here and said the following:

————————
Ok folks, here I am. It's been 8 years since I lost that incredible 80lbs and got fit. I'd love to report that I kept it all off, but I haven't. So, as in 9 years before when I started my journey, I am announcing before any success…that I intend to get healthy. Again. Last time I started at 315, this time I start at 336. More importantly, this time I start as a diabetic with out of control A1C of 11.6 and osteoarthritis in my knees. This time I have to accomplish the task in my mid fifties.
—————————

I didn’t know that “this time” I was going to get back on track. I didn’t know all the things about how to reverse insulin resistance. I hadn’t yet read the 14 books I’ve since read. I hadn’t watched the hours and hours and hours of YouTube that I’ve watched since then.

I hadn’t lose a single pound. I was still on all the meds and still scheduled to go on insulin. I had one previous year when I’d lost a lot of weight and got relatively fit. I had decades of knowing I should eat better – but not doing so. Knowing I should exercise…but not doing so.

How many times just between losing the weight last time and this time did I say “time to get back to what I know to do”? How many times did I work at it and stop? Many times.

What to take from this? I don’t have any magical powers or super character that you don’t have. Everything I now know.…there was a time when I didn’t know it. There was a time I didn’t know how to cook. There was a time I didn’t know what foods were best to eat. There was a time when I didn’t know the power and importance of fasting,

And there were times I knew much of that…and still didn’t do what I needed to do.

Just start. And if you stopped. Start again. And if you stopped 100 times, start the 101st. Believe you can do it. Believe you MUST do it. Believe that you can indeed ENJOY doing it. Believe that not only will you enjoy your life as a non-diabetic, life at a healthy weight, life physically fit…but you can enjoy the journey to get there.

I wrote that original post before any single victory happened. I write this post as someone still obese with a lot of health yet to recover. I hope I continue this forever. But if I should falter again. Then I shall also wake up again and try again and again.

That is life. It is not all success. It is not all failure. Each day is an opportunity to make good choices and good actions. Each day is a beginning of it's own. You can do it.
