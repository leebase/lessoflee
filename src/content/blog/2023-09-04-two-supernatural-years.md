Two years ago I was only a few months into my health reclamation journey. I had brought my A1c from 11.2 to 6.5 and gotten off all diabetes meds. I can’t remember the exact date I stopped needing my cane to walk, but it was only some few months before that. I was walking, however slowly and unsteadily, around the neighborhood. But, to quote a famous tv series – Winter Was Coming!

What was I going to do when winter arrived and I couldn’t go outside for walks? It wasn’t just the dark and the cold, I was too unsteady to walk on the snow and ice. I saw an ad for the Supernatural workout app for the Oculus Quest 2 headset. It looked fun. I bought the headset for the sole reason of trying out the workout app.

That was two years ago, and I’m still working out to Supernatural. I’ve gone from walking with a cane to running 5ks in that time. My A1c is down to 5.2 (normal) and I’ve been off all diabetes meds for more than 2 years. I no longer need pain remedies, pills and creams for osteoarthritis pain.

All of that because of Supernatural? No, of course not. I have been doing everything I can to regain my health: what I eat, how I exercise, working on sleep and stress management.

Still, Supernatural deserves this shout out. When I started I could barely do the shortest and most easy level. I fell a couple times trying to do the squats and lunges. However, it was FUN! SN transforms my living room into the most beautiful locations. Great and ever-changing selection of music and routines. “Live” coaches. “Hello athlete”, went from being a trite phrase to my self image. The coaches are in my head telling me to show up for myself.

It was working out with SN that strengthened my legs such that I began to walk better. I was shocked to discover that I didn’t just have arthritis, I had become weak. I could do something about that – and SN was a big help.

Two years has gone by and I still love my Supernatural workouts. I now do the most difficult and longest routines. They are so fun and challenging and ever changing. I’ve never stuck with going to a health club. Other than walking, I’ve never stuck with any exercise program before.

Supernatural has helped me recover abilities by doing more and going farther. With SN I’ve developed my mantra.

How much more? A little more.

How much farther? A bit farther.

Always.

Thank you to all the coaches, and all the people behind the scenes. You are changing lives.

Ps: as always, talk to your own health team for your specific needs and action plan.

Pps: as always, never ONLY listen to pill pushers and illness managers, take control and work on restoring your health
