One of my mantras is “Start where you are. Make progress”. It’s about not looking at others and judging yourself. I have been lifting weights. Am I strong? No, I’m weak. I ran a 6k race in Chicago last year. As I ran, with my heart rate proving how hard I was working, I was passed repeatedly by people WALKING.

What else can I do, but keep going? I can’t go back in time and be young. I can’t un-eat the junk foods that got me fat. I couldn’t just peel off the fat like removing socks. I couldn’t just throw away my cane and start running. I couldn’t just stop taking the three blood sugar meds and eat with abandon. Well, that last one I could do – but with terrible consequences.

I had to just accept that how I had been living and eating had put me in a condition of poor health: highly diabetic, arthritic, walking with a cane and driving with a handicap sticker. And then I had to make progress.

Today is Saturday Push Day. I lifted weights and did an aerobics workout.

Squats 130lb sets: 6,6,6,12,13

Bench 80lb sets: 6,6,6,6,10

Row 80lb sets: 6,6,6,10, 14

Weak, but stronger than I have been.

Then I did a Pros Only level Supernatural. Loved it. Metallica music and crazy hard, fast and inventive routines. I’ve been doing these virtual reality aerobics workouts for three years now. I couldn’t have done this routine in the beginning, not even remotely close. I did what I could, and a little more and kept at it.

Which leads to another mantra of mine. If you want to reclaim your health you have to do more.

How much more? A little more.

How far? A bit farther.

Always.

3 years and counting in my health recovery journey. A lifetime more to go.

p.s.  Always consult your own health team for your specific needs

p.p.s. Educate yourself to fully participate in your health strategy
