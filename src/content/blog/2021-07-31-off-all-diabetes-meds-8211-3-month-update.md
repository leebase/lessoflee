The headline says it all.  I met with my diabetes specialist for the 3 month follow up – and I was able to get off the one remaining medication, metformin.  My A1c has gone from 11.2 in April to 6.5 in July.   6.5 is still in the diabetic range – it’s the start, actually.   However, A1c is a three month average and my last month of blood glucose numbers put me well into the pre-diabetic range.

This was goal #1 of my three goals

- Control my blood sugar without diabetes meds
- Get to a healthy weight (not morbidly obese, not obese)
- Be able to vigorously exercise for an hour

Type 2 Diabetes is just a symptom of the underlying [metabolic syndrome](https://www.mayoclinic.org/diseases-conditions/metabolic-syndrome/symptoms-causes/syc-20351916).  It’s a line in the sand – “this point you are diabetic”.   Getting my blood sugars below that level does not mean I am free of metabolic syndrome, does not mean I am no longer “[insulin resistant](https://www.webmd.com/diabetes/insulin-resistance-syndrome)”.   It does mean that I no longer classify as diabetic.   I have much more work to do to regain my insulin sensitivity and reverse metabolic syndrome.

As to Goal #2 – I have lost 23lbs.  I’m actually a bit disappointed that I haven’t lost more.  Losing is better than gaining, of course.  I’m working so much harder than when I began the [Less of Lee journey](https://wordpress.com/post/lessoflee.wordpress.com/313) 9 years ago – and not losing weight nearly as fast.   I know…I’m older.   In addition, my metabolic syndrome has advanced further.  Still, whatever is…is – I just have to keep plugging away.

Goal #3 – I’m nowhere close to being able to exercise vigorously at all yet.  Let alone for an hour.  I have got my walking up to an hour most days, and sometimes I can make an hour and a half and go 4 miles.   I’m super soar in my arthritic knees at the end of such a walk.  Can barely walk up the 3 steps to the front door.  Progress is progress, though.  I love my walks.

“How are you doing it” – well, that’s kind of what this blog is about.   I’m reading a lot of books and watching a lot of YouTube videos.  More on those at another time – but the primary book who’s plan I’m following is [The Diabetes Code by Dr Jason Fung](https://www.amazon.com/Diabetes-Code-Prevent-Reverse-Naturally-ebook/dp/B0795BLS8D/ref=tmm_kin_swatch_0?_encoding=UTF8&qid=1627749102&sr=8-1).  Or watch the YouTube videos by [Dr Sten Ekberg](https://www.youtube.com/user/drekberg).  TLDR version is:  intermittent fasting with low carb eating.
