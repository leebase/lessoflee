Holiday travel means you can’t work out, right?

Wrong. I brought the Quest 2 with me, pushed some furnishings out of the way, logged into the WiFi and got a nice workout with Supernatural. Did a mid level boxing and a mid flow to music from Magic Dragons.

Interesting feeling since VR completely replaces what you can see – it felt like working out at home.

I’ve closed all three Apple Watch rings every day of the year so far (move 490 calories, work out 30 minutes, stand up at least once 12 hours). Low goals that I far exceed most days. But there have been a couple “near midnight walks” to make sure I closed my rings.
