Man these were good. Air fryer pork chops with a pork panko and Parmesan crust seasoned with Eat Happy Kitchen’s dill spice.

As pork is lean, I made a spicy sour cream topping with Primal Kitchen’s buffalo sauce. When you aren’t running on carbs, you need the fat.

Ingredients:

– 4 boneless pork chops

– 1 cup pork Panko (or almond flour for a low-carb alternative)

– 1/4 cup grated Parmesan cheese

– 1 tablespoon ranch seasoning

– 2 eggs, beaten (for the egg wash)

– Cooking spray (for the air fryer)

– Salt and pepper to taste

For the dip:

– 1/2 cup sour cream

– 2 tablespoons Primal Kitchen Buffalo Sauce (adjust to taste)

Instructions:

1. Preheat your air fryer to 400°F (200°C).

2. In a shallow bowl, combine the pork Panko (or almond flour), grated Parmesan cheese, and ranch seasoning. Mix well.

3. Season the pork chops with salt and pepper on both sides.

4. Dip each pork chop into the beaten eggs, allowing any excess to drip off.

5. Coat the pork chops in the Panko-Parmesan mixture, pressing gently to ensure an even coating.

6. Lightly coat the air fryer basket with cooking spray to prevent sticking.

7. Place the coated pork chops in the air fryer basket in a single layer, ensuring they're not overlapping.

8. Air fry the pork chops at 400°F (200°C) for about 12-15 minutes, flipping them halfway through. The exact cooking time may vary based on the thickness of the pork chops and the efficiency of your air fryer. Aim for an internal temperature of 145°F (63°C) for perfectly cooked pork chops.

9. While the pork chops are cooking, prepare the dip by combining the sour cream and Primal Kitchen Buffalo Sauce in a bowl. Mix well until smooth and creamy.

10. Once the pork chops are done, remove them from the air fryer and let them rest for a couple of minutes.

11. Serve the air-fried pork chops with the buffalo sour cream dip on the side.

Enjoy your delicious keto-friendly air fryer pork chops with a flavorful buffalo dip!
