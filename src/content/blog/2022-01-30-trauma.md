This is hard to write…but I can’t talk about diabetes and health stuff with something much more portent that also happened.

Last night I got a call…it was my youngest daughter and she had been in a terrible accident. She and her boyfriend are ok physically, though the mental trauma will take time to recover from.

We are talking one and a half roll of the car and landing upside down in a creek/river. Head below the water…and then the fight to survive and exit a car as freezing water rushes in and 1 foot thick ice is keeping the doors closed.

Any accident you walk away from is a blessing. It could have been so much worse.

I’m not a diabetic for a couple days. I’m not a faster for a couple days. I’ll get back on program later. Fruit…isn’t the worst way to be off plan. And I’ve eaten worse ( not photo-ed)
