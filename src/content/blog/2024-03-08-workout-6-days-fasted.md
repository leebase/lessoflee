It was raining today so instead of walks I did a medium level Supernatural aerobics workout. I’ve finished day 6 of a planned 14 day fast (no food or caloric drinks).

I was up to the challenge but it was certianly harder. The headset felt extra heavy and even though I didn’t get my heart rate up too high, it felt challenging.

I don’t intend to do intense exercise or weight lifting on a long fast. I want just enough activity to remind my body that I need these muscles – eat the fat instead. I have a LOT of food stored all over my body.

Work was light today which made the fasting a bit harder. I definitely had the “I want to eats” – which I distinguish from physical hunger. After the first couple days, the hunger signals go way down.

It’s cool, do nothing and the body consumes its fat to the tune of a half pound a day and a pound of “weight”. 14 days, 14 lbs less of Lee.

I’ll lose a lot more weight than that, but anything over a pound comes back quickly as it’s just water, liver carb storage, and food in your digestive system.

I’m mentally sharp, my mood is normal. I did have to remind myself of my goals and battle some moments of wanting to end the fast earlier than planned.

A lot of healing and repair is going on as well.

I did my body good. Hope you did your body good today as well.
