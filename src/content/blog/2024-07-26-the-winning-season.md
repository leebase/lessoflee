I’ve been actively working on restoring my health and abilities for over 3 years and have claimed some significant wins.

⁃ Gone from highly diabetic on 3 meds to non-diabetic off all diabetes meds

⁃ Walking with a cane and driving with a handicap sticker to running 5ks

⁃ Losing 85lbs of weight

There are more, but those are the highlights. It’s been a good winning season. A time in my life where I have made the hard choices, consistently applied diet, exercise, fasting and self education to good effect.

Currently I am in the middle of a down season. My exercise has fallen to just walking an hour or more every day. No Saturday Push Days, No strength training, no HIIT, no VR aerobics. I’ve been gaining weight instead of losing weight.

There are reasons

⁃ Bursitis flare up (or something similar) that gave me shoulder pain keeping me from lifting, and more.

⁃ Unemployment

⁃ New, intense job

Reasons or excuses? Doesn’t matter. Life is always filled with challenges. I will either overcome or I will see all of my health problems return and my hard won abilities go away.

How do I know? Because I’ve gone through winning and losing seasons before. In my mid-forties I lost 85lbs and got fit. It only took a year. In my mid-fifties I had gained all that weight back plus 20 more, plus type 2 diabetes, plus osteoarthritis and more.

I’m not freaking out. I’m just being transparent. I don’t do the right things all the time, never have, probably never will. But I CAN, and I WILL, do better.

I accept that seasons come and go. I have never presented myself as having arrived. I ate myself into sickness, and failed to move such that I became disabled. And then I’ve eaten myself into health and moved myself into ability. The seasons are not external forces, but internal reactions to those external forces. I can’t control the weather, but I can respond better.

I don’t focus on what I can’t control. I gain weight SO EASILY and quickly. It’s like how one can run up a credit card in the blink of an eye and take years to pay off. What value does it bring for me to despair over an aspect of my genetics I can’t control? I can control what I eat. I can exercise. There is time even after a stressful day of work.

My winning season isn’t over. It’s just a mid-season slump. I’m enjoying my improved health so much, even now. But I know that should I extend this slump into a full on losing season, my sad health state can and will return. One is only free of diabetes when you keep eating as your body needs you to do.

3 years and counting in my health recovery journey. A lifetime more to go.

p.s.  Always consult your own health team for your specific needs

p.p.s. Educate yourself to fully participate in your health strategy
