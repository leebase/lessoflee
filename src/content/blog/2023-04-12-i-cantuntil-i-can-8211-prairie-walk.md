Rediscovering the Prairie: A Journey of Personal Growth

I used to walk through the prairie preserve near my home, season after season, year after year. I would meander through the tall grasses, marvel at the vibrant wildflowers, and capture photographs of the of changing beauty.

Until I couldn't. My world gradually shrank, and one by one, things I enjoyed became "things I used to do." Was it “getting old before my time?” Was it “arthritis”? No to the former, and probably not for the latter. If taking my health seriously and making changes in my diet and exercise is what is bringing me back – then poor eating and sedentary lifestyle is what got me there.

I reached a point where I couldn't even walk across my own yard to check the mail. But today, as I ventured out for a walk along the prairie trail, I thought to myself – why not walk through the field like I used to?

And so I did.

Because now I can.

The sun warmed my skin, aiding in the production of much-needed vitamin D. The fresh air and gentle rustling of branches, coupled with the sight of a timid deer peeking through the brush, revitalized my spirit and reminded me of the importance of spending time in nature for mental health.

I'm slowly reclaiming my abilities, one at a time, as I venture further and accomplish more.

With each step, I rediscover the beauty of the prairie preserve – the dappling light filtering through the trees, the chorus of birdsong, and the ever-changing tapestry of colors and textures. These moments, captured in photographs and memories, fuel my journey towards healing and personal growth.

How have I come here? By doing more and going farther.

How much more? A little more.

How much farther? A bit farther.

Always.
