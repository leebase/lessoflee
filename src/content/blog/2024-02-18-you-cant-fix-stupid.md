If you don’t know, you can learn.

If you are ignorant, you can be educated.

But you can’t fix stupid. lol!

My wife told me to get the step ladder to replace the smoke detector batteries. But I just climbed up on the chest and reached over to get to the ceiling mounted detector. I removed the battery just fine.

And then I forgot I was standing a couple feet off the floor when I stepped down. And as no “step” was there, I fell – twisting both knees and doing something nasty and painful to my foot.

Stupid is as stupid does.

Now I’m hobbling around. No Saturday Push Day. Walked the dog but I really could have used my old cane. Which made me reflect.

I’m feeling temporary pain and trouble walking due to falling. A mere three years ago this was my every day life. Needing to hold the railing to walk down stairs. Worrying that I might fall in the shower. Needing a handicap sticker so I could park where I wouldn’t have to walk far.

Changing my diet, fasting and exercise has done far more than just get me off diabetes meds. It’s building my health and reclaiming abilities. Something pills and shots could never do.

Unfortunately, there is no cure for my stupidly.

p.s.  Always consult your own health team for your specific needs

p.p.s. Educate yourself to fully participate in your health strategy
