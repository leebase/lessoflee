A couple months ago I had a micro-stroke in my right eye.  Initially there was so much fluid in my eye that I couldn’t read with that eye.  All of that has resolved.  There was inflammation in the retina that almost needed shots in the eye.  The specialist had me wait a month and recheck.  During that time my eye got better!  He was surprised.  He clearly expected it to get worse, not better.  Another follow up a month later and no further inflammation issues.   Our next appointment was then moved out three months.  There are some bleeding issues in the non-visual part of the retina.  I appear to have escaped any significant damage.

This next part it strange, and exciting, and I’m not sure yet what to make of it.  I have worn glasses since 1st grade and my eyes get a little worse every year.  In my mid 40’s I had cataract surgery and lenses were inserted that gave me 20/20 distance vision.  I still needed glasses to read.  And, once again, each year after that my eye sight has gotten a little worse each year.  All within the ability to be corrected by glasses.

On the car trip to see my grandson, I was wearing single vision driver’s glasses.  Not my varifocals that let me see far and near.  Just the distance glasses.  I noticed when I lifted my glasses, I could read the instrument panel.  I mean – crystal clear with no glasses.  Right now I am typing on my laptop, and I have no glasses on.  I am reading the screen with a tiny font, and I can see the text absolutely clear.  This is better than 20/20 the size I’m reading. 

If I hold my phone at normal distance, it’s blurry, but if I hold it a bit further away, I can read small text clearly.  My middle distance vision has somehow improved.  Is it related to the fast?  Have II always had this ability and just never noticed?  I can’t say.  I will go have my eyes checked by an eye doctor to see if my vision has indeed improved.   

I am not jumping to conclusions about autophagy, stem cells or any of the other healing features that accompany extended fasting.  Nothing I’ve read spoke of improved vision due to extended fasting.  It’s just very curious and extremely exciting.   I just put my glasses back on and I can’t read the small text as well with them on as I can right now with them off.  What does this mean?  I”m 58 and have never experience anything like this.
