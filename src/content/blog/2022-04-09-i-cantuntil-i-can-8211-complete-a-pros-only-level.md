When I started this latest journey to get healthy, one of my goals was to be able to exercise vigorously for an hour. I’ve hit the half hour mark. I couldn’t exercise vigorously at all at first.

8 months ago I got the Oculus Quest 2 and subscribed to the Supernatural fitness app. I couldn’t complete the shortest low level workout at first. I could barely squat and had trouble lunging. Fell once or twice trying to do so.

I’ve been doing this Pros Only workout for awhile now…never able to get past the 3rd song. The only reason I tried it was for the Dream Theater song. Fortunately it’s the first song.

I kept at it. Every now and then I’d give it a try and work up a good sweat…if only for 2 songs.

This morning I was able to keep going. I finished the workout…and earned a Platinum to boot!

I work out in a variety of ways…but nothing gets my heart rate up like Supernatural does…not even the recumbent cross trainer at Planet Fitness.

Progress isn’t seen in a day – not usually in a week. But looking back at where I started after 8 months…it’s night and day.
