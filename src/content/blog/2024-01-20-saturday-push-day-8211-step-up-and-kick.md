It’s Saturday, time to push my limits. I ventured outside in sub zero windchills for a short walk. I’m going stir crazy cooped up inside. I am happy I have the Quest 2 VR headset and Supernatural workout app so I can do my push day from my living room.

To step up my efforts, I start with a short High level boxing routine. Boxing is harder for me. This is the first time I felt like I rocked boxing at this level. Bobbing and weaving, fast punches of various kinds. Keeping up with the music, it felt terrific. I am improving. But this was just the warm up.

I then did a long H level flow routine, one of my favorites. A year ago, all by itself this was a push day routine. I fought to not quit but hang in there and finish. This time I pre-fatigued myself with the boxing to up the difficulty. I had not problem completing the routine even so. i wasn’t JUST hanging on the last two songs – I was kicking it to the end. Singing out loud and whacking those targets with joyous abandon. I handled all the squats and lunges. I was able to do the underhand swirls, which were quite difficult when I first started.

Even though I did two routines instead of the one, and pushed hard to the end with no struggle, my heart rate was lower than when I first took on the challenge of this routine. I can feel my fitness level improving as I’ve done more and gone farther.

How much more? A little more.

How much farther? A bit farther.

Always.

Now I rest, today and tomorrow and start the next cycle on Monday.

I did my body good today. Looking forward to hearing how you did your body good.

p.s.  Always consult your own health team for your specific needs
