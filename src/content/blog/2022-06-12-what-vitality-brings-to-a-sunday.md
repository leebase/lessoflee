Today has been a good day enjoying improved health. It started with a nice 5k walk early in the morning. The sun was not not very high, nice and cool with just a hint of fog as I walked 3.2 miles through a beautiful prairie preserve.

This was my “walking is for mental health” practice. Love my walks and on the weekend I have the time to walk further. This was an hour an 17 minutes for me. My knees and hips were fairly sore by the end – but it’s progress from where I started.

Off to church where I have no problem getting in and out of my car. I used to have a handicap parking sticker, now I don’t need one. I used to have to very carefully and slowly get in and out of the car. Today I parked at the back of the lot and got in and out of the car like a normal person.

I love to sing at church. We stand much of the time when we sing. No problems. No having to hold on to the seat in front of me. No inward groaning about having to get up AGAIN. And, believe it or not, my singing is better. I have no phlegm or other inflammation issues affecting my throat and have a stronger diaphragm for singing. I’m not saying I sound any better – I’m part of the “make a joyful noise to the Lord” brigade.

After church was a potluck – nachos. Yeah…not much for me to eat but no real temptation either. I had a bit of ground beef with some nacho cheese. Ok, the cheese sauce isn’t perfect carnivore…but I indulged a small bit and got to enjoy the social occasion.

Then it was off to the grocery store on the way home. No need for the handicap parking space. I’m happy to not need them any more. No need to use the shopping cart as a quasi walker and hope that I could get all my shopping done before the pain would be too much.

I was an idiot and left my wallet in the car and didn’t discover this until I was at the register. Off I go, fast walking/crouch-running in my funny way out to the car and back. I got back right as they were finishing up my order.

You can see part of the beautiful path I get to walk through. I used to be able to run a 5k and perhaps I will be able to again one day. You can also see the food I bought at the hot bar at the grocery store. Some kind of baked white fish, salmon and brats. Looked to be a dusting of some kind of breaded topping. Not enough for me to worry about.

It’s been a good day. A day brought about by the hard work on diet, exercise, sleep and other health efforts over the past year.
