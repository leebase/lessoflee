The reward for years of hard work – is yard work.

I went for a 5k run Saturday to give my heart a nice workout. I didn’t have it in me. So I switched to running sprints. Higher intensity, shorter duration. I still went 4 miles, but with walking and running sprints. It’s unbelievable to me still that I have gone from walking with a cane to running sprints.

Why? Why am I pushing myself? Am I training for the Olympics? Am I aiming to win an athletic college scholarship? Nope. I’m training to be able to clean up the yard after a storm. Just the most recent moment that makes me glad that I started working on my health two years ago.

When I started I couldn’t even walk across the lawn. Now I’m using a chain saw like a man! Lol! It’s a tiny handheld that I told my wife I needed so we didn’t have to hire someone.

It feels good to reclaim the ability to take care of myself and my family. I drove 4 hours to my grandsons and then 4 hours back yesterday. I played with him and took care of errands and setting up the tech stack. I’m not particularly handy but I can help with technology. The drive was a lot harder than running a 5k for me – much more painful. But I could do it.

All the little things of life – made so much better because I can walk and carry heavy things. That’s why I push myself. I’m reclaiming my life, not just numbers on a blood sugar chart.

Join me in reclaiming your health. How much does it take? It takes more and you have to go farther.

How much more? A little more.

How much farther? A bit farther.

Always.

Ps: always talk to your own health advisors
