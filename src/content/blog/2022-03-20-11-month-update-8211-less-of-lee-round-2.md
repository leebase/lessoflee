I have to do it all over again. 10 years ago, I’m in mid 40's, lost 80lbs and got fit. 11 months ago, I was back where I started, only 20 lbs heavier, type 2 diabetic (A1c of 11.2, taking Metformin, Glipizide and Trulicity), osteo arthritic knees – and – now I'm in my mid fifties. Maybe I count as late fifties now.

I read The Diabetes Code by Jason Fung and began intermittent fasting, low carb eating. I also got a Freestyle Libre continuous glucose monitor. Those are the 3 pillars among everything else I did.

My diabetes is in remission. 8 months off of all the meds and maintaining an A1c less than the diabetic level. In fact, my A1c is 5.2 – which is in the normal range. Not diabetic, not pre-diabetic.

I’ve lost over 50lbs even as I'm beginning to add in muscle (verified with Dexa scan). I have gone from morbidly obese to just obese. I didn’t lose any weight this past month. It’s actually been a couple months now.

However. I lost 1.5” from my waist and 1” from my hips. In the last month! I’m down 7” in my waist and 8” from my hips from the start of my second journey 11 months ago.

My knees feel so much better. I sleep better. I weathered a bout of Covid in a few days. No more blurry vision first thing in the morning.

I’m walking so much better. I can walk down stairs without having to put both feet on the same stair before going down to the next. I can put my pants on without having to sit on the bed.

I am no longer sedentary. I am working out. Not merely walking. I strength train twice a week, do high intensity interval training at the health club. I am doing the high level workouts on Supernatural (a virtual reality workout)- and I couldn’t even complete a low level workout when I started. I work up a sweat playing VR table tennis most every day.

I am a former diabetic. That ruffles some people's feathers. You can consider yourself “forever a diabetic” if you want. I’m working on healing my body and my A1c is normal and continuing to improve.

I keep learning and keep improving and keep stepping up my efforts. With my doctor's counsel I have been fasting for 5 days once a month in addition to my daily intermittent fasting.

I eat more cleanly. Not perfectly, but I snack far less and go off plan far less. My two meals a day are delicious feasts. I can continue to live like this.

I have gone from “trying to reverse you 2 diabetes” to “working on my total health”. Muscle is health, so I'm working to build muscle. I’m still very obese but am casting my efforts as “body recomposition” and not merely “losing weight”. I’m working on sleep and stress management.

So many of you have cheered me on and encouraged me and I thank you. I hope you can remember that I started only 11 months ago. All I do now I couldn’t do then. All I know now, I didn’t know then. All I’ve progressed to now, hadn’t happened. Make the decision and start the journey. Learn, grow and improve along the way.

If I can do it, you can.
