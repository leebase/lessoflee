Archimedes said that if he had a lever long enough, he could move the world. I’m a believer in focusing on the big levers that can improve my health. I don’t sweat the small details unless/until I have to.

I remember clearly talking to the dietician after my diabetes specialist gave me the news my A1c had hit 11.2. Trulicity was added to the Metformin and Glipizide I was already on and I was scheduled to go on insulin at the next appointment.

The dietician took me through the government MyPlate filled with grains, whole wheat bread and fruit. Sugar, sugar and sugar. Just eat less, 3 meals with snacks. You know…the very thing so many people here advocate because they’ve talked to the same type of dietician I did.

Really? I have a problem processing sugar and you want me to eat a lot of sugar? That makes no sense. Eat carbs, then take 3 meds and then insulin to help me with my blood sugar problem? Why not stop eating all that sugar in the first place? (I already knew about low carb diets).

So the first big lever was to cut out the sugar from my diet. Meat, fish and non-starchy veggies. Call it LCHF, Keto or NSNG – all diets that use the same big lever and differ a bit in the small details.

After reading The Diabetes Code by Dr Jason Fung, I added the next big lever – intermittent fasting. I view it as an extension of the thinking behind low carb. Zero carb for hours a day.

I didn’t sweat the difference between net carbs or total carbs. I ate nuts. I eat dairy. I eat the occasional “low carb bread” or keto treat that’s still processed food. I still drink diet sodas. Small details that were more than covered for by the big levers.

Until I hit my stall. I went 5 months without losing any significant weight even though I was fasting 18hrs a day, extended fasting for 5 days once a month, eating “low carb” and exercising quite a bit.

I had to look for the next lever. I tried the carnivore diet for the month of June. But no – not a perfect version as some define it. I cut out the veggies but not dairy, not spices. And I did partake of a few portions of berries, pickles and olives during the month. The new lever worked fantastic. The small details did not matter. As usual.

It’s June 30, the last day. Tomorrow we find out how amazingly my stall was smashed. Preview: WHOOSH!
