What is better on a super cold, dark winter night than a nice bowl of chicken and dumplings? I don’t eat flour based foods any more, but this is a recipe that uses no wheat flour, is low carb and gluten free. It just doesn’t taste low carb.

Another win for the Victorias Keto Kitchen flour 2.0. You are supposed to be able to use it for anything you’d use regular flour to make. My latest delicious use was as dumplings. Both the wife and I loved this so much more than just plain chicken soup.

You make the chicken soup using whatever recipe you like. We had just “chicken soup” last night. I added another box of chicken stock to the leftovers and then made the biscuit recipe below. Then instead of making biscuits, I used a small spoon and formed dumplings that I dropped on top of the soup in the crock pot. It took about 4 hours on high for the dumplings to feel done. These are very lose dumplings which makes the soup thicker which I like.

Next time I might try baking the dumplings half way before putting them in the soup. Then it might not take as long. I might also try rolling the dough out and cuttling strips for a firmer version of dumplings.

The taste and mouthfeel was terrific. It shouldn’t be surprising as the biscuits I make from this recipe are wonderful.

You first have to follow the recipe below to make the flour. Bamboo fiber, almond flour, egg white protein powder and xanthum gum are the ingredients. There are alternatives to almond flour she talks about. The rest are required.

About 6g net carbs for all of the dumplings combined plus one biscuit (all that was left after making dumplings).

These are fiber bombs and you’ll feel it if you eat a lot. They taste like flour bread/biscuits.

I will be attempting crackers, pizza dough and maybe cookies. These dumplings, though, will definitely be made again.
