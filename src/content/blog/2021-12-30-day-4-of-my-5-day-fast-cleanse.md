There are now 51lbs less of Lee. I realize that after the fast is over, the water will come back. I’m still going to celebrate the moment. Water isn’t what we are concerned about. I’ll have lost maybe 3 or so pounds of fat. I lost 9lbs of fat over the last four months. So 1/3rd of that amount in 5 days – feeling happy about that.

Feeling just fine. No “extra mental clarity” that some have written about. I also don’t feel week or tired or angry. The hunger in the morning was easy. This afternoon, though, I really struggled a bit with food images in the books I'm reading and commercials etc. I want to eat. Looking forward to eating tomorrow. It’s compulsion, not hunger.

I went for a walk in the freezing cold. It was nice and sunny. I’m trying to make sure I walk outside as often as I can as there will be many days in the winter where I can’t.

Next time I do this, I’ll plan for a time that I’ll be more busy. A business trip would be perfect. Being distracted would really help.

My ketones are even higher than yesterday at 20. Fat burning is in high gear. My morning bs was 102 and in the afternoon was 82. I knocked my cgm out of my arm…so I’m manually testing 3 or 4 times.

Autophagy is recycling the inefficient mitochondria and misshapen proteins. Human growth hormone has surged. Stem cells are greatly increasing. My white blood cells are being recycled and refreshed. My gut bacteria is reconfiguring with the bad bugs giving way to the good. These are the “cleanse” attributes that don’t happen until day 3 and after and why these longer fasts are beneficial.

And, of course, with the fat burning comes an improvement in insulin sensitivity. I am pressing on to completely heal my insulin resistance and achieve a normal A1c. One extended fast doesn’t make that happen, but it helps move toward the goal.
