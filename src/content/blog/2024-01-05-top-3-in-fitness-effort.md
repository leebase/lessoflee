For the entire year that was 2023 – I ranked in the top 3% in exercise effort out of 31,000 people that use the FitIV app to track their fitness. Not bad for a couch potato who needed a cane to walk only 3 years ago.

From daily walks, Supernatural VR app aerobics to my Saturday Push Days, I put the daily, consistent work in. I showed up for myself just over an hour a day for the whole year.

I’m far from the top 3% most physically fit. The app uses METS (Metabolic equivalents) to keep score. This takes into account your starting base metabolic rate. I’ll let ChatGPT help explain:

1. **METs and Individual Baselines**: METs are calculated based on the energy expenditure relative to a person's resting metabolic rate. This means the same activity can have a different MET value for different individuals, depending on their fitness level, age, weight, and overall health.

2. **Leveling the Playing Field**: For someone with a lower baseline of metabolic health or fitness, an activity might be more challenging and thus result in a higher MET score compared to someone who is younger and more fit. This is because the effort and energy required relative to their baseline are greater.

So – what I managed to achieve is top level effort, applied consistently over an entire year. In any given week I might rank in the top 25-50%. For a month it might be in the top 15%. But working day in and day out, all year long – and I ended up in the top 3%.

I ran my first 5k without stopping, alone on the trail near my house just before the start of the year. I ran among 10k people for 6 kilometers in the down town streets of Chicago that spring.

I started my Supernatural VR workouts barely able to do the shortest and easiest routine two years ago. I now have done the longest and hardest they have.

I went from walking with a cane and driving with a handicap sticker, a sick and feeble coach potato to being an active person regaining abilities by doing more and going farther.

How much more? Only a little more.

How much farther? Just a bit farther.

But always.

All year long pushing myself and never settling. I’m not racing against anybody else. Women pushing strollers WALKED past me as I was running that race down town. I’m not fit and healthy. I’m just a lot better than I was. I have even more health to recover and fitness to gain.

It’s about progress, not perfection. I fell back on my weight loss journey by gaining 40lbs last year. But I at least made progress in this area.

It’s not about ranking higher than someone else. It’s about lifting my grandson up in the air, walking my daughter down the aisle, carrying the heavy luggage from the car and up the stairs. It’s walking across my yard as well as through the woods and across the sandy beach.

It’s about adding life to my years, as well as years to my life.

Don’t envy me by attributing to me qualities you think you lack. I lacked them too. How else did I get myself into such poor health in the first place. Just start. You make progress along the way. Even will power and discipline can grow and improve. If you start.

I look forward to hearing your own victory stories. It is possible to improve your health. It is hard, but you can do hard things. A little hard today and a little harder tomorrow.

p.s.  Always consult your own health team for your specific needs

p.p.s. Educate yourself to fully participate in your health strategy
