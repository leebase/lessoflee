It snuck up on me. I went to sleep last night as a high school graduate with a bright future ahead. I woke up this morning—aged 60! It’s been a wild ride, and it has gone by fast.

Thank you to everyone who has sent me well wishes for my birthday. It means a lot.

Every year, I tell my kids that all Dad wants for his birthday is a letter of encouragement. The time taken to share what I mean to my daughters is a priceless gift to me.

I’m well past throwing big parties, and while I’d love to have you all over to celebrate, that’s not in the cards. So instead, I have a request—one that I’m actually a little embarrassed to ask for, but it would mean the world to me.

If I have encouraged you, inspired you, or lifted your spirits, would you take the time to write and tell me the story?

I look forward to many more years, but turning 60 has put me in the mindset of legacy—what will my grandkids know about where they come from?

For me, hearing your stories would mean so much. And for them, it will be a window into their Paw Paw—hopefully, an inspiration for them, too.

Love you all,

Lee
