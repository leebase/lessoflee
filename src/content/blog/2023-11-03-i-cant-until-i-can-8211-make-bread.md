Normally these “Until I Can” posts are about reclaiming physical abilities. But – folks – I can now make bread from scratch! We have a “two for” here. One is that I can make bread at all. Two is that I can make bread a diabetic can eat! It’s also gluten free – bonus! (My daughter has celiac disease).

Over at Victoria’s Keto Kitchen she has a recipe for a keto flour. So not only am I making my bread, I’m making the flour from scratch. It’s not hard, but you do need a food scale and weird ingredients like bamboo fiber, almond flour and xanthum gum.

Once you’ve made the flour – you can use it for anything you’d use regular wheat flour for. So far I made Dream Biscuits – I wrote about them already. They were fantastic, and I love me a good biscuit. (In the USA, a biscuit is not a cookie, it’s a savory bread). I’ve also used the flour in making Liver and Onions, and for cube steak. It’s nice to have that old familiar texture and mouth feel versus using ground up pork rinds for “breading”.

It feels like white bread. It tastes like white bread. It’s perhaps a tiny bit more dense than bread. And, it’s almost all fiber. You will feel the “fiber effects” after eating this if you know what I mean.

There are about 5g net carbs for the entire loaf. Not for a slice, the whole loaf. You can get almost no carbs if you use pecan flour instead of almond flour.

A nice ham and cheese sandwich is my reward.

Next up, I’m going to try making a pizza crust from scratch using this flour.

The recipes and links to her videos are at [victoriasketokitchen.net](http://victoriasketokitchen.net)

A word about net verses total carbs. Most fibers are not digested and thus their carbs do not raise your blood sugar. However, so many companies can’t be trusted with what they put on the labels. Thus, foods marketed as “Keto friendly” are really just junk food. And thus, many folks will only go by the Total Carb count. In this case you know exactly what ingredients you are putting in. But, as always, test your bs an hour and two after you’ve eaten something new to find out how it affects you.

Xantum Gum – is made by having bacteria ferment sugar. The amount used is small, and it doesn't raise blood sugar as the bacteria have transformed the sugar like yeast do. It is needed to give the “gluten” springiness.
