What if OA is not mechanically based, not about overuse or injury, rather it springs from diet and lifestyle as most all metabolic diseases do? I have been puzzled to explain the improvement in my OA knees. Just how did I turn around a degenerative disease? I’ve gone from walking with a cane, having constant pain with an ever shrinking world – to walking easily, much lower pain without pain meds and creams, even progressing to running 5k's. I’ve been telling people that I made changes in diet, fasting and exercise to overcome type 2 diabetes. In doing so, it just happened to also greatly help my OA.

I speak somewhat authoritatively on diabetes-related topics even though I’m not a doctor. I have read 30 books on the topic the last few years. I haven’t found the equivalent of The Diabetes Code by Jason Fung for the topic of OA. Fung gave me the blueprint I followed that got me off all my meds and brought my blood sugar down from dangerously diabetic to normal. Ben Bickman's “Why We Get Sick” is an excellent book that teaches about the central role chronically elevated insulin has in a host of diseases, including diabetes.

I may not have found a book, but I did just come across an article: Osteoarthritis is not a death sentence... and the surprising reason why it occurs. ([https://simplavida.com/a-diagnosis-of-osteoarthritis-is-not-a-death-sentence/](https://simplavida.com/a-diagnosis-of-osteoarthritis-is-not-a-death-sentence/)). Unfortunately, most of the article is behind a pay wall.

Here is a PubMed article Metabolic syndrome-associated osteoarthritis ([https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28072592/)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28072592/).

Now I have a framework that explains my own personal experience. My own OA has most surely been metabolically related. This explains how working on my diabetes helped my OA.

I don’t know if all OA is related, but I’m certainly glad mine is. Why? Because I can do something about my metabolic health. Changing your diet is hard; exercise is hard – but at least I’m not helpless.

Perhaps the “OA is permanently degenerative” is as incorrect as “Type 2 diabetes is a progressively degenerative disease”.

I have clawed back my health and abilities bit by bit. In addition to cutting sugars and seed oils out of my diet I’ve pushed my body more and farther.

How much more? A little more.

How much farther? A bit farther.

Always.

And now I have a better understanding of why it’s been working for me. Hope others will benefit as well.

p.s. Always consult your own health team for your specific needs

p.p.s. Educate yourself and be a full participant in your health strategy
