I have embarked on my 12th monthly extended fast. I aim for 5 days, but do not always make it. 5 days, just water/coffee/tea (no sweeteners).

I’ve written before about the health benefits beyond fat loss and will link at the end.

I’ve noticed that these fasts help me reset my relationship with food. I’ve had some trouble since my daughter's wedding getting back on as strict of a way of eating. Then Thanksgiving…which I joyfully participated in all my favorites. 3 days of feasting.

So much late nite snacking has been going on. Where has my self control gone? And, of course, I’ve gained back weight I’d worked so hard to lose.

I’m a day and a half in. So far so good. I love how my bs falls into the normal range and just stays there. The ketones (an indication you are burning fat) really start kicking in by the end of day two. By day three my arthritic knees feel awesome as inflammation will be so much lower.

It is hard, though. No lie. It’s not the easiest thing I do. I have a long way to go to lose all of this fat. You have to “eat your fat”…that’s the only way you get rid of it. At my current rate I'm still a good year or more away from a healthy weight….and that’s after a year and a half of working on it.

I love the AFTER the fast part. I love how I’m feeling and looking. These fasts help remind me that I am in control. I don’t have to give into cravings. Hunger is a feeling, not an imperative. If I can say NO to food for 5 days, I can say NO to pasta or bread or any other carb laden food.

I’ve written before that you gain discipline by trying to do the things that need discipline before you have it. Discipline is built just like muscles are. I didn’t start with 5 day fasts and I haven’t succeeded every time I’ve tried. But the discipline has been building. And that has value for all in your life.
