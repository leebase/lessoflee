Look at that gut…so much more work to do.

Lost in my post on “focusing on the bad numbers and ignoring the good” – is that my cholesterol greatly improved.

What?! The guy eating all the red meat and eggs, sausage and lunch meats?

Yep. My total cholesterol is now in the normal range and my LDL improved from “very high” to “elevated”. It’s not that far away from normal.

Eating cholesterol does not cause high cholesterol.

Now – I can’t point to this or that as causal with any proof. The body is extremely complex. Trends matter more than individual readings.

I know that I have to continue doing the positive health lifestyle actions.
Low carbohydrate eating of whole natural foods
Fasting
Exercise – particularly strength training and HIIT
Sleep
Stress management

The numbers will reflect the improved health – in time.

My blood sugars continue to improve, though at a much slower rate.

My HDL is stubborn and my triglycerides are a mystery. But, that’s metabolic syndrome and it took decades to break my body and it’s not going to fix in a day.

Healing is happening. Don’t buy into the “diabetes is forever” disease mindset. Adopt a “eat healthy, move healthy, be healthy” mindset. Health isn’t forever. You have to work on it – always.
