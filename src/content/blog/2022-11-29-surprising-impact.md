I’m going to tell a story that happened to me, but I aim for it to be about you and your potential to do far more good than you can imagine. Even if you can’t see yourself that way because of this or that failure.

As happy with my own health progress as I am – I am acutely aware that I brought on my health problems. It’s as if I caused a fire, and then put half of the fire out…and people are saying to me “aren’t you so proud of yourself for putting out half a fire?”.

How can I help people become healthy when I’ve been so unhealthy? Who is going to listen to the fat guy talk about losing weight? The diabetic about healing from diabetes? Well…it just so happens…

George is a friend of mine from church. We've known each other for 20 years…but just in the fellowship. He’s seen me be fat, then lose 80lbs, and get fit. He then saw me regain all that weight, plus 25lbs. He saw that I was working on my health again and began reading my blog. He didn’t tell me this at the time.

He started implementing the intermittent fasting and low carb eating I wrote about. Again, didn’t tell me he was. He even got a Quest 2 VR system to work out! He loses a good deal of weight himself. But did he tell me? Nope.

Instead, he tells his friend, Dwayne. He starts reading the blog and intermittent fasting and he loses a good deal of weight. Did they tell me? Nyet.

They decide to start a podcast to help other folks, particularly those over 50 to improve their health via intermittent fasting, They call it Fun, Fit and Fasting and they invited me to come on as a guest.

During the show is when I learn that my friend was reading my blog, made improvements to his health, spread it to another friend who made great progress in his health….and they had started a blog to continue to help others.

How cool is that? It’s not “Lee is cool” – it’s that “you can improve your health and by sharing your journey, others can be inspired to improve theirs”. I’m still fat. I haven’t arrived. I’m no fitness magazine cover model. And you aren’t either. But you don’t have to be. Let your journey spark a fire in others.
