Working on putting together a fitness plan – going to finally get around to strength training. But before I work out the specifics of a program, I wanted to come to clarity on what my health goals for fitness are.

Strength Training Goals

It’s all about health. I have no sports or play goals at this point.

What are my health needs

Type 2 Diabetes

- Increase insulin sensitivity
- Increase metabolism
- Reduce obesity

OA Arthritis in Knees

- Increase stability
- Not fall when walking on uneven surfaces
- Walk down stairs normally and without rails or one step at a time
- Easily pick up something from the floor
- Ability to jump as in a jumping jack or jump rope
- Ability to jog
- Be able to mow lawn
- Be able to walk across grassy field

When I Get Old I Want To

- Not need a walker
- Not worry about falling
- Continue to live independent

Body composition

- Lose fat
- Build muscle In arms, chest and legs
- Nice abs
- V-shape
