Sunday is weigh in day.  Earlier in the week I had peeked, given in and weighed myself.  I had broken the 300lb barrier at 299.6.  But alas, at weigh in day, I'm at 300.8 – still, I'm headed in the right direction.  Ooops...I'm burying the lead...the big news is that I lost 1&#8243; around my belly.  I felt that my belly was smaller and that my face appeared a tad thinner.  I had chalked it up to wishful thinking.  Another milestone is that my BMI level went from "morbidly obese" to merely "obese".

I've been working from home these past two weeks.  I'm off to Milwaukee for the next three.  It will be a challenge being away from home.
