Fasting is done, feasting has begun.

Scrambled eggs with bell peppers, mushrooms and cheese.

What do -I- mean by “feast”?

A feast (my label) is not “a huge amount of food”. A feast is plenty of delicious, nutrient dense, good for you food that you look forward to eating. That you enjoy eating. That you get to eat until your BODY says “that’s enough, thank you”.

And then you are full and satisfied for many hours later. Not tempted to eat what is bad for you.

That’s what a feast is. A better, more satisfying, leading to more success – strategy superior to that of “eat everything in moderation, portion control, lots of small meals and snacks – and be hungry all the time”.

Think in terms of your daily energy intake, not just the meal. 2 feasts, no or minimal snacks.
