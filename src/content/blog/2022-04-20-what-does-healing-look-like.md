There is a perennial debate over T2 Diabetes being cured/reversed/put in remission. I think the nut of it is the notion that “for diabetes to be cured, you’d get to eat all the highly processed carb laden foods like you used to and be fine”. Nobody believes this is true – ergo – we are “diabetic forever”.

That’s simply a mistaken mental model. You can heal – but it’s back to your own starting point (at best). You don’t get a metabolism you never had.

For example – I had biscuits and gravy, a plate full, for Easter. I had a small desert and a few chips. But that was it. My bs went up to 180 and was back down about 2 hours later. That’s a normal person’s response.

Last spring my bs was averaging 260 and I couldn’t get my bs under 200 for weeks. And that was with taking diabetes meds.

I have had some measure of healing. What I can’t do is eat a basket of Easter candy, have a big carb laden Easter meal, with a couple Cokes – and expect a normal person’s sugar response.

If I return to eating like that – I’ll RETURN to being a diabetic. But I’m not going to return to that – and I’m not going to RETURN to being a diabetic.

Being someone who is unable to handle the Standard American Diet and remain healthy — that is a permanent condition.

I wasn’t a diabetic at 10yrs old, or 20, or 30, or 40. I couldn’t handle sugar during that whole time either. I wasn’t YET diabetic but my eating was turning me diabetic.

And now my eating has cured my “state of uncontrolled blood sugar”. My bs is controlled below the diabetic level by my lifestyle. I am thus — not currently a diabetic.

You can heal. Yes, you can. If you start working on it soon enough. Clearly, if you’ve had your foot amputated, diet isn’t going to bring it back. So don’t wait.
