Went to the John Ball Zoo this weekend. All hills and stairs. I sat down a few times but had little problem enjoying the outing with my wife’s family. Should have added “get to participate in life” to my list of “why I exercise”.

On my mind this morning is “how much change do I need to do”? How many carbs can I eat? How much exercise? How long to fast?

The answer is – as much you need to get the results you desire. Someone can just eat in moderation and their diabetes resolves. Someone else loses 3 times the amount of weight you have. You can’t go by what someone else does for their results.

We can learn from each other and be inspired by each other. Ultimately, we have to take those inspirations and apply them to our own bodies. We must keep on keeping on to find out what we have to do to regain our health – to the extent possible.

I find fasting a powerful tool for me. If I could get the results without fasting – I would. If I could regain health without exercising – I would.
I’m working so much harder for such slower results than 10 years ago.

But – I don’t have a choice to start with the body I had 10 years ago. I am where I am and figuring out what I have to do NOW. I can’t choose to have your body either and you can’t choose to have mine.

We offer support and encouragement to help motivate and educate each other to do whatever has to be done…for our own goals.
