Time for this month’s five day water only fast. Making slow progress on weight loss, but slow progress over time still adds up.

No baby going to pop out of me…I have to consume the fat I’ve accumulated. When fasting, that’s what’s happening. You aren’t starving. You aren’t getting malnourished. You are consuming the food your body put away to keep you going when there is no food.

Only there was always food. Too much food for too many years.

So now I am going to eat my own fat for a few days.

I wrote a series on the benefits of extended fasting: [https://lessoflee.wordpress.com/extended-fasts/](https://lessoflee.wordpress.com/extended-fasts/)
