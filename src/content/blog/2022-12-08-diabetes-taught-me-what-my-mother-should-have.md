None of us are born with Type 2 diabetes. I was born with the potential to develop type 2 diabetes by eating the type of food known as the Standard American Diet.

And that’s how I did eat for decades. You couldn’t tell the harmful effects throughout my youth and into my twenties. By my thirties I had a pot belly, love handles, a dad bod. In my forties I was obese and then type 2 diabetic sometime in my 50s. A progression that did not have to occur.

For more than a year now I have ceased being a type 2 diabetic. This claim bothers some people. “Once a diabetic, always a diabetic”. Utter nonsense, of course.

“If you eat like you used to, you’ll be diabetic”.

True.

“So, you are still a diabetic”

False, and nonsense. It makes no sense WHEN you realize that it’s the FOOD that is the disease. The food is sick and I remain well by not eating that kind of food.

That food was NEVER the food I should have been eating. It’s manufactured garbage whose sole purpose is to be tasty, addictive, cheap to make, and profitable to sell. It’s never been health food.

There are foods that we don’t like. What that is differs among us. Nobody feels diseased because they don’t eat Brussels sprouts or liver. If you don’t like something, you just don’t eat it.

Why must I label myself as diseased for refraining to eat food that will make me ACTUALLY diseased? The food that made me diabetic in the first place can make me diabetic again. So what? Just don’t eat that food any more.

Learn to like the food that makes you healthy. This isn’t a travesty or an injustice. Diabetes taught me what my mother should have taught me all along. Eat good for you food. Meat, fish and non-starchy veggies (veggies optional).

I know, easier said than done. Would I rather go blind or lose a foot? No. I am no longer a diabetic and I plan to stay that way. How? Because diabetes “lives in the food not in my body”. I can control what I eat,
