There are benefits to this form of training and I used to do sprints in a HIIT fashion. But with my arthritic knees…running of any kind is currently not an option.

I stumbled onto this device at my health club. It’s fantastic. Even with my knees I can do this. And man – does it kick my @ss. Feels awesome to be able to work out like this. And HIIT workouts do not take much time.

I can’t…until I can is one of my mantras. Just because I can’t do something today doesn’t mean it’s out of reach forever. One of the keys it to keep pressing on, keep taking that next step up.

I love walking. When I started back in this journey last April, walking a half hour was a good thing. It still is…but it is not enough. Then I walked 45min, then an hour, then an hour and a half. And man are my knees killing me when I get back to the house and have to climb a few stairs.

And then I added aerobics with the Supernatural app on the Oculus. And dancing workouts with my wife. And 6 weeks ago I started strength training. And now I'm adding HIIT.

It’s been 10 months and counting, What I CAN do now is so much more. One more “can” in the bag. I found a way to include HIIT into my routine,
