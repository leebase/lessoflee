The gate is meant to baby proof the house. And it does. My high energy toddling grandson is thwarted by the gate. You know who else is?

Pawpaw is also stymied by the gate! Well, with great difficulty I do manage to step over this monstrosity from the netherworld.

Sure I’ve gone from walking with a cane, struggling to go up and down stairs to running sprint intervals. I walk miles every day. I squat two tons of weight in a workout. I carry the heavy things in from the car and up to the third floor apartment.

But see me struggle to get up off the floor, out of a chair or over this gate and you will know – I have a lot more healing and strengthening to do.

This is why I did the work to get off diabetes meds and off of daily pain killers, lotions and potions. It isn’t about some badge of honor to be med free. It’s about regaining health and abilities.

I was 56 and already old, diseased and disabled, limited and in pain and more serious complications were next up.

I’m 59 and that’s older but I’m younger in so many ways. I’m not at “healthy” but I am certainly “less unhealthy”. I struggle stepping over this gate but I have reclaimed so many abilities and life is much better for it.

I haven’t arrived – the journey continues. I have more to do.

How much more? A little more.

How far? A bit farther.

Always.

3 years and counting in my health recovery journey. A lifetime more to go.

p.s.  Always consult your own health team for your specific needs

p.p.s. Educate yourself to fully participate in your health strategy
