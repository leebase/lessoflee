This was my last off plan meal and I’m back on track. It brings up the topic of fruit. How can fruit be bad for you?

Well…my cgm (continuous glucose monitor) let me know that even this reasonable amount of fruit raised my bs quite a bit. Fruit is “natures candy”. We all love it because it tastes so sweet.

It’s a matter of context. Lots of food that is generally healthy isn’t a good choice for someone with blood sugar control problems. We Type 2 Diabetics.

Is pineapple better than a candy bar? Sure. And that’s what you tell children … eat your fruit, don’t eat junk food. And that’s good advice.

BUT even as a former type 2 diabetic….I am not where I need to be to handle the very sugary fruits. So for me – such fruit is NOT healthy. Fruit doesn’t have an exclusive on vitamins…you can get your vitamins from non-starchy veggies…and from meat. Yes, meat is actually quite nutritious and not just about the protein.

You can see from this graph of my bs when, today, I got back on plan. It was well after this last off plan meal. And a good reminder why I have changed what I eat.
