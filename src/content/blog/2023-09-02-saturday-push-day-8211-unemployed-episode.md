As happens in my career, I’m out of work as of yesterday. What better time to go out and push my body, work out my stress and frustration.

Dealing with unemployment, for me, is quite a bit like what I experience working on my health. There is worry, there is sadness, self recrimination, anger. All the feels. The solution is also similar. Put the past behind you, and do what you can today. My career, also like my health, has it’s share of ups and downs.

I wake up after a fitful night and just go for a run. Push day’s are about getting my heart rate up around 95% of max, and for me, running is the easiest way to do that. I’m working on my speed – which is very slow. I want to get to a 10 minute mile. Right now I’m just under 14 minutes. Next up 13, then 12, 11 and 10. It doesn’t matter how long it takes or who can get there before I do. It was only a couple yeas ago I walked with a cane and drove with a handicap sticker. I’m pushing back boundaries and reclaiming abilities a little bit at a time.

Every day is a battle of some sort – or so it seems. I’m much happier fighting to run and sprint rather than struggling just to walk up and down the stairs in my house.

My daughter needed help moving this past week. She didn’t plan on needing me, but “life happens” and I was able to help move into her THIRD floor apartment. I wasn’t helping with the furniture, but made many trips up and down those stairs.

That’s why I am pushing my body. I don’t run to run. I run to get healthy to be able to do the things in life that matter.

I sure hope many of you will join me in turning around your own health. You get there by doing more and going farther.

How much more? A little more.

How much farther? A bit farther.

Always.

ps: as always consult your own health team. For the life of me, I can’t fathom why this warning needs to be there, but just in case. Don’t be stupid, don’t assume what applies to a stranger on the internet fits your exact needs. Yadda yadaa. Yeah…but also don’t JUST listen to doctors who are content to give you a pill and keep you sick. Take control. Healing is possible. Better to at least try.
