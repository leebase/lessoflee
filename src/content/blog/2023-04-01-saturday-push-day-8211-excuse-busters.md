Well, folks, I woke up today with a backpack full of excuses.

My body ached from Thursday's city escapade – walking 5 miles on unforgiving concrete, climbing ballpark stairs, and dancing to the band outside. Sure, I'm reclaiming my abilities despite osteoarthritis, but boy, did I pay the price in aches and pains. Excuse #1: I'm sore and tired.

Kids came home for the weekend? Excuse #2: Distractions galore.

Donated blood earlier today and was told not to break a sweat. Excuse #3: Doctor's orders.

So, I cozied up in my recliner for a sports marathon, hours ticking by since my blood donation. A power nap later, I confronted my excuses head-on. It was time to show 'em who's boss.

I geared up with my Meta Quest 2 VR headset, fired up Supernatural, and decided that while I might not push as hard as usual, today was still Push Day.

Miraculously, soreness faded as I tackled two high-intensity workouts back-to-back. Four songs from the finish line, I wanted to throw in the towel. But that's when I knew I was truly getting my Push Day in. So, I dug deep, ramped up my effort for the final two tracks, and hit heart rates in the 150s.

The entire day, I doubted I'd squeeze in a tough workout. I had my excuses lined up, ready to postpone the grind 'til tomorrow. But why delay what I could conquer today?

The initial push of this Saturday was shoving excuses aside and rising from the recliner.

Victorious weeks like this aren't born from one grand gesture. They're the culmination of days spent pushing past excuses, showing up for ourselves, and putting in the work. Striving for more and reaching farther.

How much more? A little more.

How much farther? A bit farther.

Always.
