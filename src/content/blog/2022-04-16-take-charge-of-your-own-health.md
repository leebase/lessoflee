You should trust your doctor! OMG, if only that were as wise as it should be. But it isn’t.

Ask your doctor “what is your plan for me to get off all my diabetes meds, including insulin”…a plan you’ve successfully helped many patients achieve.

If they have a plan, great. You have a great doctor.

If they tell you “diabetes is a progressively degenerative disease, you’ll be on these meds for life” – then you might need a new doctor.

That’s the situation I found myself in last April. On Metformin, Glipizide and Trulicity. Scheduled to go on insulin.

But I read The Diabetes Code by Dr Jason Fung and took control of my own health journey.

Now I’m off all the meds, my A1c has gone from 11.2 on the meds to 5.8 without any. And is continuing to improve.

And I luckily found a new doctor who agrees with Fung and works to help me help myself to get healthy rather than just prescribe meds.

I wish “trust your doctor” advice was wise. Unfortunately my old “meds forever doctor” was the one following the official guidelines. Don’t put your health in someone’s hands who spends 15minutes of time with you once or twice a year to prescribe you pills that help you get sicker and die…just a bit slower.

Aim for and gain health. It’s harder than taking a pill and “trusting your doctor”. It’s so worth it.
