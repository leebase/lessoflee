Oh the joy of ”clothes shopping in my closet”. Clearly I’m not yet able to wear my favorite [http://www.dad.com](http://www.dad.com) sweat shirt. And the shorts go into the ”donate pile”.

The Nike hoodie and jeans – I can wear again!

I can’t wear any of my clothes from last year (50lbs lost). I’m into my 42” pants – with some ability to wear 44” — but the 48” are in the donate pile.

I have a nice pair of jeans waiting for me to get to 40”.

The 3x-Large Talls are in the donate pile. I am into the 2x and 2x Tall.

The XL and the merely Large – will have to wait awhile longer ![😎](~/assets/posts/2022-03-11-clothes-shopping-from-my-closet/1f60e.png)
