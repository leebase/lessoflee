With 10” of snow on the ground and wind gusts of 35mph, it’s a great day to work out from home. While it can be nice to go to a gym and it is certainly wonderful to walk or run outside – I love having options for working out in my own home.

In my 40s, I did the Power 90 program at home using resistance bands. They are fantastic for strength training, particularly for a beginner like me. Two years ago I started working out with the Quest virtual reality headset and Supernatural app. I love it. It transforms my living room into the world’s most scenic views.

This year, in addition to Supernatural, I’m doing strength training with a pair of adjustable dumbbells and a weight bench. I had a friend recommend the StrongLifts.com 5&#215;5 program. It’s a great for beginners.

It’s so much more time efficient to exercise at home. I don’t have to get dressed, drive to a gym, sign in and load my coat and items in a locker. 30 minutes after deciding to work out, I’m done and recovering with my kitty purring on my chest.

I can play my own music, sing and shout, grunt and scream. I don’t care what I look like and the only pretty girl hitting on me is my own wife. 

Sometimes going somewhere can help. The year before last I went to The Exercise Coach twice a week. I loved that program and the $200/mo I paid was motivation to go. After a year I needed a break from the payments – and proceeded to NOT do any strength training for the next year.

There is no one best way for all people all the time. I simply “problem solve”. When I was walking with my cane and looking for something to do at home during the winter, I found Supernatural. To do strength training I needed a way to do so at home where I don’t have the space (or budget) for an extensive gym setup. My bench folds up against a wall and the PowerBlock adjustable dumbbells are compact. I’m already exploring options for what to do when I need more than the 50lbs my set goes to.

There are a lot of options for working out at home with little or even no equipment and not much space. You can find your own joy and regain your own health and abilities.

Finding ways to work out from home has been a key component allowing me to do more and go farther.

How much more? A little more.

How much farther? A bit farther.

Always.

p.s.  Always consult your own health team for your specific needs

p.p.s. Educate yourself to fully participate in your health strategy
