I’m drivin’ in my car…du du du du…I turn on the radio. ![🙂](~/assets/posts/2022-06-26-fire-du-du-du-du/1f642.png)

It’s morning of day four and the fat burning remains in high gear.  There were a couple times yesterday when it was hard.  I pressed on by reminding myself that hunger does go away.  And it does.    And then comes back.  Still, I did all this hard work to get to the fat burning (and everything else, see the referenced post)…so I want to let it burn.

Bs 84

Breath Ketones 41 (10 is optimal ketosis)

Blood Ketones 2.8

GKI 1.6 – High therapeutic level:  For those using keto therapeutically for the treatment of diseases such as cancer, epilepsy,

Alzheimer's disease, Parkinson's disease, traumatic brain injury, and chronic inflammatory disease

Weight 264.6 

Down 11.6 lbs for the fast.

Down 21.9 for the month

Down 71 lbs from the start of this journey

Woohoo!   Vision of what is to come.  2 days after the fast is over, a good deal of that is coming back.  It’s water – so it doesn’t matter.  I have lost less weight on this fast than the others I’ve done.  Which isn’t surprising as being on a near zero carb diet for the 3 weeks prior, I’d already lost a good deal of water weight.

The stall is definitely broken.  I’m able to wear this shirt that was given to me last father’s day.  I had to hold the shirt up for the photo because it was too small for me to wear.
