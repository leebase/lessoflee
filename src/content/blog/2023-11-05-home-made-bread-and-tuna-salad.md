Enjoying the bread I made following VictoriasKetoKitchen.net recipe. It’s less than a carb per slice, tastes and feels 90% like normal white bread. Also gluten free.

Next time I’m going to add more yeast. The recipe calls for a table spoon and I just put in 1 packet without measuring to see if it was the right amount.

For the tuna salad I used a quality tuna, Primal Kitchen avocado Mayo, mustard and diced jalapeño. A little salt and pepper to taste. I don’t measure the Mayo or mustard, just put in enough for it to be creamy and tangy.

The bread is a real “fiber bomb”. My wife confirmed the “bathroom effects” that I had mentioned. So, instead of a second sandwich, I made a plate of little cucumber treats. Sliced cucumber, salt and pepper, cheese and lettuce. Tasty,

If you don’t want to make bread, the cucumber treats are a great way to eat tuna salad.
