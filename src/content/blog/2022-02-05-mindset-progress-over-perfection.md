Quick and easy stir fry. I cut up chicken breasts, and left over steak. Browned in the wok and added a bag of frozen stir fry veggies. I also added some sliced mushrooms.

I used a store bought sauce. There are better ways for sure, but in the grand scheme of things “the dose is the poison” and it’s a lot of meat and veggies and not so much “lots of things wrong with it” sauce.

Don’t discount the value of “pretty d@mn good” verses perfection in terms of making progress with your health. There is always something somebody can find genuine “you could do this better by….”. And yet, I feel there is value in demonstrating you can make progress long before perfection is reached. In fact, I believe many people are intimidated and discouraged by the very nature of “each and every good protocol that is best to follow” such that they don’t follow anything for long.
