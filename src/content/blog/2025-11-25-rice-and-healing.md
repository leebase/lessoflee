Can we T2D heal?  Yes, yes we can.  Completely?  Does that matter more than this – every step along the healing path is bringing life, vitality and ability. One step at at time, one foot in front of the other – making progress.

Below is the result of my rice test.  A mere 50 days ago my A1c was 7.1 – an average glucose reading in the 150s.  My glucose average now is in the 90s, a non-diabetic level.  So, is that some amount of healing or simply having avoided eating carbs?  It’s both.

I ate a bowl of rice, 150g cooked white rice, with some butter and cheese.  White rice should sky rocket glucose but look at my graph as I’m wearing a continuous glucose monitor. Peak of 139 and then back down.  That’s healing.  That shows my pancreas can create insulin and my cells will respond to the signal.

Now the slow come down – that’s physiological IR from having been running on fat with minimal carbs for 50 days.  Your body can run on either fat or carbs but when you run primarily on one or the other – the pathways for the one not being used down regulate.  This is natural and they spin back up – just not instantly.

Is this complete healing?  Of course not – it took decades of effort to build my state of disease.  This was but one bowl of rice in isolation.  It’s evidence of progress – wonderful life bringing progress.

All health needs to be maintained.  T2D is vastly an input problem.  Change the inputs and your body heals.  Go back to the food that harms you – you redevelop the disease.

I write this from the waiting room for blood work.  Will know soon my new A1c and whether carnivore is wrecking my cholesterol.
