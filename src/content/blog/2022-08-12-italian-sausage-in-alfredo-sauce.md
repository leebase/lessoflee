Mmmm – delicious Italian sausage in Alfredo sauce with some jalapeño and Serrano peppers. Spicy!

Air fried the sausages on 360F for 12 minutes.

Put a half can of Rao’s Alfredo sauce in a pan in low times to be heated up about the time the sausages were done.

Took the sausages out of the air fryer and sliced and put in the sauce to get all coated.

Then onto a plate and into my tummy.
