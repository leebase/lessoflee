I haven’t shared meals much of late. I am mostly ketovore these days. Close to carnivore but with spices and occasional veggies.

Top left was an experiment. Cod liver, sardines and cheddar cheese on a Parmesan cheese “cracker” (no flour, just cheese). This was my first time trying sardines. Not bad, I thought they’d be nasty – but they taste a bit like salmon. It must not be too bad as I went back and ate all of the tin. Hot sauce made it better.

Top right is a steak cooked in the air fryer. I mashed up an avacado and my wife ad sautéed some mushrooms and onions.

Bottom left is home made tuna salad on a chaffle (cheese waffle). It’s a favorite for both my wife and I. I’ll link to the recipe.

Bottom right is “use up leftovers” meal. Couple Wendy’s hamburger patty’s with our own cheese. Some left over ground beef topped with cheese salsa and peppers. And a little bit of Kimchi (a spicy fermented veggie dish from Korea). Just a mish mash of leftovers.

All meals very low in carbs.
