Supernatural (the VR workout app I use) has an Artist Series and they just released their Metallica workouts! It was so fun. These VR aerobics workouts have been my mainstay for over two years. This year I’m prioritizing muscle building so I’ve been contemplating how the two types of exercise can be used together most effectively.

Do they conflict? Well, they can. Excessive aerobic training can shift the body's physiological adaptations towards endurance, optimizing for energy efficiency and endurance capacity, rather than the hypertrophy (muscle growth) typically sought in strength training. In addition, one of the important principles is the need for recovery after lifting.

So, here is my current plan. I do my lifting first on Monday, Wednesday and Friday. AFTER that workout, I do a Supernatural workout. That way I lifted when my muscles were fresh. I did the M level Metallica workout after lifting Monday, and a shorter H workout (also a blast) on Wednesday. I may or may not do a SN workout on my “rest” days.

I am listening to how my body feels after. How sore am I? When it comes time to lift next, do I feel ready? There is no right answer for every one nor the same right answer all the time for anyone.

My sleep has been improved since I started working out. It improved more when I started lifting. Getting good sleep is essential and this is not an area where I excel.

I started with the absolute lightest weight for my strength training program. I am just now getting into what I feel is heavy for me. I may reduce the aerobics when the lifting itself becomes much more intense. Again, I’ll play it by ear.

Still, one of the most important principles is to do what you enjoy. And I really enjoy my Supernatural workouts. Doing them after lifting also has the benefit of raising up my heart rate. I’ve been “suffering” from improved fitness. It’s harder for my SN workouts to push my heart rate up into zone 5.

I am keeping my Saturday Push Day’s. Until I can go outside and run, I’ll be using SN with a weight vest to get my heart rate up. They have a new Pro’s Only Metallica workout that I’m saving for this Saturday!

Today has been all rest. It’s doing my body good.

Hope you did your own body good today.

p.s.  Always consult your own health team for your specific needs

p.p.s. Educate yourself to fully participate in your health strategy
