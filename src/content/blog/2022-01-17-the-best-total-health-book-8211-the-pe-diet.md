Just finished The P:E Diet by Ted Naiman. It may well be the best “total health” book I’ve ever read.

The Diabetes Code by Dr Jason Fung is still the first book to be sure to read as a diabetic. I’d make The P:E Diet the second.

I really wish that “diet” wasn’t in the title. It's really not a diet book but a “how to optimize health” book. He has powerful insights into metabolic syndrome which he calls “energy toxicity”. Ever wonder how low fat calorie restriction diets AND low carb, high fat ad libitum diets can both put diabetes into remission? His theory explains it.

He then does go into diet – and why we should emphasize eating foods with high protein to energy ratio.

Then he goes into some recipes and meal planning.

And then he covers exercise.

Total health. My eating is already in line with his principles…but I was happy to learn from his thoughts on fitness.
