The opposite of an NSV brought to you by the incompetent sales staff of Menswearhouse

My daughter is getting married – time to buy a blazer to go on this 74lb less version of me.

Apparently, I haven’t lost enough weight for the staff at the Mens Wearhouse to fit me. It’s been years since I’ve tried to buy from them. I came away feeling like shit the last time too.

The beautiful 4 inch nails the saleslady had apparently has addled her brain. She couldn’t guess my size like any salesperson should. She asked me twice even though I told her my former size wasn’t relevant. She had a tape measure and STILL couldn’t size me.

After three “not even close” jackets she had me try on – I just left. It’s one thing to not be able to measure – but you have to be able to see that the shoulders didn’t fit or the belly didn’t fit and why on earth did you even have me try the “fitted, tapered in” version? Can you not see my gut?

I’m pretty sure they have Made to Measure – or they used to. It didn’t occur to her to recommend.

“You are going to like the way you look” used to be their motto. It should be “we’re going to make you feel fat, ugly and unloved and STILL not find you a jacket”.

15 minutes after I got home I had a custom tailored jacket ordered via the MTailor app.

Mens Going Out Of Business Wearhouse – I won’t shed a tear. I used to shop there all the time.
