How much? A little more.

How Far? A little farther.

These two come between: I can't until I can.

Steady progress, not great leaps. Walk before you crawl. Crawl before you run. Run before you fly.

You need to do a little more, always. How much more? A little more.

Enough more that you kick off processes of change in the body. If you do the same thing over and over your body will adjust and become very efficient at doing that thing. Nothing you do carries the same benefit forever that it does at the start.

How far? A little farther.

You have no idea how far you can go because you can’t judge by where you are.

It wasn’t that long ago I was celebrating my first couple of steps with both feet in the air at the same time. Just a couple steps and it was a victory. It filled my eyes with tears with the promise of what might be possible some day.

Today I just ran intervals. My crazy crouch-run style, but I got my heart rate into the 150s. Again and the again and then again.

How? By going a bit farther.
