The recovery of abilities, the growing of my small world – continues. I have gone from not being able to walk across the yard, to playing badminton.

I have reclaimed my title as king of the family badminton. My youngest's boyfriend beat me in the first match yesterday, but I came back today and reclaimed the title.

If I could move like days of old, I’d have crushed him. I’ll just have to settle for being ABLE to play for now.

One day, I’ll regain tennis!
