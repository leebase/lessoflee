OMG – people – I have discovered “diabetic” friendly, keto biscuits that taste like regular biscuits. Gluten free and only 1 carb per biscuit.

Now, I know my biscuits. With my southern roots, biscuits and gravy is one of my favorite foods. But you need flour for the gravy and the biscuits. Nothing I’ve ever made with almond flour tasted anything like the original.

This keto flour is a one for one replacement for any dish that needs flour. I’ve only tried these biscuits, but I can vouch for this recipe. Amazing. I can’t wait to make bread…and I’ve never made bread in my life.

The hardest thing is getting the ingredients: bamboo fiber, almond flour, allulose baking mix, and xanthum gum. And you have to measure by weight.

So, it’s a bit out of my ordinary, but really wasn’t difficult. Just weigh and mix.

I’ve made biscuits from scratch before but never “Dream Biscuits”. These were very easy to make.

A chaffle is much easier to make but these biscuits are in a league of their own. Can’t wait to try my sausage gravy with this flour and heavy cream instead of milk.
