Eww, eat your fat? Sounds disgusting. And yet, that’s really what you desire to do when you need to lose weight. The only weight of concern is the excess fat. And the only way to lose the fat is to get your body to consume it for energy. Thus, you need to eat your fat.

I’m 69 hours into my monthly 5 day fast. 5 days without food…how could anybody do that? It makes more sense when you realize that you stored fat away to supply your needs in times of no food. When you eat, some of it you use right away, some goes to construction of muscles and bones, and the rest is stored away. This is a simplification but gives the general idea.

Every night as you sleep you keep on living because your body dips into some of the excess energy you ate the day before. But there is more than just a night’s worth of storage. Your liver will store 12 hours to a day or so of energy and release it as glucose. We have a lot more storage in the form of fat. It’s for the same reason, to keep you going when you aren’t eating. Some of us have added way too much of this fat in storage. But the purpose of that fat has remained the same, to supply you with the fuel to run your body when your needs aren’t being met by what you’ve just eaten.

What about exercise? Exercise just increases your need for energy. Exercise is another way to have your body burn fat but is a relatively small percentage of the energy your body spends just keeping you going. In a sense, exercise and fasting are doing the same thing – putting your body in a state that it needs to dip into the fat stores to supply energy.

Won’t you starve? I did some rough calculations. I have approximately 82lbs/37kilos of fat. I need to lose about 42lbs/19kilos of fat to get to a healthy weight. When fasting you burn about a half pound of fat a day. I could fast for 84 days in a row and still have another 80 days of stored energy in my body. You can’t starve when you have excess fat. You are merely, finally, eating the fat that your body had stored away for that purpose.

I am not going to fast for 84 days but it does put my 5 day fasts in perspective. I don’t view my fasts as “going without food”, they are “finally getting around to eating the fat”. There are many marvelous health benefits to extended fasting that I’ve written about and will link to at the end.

What about that hunger? If it wasn’t for hunger, fasting would be quite easy and everyone would do it. I tell you from experience, it’s exactly like exercise. You build fasting muscles. What is impossible for you to do on day one gets easier over time. I’ve been intermittent fasting for 2 years and extended fasting for more than a year. I have built up fasting muscles just like I’ve gone from walking with a cane to running a 5k.

It’s such a powerful tool in one’s arsenal for both fat loss and reversing type 2 diabetes. You have to eat your fat to lose it anyway. Fasting just gets you there quicker.
