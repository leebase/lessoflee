Last night was a good sleep night for me. I’ve been working on improving sleep. It’s very important to your health and directly related to insulin resistance and obesity.

Sometimes I may only get 4 or 5 hours of sleep. I’m in the middle of a potential job change and that has upped my stress levels. In general, though, I am not a good sleeper.

I have dabbled with some herbal remedies and melatonin – but have nothing to recommend. In general, it’s a lot better to gain health without meds or supplements. So even though I continue to try this and that – for the most part I’m working on basic sleep hygiene.

Exercise
Shitting down the Tv and social media an hour before bed (a weakness of mine)
Turning off the lights an hour before bed
Reading
Keeping the house temp low
Keeping the bedroom dark

There is more to do with sleep hygiene that I haven’t got to. I find it hard to wind down. I can’t turn off all electronics and the tv (should say “won’t”).

I still get up 3 or 4 times a night to pee. At this point I think that is as much psychological as it is physical. I go back to sleep quickly. It’s the turning off of my mind that is my biggest hurdle.

One “sleep sin” I do is read my fb posts and replies when I get up to pee. Certainly doesn’t help with the whole “turn off the mind” part. On the other hand, I'm much more in tune with the Aussie participants than I would be.

As always, I'm a work in progress.
