Did I miss the window to make my New Year's Resolutions? Good…because every day I start again. People joke about how long before they’ve broken their resolutions. I fail too…all the time. But then I get back on it. Every day is the day to build your resolve, your grit.

18 months ago I resolved to reclaim my health. My T2 diabetes had gotten much worse (A1c 11.2) and I was scheduled to go on insulin. I resolved to finally get about making the changes and reclaiming my health.

I have written extensively about my journey. My A1c is 5.5 (normal) and I'm off all the diabetes meds and never went on insulin. I’ve gone from walking with a cane to running a 5k. I’m about 70lbs lighter and far more fit.

So…what to do this year? I think many of you know what I'm going to say.

I'm going to do more and go farther.

How much more? A little more

How far? A bit farther

Always

Grand visions and gestures are nice but I think they can demotivate as well as inspire. Drastic improvement doesn’t happen in a day. I strive to make a little progress – all the time in the following areas:

How and what I eat

Fasting

Exercise (walking, aerobics, strength training)

Mindset

Sleep

Stress management

I don’t work on all 6 at the same time. I make progress, build habits and then move on to one of the other areas.

I've gained back more weight that I’d want over the holidays. I loosened up too much, indulged too far, too long. Ok…that’s behind me and I'm pressing forward.

I’m doing carnivore (ish) for the month of January and maybe beyond. I had my best health and weight results last June when I did carnivore for the first time.

I only ate one meal today. I will do that more often. Fasting has been a huge lever in my health recovery. I will stick to my monthly extended fasts and strive to hit that 5 days each month.

I am considering switching up my strength training. I currently am part of a twice a week group session that runs $200/month. It’s been very beneficial but I think Im ready to just go to a gym and lift weights.

I will start working on my 5k time and running form. I have no further ambitions at this point. All I need to do is push my body a little more.

And in all the other areas….learn more….make progress.

No grand gestures. Just consistency.
