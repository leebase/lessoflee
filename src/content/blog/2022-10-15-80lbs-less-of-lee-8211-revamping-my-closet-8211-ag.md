I hit a nice milestone – 80lbs less of Lee.  That was how much I lost the last time…and then regained it all plus 25.   I was able to lose 80lbs in 10 months when I was in my mid 40’s.  This time it’s taken 18 months.  This time I was a type 2 diabetic with osteoarthritis in my knees.  This time – I was in my mid 50s.   I have probably 6 months more or longer to go to get to my goal weight of 215.

With this weight loss comes another complete overhaul of my wardrobe.    Last time I gave away all my fat clothes because “I’ll never be fat again”.  Oh the pain of not only regaining the weight, but having to rebuy all my clothes, and again and again every 25lbs or so.  At least I kept my clothes as I fatted out of them and so I went “closet shopping”.  You can see in the photo the pile of clothes that are too big for me to wear.   Parting is such sweet sorrow.

But then – there are my favorite clothes like the sweater in the photo that I loved but got too fat to wear.  Now I can wear them again.   I have another whole set of clothes even smaller to continue to lose weight to get into them.

I’ve given all of my fat clothes away again.  I never seem to learn ![😉](~/assets/posts/2022-10-15-80lbs-less-of-lee-8211-revamping-my-closet-8211-ag/1f609.png)

No going back – no going back.
