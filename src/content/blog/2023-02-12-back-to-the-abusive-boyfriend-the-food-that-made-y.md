The Standard American Diet isn't a sustainable solution for health. It's not the diets you stop following that have failed, it's the food that made you unhealthy in the first place.

Highly processed foods are engineered for profit, not for our health. They hit our pleasure points and encourage overconsumption. The food we love has been bad for us all along. It's not diabetes that's the problem, it's the food that gave us diabetes.

There's no way to eat that food and remain healthy. Choose to fight for health or fight illness with the same abusive food.

All while we debate over which diet is better and challenge each one with “that isn’t sustainable”. I will keep fighting because I’m worth it. When I go back, I get fat and sick again.

Some have taken their pot shots at me already. It was my fasting. No, it’s keto/low carb/carnivore – they aren’t sustainable. I don’t fight the diet wars. I cheer on people who improves their health whether they be vegan or Mediterranean or “everything in moderation”. If you find a way that restores your health….I’m right there cheering you on.

It’s not we who are fighting to improve our health that are the problem. It’s the Standard American Diet. Everywhere across the world, every civilization modern western industrial food is introduced…chronic disease follows.
