I am a man who likes convenience. Love my smart phone. Love ordering groceries and having them delivered. Love shopping on Amazon. When it comes to my body, though, I am learning to embrace “hard”.

It’s Saturday and on Saturdays I try to push my boundaries by doing something as hard as I can manage at the time. I want to get my heart rate up near max. I want my muscles to almost fail. I want to do something hard enough that I want to quit – but I don’t quit.

The weather is bad today so it was an excellent opportunity to work out with my Quest 2 headset and the Supernatural VR workout. I bought this about 2 years ago to have something to do inside as Chicago winters are dark, cold and treacherous to walk in for someone like me.

For the first time ever, I went into spring in better shape than I left in fall. I was able to keep improving. It was hard. It’s supposed to be hard. Two years ago hard was doing the shortest, easiest routine. Squatting and lunging were very difficult for me. I fell a couple times trying. I kept at it and I began to improve. My legs got stronger, my balance and coordination improved.

Today I did the longest, high level routine that SN has. It’s really hard for me, but so fun. The movements are so crazy inventive – and difficult. Knee strikes and leg lifts have been added to the squats and lunges. My first desire to quite came in the 7th song. There are 19 songs to the routine. By the last 3 I am really just running on determination. I will not quit.

It was hard. Gloriously hard. Hard for me. That’s all that matters. It does not matter that when I ran a race with thousands of other people….that folks walking passed me up when I was running. What I was doing was so hard – for me. But did you hear – I was RUNNING. Hard started with walking 15 minutes in my neighborhood with my cane. That was hard back then.

Using your body doesn’t tear it down, it builds it up. Don’t learn the lesson from folks working at extremes that you’ll never do. Running hours a day on pavement for years might well hurt your knees. But the guy I was, walking with a cane, osteoarthritis in my knees — needed to USE those knees. I needed to do more to enable the healing. I didn’t start with running – I couldn’t. But I did have to do that which was hard.

I did my body good today. Go do your body good starting from wherever you are. You will build yourself up by doing more and going farther.

How much more? A little more.

How much farther? A bit farther.

Always.

It’s hard. It’s always hard. Intentionally so.
