As my fitness level has improved, my walks need a little more umph…but something much less than speed walking or running. Tonight I tried a weighted vest. I put six pounds in it for an easy start.

Walking with the vest did not feel hard and I was able to talk to friends as I walked. My heart rate went up into zone 2 which was my goal. It wasn’t a bad experience – less cumbersome than wearing a winter coat.

Zone 2 is often called the fitness zone. It helps increase your ability to process oxygen, and it’s optimal for fat burning.

I was feeling it by the end of a 40 minute walk. Not that it was heavy but that I was getting tired. That’s a good thing. It allowed me to get to that point in less time.

You can see in the chart the difference in my typical walk and with the weighted vest. My heart rate went up just enough but not too much.

Funny/tragic thing is that I used to walk around with 50lbs of extra weight. And I’m still walking around with 80lbs too much.

But I’m working on it.
