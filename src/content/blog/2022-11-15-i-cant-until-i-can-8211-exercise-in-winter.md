I embarked on my health reclamation journey a year ago in the spring. Love the spring, then summer and into fall. Outside most every day going for a walk. That was about all I could do at the time…walk. Walk some, then walk more, then walk faster.

Winter was coming…what to do? Winter was coming and Covid had shut the gyms down. Time to problem solve. I can’t … becomes I can…when you flip an obstacle into a problem to solve.

What can I do to keep active in my own home when I don’t have a basement or a work out room? My treadmill was long dead and relegated to clothes hanger and eventually thrown away. Perhaps an exercise bicycle.

Then I saw an add for the Supernatural app for the Oculus Quest 2, a virtual reality headset. Looked like fun and a good workout. That was a bit more than a year ago.

SN got me through the winter and into spring stronger, more stable, and aware that weakness, not just arthritis, was a factor in my disability.

With the return of nice weather, I returned to going outside to walk and eventually regained the ability to run. I also added strength training twice a week.

Today the snow returned. I’m back in my headset. Cold weather, snow, ice – are no longer impediments to my fitness journey. Even my Saturday running of intervals has been replaced with vigorous workouts in SN from the comfort of my own living room.

VR isn’t for everyone. Problem solving is. Your obstacles are opportunities in disguise.

Turn your own “cant's” into “cans”.
