A high school pal and prolific Facebook poster wrote about “stacking habits”. I’ve never called it that – but it’s what I practice. You can’t fix everything in your life and health all at once. You can make improvements. Concentrate on building one good habit, then keep that going as you build the next.

Haven’t written about sleep in awhile but good sleep is vital to health. Poor sleep leads to higher cortisol which leads to weight gain and metabolic illness.

Like everybody else, I spent most of my energies looking for the perfect sleeping pill. Then the perfect natural sleeping pill.

I’ve found my best sleep by working for the long term health. Exercise and stress reduction. I can’t figure out how to “get to sleep right now”. I have learned many ways that I sabotage good sleep.

Staying up too late
Getting into social media fights right before bed
Eating too close to bed time
Drinking caffeine late at night
Doing mentally stimulating things (like work) right up until bed time

The better I get at not doing the stupid things, the more often I get a good night’s sleep. I’m still not a great sleeper, but it is improving.

Herbie and I just did the new Muse Artist Series flow workout. It was wonderful. The moves were innovative and fun. I was supposed to do a light workout as yesterday was strength training. This was definitely one of those “almost high” level routines.

As it was about three hours before bedtime, it should help me sleep.
