Once a week I endeavor to get my heart rate up to 95% of max. But my improving fitness is making this more challenging. The heart itself is a muscle and like any muscle, you apply stress to induce the body to rebuild it back a bit stronger. For strength training you increase the weight or resistance, with your heart you get the heart rate up.

It was Saturday Push Day but I led singing at a men's retreat in the morning and had anniversary date night with my wife. By the time 8:30 in the evening rolled around and I could do my push – it was dark outside. Running is far and away the easiest way for me to get my heart rate up.

No problem, I have my virtual reality workout, Supernatural, I can do in my house. I am back up to the HIgh level routines and picked a “Super Monster” – a 12 song workout. It was challenging and fun and I pushed myself. Another glorious workout that had had me dripping with sweat, my arms and abs burning, and breathing heavy. It was great, except…

My heart rate pretty much hovered in the 80% of max, not the 95% which is my goal for push day. How could this be as I was pushing hard? It’s simple, really. My heart has been getting more fit such that my arm muscles are the “weak link”. I may have to add a weight vest or pick a different in home exercise for my Push Days.

Don’t get me wrong, I did my body good. I did my heart good as well. It’s a good sign to have my cardiovascular system take more effort to get the heart rate up. It’s kind of like going to the gym with the intention of working on your legs and you did the bench press instead of squats. Sure, your chest and arm muscles would improve but you had desired to work to benefit the legs.

Speaking of biceps – I am getting some guns. Now I think this photo is just the right lighting, and I’d just exercised my arms. Still, it’s thrilling to see muscles appear.

I did my body good and I wish you well in doing your own body good. It happens as you do more and go farther.

How much more? A little more.

How much farther? A bit farther.

Always.
