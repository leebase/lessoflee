Did you know that donating blood lowers your A1c score?

It does. By removing the glycated blood, your body replaces with new cells. Ergo, your A1c score goes down.

Mind you – your diabetes isn’t getting better but your score goes lower. Guess what? It’s pretty much the same thing with taking meds in lieu of changing your diet. The meds lower your A1c, but your diabetes keeps progressing. That’s what they mean by saying diabetes is a progressively degenerative disease.

So – give blood to save lives. To save your own life, change your diet and start exercising – especially strength training. Don’t “manipulate the A1c score”. Do the hard work to heal and be healthy.
