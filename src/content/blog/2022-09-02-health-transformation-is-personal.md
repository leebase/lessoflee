I went out for a 4 mile walk – part of my goal to walk 10k every day in September. 87 weeks ago I committed to a health transformation. It was out of control diabetes that got my attention. Fixing my Type 2 diabetes was a milestone, my goal from the beginning was to get healthy.

I couldn’t stay the way I was and get healthy. I had to transform myself, my person. I used to call people who were careful about what they ate and who worked out “health nuts”. As if THEY were the weird ones. How absurd.

Couldn’t I fix my diabetes by taking meds? I started with metformin and Glipizide. My diabetes got worse. Trulicity was added and I was scheduled to go on insulin. Daily shots for the rest of my life? Is there another way?

There is. Stop being who I was and embrace becoming a health nut myself. I’d done it before 10 years ago but didn’t stick with it. I liked being the old me that ate whatever he wanted and laid around on the couch.

Or did I? I sure didn’t like being sickly, tired, and in pain. I didn’t like having difficulties walking and fearing that I might fall in the shower. I didn’t like what my 60s and 70s self was going to be given how pathetic 56yr old me's health.

Sometimes I look in the mirror and despair at how hard I’ve worked and how far I have to go. And then I’ll see a picture of myself before I recommitted. I like the new me much better.

You will like the new you much better as well.
