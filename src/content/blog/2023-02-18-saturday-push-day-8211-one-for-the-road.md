Headed out to Michigan to spend the weekend with my daughters. What to do about closing my rings…and it’s Saturday?

Get up at 6am and do a Monster Metal high level Supernatural workout of course. I pushed working hard to get a diamond. Only ever earned a diamond once before, on a 2 song medium level workout. Well…didn’t get a diamond (yet) on this one but I did push myself hard.

I no longer arrive at the last song totally gassed and trying to hold on and finish. Rather I pushed hard on every song and even harder on the last.

Feels great!

Making progress, pushing back the boundaries. How? Doing more and going farther.

Say it with me…

How much more? A little more.
How much farther? A bit farther.
Always.
