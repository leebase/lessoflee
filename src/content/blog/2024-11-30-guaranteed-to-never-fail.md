I have been working on my health for far longer than these last three years.  Real hard work.  Educating myself, learning to cook, learning to shop, learning to run, learning to lift.  I have lost 30+ many times.  I have lost 85lbs twice.  I have done WW, counted carbs, low carb, keto, carnivore, intermittent fasting, and extended fasting.  I’ve read over 30 books on metabolism, diet, diabetes, fasting and the like.

Every time I have started making progress I have been asked “what will you do this time to make it permanent?”.  I have never had a answer, nor do I believe there is one.  I have developed convictions on “the best way” to lose weight.  And I have changed and matured in my understanding over time.  And NEVER was there a time where “we all agreed” – so no matter WHAT I’ve ever done or believed there are always naysayers.  No.  Matter. What.

There is an unspoken assumptions that “the perfect plan will result in a permanent result”.  So when I inevitably back slide – the naysayers speak up.  See!  We Told You So!!!  Now switch to our plan and it will work permanently.   Except – EVERY plan has plenty of people, I dare say the majority of people, who do not, in fact, reach a permanent good result.

Really?  Really.  Do you know that Weight Watchers is considered to be the most effective diet plan out there according to US and World Report and yet 94% of people fail to keep off 15 pounds for more than two years.  Fact check me, as this is from memory.  But every “life style” (no one calls their plan a diet) has a high failure rate.  They ALL have high visibility success stories as well.  All of them do.

So – knowing this as I’ve been at this for decades – here has been my response to the question of “what will you do this time to have a permanent result”:  there is no plan that will keep your at a healthy weight if you return back to your fat gaining ways.  What made me fat before will make me fat again.

There are strengths and weaknesses to all diet and health plans.  There are people who will do better with one and another will do better with the seeming opposite one.  But Doritos, candy bars and ice cream will always make you fat no matter what plan you followed to lose the weight.

So don’t eat Doritos, candy bars or ice cream.  So simple.  And yet – I can go a few years with good results in saying no, seemingly blink my eye, and be back where I was.  Eating the food that makes me sick and fat.

I made it a good 3 years or so this time.  My goal now is to recover faster than before.  To get back on the right track.

But that right track isn’t going to make those who’ve disagreed with me happy.  My failure doesn’t validate your diet nor invalidate mine.  I simply have, once again, established that the standard American diet makes me fat and sick.   Quick convenient foods make me fat and sick.  Sugar and highly processed carbs make me fat and sick.  I should know better.  I DO know better.    But what can I say beyond that?   I have no excuse nor explanation for my failure.

For those of you who think YOU know it’s because:  I fast, or keto isn’t sustainable, or carnivore is dangerous.  Every time I do the above, I get healthier.  It’s demonstrably true.  It is also demonstrably true that people fail to stick with ANY diet.  Not all people, but all diets have people that stray.  ALL of them.  I blame the specifically engineered addictive foods and our fast food culture when I’m not blaming myself.

The reality is that for a lot of us, this is a lifelong struggle.  Doesn’t mean we can’t improve.  Doesn’t mean we shouldn’t reflect on our ways. It simply means the naysayers can’t use me to prove anything about fasting, keto or carnivore.  Those all work for me.  It’s why I am sticking with them as I strive to get out of my current slump.

I have never claimed to have reached perfection.  I have explicitly, time and again, said that I have and can still eat myself sick just as I ate and exercised myself healthy.  Health has to be maintained.  Not because one is “still and forever a diabetic”.  But because that’s how everybody’s body works.

There is no guarantee to never fail again.  That’s why I am a proud member of the Chumba Wumba tribe.  I get knocked down, but I get up again.  

p.s.  Always consult your own health team for your specific needs
