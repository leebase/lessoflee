Got a break from cabin fever as the weather warmed up to above freezing. So happy that I can walk outside in the snow and a bit of ice. Careful, but unafraid. Then later a workout with Lady Gaga – a medium boxing routine followed by a short low level flow. I’m on track to hit my goal of working out 30 hours for the month of December.

You can’t out workout a bad diet. Been gaining weight since the weddings then thanksgiving and Christmas and New Year's coming up. Set back about six months progress.

Oh well, I know what to do about it. I have to be strict as I simply fatten easily as Dr Lustig describes it. My physical fitness is progressing nicely. And no, it’s not muscle weight. That would be nice…and maybe I’ve put a little on, but not nearly as much as the weight.
