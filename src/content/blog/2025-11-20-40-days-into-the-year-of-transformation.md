40 days ago I finally said ENOUGH! I’d packed back on the 85lbs I’d lost and redeveloped Type 2 diabetes. My A1c was 7.1 and it was either go back on meds, or make the changes I KNEW would put me back on track.  I was 55 years old when I started the last journey and made so much progress, including putting my Type 2 into remission.  Off all the meds and A1C 5.6 or below.  Only now I’m 60 – I feel an urgency.  I don’t want to wait until I’m 65 and have already had a heart attack.  I was and am motivated to bring out the power tools.  The most powerful health recovery protocols that I learned over the past 5 years:  Extended Fasting and the Carnivore Diet.  It is my intention to do 3 week cycles of 2 weeks eating carnivore and 1 week of fasting.  Repeat until at my goal weight, then continue to live healthy.  

40 day check in.  Take a look at the before and after photos of my face.  That’s not just lost weight, it’s a serious reduction in inflammation. That’s what happens when decades of chronic high insulin finally get turned off.

- Weight down 30lbs

- Type 2 Diabetes back in remission

- Inflammation crushed – I have my “magic knees” back and this time even when not fasting

- The satiety of my diet is off the charts.  For the first time I’m not sacking and it’s easy.  I’m having to push myself to eat more to hit my daily protein goal.

- I’m eating a LOT and am still in a calorie deficit

- My glucose graph looks like I’m a young athlete who’s never been diabetic

- For the first time – living in nutritional ketosis daily.  Keto diet was a label, now it’s a reality

I’m thrilled.  If I keep this up, in 8 to 12 months I will be at a healthy weight and hopefully stave off that heart attack.
