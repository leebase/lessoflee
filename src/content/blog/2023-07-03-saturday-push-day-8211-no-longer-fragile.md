Today is the first day of vacation and I went out for my push day run when the thought “what if I injure myself and screw up my vacation?” hit me.

My next thought was “I’m no longer fragile”. The me that couldn’t walk across the lawn, was shut in all winter long fearing ice on the sidewalk – that version of me is gone. No more cane, no more handicap sticker. No more walking with my eyes at my feet navigating the terrors known as “cracks in the sidewalk”.

Off I went to run and get my heart rate up to the 95% of max. Pushing myself and initiating the adaptive response that makes my body just a bit better than it was before.

Doing more and pushing farther is how I got from fragile to … well … not exactly fit and strong yet, but making progress.

Go out and make the new, better version of you happen.

How much more? A little more.

How much farther? A bit farther.

Always.
