Ok, I admit struggling for a title. It’s day 3 of this months fast and I just put in a high score on my workout.

How? Why am I not weak after not eating for 71 hours? This is the hard thing to wrap one's head around. I am eating. I am eating my own fat.

This has been the least hungry experience. I still WANT to eat as I enjoy eating. I miss eating, but I am not wracked with hunger pains.

I'm also tired of being fat. Coming up on two years in my health reclamation journey and I love the progress I’ve made, but I am just really tired of being so very fat.

So, I’m doing something about it. I might even keep going this time.
