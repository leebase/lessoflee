Modern diseases are not caused by ancient foods.

I repeat: modern diseases are NOT caused by eating ancient foods.

But we don’t have to go back to paleo man to find a healthy eating pattern.

In the face of the criticism that low carb, keto, or fasting are “unsustainable,” I present the reality:

Until the late 1920s, none of the stuff we now eat as “normal” even existed.

If we just ate like our great-grandparents, we wouldn’t be drowning in obesity, heart disease, and Type 2 diabetes.

⸻

What is this post trying to say?

That your health can be improved.

That Type 2 diabetes can be reversed.

That arthritis can diminish.

That whole-body vitality can return.

All by changing what you eat.

But many voices out there claim it’s unrealistic to change your eating long-term.  To which I say: unrealistic compared to what?

⸻

Let’s take just one example:

“Not eating fast food is unrealistic.”

But fast food didn’t even exist until the 1950s.

So what changed?

⸻

Changing your diet to whole, natural foods changes your health.

For a while, you may need to be more restrictive — to heal a damaged metabolism.

But I’m just one of hundreds of thousands of people turning their health around. Overcoming what was once called a “progressively degenerative disease.”  Not with surgery. Not with pharmaceuticals.

Just by changing what we eat.

⸻

Real food doesn’t have an ingredient list from a chemistry set.  It is sustainable – but so is Big Food’s efforts to addict you to toxic junk masquerading as food.

Don’t fall for the marketing and the convenience.  Good-for-you food brings health to your life.

⸻

The food is the disease.

Not being able to eat ultra-processed, profit-first food without getting sick does not mean you’re diseased.

It means you’ve been poisoned.

It means you’ve been addicted.

⸻

I won’t soft-pedal the difficulty of change.

Low carb. Keto. Fasting. They’re not easy.

But they work.

And they don’t need to be forever.

Once you heal, you can widen your choices — but only to real food.

The kind your great-grandparents ate. You just can’t go back to unlimited modern food — because that food is toxic to you.

Ancient foods are not the cause of modern disease.
