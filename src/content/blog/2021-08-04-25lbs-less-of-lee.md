Scale victory – 25lbs down! I must say, it's taking a lot longer than when I did this before (in my mid 40's, now mid 50's). I'm averaging just a bit more than 1.5lbs a week. I didn't work nearly as hard before and lost much faster.I was "fatting out" of my clothes as it was...so no real size difference yet. Last time I needed all new clothes about every 25lbs. Another 25lbs and I'll have a lot of clothes I can wear. I was so stupid the first time and threw my clothes away (gave them away) because "I'm never going to be fat again".I've lost inches around my neck and chest – but so far my belly is stubbornly sticking way out. The "over hang" has come up, but that's hard to quantify.
