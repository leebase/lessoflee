I’ve been reticent to share too much regarding the specifics of the supplements I take. I take a lot – I’m a bit of a supplement junkie. However, I have little confidence that I’m doing more than just wasting money. Well, except for a couple for helping with blood sugar control as I have a way to measure their effectiveness.

So first let’s tackle the big issue – supplements will never substitute for a healthy diet, exercise, stress management and good sleep.

I admit that I have definitely held the “magic potion” mindset for most of my life. Take vitamins to fix my diet. Take pills to cure a headache. Put on rubs to do away with arthritis pain. And yes, take pills for blood pressure and blood glucose control.

The last two are more meds than supplements. Go do the doctor, find out what’s wrong, get a pill or two to fix it. Changing from a “magic pharmaceutical” to a “magic natural herbal supplement” isn’t all that much of a change in mindset.

Let’s face it – it’s a whole lot easier to take a pill than to shop and cook and eat only healthy foods. Who’s got time for exercise? Plus it makes your muscles hurt.

But – two things that look alike are not the same. I still take a blood pressure med and I take a boat load of supplements. The difference between where I was and where I am now is that I AM working on my diet and exercise, my stress and my sleep.

Supplements are now part of a “do everything I can to help” effort. They aren’t a “I’m too lazy to change so give me a pill to fix me” mindset.
