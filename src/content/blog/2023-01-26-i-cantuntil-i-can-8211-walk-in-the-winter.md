It’s day 4 of my fast and I feel terrific. I have the “day 4 magic knees”. My osteoarthritis always feels best by day 4.

You might ask…why work so hard? Why not just take the diabetes meds, the insulin, the pain remedies? “I have decent enough bs and my doctor says my 7.5 A1c is pretty good”.

It’s for days like today. It’s winter in Chicago and I have to go into the city. The sidewalks have snow, ice and slush. Up and down stairs. There is a LOT of walking ahead of me in those conditions.

I can do it. No cane, no hobbling along, taking forever to walk from the train to the office. No fear of falling. Just walk – because I now can.

I don’t exercise to get good at exercising. I don’t push myself just that little bit more to win a ribbon. I’m reclaiming my health and my abilities.

How much more? A little more

How much further? A little further

Always
