April 20, 2021 I finally met with the diabetes specialist after more than 2 years of having my regular doctor inform me I was diabetic with an A1c of 8.5.  He had put me on Metformin and Glipizide.   Only by then my A1c was 11.2, so Trulicity was added and I was scheduled to go on insulin at the next appointment in 3 months.  I weighted 336 lbs.

I finally kicked into gear.  I read the book The Diabetes Code by Dr Jason Fung and determined to reverse my Type 2 diabetes.   Meat/fish and non-starchy veggies, intermittent fasting and walking was how I started.  By July, the day I was to be put on insulin, I was removed from the last of the meds and my A1c was 6.5.  That’s still diabetic but it covers 3 months and my diabetes specialist had seen what my current blood glucose readings were as I was wearing a cgm (continuous glucose monitor).

Fast forward to today.  Just got my blood work and my A1c is 5.5 (pre-diabetes starts at 5.7).  I’ve been off all pharmaceuticals for a year and 3 months.  I do take Berberine and Curalin.  I have lost 75lbs, it was better a few weeks ago ![😉](~/assets/posts/2022-10-25-year-and-a-half-review/1f609.png)

My LDL went up since July.  After a month on carnivore my HDL and Triglycerides FINALLY both came into normal.  My ratio was a 9, and went to 2.5.  It’s 2.6 now.  You want it to be below 2, so I have work to do.  My total cholesterol went from being in the normal range to elevated with the increase in LDL.  I only was strict carnivore for the month.   This is more Info for me that I do better the closer to zero carbs I go.

The numbers tell part of the story.  I am no longer diabetic (that’s right, I said it, I’ll defend it – your doctor was wrong when he told you diabetes was forever.  And no, that doesn’t mean I can eat chips and ice cream every day as the proof of not being diabetic).

I am SO much more healthy today.  I feel so much better.  I didn’t stop at just getting my blood glucose under control.  I didn’t stop at just losing weight.  I have been getting fit.  I have gone from not being able to walk across grass and walking down stairs with difficulty, to training to run a 5k.  

No more blurry vision first thing in the morning (that’s diabetes in the process of taking away my eye sight).

No more acid reflux.

No more snoring/sleep apnea.

I sleep better.

The arthritis pain in my knees is greatly reduced.

If I drop something on the floor, I can bend down and pick it up.

My skin tags have greatly reduced.

I have far fewer headaches and no migraines.

I don’t break out in a sweat just reaching down to tie my shoes.

My resting heart rate has fallen from the 80s into the 50s.

I look younger.

It has taken a lot of hard work.  I have more hard work to do.   In fact – working hard on my health is my new normal.  Why shouldn’t it be?  Everything is more important and urgent than working on your health — until you don’t have your health.  Then nothing is more important.

I embraced the journey.  I have been changing and improving my mindset like I have my body.  Don’t envy my discipline or my motivation.  If I had those qualities in abundance, how’d I get morbidly obese and diabetic in the first place?  I developed discipline and motivation as I progressed in my journey.  You can too.

Diabetes is a death sentence.  More than that, it’s a “be sick, miserable and THEN die sentence”.  But ONLY if you keep doing what you’ve always done.   Taking meds will slow down the progression.   But the ADA and your med pushing doctor will tell you “diabetes is a progressively degenerative disease”.  

Or

Diabetes is a wakeup call to do what you were supposed to be doing all along.  Eat good for you foods, and push your body.  All the food you love to eat that made you diabetic – is like loving an abuser.   Happiness is found in breaking free.  There is new, healthy food you can fall in love with.  Food that will treat you right, make you healthy.
