My toes are 90% better and my thumb 70% – injuries from two recent falls that kept me from lifting. Slowly but surely I’ve been working my way back into my health building routines: walking, cardio, running and now strength training.

One might wonder – what’s that got to do with diabetes or osteoarthritis?

Strength training absolutely and directly helps both. However, I’m not just engaged in “disease management”. I am reclaiming my health and building up my body.

Haven’t I defeated diabetes and arthritis? Yes and not quite. I haven’t been diabetic in more than two years and I no longer need to walk with a cane. But there is more health to reclaim. I’m trying to delay and survive a heart attack. More on that in a future post.

This is the new me. I am someone who actively works to improve my health. Building muscle is essential for health for everyone, not just for diabetes and arthritis.

I have improved my health greatly, but I am far from being healthy. I have a lot more work to do.

Just how much effort is it going to take? More. Always more.

How much more? A little more.

How much farther? A bit farther.

Always.

p.s.  Always consult your own health team for your specific needs

p.p.s. Educate yourself to fully participate in your health strategy
