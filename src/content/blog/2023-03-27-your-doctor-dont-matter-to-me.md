I love it, I must admit. Go ahead and tell me that . If I had ONLY listened to my own doctor, I’d still be a diabetic to this day. That was her plan for me. She had no expectation that I wouldn’t take all my meds AND insulin for the rest of my life. I went in search of a better fate than get sick and die…but just a few years delayed. I wanted to get healthy.

Just about 2 years ago, April 22nd to be exact…I met with my diabetes specialist and learned my A1c was 11.2. The $1000/month Trulicity was added to the Metformin and Glipizide I’d already been taking for about 3 years. I was scheduled to be out on insulin at our next appointment in July. “Diabetes isn’t your fault, it’s genetic,” she told me.

I finally decided to do something. I read The Diabetes Code by Dr Jason Fung. At that July appointment I got off the last of the meds and my A1c was 6.5

A year and a half later, off all diabetes meds and never taking insulin, my A1c is 5.5. I’ve gone from walking with a cane and driving with a handicap sticker to running a 5k.

Reading that book and the 30 after it has been a huge part of how I’ve turned my health around. Every step along the way, folks have parroted the party line…the same line my “diabetes isn’t your fault, take these meds” doctor was saying.

My cholesterol used to be bad by every standard. Now it’s excellent. It became excellent when I dropped from keto (where all my blood sugar improvement came from) to near carnivore…with copious amounts of red meat and eggs.

So much knowledge is outdated and wrong, but you can take comfort that it’s “the standard of care”. The same standard that proclaims type 2 diabetes is a progressively degenerative disease. What does that mean? It means even WITH taking meds, it is expected that your diabetes will keep getting worse. I’ll stick with these books and my vastly improved health. I am no longer a Type 2 diabetic.

I asked my Dr on the day she took me off the last of my meds “Why aren’t you teaching what I did?”. Her reply, “not everyone is willing to make the changes you did”. Her words are true and I would add “and not everyone is willing to read the books, consider the science, and dare to break away from the norm”.

I didn’t stop at just accepting my own doctor's “Get sick and die, just more slowly” plan and I’m not scared when you tell me I’m going to get a heart attack according to YOUR doctor. I educated myself and took a chance by implementing what I learned. My body has given me every indication that I’m on the right path.
