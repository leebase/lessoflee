I made it to the 5th day, about half the time I fall somewhat short. This is my 10th month of doing a monthly extended fast. It was probably the easiest one.

Wednesday and Thursday nights I definitely battled the "I wanna eats". I'd say I needed about 3 or 4 hours of will power over the five days. Most of the time I'm not hungry. I'm never tired. Feel great. It helped that it was a busy week at work.

My bs was 77 this morning. Ketones are well into the high fat burning territory. I never see anything close to these numbers until day 3 of a fast. Lowest weight I've seen in a long time at 252 lbs. Down 17 lbs for the 5 day fast. I'd expect 12 or more to come back within a couple days.

Mind you – the "weight" that comes back is nothing to be concerned about. It's water, refilling up your glycogen stores and the transitory food going through your digestive system.  

I did notice I was a bit weaker for my strength training on the fourth day. Hadn't noticed that before. Not a big deal. Both the fasting and the strength training are advancing my health.

I'm breaking my fast after I go get a dexascan this afternoon. I'll be able to do a year over year comparison. I wish I'd have gotten one at the very beginning of my journey. At first, I was so fat they couldn't even get the details inside my trunk area. Now I can. I'm looking forward to seeing how the volume of my trunk has shrunk.  

I have a good 50lbs more to lose – I will be continuing with this monthly practice (that my doctor recommended) for the duration.
