Well, it's happened. I've gone and done it. I've regained 34 pounds after losing a whopping 85 pounds. I can hear Gary Taubes now, "Ah ha! I told you so! You fatten easily!"

Yes, yes I do. But let's not get too serious here. After all, laughter is the best medicine, right? So let's take a lighthearted look at this little setback.

First of all, let's take a moment to rejoice for losing 85 pounds in the first place. That's no small feat! I mean, 85 pounds is equivalent to carrying around a small pony or a large golden retriever. That's a lot of weight to lug around!

But then, there's the little matter of the 34 pounds that have made their way back onto my frame. It's like they were just waiting for the opportunity to jump back on and say, "Surprise! We're back!"

Man I would so love it if progress was always and ever forward. When I lost weight in my 40's it only took 10 months to lose 80lbs. But over the next 10 years I put it all back on plus another 25. This time I’m not letting go and bringing it all back.

I know what happened. Too many reasonable moments of eating off plan. Daughter's wedding, Thanksgiving, Christmas, New Years. But not just individual meals on the day, but a weekend or a week. And seriously, there was no pigging out involved. No binging. Just eating the foods that made me fat and diabetic in the first place. Too often. Too close together.

That’s a shocking amount of weight to gain in a few months. Clearly I can’t ever go back to how I used to eat or anything remotely close. Yes I can eat a single meal with a normal person's glucose response. No, I cannot do that often and I can’t eat carb laden food without gaining weight.

As Taube describes, I fatten easily. That is a genetic trait. Being a type 2 diabetic is a consequence of eating past my tolerance. Putting type 2 diabetes into remission is as temporary as losing weight. You never “forever arrive”. You have to eat within your tolerance. Whatever that is for you.

In January I went back on the carnivore diet…or close to it. I had such good results last June. This time I didn’t stop after a month. It’s my new way of eating. I’m down 17lbs. I’m going to keep going. And if I stumble again, then I’ll get back up.

Always
