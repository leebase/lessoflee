I’ve closed my 3 Apple Watch rings every day for over a year. Sometimes that takes some planning, or last minute near midnight walking.

Yesterday I got up early to get a workout in before heading to spend the weekend with my daughters. This morning I’m at the hotel and it’s icy outside. Don’t want to walk in those conditions. I’m thinking I’ll get home by 8pm, but will I want to do a workout then? And what if something changes our plans and I don’t get home in time?

So – down to the hotel workout room. I’m not doing so well transitioning from the strength training coaching into my own individual effort so far. So I use the weights to get some muscle building going.

I’m using 30lb barbells – one in each hand and performing weighted squats. I’ve lost more than 60lbs – just getting out of a chair or walking up or down stairs was a “weighted squat”. Think how much better my knees will feel when the next 60lbs comes off!

I finish with 10 minutes on the elliptical. Made me appreciate how fun working up a sweat with the Oculus and Supernatural VR app is.

Back to the room using the stairs instead of the elevator and my exercise ring is closed for the day.

Tomorrow it starts back at zero.
