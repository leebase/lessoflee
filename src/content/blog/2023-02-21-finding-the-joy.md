So happy to have found a form of working out that I really enjoy. Growing up it was tennis for me. I could be out on the court from morning til night during the summers.

That was LONG ago. Now I have so much fun working out with Supernatural. Today I did the new Jason Derulo medium workout. I hadn’t heard his music before. He had me dancing. Well…he had me feeling like I was dancing – I won’t vouch for anybody else calling it that.

I needed more time to get my 30 minutes in so I added a Pros Only quick hits – just 2 songs. Oh. My. Lord!

Wow – the hardest routine yet. My arms were flying around to the complex choreography to pulsing EDM music. I couldn’t believe I was able to keep up. Even then I only hit 140 heart rate. I can see my fitness improving by how much harder it is to get my heart rate up that high.

I’ve been writing a lot about my exercise of late. My diet is fairly dialed in. Today was rags and sausage for breakfast (eaten at noon) and the a couple cheese burgers with cheese and bacon (no bun), a brat and cottage cheese. Now to fast for the next 18 hours.
