Breakfast was eggs and bacon after an 18 hr fast. Dinner was a nice NY strip steak. I also had a brat (not photographed). In between I snacked on pork rinds and Vienna sausages.

My bs was 96-106 the 3 or 4 times I measured. That’s without Curalin but still with berberine as I’d already put them in my daily pill dispenser and was too lazy to pick them out.

I’m running about 20pts below my regular measurements.

My breath ketones hit 5.8 which is “reliable ketosis” according to the Ketoscan app. I’m never in ketosis outside of an extended fast. So I’m seeing one of my desired outcomes coming true.

I’m down 6lbs. I’m eating a lot…and losing weight. It’s too soon to tell if this is just a happy coincidence as my weight can fluctuate 10lbs or so without any rhyme or reason.

I pooped today for those wondering. I’m not expecting any difficulties there.

I definitely miss eating vegetables and I’m missing my cashews although I already knew I was eating too much of them anyway.

I was at a Bible study group tonight and there was cake and chips and I wasn’t even tempted.

I’m a little hungry now but it’s bed time. I was hungry just before bepreakfast and dinner but close enough that it wasn’t hard to wait the extra half hour or so.

I’m going to write a separate post about my walking/jogging. I’m experiencing no shortage of energy. In fact, I was compelled to go for a walk to burn off the energy.
