Last April I met with my diabetes specialist. My A1c was 11.2.

She told me: Don’t worry, it’s not your fault. Diabetes is genetic. We'll add Trulicity to the Metformin and Glipizide you’ve already been taking for over 2 years. Come July, we'll put you on an insulin pump. Go talk to the dietician.

The dietician shows me the government MyPlate and wants me to eat lots of grains and carbs, low fat, and only lean protein (no red meat). She wasn’t happy with me when I said I was going low carb/keto. “I have 39 years experience educating diabetics in what to eat”.

That was the road I didn’t take. That is the ADA's “diabetes is a degenerative disease” road. The meds forever, and more meds in time, and get sick anyway road.

I don’t know exactly what would have happened had I trusted in my doctor and dietician. I am fairly certain that I’d still be on all the meds, and on insulin – and would weigh even more as insulin makes you gain weight. But I can’t guarantee this as I didn’t go down that road.

Instead I put my faith in a new doctor. Doctor Jason Fung who says that type 2 diabetes can be reversed. I listened to Dr Eric Berg, Dr Jamnadas, Dr Eckberg, Dr Ken Berry, Dr Ted Naiman, and many others like Vinnie Tortorich and Thomas Delauer. I fired my old doctor and dietician and found a new doctor who teaches what Dr Fung does.

What road did I take? Intermittent fasting. No Sugars, No Grains, No Seed Oils. Call it keto if you like, close enough. I eat meat/fish and non-starchy veggies. Not perfectly so, but close enough. I fasted like an athlete…building up fasting muscles. I walked until I could do more. I added aerobics and now am also doing strength training,

Where did this road lead? Look at the photos. Down 50lbs while having gained some muscle. I got off all the meds and never needed to go on insulin. My A1c is 6.2 without meds and continuing to improve,

How about you? Is it time to switch your “diabetes is forever” doctor and dietician? I know it’s scary to think “how could my doctor be wrong”?

I can say this – you can always go back to being a “forever diabetic” if the doctors I'm listening to don’t have the answers for you. But maybe, like me, this is the road to be on. Diabetes in remission, health returning, abilities growing.

Start by reading The Diabetes Code by Dr Jason Fung.
