By month 3 I had gotten off all my meds.  My progress continues.  I am down 29lbs.  I am finally seeing a change in my size.  My belly is down 2.5”, my chest is down 2” and my neck is down 2.5”.  I am finally feeling a difference in my clothes.  I have a good way to go to get down to the next smaller size…. where I have a lot of clothes I will be able to wear again.

All is not progress, however.   When I stopped taking metformin, I saw an immediate rise in my bs of about 20pts…particularly in my morning number (known as the Dawn Phenomenon).  I’ve also experienced some stressful life events this past month and my blood sugar has went even higher… even though I’m eating very well.   If my numbers don’t come down soon, I will start using Metformin for a while until they do.

I found a new doctor via [www.dietdoctor.com](http://www.dietdoctor.com/) “find a doctor” feature.  It’s wonderful to have a doctor who is giving you a list of books to read (3 or the 7 I’d already read).  He believes that Type 2 diabetes is a dietary disease, reversable by dietary changes.  Basically, what I’ve already been doing is what he recommends.  At least now when I’m challenged “why are you fasting as a diabetic” I can say “my doctor told me to” instead of “I read it in a book”… even though I did learn it from reading Dr Jason Fung’s [“The Diabetes Code”.](https://www.amazon.com/Diabetes-Code-Prevent-Reverse-Naturally-ebook/dp/B0795BLS8D/ref=tmm_kin_swatch_0?_encoding=UTF8&qid=1629466528&sr=8-1)

I had a Dexascan taken so that I can eventually compare my results.  According to the scan I’m 43.7% fat.  But hey, that includes my brain as part of the fatty tissue…so I’m sure I’m no more than 43.6% fat ![🙂](~/assets/posts/2021-08-24-less-of-lee-reversing-type-2-diabetes-4-month-upda/1f642.png)

I have 162.4 lbs of lean tissue and 131.3 lbs of fat.  My goal weight is now 215lbs.  Last time I lost weight I got down to 230…so I think 215 is very doable.  I very much liked the experience at the [Live Lean](https://liveleanrx.com/locations/illinois/wheaton/) company in Wheaton IL.  I thought I was just paying for a Dexascan, but I received quite a bit of “how to become lean and healthy” counseling.  I also have great bones…no osteoporosis.  We already knew I had way too high of “trunk fat” – I’m pretty much a textbook case of metabolic syndrome.  But I’m working on it.
