There is a lottery in the USA right now where the jackpot has reached $850 million dollars.  Wouldn’t it be great to win the lottery?  Yes, I bought my ticket as I am want to do whenever the jackpot soars.  I pay my $2 and I dream my dreams.  What if!?

I used to dream “if only I could win the lottery, then I could pay to go to a fat camp like in the TV show The Biggest Loser”.  Seriously.  My wife and I watched every season.   I day dreamed about how nice it would be if only I could go away to a camp for weeks while a trainer and chef would whip me into shape and food would be prepared for me that would allow me to lose the weight.  The weight I felt it was near impossible to lose.

If only I could have time away from work to focus solely on my health.   If only I could have all the gym equipment.  If only I could have a chef to fix all my meals.  If only I could have a trainer to yell at me and spur me on.  If only I could have hours and hours a day to exercise.   THEN – then I could lose weight like the folks on the TV show.

One day I had a terrible night in the hospital after I had surgery to rebuild my inner ear.   It was supposed to be out patient but I needed to be held over night.  I hadn’t prepared mentally.  I was miserable the whole night.  It dawned on me that I wasn’t going to die young, I was going to live sick.  The very next day I started the Less of Lee blog ([https://lessoflee.wordpress.com](https://lessoflee.wordpress.com)). I hadn’t lost a pound, I merely announced to the world that I was going to lose weight.  I weighed 315lbs.

You can see in the before and after photo below – I was successful.  In 10 months I lost 80lbs.  Over the course of the year I also exercised and developed a nice level of physical fitness.  Surprise surprise – I did so without ever having won a lottery.  I had to work every day like normal, and I never did have or build a home gym.  No coach screamed me into submission.  No chef cooked my meals.  It took plenty of effort – it’s all in the blog if you want to go back in time and follow my first journey to health 10 years ago.

I know I’m not alone in this “if only” mindset.  Some people look at my story and communicate to me thoughts along the same lines:  I can’t afford what you do.   I promise you — there is a way.  I couldn’t lose weight the way people on the TV show did.  You may not be able to do exactly what I do.    The most important things – cutting sugars out of your diet, fasting and exercise — don’t require riches.

Fasting is better than free, fasting saves you money.  You don’t need an Oculus Quest VR system to exercise.  Walking is free.  Pushups are free.  If you are reading this, then you have access to the internet and all the knowledge is out there.

Look at a photo of Bill Gates some time.  Clearly, being a billionaire does not make you physically fit.  He can afford anything and yet, clearly isn’t putting in the work.  Money can’t buy good health.   Money can buy “health care” but that’s sick care more than  health care.   You have to do the work and you can’t pay someone else to do it for you.
