Fasting is done, feasting has begun

Another “hamburger (mince), veggies, cheese and egg” creation.

This time I used Italian sausage instead of hamburger. I used Eat Happy Kitchen's Putenesca sauce (no sugar added). Parmesan cheese and shredded sharp cheddar for the cheese.

It was quite delicious. My wife said “you’ll have to make this again”.

The pattern for all of these:

- brown hamburger in skillet
- add your choice of veggies. Mine are bell peppers, jalapeños, onion and mushroom
- season with salt pepper, spices of your choice
- add a sauce/spice
- curry, turmeric powder with some heavy cream
- pasta sauce
- taco or enchilada seasoning
- chilli spices and flavors
- simmer to allow flavors to blend

Plate

- top with a cheese
- add fried egg on top
- you can also add eggs to the skillet and allow to poach
