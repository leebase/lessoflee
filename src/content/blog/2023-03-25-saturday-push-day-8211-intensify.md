It's Saturday – time to push the limits.

Bad weather, so I can't go for a run or sprints. I'm also going to visit my sister, so I get up early to get a workout in.

You push yourself by going longer, harder, or lifting heavier. Today, I chose intensity.

I did a Pros Only flow workout. I remember the first time I did this routine. I wasn't ready for H levels, let alone "Pros Only," but it had my favorite song from Dream Theater, so I had to give it a try. I almost died trying to make it through. My arms were on fire, and I wanted to quit after each song.

Today, I'm upping the intensity to get an even harder workout in. Pushing for that Diamond. I don't hit the Diamond, but I did make Double Platinum. Those scores don't mean anything, but getting my heart rate into the 150s does. I was able to push hard in every song from beginning to end. But it's only a 30-minute routine.

So I add a boxing set after. I find boxing harder than the flow workouts. I usually box at a Low level with some Mediums thrown in.

But it's Saturday, and I push myself to do a High-level boxing workout – a shorter, 2-song set. I knocked it out, scoring a Double Platinum – and more importantly, I hit into the 160s for heart rate.

It's at the intense end that spurs on adaptations from your body. It's how you get stronger, faster, harder. And that's how you recover your health and vitality. You gotta do more and go farther.

How much more? A little more.

How much farther? A bit farther.

Always.
