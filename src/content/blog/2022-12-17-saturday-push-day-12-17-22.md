It’s Saturday and on Saturday's I push myself. Winter snow and ice on the ground so no running intervals. Instead, I did a set of workouts with the Quest 2 VR headset. It may be a bit nerdy, but look at my heart rate, it’s a good workout.

I warmed up with a Beat Sabre song as the Supernatural app had a new update to install. I then did 3 Supernatural app routines. High intensity flow to start out, a medium boxing and then a medium Sweat Symphony.

It’s hard to comprehend how much progress I’ve made in the last year. I could barely make it through the first low intensity workout. Had trouble with the squats and lunges. Now I'm flying through high intensity levels and throwing in some “Lee flare” as I'm movin' to the groovin'

The boxing was so hard at first. Worse than the squatting and lunging with the bobbing and weaving. My knees and legs complain. I'm getting the hang of it though.

It was fun working out to classical music to finish off.

The whole point of Saturday Push Day is to extend past what I’ve been able to do. If I don’t feel like quitting once or twice, I haven’t pushed hard enough. My runs work my heart more than today's workout…I’ll push harder next time.

This from a guy walking with a cane and driving with a handicap sticker a couple years ago. Dropping something on the floor was a minor tragedy. Do I really need it…or should I just leave it there? It was difficult to bend down and pick it up.

Bit by bit I'm regaining my health and my abilities. How has then been achieved? Doing what I can and more. Going where I could and then further.

How much more? A little more.

How much farther? A bit farther.

Always.
