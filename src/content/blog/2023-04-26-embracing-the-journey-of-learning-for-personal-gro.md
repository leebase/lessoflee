It's a fact of life: no one excels at everything. There's always a wealth of knowledge waiting to be discovered, and embracing that learning process can lead to personal growth and improvement.

I recently found myself pondering this idea while using a meditation app to manage my glaucoma. By combining medication with mindfulness practices and a healthy diet, I aim to not only slow down the progression of the condition but potentially even improve it.

The app I've been using is called Calm, and I've enjoyed it so much that I decided to invest in the annual subscription.

This meditation session prompted me to revisit my strength training regimen. Last year, I invested in a fantastic program that yielded great results. However, this year, I chose to save some money and venture into the world of strength training on my own.

Admittedly, I've been neglecting this crucial aspect of my health journey, mainly because it's "not my thing." But just like any other skill or practice, I can learn and make progress. While I might not be an expert initially, or perhaps ever, I can still grow and develop. Each day presents an opportunity to be better than the day before.

On the topic of personal improvement, it's also time for me to invest in a new pair of glasses and some stylish shades.

I encourage all of you to embrace the journey of learning and self-improvement. Don't be afraid to start from where you are, and make progress one step at a time.

Embrace the process, and remember that every day offers a chance to learn and grow.
