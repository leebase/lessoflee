Everyone who’s quite obese wants to lose weight. We know the scale lies…but d@#n it – we still want to see that number go down as the months go by if not the days.

I hit my 50lbs lost by the end of the year. It’s May and I’m still at 50lbs loss. In January I did lose an extra inch around my belly so I could see the progress despite no loss in weight.

But since then…my weight bobs down and then back up and no more inches off the widest part of my belly. So…no weight loss AND no inches lost…it’s frustrating to work so hard and be stalled/plateaued.

Then a friend of mine who met me in January and then hadn’t seen me until a week ago says “I can see the body composition change”. Hmmm.

So I went and grabbed a photo from this past January on the left and one from this week on the right.

By gosh – I can see it too. It’s like I’m getting a belly lift. I have indeed noticed that I can wear the next size down in clothes…and in the mirror after I shower…I can see more of me under the “over hang”. It’s both encouraging and depressing.

I’m encouraged to see the belly hang come up…but I see more fat pockets. I got fat every where. No wonder it’s taking so long. My fat rolls have fat rolls ![😳](~/assets/posts/2022-05-17-the-stall-and-the-belly-lift/1f633.png)

I posted just before this the photo with the barely-bulging proto-muscles. Yes, muscles are coming. Yes, for the same volume…muscle weighs more than fat. So, I am making progress…just not in the two measurements I have my eye on – weight and belly circumference. If I had a way to measure belly volume, I’m sure it would show progress.

And my face continues to lean out. I’ve even noticed that my feet appear to have gotten a bit leaner as have my calves.

Sometimes you just have to trust the process. I'm eating well, fasting, exercising and strength training. I am working on my sleep and my mindset. I am clearly gaining strength. Put 10 more lbs on that exercise. put another 10lbs. And another. Some of my exercises have seen a 40% and more improvement in strength since I started working out at the end of January.

Am I lifting buses and jumping over sky scrapers? No….I was just very weak to start with and am slightly less weak now ![😎](~/assets/posts/2022-05-17-the-stall-and-the-belly-lift/1f60e.png) – I have a long way to go to be “strong”.

I can walk so much better. I can vigorously exercise for an hour or more. Improvement is happening. And I am enjoying the process. That’s why among all the choices I have for “how to break a stall” – I'm going with “keep on keeping on”. At least for now.
