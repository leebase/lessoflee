I’ve been tweaking the carnivore diet.  Next to fasting, carnivore remains the most powerful tool in my toolbox for overcoming Type 2 Diabetes and more.  I had intended to kick off a 7 day fast after my 4 days of planned Thanksgiving feasting – but I have a surgery tomorrow and thought it better to go in full strength.  Thus I’m having the opportunity to see how carnivore itself helps me recover from having reintroduced carbs in a big way and gaining 8 lbs.  The answer is  – very well.  Not as powerful as a full on water fast but then again, I’m eating – a LOT and not snacking at all.  Why?  I’m so full I have no desire to snack.

There are a number of ways to lose weight and put Type 2 diabetes into remission.  Being able to eat to being full and satisfied for many hours at a time is certainly a LOT easier than white knuckling through hunger and deprivation.

I started with BBBE – Beef, Butter, Bacon and Eggs for the simplicity.  Delicious and filling.  To hear most everybody who talks about carnivore, you are supposed to eat a lot of fat.  The satiety comes from the fat, and being full and sated helps you eat less and lose weight.  There’s a lot more benefit to one’s health eating carnivore, but I’m focusing on weight loss and glucose control for the moment. 

Thing is – I have a lot of fat on my body so it doesn’t make sense to add butter to just add more fat.  I want to burn the fat that’s already on my body.  But the experts assure me that you gotta load up on that fat.  And I did for the first month and it was overall a good experience.  But I want to lose weight during my eating periods, not just the fasting ones.  

I’ve started adding lean meats (chicken, pork, turkey) in addition to the fatty meat, eggs and bacon.  I’ve stopped adding copious butter. I’m not trying to be low fat, not even close.  I’m not fearing fat causes heart disease – it doesn’t.  It just makes more sense to eat less so that my body burns my own fat.  

The result?  Even more satiety.  For the first time I’m not snacking and it’s not even hard to resist.  I’m eating a LOT.  I’m having to work hard to finish my meals they have so much protein.  I’m aiming for 170-190g of protein for my size. That’s more than I’ve ever eaten. I’m experiencing being full with no interesting in eating like never before.  Amazingly, I’m not eating that many calories.  So I’m giving my body optimal protein and an energy deficit at the same time.

My glucose is practically a flat line.  It rises some in the morning, dawn phenomenon.  But comes down.  It basically hugs the 100 line.  It’s obvious my liver is intentionally keeping my glucose at that level.  You can’t even tell from my graph when I eat. Protein and fat are not raising my glucose. 

Insulin has fallen.  How do I know?  On day 3 after the feast and eating only protein and fat with very little carbs – I dropped 5 lbs in one day.  Clearly that’s water weight.  So I lost 5 of the 8 lbs gained from the feasting.  That whoosh is a sure sign that my insulin levels have fallen allowing the kidneys to release salt and water.  Living in a low insulin state is how you heal insulin resistance.  

So I’m losing weight, my glucose control is phenomenal, and my insulin resistance is healing.  All while eating a lot and not being hungry at all.  This is a very cool eating plan. I will finish two weeks and then do the next 7 day fast.  My blood work confirmed that my body is handling this protocol very well.  All of this on the heals of a 4 day planned feast with all the carbs and sugar or a traditional holiday.  By the second day my glucose was back under control.  Frankly, because of the 7 weeks prior of fasting and carnivore, my glucose was in normal ranges during the feast too.  But nothing like beautiful 100 hugging line I have now.

Example day: 

  Breakfast:  Five scrambled eggs with cheese and ham (leftover from Thanksgiving, less fat than bacon)

  Lunch:  4oz baked chicken tenders and 2 brats

  Dinner:  7oz of thin pan fried steaks

≈ 180 g protein ≈ 130 g fat < 5 g carb ≈ 2 000 kcal

That’s 180g of protein and still approximately 1000 calorie deficit.  Being FULL and free of hunger all day until late into the night when there’s a much smaller “let’s eat”.   Right now I’m actually weighing my protein and calculating the macros – just to dial this in and learn what such eating looks like.   This way of eating so very easy.  I meal prepped and entire pack of chicken tenders.  Then I just add one or two to whatever else I’m eating to add protein with little to no fat.
