You do what you have to do. I fast as much as —I— need to do to make progress in my health.

I started on metformin. Then Glipizide. Why? Metformin wasn’t enough. Then Trulicity was added. Why? Metformin and glipizide wasn’t enough. I was scheduled to go on insulin.

That’s when I got serious about changing my diet and exercising.

If I could eat everything in moderation and that would work, for me…that’s what I’d do. And that probably would work if I was willing to take meds and Insulin the rest of my life and STILL get sick – just later in life. That’s the ”diabetes is a progressively degenerative disease” path.

If 100carbs a day were enough for me, that’s what I’d do.

I may have to go carnivore. I won’t if I don’t have too…but my triglycerides remain very high and that means I’m still eating too many carbs — for me. If I can get there without carnivore…I will.

I fast daily – typically 18 hours. And once a month I do a five day fast. And I’m still losing weight at an extremely slow pace.

I’m exercising daily. I love doing it. I’m getting stronger and more fit. I’m gaining back abilities I’d lost. My total cholesterol has come down into normal, and my ldl has gone from very high to slightly elevated. Those are some of the benefits to strength training.

If I could get those results without strength training – I would.

If I were satisfied with remaining sick and getting sicker – I wouldn’t need to do any of this.

I’m tired of being sick and tired. I’m tired of being obese. I’m tired of not being able to run.

My focus is on the healthy and fit me that’s inside and trying to get out. I’m going to do whatever I need to do – as much as I need to do it.

If you start before you have broken your body as much as I have, you won’t need to do as much as I do. Or maybe you need to do more. Wherever you are – that’s where you start.

Make progress. Gain ability along the way. It’s worth it, I promise.
