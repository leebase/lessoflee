It is indeed a happy birthday. Time to reflect on my health journey over the last two years.

Two years seems like a long time but it went by in the blink of an eye. In fact, 58 years has flown by. I try to keep that in mind as I continue to work on my health. While you are putting in the daily effort it seems like it’s taking FOREVER to make the changes I wish to make. Let’s take a step back a mere 2 years ago.

Feb of 21, we are still locked down due to Covid. I am not working on my health at all. I’m eating about as badly as ever. Daily chips and dip. Daily ice cream. Yep…pretty much every day. I was taking metformin and glipizide but hadn’t checked my blood sugar in over two years. I was walking with a cane (although I’m not 100% sure of when I was able to stop that) and driving with a handicap sticker. I work from home and I was grateful to not have to commute into the city as I had difficulties with stairs and walking long ways across the city. I had difficulty with the stairs in my own home and wondered at what point we’d have to sell and get a house without stairs.

Ten years prior I had lost 80lbs and got fit. I had tried off and on over the intervening years to get back on track but couldn’t stick with it. Feb 21 – I wasn’t even trying. We’ll skip the part of the story that starts the turn around in April of my 56th trip around the sun and talk about the now.

Brief recap: I have put my type 2 diabetes into remission and have gone from walking with a cane to running a 5k. I’d lost 85lbs but put some of that back on. Every muscle group is at least twice as strong from my 57th trip around the sun. Several markers of heart health have greatly improved including having fantastic cholesterol profile. Plenty more improvements I’ve written about before.

What now? The answer is to keep going. Health is not a steady state. Weight that is lost can be regained. Strength that has been gained can be lost. Blood sugar control can get out of control. There is so much more to improve with my heart health. A good 60 more pounds to lose, more muscle to gain, my blood pressure still needs meds. I wrestle with not being in a hurry to make all of these changes. I have been adopting the mindset of “this is the new me, this is how I live” such that it’s not about meeting a particular goal in a particular time frame. I admit, there is a lot of self talk going on – deep seated me wants to meet my goals – YESTERDAY!.

A little more…a littler farther…always and forever.

To all my fellow Groundhog babies – Happy Birthday!
