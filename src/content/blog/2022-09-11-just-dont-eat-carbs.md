My dietician wanted me to eat a diet high in grains and fruits. She also expected me to be on bs lowering meds and insulin. After all, she’s been teaching diabetics how to remain diabetic for 40 years.

Or so she told me when I said no thanks, I’ll be eating low carb/keto. She would have freaked had I told her I was going carnivore (but that wasn’t to be for a year later).

It just makes no sense to eat sugar and then take meds to help you lower your sugar.

Ps: she did throw the “I’ve been teaching diabetics how to eat for 40 years” at me. Obviously she didn’t say “so they could remain diabetic” – but that’s the truth. Far and away most doctors and dietitians believe type 2 diabetes is forever.
