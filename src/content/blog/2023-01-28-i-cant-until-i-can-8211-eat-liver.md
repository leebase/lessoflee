Almost all the food I liked and ate the most – led to me being a type 2 diabetic and morbidly obese. Changing what you eat can be a daunting task. The trick is to find new foods that are good for you that you can like.

But liver?

Liver is an amazingly good for you food – that’s easily established. Particularly important if you are mostly eating animal based foods. Eggs and liver stand heads and shoulders above the rest for being chock full of vitamins and minerals.

But liver?

I just made beef liver and onions – and it was delicious! I hadn’t eaten this since I was a boy – my father used to make it. I remember liking it but hadn’t eaten liver since then almost 40 years ago. I’d never made it for myself. I was scared to try. The wife is out of town so it was a good opportunity (she hates liver).

From the first bite it was “OMG – I love liver”. I had forgotten.

I followed this video on YouTube. I changed it some – adding Pork Rind Panko, Parmesan cheese, Italian seasoning and Cayenne powder to the “breading”. Play with it any way you wish.



I’m including a photo of my Tuna Salad with Cod Liver. It took a few tries for me to come up with a way of eating cod livers that I actually like. This is good. Just take any tuna salad recipe and add a can of cod livers. I drain the oil first.

I don’t like every healthy food I’ve tried. I’m never going to like Brussel sprouts. I’ve tried them many different ways and at good restaurants. I’m done trying.

You don’t have to like every healthy food – you just have to try and find foods that you can enjoy eating. It takes some effort and an adventurous spirit. It’s worth it.
