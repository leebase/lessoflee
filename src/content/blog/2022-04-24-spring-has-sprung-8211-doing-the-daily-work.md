A glorious spring day. Started with a walk…jammin to tunes and effortlessly keeping a 17-20 min walking pace. Normally my pace is in the 24-28 min range.

Breakfast outside with my youngest who’s moved back home for awhile.

Another walk – this time for an hour. My arthritic knees and my hips were barking a bit by the end of this one. I’m not cured – I’m just pushing that limit out further and further – regaining lost “ableness”.

I’ve come out of Winter weighing less, stronger and more fit. I’m starting spring with the gains not normally felt until end of summer.

Dinner was grilled chicken, meatballs, roasted veggies and a Salad. Then back to fasting.

My bs graph shows how the week after my monthly fast I’m a good 20pts lower than my normal. The insulin sensitivity you gain in the extended fasts lasts beyond the fast itself.

I’m a bit bummed that my stress fueled couple of “off plan” meals ended the ketosis state. When I eat cleanly after my monthly fasts I’m also able to experience a week or more of high ketosis (fat burning mode).

Oh – and I cut 6” off my belt. I have these cool “ratchet” belts. Easy to adjust. Just don’t need the belt to be that long any more.

Doing the daily work – making progress.
