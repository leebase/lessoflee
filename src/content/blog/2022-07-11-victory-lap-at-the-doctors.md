A deconstructed Spam and cheese sandwhich. No bread. Started out thinking of a lettuce wrap, ended up more like a spam salad ![](~/assets/posts/2022-07-11-victory-lap-at-the-doctors/1f642.png)

The second meal of the day was brats and baked chicken topped with bacon. Not in the photo, but I added some salsa on top. We had some fresh salsa from the local famer's market.

The tomatoes – they are in season and are glorious ![](~/assets/posts/2022-07-11-victory-lap-at-the-doctors/1f642.png)

My old doctor – the one who agrees with Dr Jason Fung's The Diabetes Code retired. My new doctor, I met with him today. It was in the wake of the recent lipid results. It was a victory lap. He's in favor of what I'm doing. No pushing of statins. No fear of fasting or eating carnivore.

I do wonder what a traditional doctor would have though with his new patient eating mostly meat with plenty of fat and very few vegetables. Then again, this patient has gone from A1c of 11.2 while on Metformin, Glipizide and Trulicity to 5.6 and off all the meds for a year. Down 65lbs. Total cholesterol, HDL and Triglycerides all good. LDL a little high but way down from before. He wasn't even concerned about that.

We talked about heart disease and my very high CAC score. Since I'm doing all the life style and diet changes that can help my heart – there's nothing to add. No worries about the intensity of exercise. Just keep doing what I'm doing.

It's been a good day.
