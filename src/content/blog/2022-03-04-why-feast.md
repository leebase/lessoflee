Pedantics rejoice, we shall commence the argument over the word: feast.

I submit definition #2 – a meal that is well prepared and abundantly enjoyed

It's the good life. Enjoying good food. I feast, then I fast.

Why not something more boring like “I eat, then I stop eating”? Because people need to know that diabetes can be reversed, obesity can be overcome without life having to suck eating terrible but “good for you” food in tiny amounts leaving you hungry all the time.

I eat well. I enjoy what I eat. This is the good life. And the life that is restoring my health. It’s a feast!
