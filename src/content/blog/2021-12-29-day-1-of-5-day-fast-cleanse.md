38 hours into my 5 day fast and I’m feeling fine and I’m down 5lbs.

I’m just below the weight I’d hit before the holiday carb eating began. So, yes, I’m basically losing the rapid water gain. I didn’t instantly gain 10lbs of fat over Christmas, and I haven’t instantly lost that much fat.

But – one can expect to lose .57 lbs of fat every day you fast. 3500 calories to a pound, BMR around 2200 or so.

I had my second dexascan recently. It was four months after my first. It was also the period of my long stall. Still, of the 14lbs I lost, 9 were fat. I also gained 3 lbs of “lean tissue” in my arms. My workout routine seems to be helping.
