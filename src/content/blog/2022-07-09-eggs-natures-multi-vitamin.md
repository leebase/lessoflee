Not just eggs – but a power packed multi-vitamin.

I diced up ham and a jalapeño and sautéed in butter. Then I add in 4 eggs and soft scramble. Then spread the eggs over the pan, add the cheese and thin sliced mushroom, and let set a bit. Fold and top with fresh salsa.

Delicious and nutritious.
