That I’m writing this is proof that I didn’t die.  Ok, apologies for the gallows humor.  There was nothing mortal on the line.  It did change my fasting schedule – so I’m eating my protein forward version of carnivore for a couple weeks and not exercising except walking.

Why the fat photo?  I sat on the hospital bed I looked at what must surely be a Fun House mirror distorting my image.  But no, it’s me slouching a bit and the enormity of gut just heaped in a puddle.  

A dangerous, active organ sending out inflammation signals to the rest of my body (otherwise known as visceral fat).

It’s a picture of the enemy – and the task at hand.  And why I’ve brought out the “strong medicine” of extended fasting and carnivore cycling to knock this out in about a year – maybe less.  The surgery was never going to kill me – visceral fat is working to shorten my life.

That’s the real threat and this the real battle.
