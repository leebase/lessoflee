I’ll start and restart as many times as it takes. Where did all my discipline go? Discipline isn’t something you are born with, it’s something you build.  And discipline isn’t permanent – (sigh).  How and why?

I’m coming off a mediocre year, a bad autumn and a terrible winter.  I haven’t  been eating well, exercising like I had been – and then I caught every single bug that was going around.  Now it’s spring and all my disciplines are in shambles: diet, fasting, exercise. I have lost ground on all the health I had worked so hard to build.

Time to pull out one of my mantras – start where you are, make progress.

Bearing myself up won’t help.  Likewise, though, I cannot instantly do what I was able to do only a year ago.  I haven’t been able to fast for a day – even though I had done a 14 day, water only far.  A day fast is supposed to be nothing to me.  I’m struggling sticking to the foods that are healthy for me to eat.  I can’t go out and run a 5k without stopping.

How to get discipline back is the same way one builds discipline to begin with.  You have to do something that takes discipline to do in order to build discipline.

It’s a lot like building strength.  Let’s say you want to bench press 250 lbs – but all you can do in the moment now is 80 lbs.  You can’t just put 250 lbs on the bar and work out.  You lift what you can and a little more.  From 80 to 85, then to 90.  It’s called progressive over load.   If you go past easy, lift what is challenging, your body will respond and build up strength.  Then it’s a matter of continuing on and building strength.

Discipline is the same.  Though I WANT to just jump right back to where I have already achieved – it’s not working.

I need to start where I am now – not where I have been – and make progress.  Eat a little better, fast a little longer, exercise a little more.  And keep building.

Ready to join me?

How much more? A little more.

How much farther? A bit farther.
