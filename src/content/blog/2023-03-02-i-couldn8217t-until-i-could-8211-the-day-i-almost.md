A year ago today I was moved to tears because I could ALMOST, but not quite, run. I was up to speed walking, from starting with walking with a cane. That glorious spring day my body ACHED to break off and run. But I couldn't quite pull it off. But I felt that it had become possible and would happen one day.

A year later and I am able to run a 5k without stopping.

Praise God!
