The morning started with a victory in a virtual reality table tennis match. I love my Quest 2 and playing VR table tennis. The opponent was a sweet lady who lives in Denmark. Very close match.

But – it’s Saturday, and on Saturday I run intervals. Last Saturday my daughter got married – so no intervals were run. I did run them on Monday.

So, I had already exercised – and it’s 32f/0c outside. Freezing, but clear and sunny. No impediment to going out for a run.

I’m going to have to ignore all my previous times as I think something was up with the app I was using. This morning’s run took 51 minutes – and that seems more realistic at 16min miles. I had to run a good deal further than what I had THOUGHT was a 5k distance. Oh well, what’s important is getting fit and that’s happening no matter how wonky my time recordings may have been.

From the beginning, I had set a goal of being able to exercise vigorously for an hour. I’m certainly doing that now. My heart rate peaks into the red zone and is in the 140s a good deal of the time. I can see an improvement as my heart rate isn’t going as high as it did at first and recovers more quickly.

No time victories, and no “running without stopping” records. I did finally manage to run up the tallest hill of my course for the first time this morning.

I’m getting closer to a normal stride. With my arthritic knees, running at all has been quite the challenge. A year ago I couldn’t get both feet into the air at the same time. I’m working on a mid to fore foot strike and keeping good posture.

Funny thing – but this running is less hard on my knees than walking the entire 4 mile trail. When I started it took time to work up to being able to walk that far. And then, when I’d get home, I could barely walk up the two steps to my door. Even now it’s harder for me to do that after a walk than after running intervals. I think it’s because I walk heal to toe, and don’t land on my heal when running my style.

I press on to meet the next goal – running the entire 5k without stopping.

How much more? A little more.

How far? A bit farther.

Always

You can do it too.
