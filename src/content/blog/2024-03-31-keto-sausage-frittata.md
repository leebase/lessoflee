### Keto Ground Pork Frittata with Diced Jalapeños

#### Ingredients:

– 1 pound ground pork

– 1 jalapeño, diced (seeds removed if you prefer less heat)

– 8 large eggs

– 1/4 cup heavy cream

– Salt and pepper to taste

– 1 cup shredded cheese (cheddar, mozzarella, or a blend)

– Fresh cilantro or parsley for garnish (optional)

#### Instructions:

1. **Preheat Oven and Prepare Ingredients:**

– Preheat your oven to 375°F (190°C). Meanwhile, dice the jalapeño.

2. **Cook Ground Pork and Jalapeños:**

– Place the ground pork in a cast iron skillet over medium heat. Add the diced jalapeño to the skillet. Cook until the pork is browned and fully cooked through. Season with salt and pepper to taste.

3. **Prepare Egg Mixture:**

– In a mixing bowl, whisk together the eggs and heavy cream until well combined. Season the egg mixture with salt and pepper to taste.

4. **Assemble and Cook Frittata:**

– Spread the cooked pork and jalapeño mixture evenly in the skillet.

– Pour the whisked egg mixture over the pork and jalapeños.

– Sprinkle shredded cheese evenly over the top of the frittata.

5. **Bake in Oven:**

– Transfer the skillet to the preheated oven and bake for 20-25 minutes, or until the eggs are set and the top is golden brown.

6. **Serve:**

– Once done, remove the skillet from the oven and let the frittata cool slightly.

– Slice the frittata into wedges, garnish with fresh cilantro or parsley if desired, and serve hot.
