It’s morning of day 3 of June’s 5 day fast. 62 hours in and I’m halfway there. I was plenty hungry at times yesterday. Not “mad cravings” hungry but I certainly wanted to eat. I’m so glad I didn’t because:

Bs 98
Breath ketones 24 – burn baby burn
Blood ketones 1.8
GKI 3.0 – high therapeutic level of ketosis
Weight 266.2 (-10lbs)

See those ketones! Burn baby burn!!!

This is why I do the extended fasts – to get to the health benefits. My GKI is in the therapeutic range – where people with epilepsy try to get to. And while I’m not epileptic – I’m definitely benefiting from the fat burning, autophogy, human growth hormone, BDNF, stem cells and immune cell refreshing.

Feeling great. Went and trained for a 5k. Doing intervals between walking and my crouch-jogging. Getting better. I even did the “finish with a kick”. I used to always sprint this last section finishing hard. I can’t sprint, but I did push myself to crouch-jog the whole way. See photo.
