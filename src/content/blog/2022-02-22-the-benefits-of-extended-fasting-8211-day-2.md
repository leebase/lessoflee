Fasting is an amazing health practice.  Intermittent fasting helped me get off all my diabetes meds and put type 2 diabetes into remission.  With my doctor’s recommendation I have begun a monthly 5 day fast.  This is a series of posts to describe the benefits.   I have just finished day 2.

Autophagy – this is the prime benefit of the extended fast.  Many fasting benefits come from regular intermittent fasts – also known as time restricted eating.  I got off all my diabetes meds and put my type 2 into remission without extended fasting.  But not every benefit of fasting occurs in fasts of a day or shorter.

Some time around the second day, your body starts recycling and recovery of mis-shapen proteins and old cells.

Research suggests that some of the most important autophagy benefits include:

- Providing cells with molecular building blocks and energy
- Recycling damaged proteins, organelles and aggregates
- Regulating functions of cells’ mitochondria, which help produce energy but can be damaged by oxidative stress
- Clearing damaged endoplasmic reticulum and peroxisomes
- Protecting the nervous system and encouraging growth of brain and nerve cells. Autophagy seems to improve cognitive function, brain structure and neuroplasticity.
- Supporting growth of heart cells and protecting against [heart disease](https://draxe.com/health/coronary-heart-disease/)
- [Enhancing the immune system](https://draxe.com/health/how-to-boost-your-immune-system/) by eliminating intracellular pathogens
- Defending against misfolded, toxic proteins that contribute to a number of amyloid diseases
- Protecting stability of DNA
- Preventing damage to healthy tissues and organs (known as necrosis)
- Potentially fighting cancer, neurodegenerative disease and other illnesses
