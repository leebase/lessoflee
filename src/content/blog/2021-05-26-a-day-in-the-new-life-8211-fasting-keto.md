How I’m currently managing my diabetes. 

Low carb dinner last night (steak and veggies). Blood Sugar (bs) peaked at 162 and I went to bed with bs of 122.

Bs stayed within 10 of 140 all throughout the night. I’m fasting 16:8+ meaning I don’t plan to eat until noon, but I’ll watch my bs and gage how hungry I am. If I’m not that hungry, I’ll go to 2pm.

10am I drink a bulletproof coffee (coffee, ghee, mct oil) which quells the minor hunger I was feeling. Doesn’t spike bs at all. Bs doesn’t really start dropping til 9am (119) falling to 110 by noon.

Wife has my salad ready for me. I’ve made the vinaigrette dressing (olive oil, acv, mustard, herbs). But I’m “I could eat but I really am not that hungry).

By 1:30pm I’m hungry and go and make a tuna salad (quality canned tuna, Avacado oil Mayo, mustard, peppers, celery).

It’s 2pm, I’ve fasted for 20hours. My bs is 96. I have this delicious and filling lunch. 

I might snack at 4. Dinner scheduled for 6pm. 

It will be fettuccine Alfredo sauce over Palmini noodles (first time I’m trying them), Italian sausage and peppers. This was delicious last week over fettuccini noodles.

It’s a good day.
