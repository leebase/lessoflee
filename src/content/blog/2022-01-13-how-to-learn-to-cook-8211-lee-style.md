How to learn to cook – Lee style. I’m frequently asked about the recipe for my meals….or am encouraged to put together a cook book. I am about to reveal the secret to “learning to cook like Lee”. Here it comes….are you ready?

Go to YouTube/Google/Instagram/Pinterest and search: how to make

When I post my food pictures it’s to help spur ideas. “what can diabetics eat”…because almost all of us have been eating foods that we shouldn’t eat. If you can’t eat bread, pasta, rice, potatoes, crackers….what do you eat?

So, when you see any of the meals folks post….and you think you’d like to try such a meal…just go to YouTube and search. That’s Lee's big secret for how to learn how to cook.

And don’t get too caught up in the exact recipe. Feel free to improvise based on what you have on hand.

Watch some cooking tutorials too. How to chop an onion. How to make an omelette. How to use the air fryer. 90% of my cooking is chopping and stirring. Bake, sautee', sous vide, grill, pan fry, boil, steam, roast and microwave. I’ll never be an Iron Chef. You don’t have to be in order to cook the meals I post.

If you learn to cook just one new meal every other week….in a year you’ll have 26 meals you can make. Even 10 different meals that you like is plenty of variety for a 2 week rotation.

You can do it!
