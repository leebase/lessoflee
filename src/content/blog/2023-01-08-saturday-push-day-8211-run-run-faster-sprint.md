I didn’t feel like going out. I had a number of vigorous Supernatural workouts during the week. But it’s Saturday, and on Saturday's I push my limits. Out I went into the cold.

It's only been a week since I ran the 5k without stopping for the first time. Some people cheered me on by saying “next up, 10k”. My intention, instead, is to work on my speed and form. My “running” and “speed” do not belong in the same sentence. I’ve had women power WALK past me while I was “running”. My heart rate lets me know I’m pushing myself nonetheless.

I run at my slow pace until I feel like walking. Then I kick up the speed until I know I need to walk. Then I’d sprint….actually sprint on my arthritic knees! Finally I walked to recover my breath and let my heart rate recover. This is the speed training I intend to do. My new goal is to run a 5k with intervals…only instead of walking intervals, I will run fast, run slow.

My 5k time today was 46min 15s.

Can I get down below 40min? I can’t….until I can.

What comes between I can’t and I can? Doing more and going farther.

How much more? A little more

How much farther? A bit farther

Always
