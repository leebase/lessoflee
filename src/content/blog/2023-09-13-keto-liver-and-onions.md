### Air-Fried Pork Panko-Crusted Liver

#### Ingredients:

– 1 lb beef liver, sliced into 1/4-inch to 1/2-inch pieces

– 1 cup pork panko

– 1 egg

– 2 tbsp olive oil or melted ghee (or beef tallow for a strict carnivore approach)

– 1 tsp sea salt

– Optional: 1/2 tsp black pepper, garlic powder, or other seasonings (omit for strict carnivore)

#### Equipment Needed:

– Air fryer

– Mixing bowls

– Tongs

– Whisk

#### Instructions:

1. **Preparation**: Rinse the beef liver slices under cold water and pat them dry with paper towels.

2. **Season**: In a mixing bowl, mix the pork panko with salt and optional seasonings, if using.

3. **Egg Wash**: In another bowl, beat an egg.

4. **Coat the Liver**:

– First, lightly coat each liver slice with olive oil or melted ghee.

– Dip the oiled liver slice into the beaten egg to coat evenly.

– Finally, roll the liver slice in the pork panko mixture, pressing gently so the crumbs adhere well.

5. **Preheat Air Fryer**: Preheat your air fryer to 375°F (190°C) for about 5 minutes.

6. **Arrange in Air Fryer**: Place the breaded liver slices in a single layer in the air fryer basket, making sure they don't touch.

7. **Cook**: Air fry at 375°F (190°C) for 6-10 minutes, depending on how well-done you like your liver. Halfway through the cooking time, carefully flip the liver slices for even browning.

8. **Check**: After the cooking time is up, check one piece to ensure it's cooked to your liking. Adjust the cooking time or seasoning as needed.

9. **Serve**: Serve the crispy, breaded liver hot, optionally topped with your creamy caramelized onion sauce.

This pork panko crust will add a delightful crunch and flavor to your liver, making it even more enjoyable while keeping it keto and carnivore-friendly. Enjoy your meal!

Here's how you can make a creamy caramelized onion sauce to pair with your air-fried liver:

### Creamy Caramelized Onion Sauce

#### Ingredients:

– 1 large onion, thinly sliced

– 2 tablespoons olive oil or ghee (or beef tallow for a more carnivore approach)

– 1 cup heavy cream

– Salt to taste

– Optional: black pepper, garlic powder, or other seasonings to enhance flavor

#### Instructions:

1. **Caramelize Onions**: Heat the olive oil or ghee in a skillet over medium-low heat. Add the sliced onions and a pinch of salt. Cook, stirring occasionally, until the onions become golden brown and caramelized. This can take around 20-30 minutes.

2. **Season**: Once the onions are caramelized, you can add optional seasonings like black pepper or garlic powder for additional flavor. Mix well.

3. **Add Heavy Cream**: Pour in the heavy cream and stir to combine with the caramelized onions. Allow the mixture to come to a gentle simmer.

4. **Reduce**: Let the sauce simmer for about 5-10 minutes until it thickens slightly. The time may vary depending on how thick you want your sauce.

5. **Adjust Flavor**: Taste and adjust the seasoning as necessary.

6. **Serve**: Once your liver is done air frying, plate it and generously spoon the creamy caramelized onion sauce over the top.

This sauce will add a luxurious, creamy, and sweet element to your dish, which should pair wonderfully with the rich, earthy flavor of liver. Enjoy your gourmet keto or carnivore meal!
