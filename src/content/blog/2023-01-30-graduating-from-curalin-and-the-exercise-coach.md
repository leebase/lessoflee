There are two very helpful products/services that I’m ceasing to use: Curalin and The Exercise Coach strength training.

I describe this as graduating because I have been very happy with both. I'm not stopping because I find fault but my need for them has gone away.

I have a normal A1c and my morning fasted bs is around 105. I haven’t been taking Curalin for a couple weeks and my bs has not gone up. This is what you want to happen. Use Curalin to help you get your bs under control while you improve your diet, exercise, sleep and stress management such you no longer need them.

I’m at least twice as strong in every muscle group than I was a year ago when I joined The Exercise Coach. 20 minutes, 2 days a week. No worries of injury. Not needing to know anything about building strength. A great group of coaches in a small studio environment. They also worked with me on how to get myself to be able to run. A little free rehab advice.

I’m now ready to go to a regular gym or lift weights at home.

The money I paid for Curalin and The Exercise Coach was well worth it. I am glad it wasn’t a lifetime commitment. They were tools I used when I needed them to get me to where I don’t.

I’ll still keep a bottle of Curalin around for those time I do have an off plan meal at a party or family event.

Thanks Curalife and The Exercise Coach
