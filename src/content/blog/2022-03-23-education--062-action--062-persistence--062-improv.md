Last night – sleep not so good. I share this, not because I think my daily sleep results are of much interest.

It’s to demonstrate that every day isn’t a success. I’m working to improve my sleep as it’s important for health. I’m having to work on it because — I’m not a good sleeper…and that contributes to my obesity and my insulin resistance.

As I put effort into an area of health, good days come once in awhile. Then they come more frequently. Maybe the day will come where getting 7 or 8 hours of quality sleep will be my norm. I hope so.

In the mean time…there are a few bad sleep nights, a lot of mediocre sleep nights and a few good sleep nights.

Education – action – persistence begets improvement.

Progress, not perfection.
