I’m a brand new grandpa…so of course it’s time to start slowing down.  Right?!

Nope.  It’s Saturday Push Day and I’m in my hotel room working up a great sweat.  I start off with a special Stars Wars edition of the Supernatural VR workout.  So fun.  And then I do a “Super Monster” high level routine.  90 minutes of pushing myself.  I wanted to quite after the first song of the Super Monster – but there were 11 more to go.  I made it through, and pushed extra hard on the last song.  

Getting older is more motivation for taking care of the body.  It’s more important now than when we are young.  Don’t just give in to aging before your time.
