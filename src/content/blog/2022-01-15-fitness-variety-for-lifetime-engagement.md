Today my wife and I did a dance workout using Apple Fitness+. It’s an exercise we can do together. It’s winter in Chicago where I live so my favorite exercise – walking outside – isn’t much of an option.

I’ve been working out for a couple months with the Oculus Quest 2 virtual reality headset and the Supernatural app. It’s a lot of fun, it’s beautiful, and multiple levels of difficulty to choose from. I can already see a nice improvement in my abilities.

And yet – the dance exercise kicked my butt. I’m pathetic and ludicrous. It doesn’t matter, I’m in my own home and I will get better. That’s how your body works. You use it, you are pathetic, and your body starts reconfiguring itself so you won’t be quite so pathetic next time. Focus on the process, not your current status.

I treated myself to a set of PowerBlock dumbbells for my birthday (right around the corner). I want to start putting on some muscle. Not for looks, though that would be nice. But muscle is health. So many health benefits from adding muscle.

I am also going to be stretching. I’ve always found stretching to be boring – well, lifting weights too. But, as I better understand the health benefits and the quality of life benefits, I’m more motivated to put in the work.

The variety is my strategy to incorporate exercise to improve my health. Just like with cooking, it’s important to find a way to enjoy the process if you expect to keep it up for a lifetime.
