3 Days Without Food…Still Alive

I’ve finished day three of five planned for this month’s extended fast. All is well.

My blood glucose is 78 – I really look forward to having this kind of reading without long fasting. And I’ll get there. At this stage insulin has dropped very low. Low insulin allows your insulin resistance to heal. Insulin resistance IS type 2 diabetes. Your immune cells also start regenerating at this stage.

I am finally in optimum ketosis. In fact, I’m in the level that is for helping with epilepsy, Alzheimer’s and Parkinson’s. Your brain loves ketones. I didn’t kick into full ketosis until two and a half days. Yep, I’m still quite insulin resistant.

I did a light workout with Herbie, my imaginary workout hippo. I might have been tempted to forgo working out – but I couldn’t let Herbie down.

Am I hungry? Yes, of course. But it comes in waves around meal times. I could eat and the hunger would go away. I can keep fasting and the hunger will also go away.
