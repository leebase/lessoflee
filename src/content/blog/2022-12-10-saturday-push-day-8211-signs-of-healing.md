It’s Saturday and on Saturdays I push harder. At work we had a challenge to run a 5k. So that’s what I did this morning. Can’t make it without stopping to walk yet, but I’m making progress.

I no longer have to “crouch run” – where I bend down a bit so I’m not taking the impact with my arthritic knees. Kind of like running on an eliptical.

I’ve been hesitant to claim my knees are healing, but I think it’s clear by now – they are.

I’m running very slow, but I’m running and giving my heart a great work out. I use a mid to fore foot strike so it’s not so hard on the knees.

How’d I go from walking with a cane and having a handicap parking sticker to running?

By doing more and going farther.

How much more? A little more
How far? A bit farther
Always
