I liked the hamburger meatball recipe so much, I tried a similar recipe with sausage balls. I made a sour cream dip to go with.

Sausage Balls:

Ingredients:

1 lb ground sausage (pork, chicken, or turkey)
1 cup pork panko (crushed pork rinds)
1 1/2 cups grated sharp cheddar cheese
1 large egg
1/4 tsp black pepper
Optional spices (e.g., 1/2 tsp garlic powder, 1/2 tsp onion powder, 1/2 tsp smoked paprika, or a pinch of cayenne pepper)
Instructions:

Preheat your oven to 350°F (175°C) and line a baking sheet with parchment paper or a silicone mat.

In a large bowl, combine ground sausage, pork panko, 1 cup of grated cheddar cheese, egg, black pepper, and any optional spices. Mix until well combined.

Using your hands or a cookie scoop, form the mixture into 1-1.5 inch balls and place them on the prepared baking sheet. Leave some space between each sausage ball to ensure even cooking.

Bake the sausage balls in the preheated oven for 15 minutes. Remove them from the oven and sprinkle the remaining 1/2 cup of grated cheddar cheese on top of each ball.

Return the sausage balls to the oven and continue baking for another 5-10 minutes, or until they are cooked through and have reached an internal temperature of 160°F (71°C) when measured with a meat thermometer.

To cook the sausage balls in an air fryer, follow these instructions:

Preheat your air fryer to 350°F (175°C) for a few minutes.
Place the sausage balls in the air fryer basket in a single layer, making sure they're not touching. Depending on the size of your air fryer, you may need to cook them in batches.

Cook the sausage balls at 350°F (175°C) for 12-15 minutes, or until they are cooked through and have reached an internal temperature of 160°F (71°C). It's a good idea to flip the sausage balls halfway through the cooking time for even cooking.

To add cheese on top, pause the air fryer with about 2-3 minutes left in the cooking time. Sprinkle the grated cheddar cheese on top of each sausage ball and then continue cooking for the remaining time, until the cheese is melted and bubbly.

Sour Cream Dip:

Ingredients:

1 cup sour cream
1/2 tsp garlic powder
1/2 tsp onion powder
1/2 tsp dried chives or 1 tbsp fresh chives, finely chopped
Salt and black pepper, to taste
Instructions:

In a small bowl, mix together sour cream, garlic powder, onion powder, and chives until well combined.
Season the dip with salt and black pepper, to taste. Adjust the seasonings as needed.
Cover the dip and refrigerate it for at least 30 minutes to allow the flavors to meld.
Serve the warm sausage balls with the chilled sour cream dip and enjoy!
