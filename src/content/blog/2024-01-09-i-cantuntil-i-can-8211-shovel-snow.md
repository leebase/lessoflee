I retired from shoveling the snow a few years ago. I needed a cane, and just walking in snow and ice was problematic – let alone shoveling. It’s also a heart attack risk for someone in my shape: morbidly obese, arthritic and diabetic. Middle age men like me die shoveling the snow every year. So I retired myself and hired a service. Never regretted it and I have a great guy who reliably takes care of the snow for me.

This morning I took the dog for his walk and there was an inch or two of wet slush with a follow up snow to come. I thought – here is an opportunity to improve my fitness. I don’t exercise to excel at exercising. I’ve been exercising to reclaim my abilities.

I won’t be engaging in heavy snow shoveling at this point – but I added “can clean off a couple inches of icy slush” back on the list of things I can do.

I was careful and didn’t push to exhaustion. I finished off the driveway and front sidewalk. I feel great. It’s nice to have a snow service, but it’s wonderful to be able to care care of more of my needs myself.

p.s.  Always consult your own health team for your specific needs

p.p.s. Educate yourself to fully participate in your health strategy
