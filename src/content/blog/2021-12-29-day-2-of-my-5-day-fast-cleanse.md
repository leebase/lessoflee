Day 2 is supposed to be the hardest. For me it felt pretty much like any other day when I’ve been fasting. I get hungry a few times. The hunger goes away. Right now I have a very slight headache.

I exercised. I drank black coffee and unsweetened tea. I drank a tbl spoon of ACV and tsp of minerals before the workout.

Yes, I want to eat. But it’s more in the habit style, not some famished hunger feeling. I like eating. Who doesn’t? So far this feels more like giving up the internet or tv. Frankly, I think those two would be harder for me.

My weight is at the lowest since I started on this journey. 8lbs down. Yes, I know that’s not all fat loss. But it likewise wasn’t all fat gain over the holiday. Water comes and goes. I cracked through the 290's with a weigh in at 289. There are currently 46lbs Less of Lee.
