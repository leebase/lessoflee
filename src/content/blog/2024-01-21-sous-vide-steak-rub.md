![🥩](~/assets/posts/2024-01-21-sous-vide-steak-rub/1f969.png): Steak Dry Rub Blend

– 1 tablespoon of kosher salt

– 1 teaspoon of freshly ground black pepper

– 1 teaspoon of onion powder

– 1/2 teaspoon of dried thyme

– 1/2 teaspoon of dried rosemary

– 1/4 teaspoon of dried oregano

Instructions:

1. Combine all the ingredients in a bowl and mix them well.

2. Generously coat each side of your steak with the rub. Since you're using this rub for sous vide, you don't need to worry about it being too thick.

3. Once the steak is well-coated, proceed to vacuum seal it.

This blend balances the robust flavors of the herbs with the classic combination of salt, pepper, and onion powder, enhancing the natural flavors of the steak without overpowering it.
