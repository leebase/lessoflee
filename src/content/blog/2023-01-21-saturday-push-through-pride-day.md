I did a hard HIIT workout on Friday so I went for a different kind of push this Saturday. I went to the health club and did my strength training.

I do not belong in a health club – or so the high school boy still in me says. Memories of the school weight room “No Pain, No Gain”…I can still hear it. I can still feel the “I don’t belong here”. Here is weakness. Here is other people watching me be weak, weaker than them. Mocking, teasing. Why be hear and be the weak one when I can be in class where I am among the smartest?

I don’t know the machines. Yet, I’ve been doing strength training for a year in a small, wonderful place – The Exercise Coach in my town. That place doesn’t intimidate me, but “the gym” does. Here I am the lowest performing student in the class.

But I go. It’s just a different way to push myself. I’m learning to embrace “hard”. Pushing back the boundaries bit by bit. Reclaiming abilities that I’d lost. There truly is no gain without pain, without discomfort, without pushing yourself.

How much more? A little more

How much farther? A little farther

Always
