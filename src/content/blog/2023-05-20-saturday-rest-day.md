2 days ago I ran the JP Morgan Corporate Challenge 3.5 mile race in Chicago. My first ever race of any kind. 30,000 participants on the streets of Chicago.

It was a Push Day Thursday ![😎](~/assets/posts/2023-05-20-saturday-rest-day/1f60e.png). My heart rate was right near or just over the redline for an hour. I did my body good.

Now I rest and recover. This is also doing my body good. It’s actually during the rest phase that your body rebuilds itself a bit stronger. When you push yourself you send signals to your body to make adaptations. Resting is when those adaptations occur.

I’m feeling pretty good. I was certainly quite tired and achy when I got home at 11pm that night. On my normal runs I get in 10k steps. I put in over 26k steps that day because of all the walking to and from the train station to my office, to the park where the race started, the race itself, and then back.

I’ll get in a leisurely 30 minute walk in sometime today as “active recovery”.

These efforts haven’t cured by arthritis. I’m not living pain free. I am living with much reduced pain and far more ability. Along with the exercise, cutting sugar out of my diet for type 2 diabetes helped a great deal.
