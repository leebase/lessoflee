When I shared a progress photo recently, I didn’t give SN the credit for the weight loss. I know that some are looking to SN for that very thing. It’s known, though, that you can’t out exercise a bad diet. Calories in/Calories out is a flawed understanding of how your body works.

I don’t want to dwell too much on this part of the story. Just know that your body compensates. Your hunger increases, for one thing. There are more ways that your body can and will defeat the effects of exercise for weight loss.

However…there are MANY health benefits and body composition benefits to exercise…and thus to SN. SN is fun and convenient. I bought the Oculus after seeing a SN add – I saw it as a way to keep exercising as winter hits Chicago and my walks outside are compromised for months. I didn’t want to have to start all over in the spring as I’ve done year after year.

And for that – SN has been a huge success. Doesn’t matter the weather, I can work out. I love how SN transports me to all of these beautiful places.

I have arthritic knees. I can’t run or even jog. Can’t. Not “don’t like to” or “not in shape enough”. I literally can’t (hopefully that’s followed by “yet”). This means the most intense exercise I’ve been doing for years is a relatively mild walk.

SN has allowed me to progress to truly aerobic exercise. At first I was so weak and out of shape I could barely get through a Low workout. Five months later and I’m doing some of the shorter High workouts and getting my heart rate up into the 140s and 150s. I haven’t had the joy of experiencing those kinds of workouts for years. Years.

So, how has SN helped if not for weight loss?

I’ve gained 5lbs of muscle in my arms. I had a dexascan last August about the time I started with SN. And I hadn’t started my strength training in earnest yet. So, SN is clearly how my arms got stronger.

I have so much more stability walking. I could barely squat or lunge at first. I’ve gotten so much better as SN workouts have strengthened the muscles around my knees.

I can actually put on a pair of pants without having to sit down. When I drop something on the floor, it’s no big deal to reach down and pick it up. I can walk down stairs without having to out both feet on the same stair. I’m still a bit wobbly with this but I’m clearly making progress.

I’m sleeping better and breathing easier.

One of my goals was to be able to exercise vigorously for an hour at a time. I couldn’t exercise vigorously at all 5 months ago and have met my goal already.

I play table tennis in VR as well. At first I was so limited because of the need to squat down and lunge and move to get to the ball. I’m so much better able now…and because of that, having more fun playing.

SN has helped me build up capability and capacity to begin other forms of beneficial exercise and activity. My philosophy is sustained incremental improvement. SN is very helpful with its variety of levels of difficulty and time length. I’m up to short bouts of level high, medium length Medium workouts and I still do long workouts on Low.

Today I’m going to go play pickle ball for the first time. I don’t know quite yet how that is going to go. But I know that had I not been working out with SN for the last 5 months, I wouldn’t even try. I went bowling last weekend. I can just barely bowl at this point, but that means that SN workouts helped me to at least get on the lanes.

I'm just listing the few obvious ways SN has helped me – excluding weight loss. You can read up on all the many ways aerobic exercise is beneficial for health.

And yes…SN has indeed helped with weight loss…in the context of a keto diet and intermittent fasting. SN will continue to be a part of my total health recovery plan as I press on with strength training, continue with low carb eating and fasting and work toward losing the next 75lbs while putting on more muscle.
