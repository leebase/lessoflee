Everything doesn’t go as planned. And you don’t make the perfect choice all the time. I certainly don’t. So, here was my yesterday.

I was on day 4 of my monthly fast. I was planning a 7 day fast to help break my stall. But day 4 I had a quarterly work meeting with food and drinks. I was prepared to just go and eat the one meal and go back to the fast. I lucked out in that it was just food brought around. I was able to mix and mingle without anybody knowing I was fasting. Great, right?

Well, I lose my phone in a cab…take the train home…then start messaging my family from my iPad trying to get someone to call the cab company. It didn’t go smoothly.

Then ANOTHER major family drama, unrelated to the phone, happened. And I just didn’t feel like fasting. And I didn’t eat good food. And it was late at night. (Why there was any bad food in the house is another story).

So, this morning, I head into the city again. I’d contacted the cabbie who had my phone. Got my phone back. But I had McDonald's breakfast. I intended to just order eggs and sausage patties- but ended up ordering my favorite…breakfast burritos (3) with hash browns.

Side note…by bs right now is 104 a couple hours after. But as I have my cgm, I know my bs spiked up quickly to 180 and then crashed back down. If you eat high sugar…and then it appears you “got away with it” – you didn’t. You just didn’t test often enough to see the spike.

Now…time to get back on plan. No beating myself up. Make the good choices most of the time. Roll with life as it comes at you. Sure…you can make better choices when under stress and pressure. I’ll waggle my finger at you and say tsk tsk. But really, it’s no big deal unless the bad choices are the norm.

As for the fast. This month my fast will be 4 days. It’s a tool, not a straight jacket.
