Well…first…it became a 4.5 day fast. This fast was difficult. For whatever reason, I was hungry and wanted to quit every day. Mostly of the “cravings type” of hunger. Last night was really difficult. I was driving somewhere and went past “fast food row”…a couple miles of restaurants and I was definitely triggered.

I pushed through and as I got home at 9pm, I went to bed early. Didn’t want to miss out on the “fasting while sleeping” portion. Then I broke my fast at a normal breakfast time.

I am totally fine with this. Fasting is a tool. It’s not an obligation or a rule. I would be fine with myself had I pressed on (as I did on night 4), and I’m fine with ending the fast the morning of day 5.

Weight – down 16 lbs. Yes, two days from now 12 of those will come back. So what? That’s the “weight” I’m not concerned about. It’s the liver refreshing it’s glycogen stores and the extra water that comes with it. It’s the content of your stomach and the rest of your digestive tract as you are eating again. It’s extra water as your normal state of inflammation returns. None of that is worrisome.

I have noticed that I’ve gained weight the last 2 months. How is this, with all the fasting and low carb? Surely it’s the feasting! I’m going to bet that it’s the result of the strength training and I’m putting on muscle. I’ll verify this in a week with my next dexascan. I lost 1.5 inches around my hips and 1” on my waist this past month. Pretty confident the weight gain isn’t from fat.

Blood sugar was excellent. By day 3 it was in the 70s and 80s.

I was in ketosis by the end of day 2 and it really cranked up days 3-5. Fat burning was definitely going on. By the morning of the 5th day I was in the “therapeutic” level of ketosis. This is what epilepsy, cancer, Alzheimer’s patients are helped by. Maybe some day I’ll turn these fasts into 10 days to spend longer in the therapeutic state.

While I did have to ignore the occasional waves of hunger…I felt great. Absolutely no weakness. I exercised every day, did my regular strength training, and went to the gym for HIIT exercise.

By day 4 my knees were feeling fantastic. I have osteoarthritis.

I did get light headed on day four. That has happened before. So this time I knew to take some more salt and drink water which fixed the problem.

I also had trouble sleeping more than 5 or 6 hours a night. I wasn’t tired during the day. No naps needed.

These fasts are not easy, but they also aren’t all THAT hard. It’s more self control than suffering. Well, it’s not suffering at all.

This was my fourth one and I see no problem with continuing these once a month. By the end of the fast I’m totally ready to stop. After a week or so I start looking forward to the next.
