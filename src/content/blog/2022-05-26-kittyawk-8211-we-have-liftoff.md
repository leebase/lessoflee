It’s morning of day 5 of this month’s extended fast. I went out for a nice early morning walk. Feeling energetic. Put on my old running playlist and I was booking it. Fast walking – arms pumping, butt wiggling ![🙂](~/assets/posts/2022-05-26-kittyawk-8211-we-have-liftoff/1f642.png)

The playlist is the one I used all year when losing weight and getting fit 10 years ago. It brings back memories. All those days walking, walking longer, then jogging, then running a 5k. Over a period of a year.

Well…my body responded to the music and memories and I started jogging. I’m not saying I jogged very fast or very long. It was more like the first flight by the Wright brother’s at Kittyhawk…it was a short but momentous time in the air. As in – both feet off the ground at the same time.

Maybe by the end of this summer I’ll be able to jog. I can’t — until I can — is one of my mantras. Put in the work, gain ability as you progress over time.

No doubt the state I’m in on day five of a fast helped. So much energy, so little inflammation. I was fast walking in the 16min pace. Any faster than that and I’d have to be jogging. I think I’ll restart the “Couch to 5k” program only with “fast walking” instead of jogging. I’d like to get myself up to being able to fast walk a 5k.

As far as the rest of my morning of day five stats. My bs was 94, my breath ketones 33, my blood ketones 2.8. I’m in very rapid fat burning mode and a GKI of 1.8 is in the therapeutic range (the highest). These are the feelings and measurements that have me pushing through the hunger to obtain. And I take on faith all of the other benefits that I can’t “feel” or directly measure: autophagy, human growth hormone, Brain Derived Neurotrophic Factor, stem cell growth, immune cell refreshing.
