Baked Cod with Egg Salad

These days, when I’m eating on plan, I’m Carnivore-ish, or Ketovore. The largest part are animal based foods with spices and, if I feel like it, some veggies. I’ve gone strict carnivore for a month with good results. However, I didn’t have any particular benefit in terms of “if I eat plants, I get sick” the way some people do. I am past, however, believing that one HAS to eat their veggies. Plenty of vitamins and minerals in a carnivore diet with meat and eggs. I find it much easier to prepare these types of meals which is an added bonus.

The first meal is baked cod filets. They came with a sauce packet that you can be assured contained seed oils and sugar. So, probably would have been better to whip up my own sauce, but I gave into convenience. The dose is the poison, I don’t fret over small amounts.

I made the egg salad which is quick and easy to do. Cut up eggs, salt and pepper, add Mayo and mustard. Relish is traditional but I use diced jalapeños. I use Primal Kitchens Mayo as it uses avocado oil.

Meatballs with Summer Veggies

The second meal was my Carnivore-ish meatballs made with hamburger, pork panko, and Parmesan cheese. I put jalapeños in mine. It’s summer so I eat the fresh tomatoes and cucumbers as they are in season.

Hamburger is very economical and nutritious.

The pickles for both were from the farmer's market.

I heated up a cheese sauce (1g carb) and put on a spicy Serrano pepper hot sauce. Absolutely delicious. I make these often. You can spice them differently or use sauces to change up the flavor. A keto marinara also goes well.

Recipe [https://lessoflee.wordpress.com/2023/04/06/keto-meatballs-via-air-fryer/](https://lessoflee.wordpress.com/2023/04/06/keto-meatballs-via-air-fryer/)

I typically also have some plain or low carb Greek yogurt with berries.

Sausage Frittatas

The Frittatas have been a family hit. I make them once or twice a week. I typically get three or 4 meals from a batch. They heat up well the next day.
