The muscles are coming…the muscles are coming!

Barely visible through the fat – true. But I can see the beginning of the appearance of the bicep, some definition in the shoulder, some neck muscles and my calves are beginning to pop.

Not exactly body builder material – but building my muscles is helping reduce insulin resistance and bringing back more abilities.

I’m turning back the hands of time from my “early onset elderlyness”.

The weight loss stall continues – but so does the body recomposition.
