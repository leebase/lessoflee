Regaining health is not easy. It’s a battle. But I’d rather battle to get healthy than battle sickness.

I did all this hard work 10 years ago. Lost 80lbs, got off blood pressure meds. Got fit. Felt great, looked great.

Then the fear went away. Then the hard work went away.

Then the old habits returned. The old eating returned.

Then the weight returned.

Then the blood pressure meds returned.

Then the sickness came. Osteoarthritis and diabetes.

Then came med and more meds and insulin was sound the corner.

The meds didn’t fix the diabetes. Insulin wasn’t going to fix the diabetes. Insulin would have led to even more weight gain. Each new med, each increase in the dose – that’s diabetes getting worse. That’s “diabetes is a progressively degenerative disease”.

My world had shrank. Couldn’t walk far. Walking up and down stairs was problematic. And our house has stairs everywhere.

The road back to regaining health has been a bitch. If only I’d kept up with keeping healthy.

The Doritos were NOT worth it. It sickens me now to reflect on how I chose “tasty treats with meds” over actual health.

I despise the old me that led to the sick me with the small painful world.

The new me is hard. I won’t lie. The new me takes work. The new me is so worth it.
