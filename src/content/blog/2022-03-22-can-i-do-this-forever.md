Is This Forever?

I’ve been asked if I plan to feast and fast the rest of my life. It’s a good question. It gets to the heart of sustainability. While it’s great to lose weight, if you can’t keep on doing what you’ve been doing…you’ll gain it all back.

I don’t know if you have been following my food posts…but I eat like a king. That’s why I call them feasts. I eat good food, it tastes great. I look forward to my meals. There are a lot of foods I used to eat that I don’t any more beyond the occasional indulgence. Chips, fries, rice, bread, pasta, pizza rolls, tacos. On and on I could go. And yet…I love the food I’m eating now just as much.

As my insulin resistance heals, I can eat my favorite foods that I currently don’t. Just not all day every day. I will have pancakes with syrup…some day. But that will be the indulgence for the week. No more sugar for breakfast, sugar for lunch, sugar for dinner, sugar snacks and sugar desert. Day after day after day. I’m not “still a diabetic” – but if I revert to the old diet of mine, I could redevelop type 2 diabetes.

What about the fasting? I do not expect to continue monthly five day fasts after I reach my goal weight. I will probably do them at least once a year if not twice for the health benefits I’ve previously written about.

The two meals a day intermittent fasting will probably stay. It’s a very easy life style. I can adjust the number of days I do it or how much I eat to stay at whatever my maintenance weight settles in at. When you eat nutrient dense protein and fats with fiber as I do…you really aren’t that hungry.

Making a change in what you eat is definitely challenging. BUT – it doesn’t stay a challenge. You fill your pantry and your fridge and your freezer with food you can and should eat. You’ve learned how to fix meals you like and are good for you. After awhile it is every bit as easy to eat this new way as it was the old way.

Diabetes need not be forever. Health has to be maintained- this isn’t something that you have to do because “you are still a diabetic”. You work hard to get fit. What happens if you go back to being sedentary? You lose all your fitness. That’s just how your body works. EVERYBODY's body.

That’s why I work so hard on mindset…on finding joy in the journey. Joy in the food. Joy in the exercise. I can’t guarantee that life can’t throw me curveballs and knock me off my path. I’ve showed the photos of how I’d done this same journey 10 years ago. So much of my current progress has come from the lessons I learned then. That journey wasn’t in vain.

I should be able to live this way forever. I won’t guarantee it.
