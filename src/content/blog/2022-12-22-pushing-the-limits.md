Thursday I do strength training but due to the storm it was canceled. I walked for an hour before the storm and decided to get a Supernatural workout in later in the day.

I’m solidly at the Medium intensity workouts and have dabbled a few times with high level but short duration ones. And yet…staring at me on today's list was Metallica….High level MONSTER workout. Monsters are just much longer routines. Haven’t even wanted to try one of those yet. But it was METALLICA….I had to give it a try.

Loved the first song and was wanting to quit when it was done. 6 more to go but my arms were already on fire. Off I went into the second. Fantastic, loved the choreography and I'm doing well.

But I still want to quit. I don’t, I press on but with a little less power. I focused on hitting all the targets and doing all of the lunges and squats.

Song 4 done and 3 to go. Time to towel off and unfog the lense and then onward I press. Just do one more and one more.

Final song and I'm ready to go out with a bang. Bring it on! My arms are on fire but it’s Enter Sandman … how can I not give it my all on that song?!

That song is Looooong. I hang in and finish strong. I have completed a High level monster workout.

My heart rate averages 130 with peaks in the 140s. My arms were on fire (in a good way).

I’ve not been one of those eager to do the harder routines. I’m happy doing L’s and only move up when the L’s don’t get my heart rate up enough. Now it’s the M's that don’t get my heart rate up where I want. Some day I’ll be crushing then H's. Then what? A delima for another day.

Push your body and it adapts. What’s hard today is easy tomorrow.

How much more? A little more.
How much farther? A bit farther.
Always.
