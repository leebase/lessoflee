Today was the obligatory steak and eggs breakfast. My grill is on the fritz and my daughter has a puzzle spread out on my pan-searing table in the garage. Then my Sous Vide stick stopped working and so instead of a quick sear, I pan fried the steak in the kitchen.

Holy Smokes Batman! I filled the whole house with delicious steak fumes. Was totally worth it. Delicious.

I had “finger food” for dinner. I may be the first person in history to eat this combination. Flaming hot pork rinds, cream cheese, cod liver and Vienna sausage. It was quite interesting, verging on tasty. More of a “the wife is gone and I’m not in the mood to cook” meal and a “how can I mask the flavor of these cod livers” meal. Mission accomplished. I will eat this meal again.

Why liver? It’s where so much of the vitamins are. It’s the broccoli, spinach and kale part of the animal food diet. I actually like beef liver. My dad used to make liver and onions. Just didn’t have any in the house. Cod livers come in a can and are super convenient and not nearly as expensive as grass fed NY strip steak.

Day 10 and my bs continues to be excellent – in the 80s and 90s. Still in mild to moderate ketosis. Knees feel terrific. A little bowel movement.

The whole “satiating” thing is true. I’m not snacking and it isn’t taking will power. I eat 2 large meals and fast about 18 hours.

Down 11.4lbs – and I’m really starting to believe the stall has been broken.
