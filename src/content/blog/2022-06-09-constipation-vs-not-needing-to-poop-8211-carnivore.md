Today was the first time I experienced a bit of constipation. I pooped daily the first 5 days, didn’t poop days 6 and 7, pooped day 8 – but day 9 is the first time I felt a bit constipated. No farts…seriously. That’s a plus.

Constipated is when you wish to poop, kind of feel the need, but can’t. Not having to poop is different. Sometimes there just isn’t much that needs expelling. We will see if this continues.

Bs was in the 80’s and 90’s. There is no doubt that carnivore has been wonderful for my blood sugar. I am always in ketosis or just below. That is also an improvement over my regular low carb diet.

My weight ticked up a bit today which is a good thing. It lets me know that I’m past just losing water.

Dinner was Wendy’s baconator double without the bun and veggies and a grilled chicken breast with cheese. Breakfast was eggs with cheese and sausage.

I had a wonderful mental health walk in the morning. I love this team of year with the cool morning weather. I did my strength training at night. Other than not eating veggies, my life moves on as per normal. Feeling fine. Well, my knees are feeling better than normal. I’d definitely say I’m beginning to experience the inflammation being less.

So far no scurvy or rickets.
