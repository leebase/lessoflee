It's been a month since I had my first meeting with my diabetes specialist who informed me of my 11.6 A1c blood glucose.  That's scary high.  In addition to taking the meds prescribed (one new med added), I have embarked on moving to a keto/low carb diet and am using "time restricted eating" (the word "fasting" seems to scare people).  

Most days I my eating window is 8 hours (16:8 intermittent fasting). I throw in one or two "2 hr eating window" (also known as One Meal a Day or OMAD).   I'm walking every day the weather permits.  

The results? My average blood sugar has gone from the 260's to the 130's. I am shocked and thrilled with how fast this has happened.

I have lost 8 lbs, for a 2lb a week average. While I'm quite happy about that, it's a lot slower than 9 years ago when I did a LOT less changing to get the same results. Welcome to getting older.

My arthritic knees feel so much better. This was surprising to me as I wasn't expecting it. After doing some research, I have found plenty of support that cutting back on sugars reduces inflammation and thus, reduces arthritis pain.

My walking distance has improved. I walked 5 miles today for the first time in a long time. 3.2 miles in my morning walk. Haven't "walked a 5k" in a great while either.

Yes, I am sometimes hungry. I am fasting after all. But really, it's not as bad as I thought. If I get too hungry, I eat something. But before I get to that point I drink some water or have a coffee (if it's not at night). On the "one meal" days, I'll have a "Bulletproof" coffee (add grass-fed butter and some MCT oil to the coffee) for lunch to quell the hunger and provide some "non-insulin raising" calories tide me over.
