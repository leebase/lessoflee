Which I never seem to learn. The kids were back home for the weekend and my oldest's baby shower. A weekend of eating food "a diabetic shouldn't eat".

My knees started aching again. I gained weight. Had blurry eyes first thing in the morning. All things that got better when I'm eating the food I SHOULD be eating.

"Didn't you learn this lesson already?" Yes, more than once. More than twice. More than three times.

"Why did you eat that food again?" Because it tastes good. Because it was there in my house when usually it isn't. Because…well…because in the moment I'm not thinking of the consequences.

It's not "diabetes keeping me from eating food I love" — it's "that food hates me and makes me fat and sick".

A lesson I apparently haven't YET learned. My daughter's dog was offering to help me out.
