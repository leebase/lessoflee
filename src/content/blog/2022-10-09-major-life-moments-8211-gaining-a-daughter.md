Meet Mrs and Mrs Harrington – welcome to the family Alyssa. Finally – I have a daughter that is into IT and works with data!

I ate all kinds of foods I shouldn’t and in amounts I shouldn’t. My BS was 104 this morning ![😀](~/assets/posts/2022-10-09-major-life-moments-8211-gaining-a-daughter/1f600.png)

Tomorrow it’s back to the way of eating that got me off 3 diabetes meds and my A1c from 11.2 to 5.6. Also down 85lbs – but probably not at this particular moment.

My arthritic knees are greatly improved. Up and down stairs and beach – carrying heavy things. Funny about that – I carried a heavy 35lb bag of food and thought – I lost more than twice this weight. And it was HEAVY to carry.

A year and a half of working on my health – and a lifetime to go. A longer healthier lifetime.
