From my daughter’s wedding in Oct through all the holidays I gained a lot of weight back so it was time to turn things back around. More on that later. I chose to do what worked so well for me last June – go to a animal based/carnivore way of eating. Today is the last day and once again, my body is telling me it loves me eating this way.

It’s kind of annoying. Well, it’s more than “kind of”. While I love eating meat and dairy, I also like nuts, seeds and veggies. Those are healthy – but I simply do better when I don’t eat them. I’m probably more ketovore than pure carnivore.

A common question is “what about your cholesterol, your LDL – you are going to have a heart attack”. Those are naturally asked when you see me eating 5 eggs and sausage or bacon from lunch. Fatty hamburger or steak for dinner. Well, I had my numbers run this week. All are excellent – even my LDL

HDL 46

LDL 76

Triglyceride 82

Total 138

Trig/HDL 1.78

Insulin is good

My Triglyceride / HDL ratio makes me very happy. Before I went carnivore last June, my ratio was 9 – you want it to be below 2! Even with all the weight loss and reversal of type 2 diabetes I had high risk factor heart attack. The Trig/HDL ratio is more predictive than high LDL. So why do doctors almost solely look at LDL? Because there are drugs to sell (statins). My original “diabetes isn’t your fault” doctor wanted me to go on statins (as well as Metformin, Glipizide, Trulicity and Insulin). I got off all the diabetes meds and never took statins. And now even my LDL is excellent.

My old hospital-nutritionist would have a fit if she saw what I eat. She wanted me eating grains and fruit as a diabetic. When I told her I was going to eat a ketogenic, animal heavy diet with non-starchy veggies she got indignant with me. Imagine how’d she’d react to not even eating the veggies.

Surely eating fat causes you to get fat and clogs your arteries. Not so. I can’t speak for everyone and never intend to. But my numbers tell the tale for my body.

“Didn’t you just gain a whole bunch of weight”. Yes, because I was eating sugar way too much. More on that story in another post.

I did manage to lose 12lbs this month. By bs this morning was 99.

Unlike last time, I’m going to keep going with this way of eating longer than a month.
