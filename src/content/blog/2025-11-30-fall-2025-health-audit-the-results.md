I had a rough year. I regained 85 pounds after having reversed my diabetes and held a normal A1c for two and a half years. I drifted, restarted badly, stopped, restarted again—it was a mess.

But this fall I finally got my mindset right and went back to the two most powerful techniques that I know work:

1-week water fast, then 2 weeks carnivore, repeat.

I’ve done 18 days of fasting total in the last 7 weeks, and carnivore/near-zero carb the rest. My doctor is informed and supportive.

This blood work was done earlier than would normally be done to see a change in the A1c but I wanted the numbers prior to the planned feasting of the Thanksgiving holiday.  It does provide a baseline for future comparisons as well as an excellent look at my current health.

Here’s what the labs show, without publishing every number.

1. The Headline: Reversing Diabetes

A1c: 6.6% (Down from 7.1% just six weeks ago)

Status: Significant improvement.

Context: A1c is a 3-month average. This number is blended between:

• Old Diabetic Me (Aug–Sept)

• New Healing Me (Oct–Nov)

My CGM already shows non-diabetic glucose patterns. This A1c will keep dropping.

2. Safety Check: Is My Protocol Hurting Me?

Here’s the real question everyone asks: “Is fasting + carnivore dangerous?”

According to my labs: No. My body loves it.

Kidney Function: PERFECT

• BUN and Creatinine are normal

• Microalbumin/Creatinine: so low it was undetectable

This is the gold-standard diabetic kidney test.  My kidneys are sealed tight—not leaking protein.

Thyroid: OPTIMAL

TSH is dead center healthy. No “starvation mode,” no metabolic slowdown.

Immune System: NORMAL

White blood cells, platelets, hemoglobin — all healthy.

Gout Risk: NORMAL

Uric acid right in the middle. So much for “red meat causes gout.”

Vitamin D: Healthy

Right where it should be.

Liver: Normal

Processing fasting, fat, and protein without any trouble.

Bottom line: This protocol is not harming me. All the main systems are operating cleanly.

3. The Genetic Win (Huge)

Lipoprotein(a): LOW (Optimal)

This is the genetically determined “widowmaker” marker—statins can’t fix it. You’re either born with high Lp(a) or you’re not. Mine is in the lowest-risk category. This isn’t because of diet. It’s simply fantastic news about my long-term heart risk profile.

4. The Challenges Still Ahead

I intentionally ate a bowl of rice the night before testing (“The Rice Hack”), to see how my metabolic machinery responded. It revealed exactly what I expected.

Fasting Insulin: HIGH

This is the biggest warning sign and the most important number of the entire panel. Even one bowl of rice kept my insulin elevated for 12+ hours.

This means:

• I am still significantly insulin resistant

• My pancreas works, but my cells don’t listen

• Carbs hit me like a freight train

• Insulin resistance remains my #1 battle — before, during, and after diabetes

• Explains how/why I gained 85 lbs in a year.  Everything in moderation was never going to work for me.  Not until I heal from my current high level of insulin resistance.

This is why I must fast. This is why carnivore works for me. This is why I regain weight if I “eat normally.”

Lipid Panel: Predictable/Expected

Because of the rice:

• Triglycerides: normal, but temporarily elevated.  Improved from my norm.

• LDL: mildly high (blunted by the rice)

• HDL: low (a long-term insulin-resistance marker)

LDL is the number everyone obsesses over because it’s tied to profitable medications. Mine is slightly elevated — but given the context, there is nothing surprising here. No “carnivore raises LDL dangerously”.  Not for me.

Inflammation (CRP): High

Likely acute/temporary:

• I felt like I had a mild cold

• I’d been lifting

• I’d been pushing hard for weeks

BUT my body appearance, joint pain, and CGM all suggest systemic inflammation is way down compared to months ago. This spike was almost certainly temporary.  Still, this value is high and my doctor and I will be discussing and keeping a watch on it.

Visceral Fat

Not a lab value — but obvious. I still have the classic “apple” shape, which is pure insulin resistance territory. By BMI standards I have about 125 pounds to lose.

This is the mountain I must climb. This is what my protocol is designed to destroy.

The Verdict – the protocol is safe and working

Now—it’s back to work.
