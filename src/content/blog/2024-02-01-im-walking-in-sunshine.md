I’ve been cooped up in the house for weeks due to the sub zero temps. Also the sun has been invisible behind drab clouds for weeks. But not today. Today it was a day of early promise of spring. It won’t last, of course, but it was nice to be outside wearing shorts, long sleeve shirt and sun glasses.

Someone mentioned recently that I used to write about walking. Yep, I sure did. I still walk far more than any other form of exercise. When I started, I was walking with a cane for 15 minutes or so around the neighborhood. In fact, my next door neighbor and I were just talking about that. He’s been across the street for 30 years and has seen me through many seasons. From early dad bod, to morbidly obese. Then in my 40’s I lost 80lbs and got fit in a year. Then over the next 10 years I gained all that weight back plus 20 more pounds. In addition I developed type 2 diabetes and osteoarthritis. Today we reminisced about him seeing me with my cane hobbling down the sidewalk.

Walking may no longer be that “extra push” that I’m doing to heal my body and reclaim my health and abilities – but it remains the foundation of my physical activity. I NEED my walks. I find them to be for mental health as much if not more than physical exercise. I have said many times that I don’t exercise to get good at exercise. It’s all about regaining my health. I had trouble walking at first and my exercise helped me get to where I could easily walk. Now I get to enjoy walking for the joy of walking.

There is still arthritis pain – just a combination of lesser inflammation, stronger muscles and a willingness to endure the pain. The joy, though. I’ve always enjoyed my walks. I make phone calls, listen to audio books, podcasts or music.

It’s good to be out walking again.

p.s.  Always consult your own health team for your specific needs

p.p.s. Educate yourself to fully participate in your health strategy
