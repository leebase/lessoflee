New Year’s Resolutions are like gym memberships — they sound great in January, but most people have abandoned them by March. I’m taking a different approach this year: no resolutions, just systems/habits.

It’t the new year and time for New Year’s Resolutions.  I’m a big fan of ANY day that you decide to improve your life.  For me, I’m taking a different tack this time.  Instead of Resolutions, I am committing to a few systems, or regular habits.

What’s the difference?  Resolutions to “lose 100lbs” or “lower my A1c” are wonderful, but they can also be daunting and promote feelings of failure.  A system is a healthy promoting practice that will lead to improvement.  In other words, focus on the process and not the destination.  

I had a fairly rotten 2024.  I have my excuses – er – reasons.  After a great start, I got off track – so far off track that “falling off the wagon” is a great description.  For excuses/reasons – I devolved back into many of my old bad habits and definitely my old bad diet.   My body didn’t care one whit about my resolutions and simply responded to how I treated it – gaining back much of the weight I had worked hard for a couple years to lose.

Now – I did have a great run for a couple years.  I learned a lot.  I accomplished a lot.  I read 25 or so books on health, changed my diet, fasted like a “fasting athlete”, exercised a little and a little more until I was able to exercise a lot.  I put Type 2 diabetes into remission, lost 85lbs, stopped needing a cane to walk and ran a 5k without stopping.

So, what now?  Since I regressed, do I throw out all I learned and try NEW ways?   Pretty much everyone who disagreed with me all along is sure that’s what I need to do.  “Go Vegan”, “just take meds”, exercise THIS way or THAT way. It’s fairly obvious that had I done any of those things – followed by “falling off the wagon”, I’d still have regained my weight.  Because that’s how everybody’s body works.  If you do  and it makes you fat, and then return to  — you will get fat again.

My plan is to focus on a few of the most powerful levers that helped me lose the weight and get my diabetes into remission.  A very low carb keto/carnivore diet.  Fasting, both intermittent and extended.  And exercise, both strength training and aerobics.  I will build my routine and discipline back up.  I will focus on the process, the systems of behavior, and let the results come in their own time.

I am not putting a time limit on myself to achieve x result by y date.  I really HATE that I regressed and have years ahead of me just to get back to what I’d already achieved. BUT, I’m not focused on the past.  Nor am I fretting about the future. 

I resolve to just do the daily doings, the systems that built my health back up before and will again.  

My past successes are just as irrelevant as my past failures.  I aim to live in the now and just live the life as I have learned brings health.

p.s. Always consult your own health team for your specific needs.  

p.p.s. Educate yourself to fully participate in your health strategy.
