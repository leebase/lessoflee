I look in the mirror and two men stare back.

The first has a face that has leaned out. I see contours I haven’t seen in a long time. Where has my double (triple) chin gone? My neck, what a difference. Onto the shoulders…dare I call them muscles I see popping up between my shoulder and neck? Never had those before.

Alas, the other man comes into focus. Flaps under my arms, and too much fat to see the burgeoning bicep. Onto the gut…that kegger is still there, spills out over my belt area. Smaller? For sure. But still “pregnant lady” big. I’m a fat man, obese. That I was even more humongous doesn’t keep my eyes despairing a bit over the work that remains.

A little turn to the side and craning of the neck and I can see both NoAssitol AND elephant-butt-itis…an odd combination.

Thick thighs but no longer “rub together when I walk” thick.

Then the first guy reappears when I look at my calfs. Look at these muscular beauties. Along with my feet the only part of me that isn’t fat at all. I can see my strength training nicely popping out muscles. Some day that will be my chest and biceps. But at least today there is some part of me that shows the work I’ve been putting in.

I have been on a journey to reclaim my health. Body recomposition is a nice side effect. I will be more attractive and fit in my sixties than ever before in my life.

Part of that man has started to appear. Much more work to do.

How much more? A little more.

How much further? A bit further.

Always
