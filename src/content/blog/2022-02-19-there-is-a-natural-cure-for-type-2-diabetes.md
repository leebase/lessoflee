Is there a natural cure for Type 2 Diabetes? D@mn straight there is.

And some of my best content comes from the “diabetes is forever” club who never tire of discouraging folks trying the natural way. I wrote this as a comment but it deserves to be a post.

Last April I was on Metformin, Glipizide and Trulicity and was scheduled to go on insulin. I am now off all of those and never needed to go on insulin.

It took only 3 months to get off the meds, and I have a lot more w metabolic syndrome to heal from. Diabetes is just one facet.

Anywho…what if…years from now….I stop eating as I should, stop exercising as I should. I know…I’ve done this once before. It happens.

All those meds will still be available. Insulin will still be available. Just as I got off my blood pressure meds before and had to go on them again years later.

That’s years of good health. That’s resetting my health point. If I hadn’t done the work then….how fat would I have gotten to? How much more severely diabetic? Hear attack? Stroke?

Yes…good health is not a steady state. You have to keep eating healthy and keep moving healthy and keep sleeping healthy and keep a healthy response to stress.

You know…just like you have to bathe regularly and brush your teeth regularly. There is no end…until THE end.
