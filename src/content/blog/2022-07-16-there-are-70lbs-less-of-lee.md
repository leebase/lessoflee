There are 70lbs less of Lee.  

I hit this milestone in the middle of my fast last month. I don't count my low points that occur during my 5 day fasts.

Today I hit it after my long walk with "sprint" intervals. I always count my lowest weight as "my weight" until the next lower. So for now, I'm down 70lbs.

Before long that will be my weight when I wake up in the morning and not after a walk/run.

After that I will be down 70lbs at the end of the day.

Then I'll be down 70lbs after dinner.

Then 266 lbs will be in my rear view, never to be seen on my scale again as I continue on toward a healthy weight.

I'm still obese, but climbing down from class 3 to class 2. Then class 1. I have a good ways to go to be merely "over weight".

I'm only 10lbs away from the mark I hit 10 years ago when I lost 80lbs in 10 months. It's been 15 months already and I have to lose 20 more than before to get down to the 230 I reached back then. I'm also having to work a LOT harder than before. Doesn't matter. I move forward.

My goal weight is 215. Lots of work to do.
