Here's a video from Doctor Berry (author of Lies My Doctor Told Me) regarding the improvement of osteoarthritis that comes with a low insulin diet (ex very low carb or carnivore).



I found this to be true for me. I cut sugars/carbs out of my diet in an effort to heal my type 2 diabetes (it worked!). A couple weeks into it I noticed that my knees felt better. I wasn’t expecting it – and then went on to confirm that there are actual studies in this. One such study is linked in this video.

My whole approach to my arthritic knees changed from “what natural pain rub” or “what herbal anti-inflammatory” can I take to replace NSAIDS or diclofenac. Now I’m working on health. Proper diet (for me). Exercise. Sleep. Getting to a healthy weight (a gargantuan task).

This mirrors my approach to healing my type 2. I’m not looking to “eat what I want” and then “what pill can I take to lower my blood sugar”.
