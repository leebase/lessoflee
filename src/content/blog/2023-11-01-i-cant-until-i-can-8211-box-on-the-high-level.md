It’s been awhile since I wrote a “I can’t … until I can” post. I’m 2.5 years into my health reclamation journey. There were so many things I couldn’t do, that now I can. I have added another new ability: I can do the high level boxing workouts of the Supernatural app.

Supernatural has two types of exercise, flow and boxing. Boxing is the most difficult for me. So much bobbing and weaving, keeping your arms raised and throwing punches. It’s not real boxing, but it’s challenging enough for me. My knees, balance and arm strength were not up to the task at first.

The high level was impossible. I couldn’t react fast enough. I couldn’t dodge and weave as quick as needed. I couldn’t keep my arms up with the speed of the punches. Basically, the targets came at an overwhelming pace I couldn’t come close to keeping up with.

That was then. This is now. I’ve completed my second high level workout. Short but intense, just two songs worth. It’s progress.

It’s the middle of the week and and I do a nice high level flow workout. Dance music and coach Dawana spurring me on. The routine was fire! So fun.

A nice hard midweek exercise session, but I wanted a bit more. And now I can add a high level boxing routine as extra oomph.

It snowed this week. I got the virtual reality headset to let me workout at home when the snow kept me from walking outside. My balance was so bad with my arthritic knees that I was practically a shut in during winter.

No longer. The value in exercise isn’t the ability to do harder exercise. It’s freedom and mobility.

I welcome you to join me in overcoming limitations and making your world a larger place. You get there by doing more and going farther.

How much more? A little more.

How much farther? A bit farther.

Always.

p.s. Always consult your own health team for your specific needs

p.p.s. Educate yourself and be a full participant in your health strategy
