It’s Mother’s Day weekend

I’m out of town

I didn’t sleep much the night before

Too many mosquitoes

The hotel WiFi isn’t working well

I’ve been eating off plan

Where am I going to run

Those and more kept me from working out Saturday until about 6:30pm. I finally pushed past my excuses and went out to run a 5k.

Strange area, hard concrete. And I didn’t feel like running. I couldn’t run fast. No sprints or speed work today.

In a way it was good prep for my first actual race coming up this week. I just started and trusted that I could make it to the end.

It took about a kilometer for me to begin to feel like running.

I can do this. Now.

I’ve gone from walking with a cane to running a 5k by doing more and going father.

How much more? A little more.

How much further? A bit further.

Always.
