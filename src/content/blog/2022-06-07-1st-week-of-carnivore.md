7 days down, 23 more to go. I didn’t want to do carnivore. I’ve been hearing about it on support groups, watching YouTube videos. I bought a couple books. But I didn’t want to do it myself.

And then I hit a stall. I haven’t lost significant weight in the last 5 months even though I’m eating low carb, fasting 18hrs a day and once a month I do a 5 day fast. I’m also strength training and putting on some amount of muscle. Not enough to explain the lack of weight loss IMHO. I’ve shown photos, my body is busy at work reconfiguring itself. It’s just not been all that interested in losing weight.

Also – I have high triglycerides, low HDL and a terrible ratio – and that’s a recipe for heart disease. More so than having high LDL. Which, btw, my LDL has come down and is only slightly elevated.

So, I decided to give carnivore a month as a stall breaker and an elimination diet. I’ll get my blood work done after it’s over. But in the mean time – I have to say I’m impressed with the progress.

I’m down 10lbs. I wouldn’t be TOO excited as I know I can quickly drop and regain weight when I do my once a month 5 day fasts. But this time I’m not fasting. I’m eating a lot. Maybe the fabled “woosh” has finally happened, spurred on by the complete elimination of carbs.

My bs has been in the 90’s all day. When I wake up, at dinner time and before I go to bed. Without meds (I’ve been off them for almost a year) and without my herbal supplements. This shouldn’t be too surprising as I’m not eating sugar at all. Just what my liver creates from the fat and protein.

I’m not tired, I’m not hungry, I’m not “hangry”. I eat until I’m stuffed and then I’m not hungry for hours on end. No snacking after dinner and either no snacking between the meals or a lot less than normal.

Today, they 7th day, was the first day my knees felt better. When I do extended fasts, I usually can feel it by day 4. There is definitely something inflammation inducing by my old diet, and apparently, even my low carb diet could be improved upon. When I introduce veggies back in – one at a time – maybe I’ll find out which ones I have problems with.

I am in ketosis. Not the raging ketosis I get to by days 3 and 4 of an extended fast. But, at least I am in ketosis. I’m not normally outside of the extended fasts.

I’m eating well. I eat a lot. Of course there are foods I like to eat that I’m not eating for the month of June. This feels far more restrictive than going from the standard American diet I was on to eating just meat/fish and non-starchy veggies. I was kind of hoping this wouldn’t work for me as it’ll be a real tough decision if I found out that I thrive on carnivore.

Today’s meal were eggs with cheese, sausage and bacon for breakfast. Fajita chicken and brats for dinner. Is that tomatoes in my chicken? Yes. I wasn’t thinking and I put in my usual bottle of salsa in with the chicken in my InstantPot. But I still ate it. I forgave myself and chowed down. Sue me, purists. Of course, the purists object to the dairy as well.

If I don’t talk about the pooping, people will ask anyway. Pooped just fine the first 5 days. Didn’t poop yesterday or today yet. I’m not constipated. You won’t poop as much when you are eating carnivore because most of the meat is actually absorbed. No kernels of corn just passing through and coming out the other end.

This is an “n of 1” experiment. I have read enough to have no fear of adverse health consequences. However, I am not advocating this for anyone. I don’t know AS much about it as the other things I’ve been doing. I’m learning and sharing my experience.
