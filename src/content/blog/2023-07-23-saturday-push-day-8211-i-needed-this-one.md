It’s been “terrible, horrible, no good, very bad, day”. No need for the details, just setting the stage for my mindset when I arrived back home at 7:30pm in the evening. I hadn’t done my “Saturday Push Day”. That’s ok. Working on your health is about consistency over time, not slavery or obsession over goals and schedules.

And yet…

What is good for when you are tired, worried and stressed? A nice hard workout. Really? If you are tired you should work out? Yes. If you are worried and stressed, you should work out? Yes. I could have run to the store bought some chips, dip, ice cream and chocolate sauce. I could have ate myself into a food coma to provide temporary comfort. The Supernatural (the VR workout app I use) coaches words are embedded in my brain now. They have become a part of my “good angel” on my shoulder. “What you need to do is show up for yourself and give your body what it really needs – a nice hard workout”.

I did the high level “Molten Rock” workout with coach Leanne. 20 minutes of taking out my frustration, anger and worry on flurry of targets. The routine designers at Supernatural are on fire…these are so fun and creative. Not to mention great for getting my heart rate up.

After I did some pushups and squats. I’ve been so lax with my strength training ever since stopping TheExerciseCoach. But for this day, I “did something” rather than thinking that if I couldn’t do a whole routine, I wouldn’t do anything.

Exercise doesn’t make everything better. All the reasons for the stress and worry are still with me. My experience of that situation is improved. I did my body good and that makes everything better, by doing more and going farther.

How much more? A little more.

How much farther? A bit farther.

Always.
