I don’t exercise to lose weight.

Let’s consider this past Saturday. I went out and ran intervals for 5k. I also played table tennis for an hour. A lot of exercise for one day. According to my app I burned something like 1200 calories.

I’d have to do that 3 times to lose a pound according to “calorie math” where 3500 calories is required to burn a pound of fat.

That pizza I ate? 3,000 calories.

I don’t buy into the calorie model for losing weight. However, if I did, clearly you can’t out exercise a bad diet.

More important than calories are hormones. High sugar meals stoke insulin which puts your body into storage mode. If your body is in storage mode…and you eat fewer calories….your body will adjust and slow down. You’ll get tied and you will be very hungry as your body is storing more of what you eat.

So I do exercise…for all the rest of the health benefits.

To lose weight, burn fat, I avoiding eating sugar/carbs. Fats and proteins satiate. You won’t be as hungry and you will thus eat less. You won’t be in energy storage mode, but fat burning mode (ketosis). Combined with the exercise your energy level rises and exercise/activity becomes natural.
