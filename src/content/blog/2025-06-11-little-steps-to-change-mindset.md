Supernatural is my favorite way to exercise.  It’s an aerobic workout set to great music in a virtual reality setting.  I hadn’t worked out in awhile, so I choose a medium level for 25 minutes.  It was a lot of fun, and while I meant it to be easy, it was hard because I hadn’t worked out in so long.

My foot is about 80% of normal.  I don’t need NSAIDS for pain relief any more.  I am keeping the orthotic insoles in my shoes and I worked out with shoes on instead of bare foot.  I had the tiniest difficulty with balance but only a small amount of discomfort.  Yet again, I see my body can heal.

But MAN was I sore a half hour after the workout.   Apparently I had overdone done it while thinking I was taking it easy.  It was just inflammation, not injury.  I was fine the next day.  I’ll shorten my next workout.  Turns out I hadn’t worked out since January!!?  How did that happen?  Btw, there is a term for this: DOMS – delayed onset muscle soreness.  It is indeed an inflammation, not injury.  I took an ibuprofen only because the pain was significant and walking was difficult.  I really did think at first I had injured myself.  I try to stick to natural remedies, but I needed something for the acute pain.  And didn’t need any further.

I’d forgotten that I kept getting sick this past winter.  Eating terribly has consequences besides outgrowing my shirts. My eating has improved, though it’s not clean yet.  I’m feeling the urge to exercise and plan on restarting my strength training.

I can feel my brain coming out from being stuck.    I’m not all the way back but I’m making progress.   I just had to get myself started.   Make a good choice,  it a perfect day of good choices.

I hadn’t realized just how far I had let myself go.  Time to get back to body building.  No, not oiling myself up and posing.  Eww.  That’s an image I didn’t need in my head.  I mean that I’m back to working on my body, building back health. 

I just have to do more.

How much more?  A little more.

How much farther? A bit farther.

Always.

p.s. Always consult your own health team for your specific needs.  

p.p.s. Educate yourself to fully participate in your health strategy.

p.p.p.s.  I really do strive to not use NSAIDS, chronic use is hard on the kidneys.  This acute pain level needed immediate help.  And I’m doing the exercise and dietary changes that keep me from using them daily like I used to.
