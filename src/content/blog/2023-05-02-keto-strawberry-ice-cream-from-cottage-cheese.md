Here is a recipe for keto ice cream using cottage cheese, frozen strawberries, and Lakanto powdered monkfruit:

Ingredients:

• 1 cup cottage cheese

• 1 cup frozen strawberries

• 1/4 cup Lakanto powdered monkfruit

• 1 teaspoon vanilla extract

Instructions:

1. Add all ingredients to a bowl and blend with a stick blender until smooth.

2. Pour the mixture into an airtight container and freeze for at least 4 hours, or overnight.

3. Once frozen, scoop and enjoy!

This recipe is a great way to enjoy a delicious and refreshing ice cream treat without any guilt. The cottage cheese provides protein and calcium, while the strawberries add sweetness and antioxidants. The Lakanto powdered monkfruit is a natural sweetener that is zero-calorie and sugar-free.

Tips:

• Use full-fat cottage cheese for the best texture.

• Make sure the strawberries are frozen solid before blending.

• Add a splash of heavy cream or milk for a creamier texture.

• Experiment with different flavors of frozen fruit.

• Enjoy!

Here are the macros for the keto ice cream recipe:

• Calories: 180

• Fat: 14 g

• Protein: 12 g

• Net carbs: 4 g

• Fiber: 2 g

This recipe is a great option for those following a keto diet. It is low in calories and carbohydrates, and it is a good source of protein. The strawberries add sweetness and antioxidants, and the Lakanto powdered monkfruit is a natural sweetener that is zero-calorie and sugar-free.
