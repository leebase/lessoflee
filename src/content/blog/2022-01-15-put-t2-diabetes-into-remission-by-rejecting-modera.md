Moderation, by definition, is deprivation. You have to make changes to get different results. If you eat all the foods you’ve ever been eating…just “in moderate amounts” you are still depriving yourself from what you used to eat.

And that may work for you depending on your level of insulin resistance.

Wearing a cgm (continuous glucose monitor) cured me of “everything in moderation”. My first time with a cgm and going to McDonalds…where I ordered less food than I’d normally ordered….I saw my blood sugar skyrocket to 400. It was in the 260s at the time normally. And that was with taking the meds.

I read the book The Diabetes Code by kidney doctor Jason Fung. His advice was quite contrary to what my dietician had told me. But, my doctor (at that time) and dietician never expected me to stop being a diabetic. “it’s not your fault, it’s genetic, here take these meds. Eat grains, low fat.

Well….I took a chance on what Dr Fung (and many others) was teaching. Intermittent fasting and a low carb diet.

In 3 months I was off all the meds, and three months after that I met the qualifications of having my diabetes in remission (A1c less than 6.5 without taking meds or insulin).

I’ve since fired that doctor and that dietician…and love my life as a former diabetic who is continuing to work on reversing the entire stack of metabolic syndrome health problems
