Tomorrow I start my monthly 5 day fast. I went back over the last 5 months of my stall.

My starting weight each month was within 2 lbs of 290. My weight after the fast was within 2 lbs of 275. On average I’d lose 15 lbs over the 5 days. But by the next month, I was back where I started. That’s why I decided to switch things up a bit and go carnivore-ish for the month of June.

My lowest weight, yesterday was 273.1 – so I’m starting 17lbs less than where I’ve been on average over the last 5 months.

It would expect that my weight loss won’t be in the 15lb range because I’m starting from a lower sugar status. And I’d imagine the bounce back won’t be as high as I will still be eating the carnivore-ish diet. My liver isn’t carrying as much glycogen because I’m eating hardly any carbs.

I’m already in ketosis and I don’t normally get there until day 2 and sometimes 3. It will be interesting to see when I get into the optimal ketosis range. It’s usually day 3 – and I have not got there during this month of a carnivore-ish diet.

I’m projecting that I’ll be burning more fat than normal as I’ll be in fat burning mode from the get go.

It will be interesting to see what difference there will be with hunger. Again, I’m already in ketosis and already experiencing less hunger.

Typically my knees start feeling awesome around day 4. Wonder if that will happen sooner.

I’m hoping that I will continue on losing weight and that my stall will truly be over. Been working so hard. I’ll eagerly await the 2 days after the fast is over and see where I end up.

Monday, the end of the fast, I’m having a dexascan and I’ll get to see the body fat change. Particularly interested in the visceral fat amount. Then after the end of the month I’ll get my blood work done.
