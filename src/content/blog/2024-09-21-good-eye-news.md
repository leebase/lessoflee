Today my eyes passed with flying colors.  Last fall I had a “micro stroke” in my left eye.  A small blood vessel burst and fluid filled my eye obscuring my vision.  I couldn’t read from that eye.  The eye specialist was never worried about the fluid and he was right.  It cleared up in a matter of weeks.  However, he discovered I had swelling in my retina.  It was JUST on the border of needed eye injections.  But he held off for a month expecting me to need them, but my eyes got better.  And as of this morning, he has declared them completely resolved.  I will have them checked in a year, but could have waited for two.  {happy dance}

Also noticed was that my glaucoma had worsened.  It’s still not to the point of affecting my vision, but if it continues to worsen, it will.   It’s been about 9 months of adjusting the medications.  But we seemed to have found the right combo and my eye pressure was good today.  {more happy dancing}.

Everything about your health is not a matter of diet and exercise.  Of course.  And it really sucks when faced with health issues that there is no “do xyz and you can get better”.   But folks, make no mistake, start eating and moving for health and SO MUCH of your health can indeed get better.

We have been a lot closer to animals a lot longer than we’ve been modern humans.  Modern foods and lifestyle have made us ill.  You don’t have to go off into the mountains or jungle and live in a hut.  But you will do well to just eat like we had a million years evolving to eat.  We’ve only had agriculture for 12,000 years.  In the last 100 years?  So much of what we eat, we never ate before – and that’s why there has been an explosion in illness even as we have healthcare and medications and technology unimagined not so long ago.

And we have industries dedicated to making them money – and keeping you hooked.  Both Big Food and Big Pharma – are not your friends.

Eat real natural foods.  Real food spoils.   Real food doesn’t have 50 ingredients.  The reason we can get better when we stop eating modern manufactured “food like substances” is because we aren’t diseased — the MODERN FOODS ARE THE DISEASE.

Make no mistake, all this nonsense about meat and animal fat being bad for you is propaganda by big business who’s profits are in peril if you stop eating their products and taking their drugs.

Ok…getting off my soap box.  It’s a happy day for me with the good news about my eyes.  Wishing you the best of days brought to you by your own good choices.
