I can’t bowl well, mind you.  But for my birthday my children thought going bowling would be fun.  And it was.  The non-scale victory is that my knees and legs have improved enough that I could actually bowl.

The sad fact is that when your health declines, there are more things that you just can’t do any more…like play tennis or badminton…or bowl or play basketball.  

Slowly but surely I’m clawing my way back.  I’m playing table tennis in virtual reality almost every day.  I’m nowhere close to being able to play tennis, but that day will come.  I’d have a good deal more improvement in leg strength to regain before I could bowl well, but at least I can start.

It’s frightening to ponder on how frail I was going to be in my 70s…if I even lived that long.  By the end of this year or next, I may well be stronger and more fit than I have ever been.
