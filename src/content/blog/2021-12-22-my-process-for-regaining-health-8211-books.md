How do I keep my motivation? What is my process?

I’m still pondering the motivation question as I don’t “feel” particularly motivated. How do I explain all the years I knew what to do – had done it – and yet, couldn’t get my mojo going again? What’s different now.?

So while I can’t yet explain “how to be motivated” – I can share some of my process for making changes. For me, engaging my mind is necessary. I dive into any topic that I'm currently focusing on whether it’s my career, being a dad, learning to cook, anything.

Here are the books I’ve read this year as I engaged once again on my Journey to Health.

12/21/21 The Clot Thickens – Dr Malcom Kendrick (kindle)

11/20/21 The Great Cholesterol Myth – Johnny Bowden, Stephen Sinatra (kindle,audible)

11/01/21 Burn – Herman Pontzer (kindle, audible)

08/30/21 Lies My Doctor Told Me – Dr Ken Barry (kindle, audible)

08/17/21 Metabolical – Robert Lustig (Kindle, Audible)

08/16/21 The Case for Keto – Gary Taubes (Kindle, Audible)

07/12/21 Life in the Fasting Lane – Jason Fung (kindle, audible)

06/22/21 Fast This Way – Dave Asprey (kindle, audible)

05/21/21 The Diabetes Code – Dr Jason Fung (kindle,audible)
