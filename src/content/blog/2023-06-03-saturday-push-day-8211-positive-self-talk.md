Be as encouraging to yourself as you are to others. This thought came to me as I’m out on the trail pushing myself that much more and going a bit farther. My heart rate was above 90% of my max when this young woman with her dog WALKED past me! Some push day. I’m so very slow. I came in the bottom 10% of the racers a couple weeks ago, doing my very best and still slower than many walkers.

I don’t like to go to a gym because I’m embarrassed to be seen as weak as I am. When I first started on the trails I was very conscious of how morbidly obese I was. And yet, never has anyone ever said a mean word. It’s my OWN self talk that is discouraging. It’s me, judging myself and being unkind and I’d never be that way to someone else.

Before you encourage me – I ask you – are you talking positively to yourself? As you prepare to give me a boost, commit to being your own hype man. Next time you are experiencing self criticism, take a step out of yourself. Imagine you are someone else working on their health. What do you say to them? Do you put them down? Do you kick them over and over for their past mistakes? Tell yourself what you’d say if you were speaking to a friend. Lift yourself up the way you would somebody else.

Let’s cheer ourselves on to doing more and going farther!

How much more? A little more.

How much farther? A bit farther.

Always.
