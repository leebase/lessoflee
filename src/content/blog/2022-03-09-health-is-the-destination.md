I hit 5.2 on my A1c, so I’m done, right? Unfortunately – not even close.

I am excited for having reached a huge milestone…but it is only a milestone. Health is the destination. Ignoring the very important issue of health maintenance for this post…there is a lot more work to be done in regaining my health.

First – diabetes is a symptom, not the disease. Or…it’s a LEVEL of disease progression. The disease is insulin resistance/hyperinsulinemia. We type 2's have been developing the disease of insulin resistance for decades before your body gets to the point of not being able to control blood sugar.

Insulin resistance is at the center of a host of diseases including heart disease, cancer, PCOS, Alzheimer’s and many more. Read Why We Get Sick by doctor and scientist Ben Bickman for further education on this.

I have heart disease, I have osteoarthritis. I am still obese. Very obese. Who knows what other insulin resistance effects are in progress in my body that just haven’t revealed themselves yet.

Thinking of diabetes and bs control as the disease is WHY “type 2 diabetes is a progressively degenerative disease”. Taking drugs to control the bs does not address the disease – insulin resistance. And as the disease isn’t being addresses, it just gets worse. That means meds, then more meds, insulin then more insulin. That’s your disease getting worse. And you get sick anyway.

What heals insulin resistance? Lowering your insulin. What does that? Eating foods that don’t raise insulin as much (low carb diet) and going longer time between eating (fasting). Also, increasing your muscle mass – strength training which helps you have more places for sugar to go and more insulin sensitivity of you muscles.

Do those and you will heal your insulin resistance – which is your disease. Do that and you will cure your obesity as well. Any method of losing weight will also help with insulin resistance.

As you can see in this fairly recent photo – I have a lot of work to do. I have not arrived. The journey isn’t over. And a lifetime of work remaining healthy will follow.
