It’s Saturday and I don’t ask if I feel like pushing myself (I usually don’t), I just go do it.

Today was the first day after my 5 day monthly fast. I had difficulty with a medium workout yesterday and there’s no way I was going to be able to go run sprints.

I decided to do the medium boxing workout with the artist Muse. Boxing workouts are harder for me and are a good way to push myself out of my comfort zone. So much bobbing and weaving and keeping my hands up. Great music, fun routine.

I needed more time so I did a short low level flow workout. I tried hard to go easy and work on breathing. I wanted to quit a couple times – the hallmark of pushing myself.

Herbie, my imaginary workout partner was so excited as today was his first time working out with his brand new black belt.

My heart rate told me I was doing moderate exercise. My arm muscles and abs were telling a different story. It’s kinda cool to see that my progress makes me have to work that much harder to get my heart rate up. How I felt over all let me know that I had indeed had pushed myself.

I’m also going to push myself in another way – I’m cooking liver and onions. My dad used to make it when I was growing up. I liked it then but never made it myself. I have been working on ways to add liver to my diet as it’s so good for you.

How much more? A little more
How much farther? A bit farther
Always
