It’s that time of the month when I embark upon a 5 day water only fast.

I’ve been doing this since last December when I asked my doctor about such a fast. I was thinking annual. He said “sounds great, you should do so once or twice a month”!!!

How can you go without food for 5 days? I don’t. I go without putting food in my mouth, true. I’m eating the whole time...just eating my own fat. And boy do I have a lot of food stored around my body just waiting to get me through the famine that never came.

Doesn’t your blood sugar crash? No. Your liver creates glucose after you “eat through” the days worth it has stored. My bs falls into the low normal range and stays there. It’s the only time I experience completely normal levels of bs.

Don’t you feel tired? The opposite, by day three I’m brimming with extra energy as ketone production (the by product of fat burning) kicks into high gear. I do everything I normally do including exercise.

“When I fast my glucose goes up”. Yes, in the short term than can and does happen. It’s because you are insulin resistant and your counter regulatory hormones go up as your insulin levels fall when you fast. In that state, when your liver releases glucose, it just hangs around until you burn it. It doesn’t get stored. This is a good thing. The low insulin state allows your insulin resistance to heal. That is your diabetes healing. Normal bs represents about a teaspoon of sugar. If your bs rises to 150, that’s just a half teaspoon more. Your body doesn’t see this as an emergency so it doesn’t rally insulin to take care of it. A medium banana has 4 teaspoons of sugar. A Coke has about 10 tsp of sugar. That’s why your bs spikes up and then drops, as it’s an “emergency” to have that amount of sugar entering the blood. Not so with “the dawn phenomena” – which is what you experience when fasting. You will use up that sugar and then experience normal bs the longer you fast.

“I get starving angry/hungry if I don’t eat every few hours”. That’s because your body is running on glucose. Once your body begins to run on fat, the experience of going without food feels quite different.

“Don’t you get hungry?” Of course. But it goes away. If you eat, the hunger goes away. If you don’t eat, the hunger goes away. There are great benefits to not eating so you can learn to ride out the hunger wave. By day 3, many people stop feeling hungry. Remember, your body isn’t running on empty, it’s eating your own fat.

I didn’t start out with 5 day fasts. Like with running a 5k, I built up to it. And like my 5k training, I don’t always feel like doing it. I’ve made it a habit and just begin my fast anyway.

I don’t always make the full five days. That’s ok. You can’t fail fasting. Whatever you do benefits your body.

What is allowed? I drink water, coffee and tea without sweeteners. Mostly just lots of water. Some people drink bone broth and some even a little cream in their coffee.

What about vitamins? I take salt and electrolytes. You definitely need salt. You’ll get light headed…and that is fixed with salt. You won’t become vitamin deficient in 5 days. The salt is essential. I don’t know which supplements might break the fast so I just do without.
