I played VR table tennis for just shy of an hour yesterday. Then I remembered that I had set some goals in the beginning.

- To reverse my Type 2 diabetes defined as “control my blood sugar at a non-diabetic level via diet and exercise”.
- Get down to a healthy weight – defined as “not overweight”, which for me is going to be somewhere around 180lbs
- Fitness: Be able to vigorously exercise for an hour.

I’ve achieved goals one and 2. I found this list from my blog's “2 Month Update”. I forgot just how fast my blood sugars came down. From the 260s to the 110s in 2 months. I was still in the process of getting off the meds at that point.

Meanwhile…it’s taking a lot longer to lose the weight. I lost 15lbs the first 2 months. If I’d been able to keep that pace up I’d be down 90lbs. I am down 50

I’m so glad I’ve written these blog posts so I can remember the journey.
