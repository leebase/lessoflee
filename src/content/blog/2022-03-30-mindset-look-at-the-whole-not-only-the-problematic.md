I have to admit, I’m frustrated. I’ve been expecting my blood pressure and triglycerides to improve greatly. Neither one has. My prior experience with losing weight, and pretty much everything I’ve read – leads me to believe that those two are usually quick improvements when going on a very low carb diet.

It’s been 11 months and my blood pressure hasn’t improved, and I still need to take my bp medication. My triglycerides were improving, albeit slowly. Then this last checkup and they rose sharply, higher than when I started. And my HDL dropped when I wanted it to go up.

But – I’m doing that thing that I also do as a father. My kid shows me her report card and I skip over all the A's and B's and ask “what’s with this D”? I take all the good in stride and laser focus in on whatever needs improving.

My LDL improved a good deal, for the first time. My A1c continued its improvement. My official A1c came in at 5.8 – which is higher than the the home test of 5.2. But – it was 11.2, then 6.5, then 6.2 and now 5.8…without any diabete meds for the last 8 months.

My weight loss has been frustratingly slow the last couple of months and yet more inches have been coming off. Oh…but the belly inches…the one I’m most concerned about, seems to come off the slowest.

There is a truth about my default mindset – no matter how much progress I make, I want to focus on what hasn’t improved. I didn’t get into this condition in a day, and I’m not going to heal it all in a day. The difference is that I wasn’t paying attention before, and now I am.

I’m healthier than I’ve been in years. I’m looking better than I have in years. I’m feeling better than I have in years. I’m clawing back ability after ability. I’m heading in the right direction. I just have to remind myself of the overall picture and not just the problematic numbers.
