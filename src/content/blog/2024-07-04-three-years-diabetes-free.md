It was three years ago in April that my doctor said “Diabetes isn’t your fault, it’s genetic. We are adding the $1000 per month Trulicity to the Metformin and Glipizide you are already taking. Come July, we will put you on insulin”. My A1c was 11.2 – and she was laying out the plan for getting my diabetes under control. Only, I didn’t listen to her. Or rather, I decided to DO something about my health as I KNEW that my actions, not my genes, was responsible for diabetes. I then read The Diabetes Code by Dr. Jason Fung. He believes Type 2 Diabetes can be reversed – and he’s not alone, just happened to be the book I read first.

What happened?

Come July, rather than starting Insulin, I was able to get off the last of the meds! I went strict NSNG (no sugars, no grains) that I had learned from Vinnie Tortorich years prior but had fallen away from. Meat, fish and non-starchy veggies. Plenty of delicious food to eat.

From Dr Fung I learned the benefits of fasting and started with intermittent fasting. It was tough stopping snacking after dinner, but skipping breakfast wasn’t hard.

And I started walking. I also have osteoarthritis and walked with a cane and drove with a handicap sticker.

It took only three months To go from highly diabetic even with meds to non-diabetic free of diabetes meds. That was three years ago and I’ve remained free of diabetes and free of the need for diabetes meds ever since.

It was just the beginning of what I dubbed my health reclamation journey. The light had gone on and I came to grips with my culpability in my poor health and disease states.

I lost a lot of weight and yet I still struggle with weight. It’s a struggle at the fridge or even grocery store. I’ve proven time and again that eating fattening food works. I gain fat easily. It’s no mystery but my resolve to eat well remains a work in progress. I ate myself sick, and I’ve eaten myself healthy.

However, the health gains have been tremendous as I do the right things more often than the bad. It doesn’t take perfection, but it does take persistence. You don’t get obese, diabetic, arthritic and weak in a day and you don’t heal in one day either.

I’ve gone from walking with a cane and struggling with stairs, to running 5k's and sprint intervals. There are no words that can describe how much better life is when mere walking is no longer a challenge.

I no longer pop antacids all day long.

I no longer snore.

I haven’t had an anxiety attack in years.

I am not pain free but have MUCH less.

I get sick less often and recover faster.

My resting heart rate has gone from the 80s to the low 60s.

I still take blood pressure meds but my pressure is better.

My cholesterol and triglycerides have greatly improved.

This isn’t the complete list of all the ways my health has improved.

I am so glad that I am arrogant enough to think I might know better than my doctor. Type 2 diabetes is NOT a genetic destiny. My genes may indeed set me up for illness WHEN I eat modern processed foods, grains and sugars. BUT – I can choose to eat real food, meat, fish and non-starchy veggies (veggies optional).

That day my doctor took me off the last of my diabetes meds, that day where I was supposed to start on insulin had I followed her “take meds” plan….that day I asked: Why aren’t you teaching what I did?

My doctor said, “Most people aren’t willing to make the changes you did”.

As I read on theses online forums, I have to conclude my doctor is right. Most people.

Most people.

Most people will not do the hard work of learning what is healthy for them to eat, refrain from eating carb laden foods, fast and exercise. And keep on improving, keep on doing the hard things to reclaim their health.

My doctor was WRONG about me.

All along my journey we talked about my choices. She knew EVERY principle I followed. She knew they would work. She was very supportive of all I did and reduced my medications one at a time during that three months. She just didn’t teach those thing to me or anybody else because she’s convinced that people won’t make the changes.

My doctor was wrong about me. Is she right about you?

I’m not a unicorn. I’m not a miracle. People by the tens of thousands are turning their health around.

It’s hard. But you can do hard things.

Yes. You can.

3 years and counting in my health recovery journey. A lifetime more to go.

p.s.  Always consult your own health team for your specific needs
