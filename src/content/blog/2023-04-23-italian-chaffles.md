Italian-Style Low-Carb Mini Chaffles

Prep Time: 5 minutes

Cook Time: 9 minutes (3 minutes per chaffle)

Total Time: 14 minutes

Servings: 3 mini chaffles

Ingredients:

• 1 cup low-moisture mozzarella cheese, shredded

• 2 tbsp almond flour

• 1/2 tsp xanthan gum

• 1 large egg white

• 1 tsp Italian seasoning

• Optional: salt and pepper to taste

Instructions:

1. Preheat your Dash Mini Waffle Iron. Make sure to lightly grease or spray it with non-stick cooking spray if it isn't a non-stick waffle iron.

2. In a medium-sized mixing bowl, combine the shredded low-moisture mozzarella cheese and almond flour. Stir until the cheese and almond flour are evenly distributed.

3. Add the xanthan gum and Italian seasoning to the cheese and almond flour mixture. Mix well to ensure even distribution of the ingredients.

4. In a separate small bowl, whisk the egg white until it becomes slightly frothy. This will help to create a better texture for the chaffles.

5. Add the whisked egg white to the cheese and almond flour mixture. Stir until all ingredients are well combined and the mixture forms a thick, slightly sticky dough. If desired, season with salt and pepper to taste.

6. Divide the chaffle dough into three equal portions. Place one portion in the center of the preheated Dash Mini Waffle Iron, then close the lid. Cook for about 3 minutes or until the chaffle is golden brown and crispy on the edges. The exact cooking time may vary depending on your waffle iron, so keep an eye on it.

7. Carefully remove the cooked chaffle from the waffle iron using a fork or silicone tongs, then place it on a wire rack to cool slightly. Repeat the process with the remaining chaffle dough portions.

8. Enjoy your Italian-style low-carb mini chaffles! They can be served with a variety of toppings, such as marinara sauce, fresh basil, or a sprinkle of grated Parmesan cheese. These chaffles also make a great base for a sandwich or can be used as a low-carb alternative to pizza crust.
