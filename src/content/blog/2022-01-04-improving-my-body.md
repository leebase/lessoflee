I never cease to be amazed at the concept that your body can get better. I started out barely able to complete a low level exercise in Supernatural. I have osteo arthritis knees. Morbidly obese and sedentary beyond walking.

Now I’m doing medium routines and doing a middle age white man's version of dancing as I’m wacking away at the targets. I couldn’t be more thrilled with the Oculus Quest 2 and Supernatural app for exercise.

It’s winter in Chicago with snow on the ground. I couldn’t go for a walk right now even if it wasn’t below freezing outside. Inside….I’m working out in some of the world's most beautiful places…dancing along to great music and working up a sweat.

Also, I convinced another middle age friend of mine to get one. We used to play ping ping decades ago. Now we are playing each other most every day. Me in my home, he in his. Very competitive and fun exercise.

Outside of exercise I have noticed an marked improvement in my stability. I Could put on my pants without having to sit down or grab a wall. Barely…but I could. I can easily and comfortably stand on the scale.

Onward and upward. I will be starting some resistance exercises soon.
