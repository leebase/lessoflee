A tale of three charts of progress.

Weight loss was fairly rapid at first then the 5 month stall and then resumption of the loss. Have to keep in mind it’s not a sprint but a life long journey.

The second is perhaps the most significant. My resting heart rate has gone from the 80s into the 50s. If you can believe it, my rate falls into the excellent category and is verging on athletic. Me…athletic!

I’ve written before that I’m “less unhealthy”…like a car that was speeding along at 180mph and slows down to 140mph. That’s improvement but it’s still speeding. Diabetes is in my rear view mirror, but heart disease is still a present concern. My resting heart rate is an important metric and goes to the value of exercise. I want to be healthy, not just less unhealthy.

The third chart is my average heart rate while walking. You can see that my heart is having to work less hard as my fitness has improved. What you can’t see on the chart is that my average walking pace has gone from 28min per mile to 22. I’m walking faster and my heart is having to work less hard even so.

The uptick at the end is me starting to jog and do intervals to get my heart rate up. One of my mantras is: How much? A lttle more. How far? A little farther.

As your body adjusts to what you are doing, you have to up the intensity or lengthen the duration to keep making progress.

Health – I want ALL the health. Diet and exercise and sleep and stress management. Progress, not perfection. But always working for a little more progress.
