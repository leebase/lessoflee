Me walking with a cane a couple years ago. Me just before I ran my first 5k without walking 2 weeks ago.

In between has been a complete health reclamation journey that started when my A1c hit 11.2 and Trulicity was added to the metformin and Glipizide I was already taking and I was scheduled to start insulin.

What did that have to do with arthritis? Cutting the sugars out of my diet for my diabetes greatly decreased my knee pain. Then as I began to exercise with squats and lunges (which I could barely do), again for helping my metabolism, I noticed that I could walk better.

It finally dawned on me that my trouble walking wasn’t just arthritis but weakness.

I’ve continued walking, strength training and aerobics – pushing back the limitations a little more day by day.

This has helped me far more than any pill, herbal remedy or lotion…and I tried them all.

How much more? A little more
How far? A bit farther
Always
