I haven’t posted a new ability in a LONG time.  There used to be fairly regular wins as I regained abilities, like walking without a cane.  Over the past year I’ve been regressing, not making progress.

But tonight I earned my first Diamond. Well, i think I earned one on the very lowest and shortest level a long time ago.   I’m still at the beginning of recovering my fitness and this moment happened!

I work out with the VR Supernatural app.  It has gamification that I mostly ignore,  what matters is moving your body – and pressing for a bit more.  And I saw myself move up over the years from the easiest and shortest levels that I could barely do.  I fell a couple times at first.  But my body responded and I got better and didn’t need a cane to walk any more.  I moved up in time to the very hardest and longest workouts they have.   Testing myself, pushing myself – just a bit more.

And I’d get triple platinum scores over and over.  Never a diamond.  I was Mr Triple Platinum on my best days.  I stopped even trying for diamonds as I didn’t want to be discouraged.  Use gamification when and if it motivates you, ignore it when you feel discouraged.

Tonight, gamification was VERY encouraging.  What an unexpected surprise.  It was only a 21 minute, medium difficulty routine, but it wasn’t easy for my current level of fitness.

Onward I go, pressing for more.

How much more?  A little more.

How much farther? A bit farther.

Always.
