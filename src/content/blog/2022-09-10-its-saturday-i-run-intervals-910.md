My company is having a “steps challenge” for September. I set a goal to walk 10k a day. That’s about 40% more than I average and it’s difficult to do every day with my arthritic knees. What has been my once a week long walk is now my daily amount.

I walk out to the trail and I do not want to run. But it’s Saturday and on Saturdays I run intervals with the goal of being able to run a 5k without stopping. That day won’t be today.

I run about 30 seconds and then walk already. On and on with those very short intervals. I think I’ll just run the first mile and then walk the rest.

On I go running to that flower or the beginning of shade. Just a bit farther. I almost never make it as far as I intend before walking again.

I keep at it, it’s only for a mile. The mile comes and I’ve loosened up a bit. So I keep going – I’ll run intervals for two miles and walk the rest.

I get to the second mile and my intervals are longer than when I began. Feeling fine. Why stop now when it’s only 1.1 more to go?

I have to push myself the last half mile. Nothing too bad but I really was ready to stop and just walk home.

I make it, 5k in under 45 minutes. Slower than the last couple weeks. The victory was in persisting to completion.

Listen your body but push it was well. I felt better at the end than the beginning. I’ve made the 10k steps every day but one so far. How much? Just a little more. How far? Just a bit farther.

I weighed myself after and finally broke into the 250s at 259.4 – plenty more work to do. Making progress.
